# 9.4.2 和現有設定的衝突

所屬：[9.4 變更前檢查](../9-4.md)

## 練習題

**1.** When a NE performs autonomous re‑configuration, what does the specification require to avoid conflict with existing settings?

- A. The change can be applied silently.
- B. The NE must shut down before re‑configuration.
- C. The re‑configuration should always be reported.
- D. The NM must reject the re‑configuration.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The text states that autonomous re‑configuration must be reported, which helps keep other systems aware of the change.

</details>

**2.** To prevent conflicts when maintenance personnel access NE data locally, what method is recommended?

- A. Directly modify NE data via the OS.
- B. Apply a remote OS terminal for local access.
- C. Use only automated scripts without supervision.
- D. Disable the NM during maintenance.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Using a remote OS terminal ensures changes are controlled and can be reported to the managing system.

</details>

**3.** After the NM retrieves all management information via Itf‑N in the audit procedure, what must it do next?

- A. Store the data without further processing.
- B. Compare the retrieved information with its own data.
- C. Immediately forward the data to the operator.
- D. Delete the retrieved information.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The NM is required to compare the newly retrieved data with its own records to detect inconsistencies.

</details>
