# 9.4.4 變更後驗證

所屬：[9.4 變更前檢查](../9-4.md)

## 正文

When a configuration change is applied to a 5G core or radio network, the operator must confirm that the intended service quality is still delivered. In the 3GPP framework, this confirmation is performed by measuring the end‑to‑end Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) defined in TS 28.554 §6. If the KPI values after the change deviate from the baseline, the change may have introduced a regression that could affect accessibility, reliability, or capacity for subscribers. Continuous verification therefore protects the network’s Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and prevents silent degradation.

### KPI‑driven verification checklist
The verification step consists of collecting the KPI measurements that are directly impacted by the change and comparing them against the pre‑change baseline or against contractual thresholds. The most common accessibility KPIs used for this purpose are:

| KPI | Symbol | Definition | KPI type |
|-----|--------|------------|----------|
| Mean registered subscribers of network slice through AMF | **AMFMeanRegNbr** | Mean number of subscribers registered to a network slice instance (counted in AMF) | MEAN |
| Registration success rate of one single network slice | **RSR** | Ratio of successful registration procedures to attempted registration procedures for the AMF set related to one network slice | RATIO |
| Partial DRB Accessibility for UE services | **Partial DRB Accessibility** | Product of RRC connection setup success, NG signalling connection setup success, and DRB setup success (excluding mo‑Signalling causes) | RATIO |
| PDU session establishment success rate of one network slice (S‑NSSAI) | **PDUSessionEstSR** | Ratio of successful PDU session establishment requests to attempted requests for all SMF related to one network slice | RATIO |
| Total DRB accessibility for UE services | **Total DRB accessibility** | Ratio of successfully established DRBs to the number of services intended to be set up, taking into account RRC and NG‑connection success ratios | RATIO |

Each KPI is measured over the appropriate scope (e.g., **SubNetwork**, **NetworkSlice**, **NRCellCU**) as indicated in the specification. The verification engineer must therefore select the KPI(s) whose scope matches the part of the network that was changed.

### Formulas and symbol definitions

*Registration success rate (RSR)*  

$$
\text{RSR} = \frac{\text{Number of successful registration procedures}}{\text{Number of attempted registration procedures}} \times 100\%
$$

*Partial DRB Accessibility (per 5QI)*  

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Partial DRB Accessibility}_{5\text{QI}} = &
\left(\frac{\sum \text{RRC.ConnEstabSucc}.\ast\text{Cause}}{\sum \text{RRC.ConnEstabAtt}.\ast\text{Cause}}\right) \times
\left(\frac{\sum \text{UECNTXT.ConnEstabSucc}.\ast\text{Cause}}{\sum \text{UECNTXT.ConnEstabAtt}.\ast\text{Cause}}\right) \\[4pt]
& \times
\left(\frac{\text{DRB.EstabSucc.5QI}}{\text{DRB.EstabAtt.5QI}}\right) \times 100
\end{aligned}
$$

*Total DRB Accessibility (per 5QI)*  

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total DRB accessibility}_{5\text{QI}} = 100 \times
\frac{
\text{DRB.InitialEstabSucc.5QI} +
\bigl(\text{DRB.EstabSucc.5QI} - \text{DRB.InitialEstabSucc.5QI}\bigr) +
\text{DRB.ResumeSucc.5QI}
}{
\frac{\text{DRB.InitialEstabAtt.5QI}}{(\text{RRC conn. setup}/100)\times(\text{NG‑conn}/100)} +
\bigl(\text{DRB.EstabAtt.5QI} - \text{DRB.InitialEstabAtt.5QI}\bigr) +
\frac{\text{DRB.ResumeAtt.5QI}}{\text{RRC Resume}/100}
}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Symbol glossary**  

* **RRC.ConnEstabSucc** – successful RRC connection establishments (excluding mo‑Signalling).  
* **RRC.ConnEstabAtt** – attempted RRC connection establishments (excluding mo‑Signalling).  
* **UECNTXT.ConnEstabSucc / Att** – successful / attempted UE‑associated logical NG‑connection establishments (excluding mo‑Signalling).  
* **DRB.EstabSucc.5QI / Att** – successful / attempted DRB setups for a given 5‑QI.  
* **RRC Resume** – success ratio for RRC resume procedures (excluding RNA update).  

All ratios are expressed as percentages unless otherwise noted.

### Worked example: verifying a slice‑wide registration change
Assume a network slice was re‑configured to add a new AMF instance. Before the change, the operator recorded the following 5‑minute counters for slice *S1*:

| Counter | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Successful registrations | 9 200 |
| Attempted registrations   | 10 000 |

The pre‑change **RSR** is therefore  

$$
\text{RSR}_{\text{pre}} = \frac{9\,200}{10\,000}\times 100\% = 92\%
$$

After the change, the same 5‑minute interval yields:

| Counter | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Successful registrations | 8 500 |
| Attempted registrations   | 10 000 |

The post‑change **RSR** becomes  

$$
\text{RSR}_{\text{post}} = \frac{8\,500}{10\,000}\times 100\% = 85\%
$$

Because the post‑change RSR falls below the operator’s SLA threshold of 90 %, the verification step flags the change as a regression. The engineer would either roll back the configuration (as described in §9.4.3) or investigate the cause (e.g., AMF overload, mis‑aligned routing). This simple arithmetic demonstrates how KPI formulas directly support post‑change validation.

### Common pitfalls and practical notes

* **Ignoring scope qualifiers** – KPIs must be collected over the exact scope defined in the spec (e.g., *NetworkSlice* for RSR). Measuring at a broader *SubNetwork* level can mask slice‑specific regressions.  
* **Including mo‑Signalling attempts** – The Partial DRB Accessibility formula explicitly excludes setups with establishment cause *mo‑Signalling*; counting them inflates the denominator and yields an artificially low accessibility value.  
* **Mismatched time windows** – KPI baselines should be taken over the same measurement interval as the post‑change values; otherwise seasonal traffic variations may be misinterpreted as a configuration impact.  
* **Neglecting cumulative KPIs** – For KPIs of type *CUM* (e.g., **AMFMaxRegNbr**), the verification must compare the peak observed value after the change to the historic maximum, not to a mean value.  
* **Automated collection** – Manual extraction of counters is error‑prone. Operators are encouraged to use the standardized measurement objects defined in 3GPP TS 24.501 (e.g., *RRC.ConnEstabSucc.*Cause*) to feed the KPI calculators automatically.

By grounding post‑change verification in the KPI definitions and formulas of TS 28.554 §6, engineers obtain an objective, repeatable method to confirm that a configuration change has not degraded network performance.
