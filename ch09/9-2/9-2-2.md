# 9.2.2 相依條件

所屬：[9.2 參數](../9-2.md)

## 正文

In a 3GPP‑managed network the parameters that appear in a Managed Information Base (MIB) are not independent; their validity depends on a set of **dependency conditions** that tie together the Network Manager (NM), the Element Manager (EM), and the Network Elements (NEs). These conditions ensure that the *logical* view held by the NM/EM matches the *physical* representation inside each NE. If a parameter is changed without respecting the dependency conditions, the network can exhibit incoherent behaviour, leading to service degradation or fault propagation.  

### Where Information Is Stored
The reference model distinguishes two architectural choices for storing Managed Object Instances (MOIs):  

* **NE‑local MIB** – the MOIs exist only on the originating NEs. Consistency therefore needs to be preserved **only between the NEs**.  
* **Mirrored MIB** – a copy of the MOIs is also kept in one or more OSs (e.g., the NM/EM). Consistency must be preserved **between the NEs as well as the NM/EM and the NEs**.  

The choice is product‑specific and influences the subsequent audit procedures [TS 32.600 §4.2.1.3]​.  

### Consistency Maintenance Procedures
Both architectural choices rely on a set of operational steps that the NM must execute:

* **Mirrored MIB**  
  1. The NM retrieves *all* management information from the EM and each NE via the Itf‑N (periodically or on request).  
  2. The NM compares the retrieved data with its own copy and, if needed, also compares related information between connected NEs.  
  3. Any deviations are reported to the operator.  
  4. The NM (or the NEs, depending on the designated “master”) automatically corrects the deviating information.  

* **NE‑local MIB**  
  1. The NM retrieves *all* management information from the EM and each NE via the Itf‑N.  
  2. The NM compares the retrieved information **between connected NEs**.  
  3. Deviations are reported to the operator.  
  4. The NM (or the NEs) automatically corrects the deviating information in the NEs.  

These steps are explicitly recommended in the specification [TS 32.600 §4.2.1.3]​.  

### Example of Consistency Check
Consider a small cell cluster with two NEs (NE‑A and NE‑B) that share a common parameter **`cellLocalId`** (a mandatory, readable, writable, notifyable attribute defined in TS 28.541). Assume the network uses the *mirrored MIB* approach and the NM stores the value **`12345`** for both NEs.

| Entity | Stored `cellLocalId` |
|--------|----------------------|
| NM (mirrored) | 12345 |
| NE‑A (local)  | 12345 |
| NE‑B (local)  | 12340 |

**Step 1** – The NM retrieves the attribute from NE‑A and NE‑B.  
**Step 2** – It finds a mismatch: NE‑B reports **12340** while the NM holds **12345**.  
**Step 3** – The NM generates a deviation report to the operator.  
**Step 4** – Because the manufacturer designates the NM as the master for `cellLocalId`, the NM pushes the correct value **12345** to NE‑B, restoring consistency.  

The example illustrates the logical condition  

$$
\forall \text{NE}_i\;:\; \text{MOI}_{\text{NM}} = \text{MOI}_{\text{NE}_i}
$$  

which must hold for all managed attributes when a mirrored MIB is employed.  

### Practical Tips and Common Pitfalls

* **Never assume autonomous re‑configuration is invisible.** The specification mandates that any local NE re‑configuration, whether triggered autonomously or by an operator, **should always be reported** [TS 32.600 §4.2.1.3]​.  
* **Audit frequency matters.** Periodic retrieval of the full MIB is essential; relying solely on event‑driven updates can leave silent divergences.  
* **Master selection is manufacturer dependent.** When the NM and NE disagree, the corrective action follows the master role defined by the vendor [TS 32.600 §4.2.1.3]​.  
* **Remote OS terminals are preferred for local access.** Direct manual changes to NE data without OS control break the consistency chain [TS 32.600 §4.2.1.3]​.  
* **Confusing the two approaches leads to false alarms.** Applying a mirrored‑MIB audit procedure to an NE‑local‑MIB deployment (or vice‑versa) will generate unnecessary deviation reports.  

By respecting these dependency conditions, engineers can guarantee that parameter changes propagate correctly across the management hierarchy, preserving network stability and service quality.

## 練習題

**1.** 哪一項**不是**文件中列為「一致性」所依賴的條件？

- A. 哪些資訊在NE與管理系統之間交換
- B. 資訊在NE與管理系統之間的交換方式
- C. NE的硬體供應商
- D. 資訊儲存於哪裡（NE‑local MIB或mirrored MIB）

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。文件列出四項依賴條件，並未提及硬體供應商。

</details>

**2.** 在「mirrored MIB」方法的程序中，NM在取得管理資訊後必須執行哪一步？

- A. 立即自行更正所有差異
- B. 將取得的資訊與自身資料進行比較
- C. 刪除取得的資訊
- D. 不經分析直接轉交給操作員

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。程序要求NM先比較取得的資訊與自身資料，以判斷是否有差異。

</details>

**3.** 在「mirrored MIB」方式下，決定哪一方為「master」以修正偏差資訊的依賴條件是什麼？

- A. NM永遠是master
- B. NE永遠是master
- C. 取決於製造商的決策
- D. 由操作員手動指定

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。哪一方為master取決於製造商的實作選擇。

</details>

**4.** 哪項程序是為了確保「自主NE重新配置」被視為相依條件而必須執行的？

- A. 定期資料檢索
- B. Awareness of autonomous NE re‑configuration
- C. Mirrored MIB 同步
- D. 使用遠端OS終端機

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。文件明確要求對自主的NE重新配置保持警覺並通報。

</details>
