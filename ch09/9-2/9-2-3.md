# 9.2.3 不同設備版本的差異

所屬：[9.2 參數](../9-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following CM functions is *not* listed as employed for a system up‑grade in the specification?

- A. Creation of NEs and/or NRs
- B. Deletion of NEs and/or NRs
- C. Modification of NEs and/or NRs
- D. Monitoring of traffic

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。The spec lists creation, deletion, and modification, but does not list monitoring as a CM function for upgrades.

</details>

**2.** According to the specification, what must be true before new software can be activated on a network element?

- A. The corresponding hardware functionality must already be available.
- B. All ongoing traffic must be permanently blocked.
- C. The operator must obtain a regulatory licence.
- D. The configuration must be logged for audit.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The upgrade sequence requires that new software depends on the availability of the new equipment functionality.

</details>

**3.** Which capability allows an operator to create a logical NE/NR without the physical hardware being present?

- A. Logical provisioning
- B. Physical provisioning
- C. Software patching
- D. Traffic inhibition

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The spec permits creation of an additional logical NE/NR without installed physical resources.

</details>

**4.** When an NE/NR is extended, under what condition may it be placed back into service?

- A. Immediately after the extension command.
- B. After all needed parameters and equipment are initialised.
- C. After traffic has been inhibited for 10 seconds.
- D. After the operator logs the change.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states that an extended NE/NR cannot be placed into service until all required parameters and equipment are initialized.

</details>
