## 練習題（8 題）

### GSMA 練習題（8 題）

**1.** According to the O-RAN Drafting Rules, what is the correct interpretation of the modal verbs "shall", "should", "may", etc. in O-RAN specifications?

- A. They are interpreted based on their common English usage.
- B. They are defined in clause 3.2 of the O-RAN Drafting Rules.
- C. Their meaning is determined by the context of the document.
- D. They are not used in O-RAN specifications.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `oranbench-train-70`）

</details>

**2.** Which of the following statements accurately describes the normative nature of information within the main body of the Open Radio Access Networks (O-RAN) Conformance Test specification?

- A. All information within the main body is informative, meaning it is not binding on compliant systems.
- B. All information within the main body is normative, meaning it is binding on compliant systems, unless explicitly described as informative.
- C. Information within the main body is only normative if it is described as "optional".
- D. Information within the main body is only informative if it is described as "optional".

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `oranbench-train-294`）

</details>

**3.** Which of the following modal verbs are **NOT** allowed in O-RAN deliverables, except when used in direct citations?

- A. shall, shall not, should, should not, may, need not
- B. will, will not, can, cannot
- C. must, must not
- D. all of the above

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `oranbench-train-478`）

</details>

**4.** According to O-RAN Drafting Rules, which of the following modal verbs is NOT allowed in O-RAN deliverables except when used in direct citation?

- A. Shall
- B. Must
- C. May
- D. Can

正解：B　（GSMA `oranbench-train-570`）

</details>
