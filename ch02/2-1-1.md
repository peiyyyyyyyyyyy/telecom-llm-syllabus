### GSMA 練習題（4 題）

**1.** Which TSGs are responsible for the definition, development, and maintenance of the overall system architecture and service capabilities?

- A. Radio Access Network (RAN) TSGs
- B. Service and System Aspects (SA) TSGs
- C. Core Network and Terminals (CT) TSGs
- D. SA WG 1
- E. CT WG 1

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `teleqna-train-2477`）

</details>

**2.** Which TSGs (Technical Support Groups) are responsible for conformance testing of UEs and base stations?

- A. Radio Access Network (RAN) TSGs
- B. Service and System Aspects (SA) TSGs
- C. Core Network and Terminals (CT) TSGs
- D. RAN WG 5
- E. SA WG 5

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A　（GSMA `teleqna-train-2758`）

</details>

**3.** Which TSGs are responsible for the definition of terminals, interfaces, and capabilities?

- A. Radio Access Network (RAN) TSGs
- B. Service and System Aspects (SA) TSGs
- C. Core Network and Terminals (CT) TSGs
- D. CT WG 1
- E. RAN WG 1

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `teleqna-train-5691`）

</details>

- A. RAN and SA only

- C. RAN and CT only
- D. SA and CT only
