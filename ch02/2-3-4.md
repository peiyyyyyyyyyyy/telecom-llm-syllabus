## 練習題（5 題）

### GSMA 練習題（5 題）

**1.** What is a Downlink operating band?

- A. The part of the operating band designated for uplink transmission.
- B. The part of the operating band designated for downlink transmission.
- C. The part of the operating band designated for bidirectional transmission.
- D. The part of the operating band designated for multicast transmission.
- E. The part of the operating band designated for unicast transmission.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `teleqna-train-1647`）

</details>

**2.** What is noncontiguous transmission?

- A. A STA that is a potential service set transition candidate.
- B. A basic service set (BSS) operating on the same channel as the STA's BSS.
- C. A transmission that uses nonadjacent frequency segments.
- D. The operating channel in which beacons are transmitted.
- E. A channel that is not the operating channel of the basic service set (BSS) of which the STA is a member.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `teleqna-train-4164`）

</details>

**3.** What is a contiguous transmission?

- A. A transmission that uses multiple frequency segments
- B. A transmission that uses only one frequency segment
- C. A transmission that occurs at a continuous time interval
- D. A transmission that occurs at a random time interval

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `teleqna-train-5380`）

</details>

**4.** What is channel spacing?

- A. The concept that, for a given multi-octet numeric representation, the most significant octet has the lowest address.
- B. A neighbor mesh STA that meets eligibility requirements to become a peer mesh STA.
- C. The difference between the center frequencies of two non-overlapping and adjacent channels of the radio transmitter.
- D. A station (STA) that receives a physical layer (PHY) protocol data unit (PPDU) using a beamforming steering matrix.
- E. A matrix used to determine the channel between a transmitter and an intended receiver.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `teleqna-train-5711`）

</details>

**5.** What is the Base Station RF bandwidth?

- A. The frequency of one of the edges of the Base Station RF bandwidth
- B. The time during which the base station is receiving data subframes or UpPTS
- C. The bandwidth in which a Base Station transmits and receives multiple carriers and/or RATs simultaneously
- D. The management of specific radio resources in a set of cells
- E. The allocation and release of radio resources for establishing connections

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `teleqna-train-7045`）

</details>
