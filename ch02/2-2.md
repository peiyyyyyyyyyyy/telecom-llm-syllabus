## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What are the high-level features specified for 5G Location Services? [3GPP Release 16]
>
> A. LCS Quality of services
> B. LMF selection, UE LCS privacy handling
> C. LCS Quality of services, LMF selection
> D. UE LCS privacy handling, LMF selection
> E. UE LCS privacy handling, LCS Quality of services

正解：B. LMF selection, UE LCS privacy handling

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the size of the eCPRI common header?
>
> A. 4 bytes
> B. 8 bytes
> C. 16 bytes
> D. 32 bytes

正解：A. 4 bytes

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which interface is used to forward short messages in the network with the GLR in case of SMS transfer over GPRS? [3GPP Release 17]
>
> A. GLb-interface
> B. GLg-interface
> C. GLe-interface
> D. GLf-interface
> E. GLj-interface

正解：D. GLf-interface
