## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What regulatory obligations can a Participating Operator have regarding PWS support? [3GPP Release 18]
>
> A. Initiating the broadcast of PWS messages
> B. Receiving PWS messages from other Participating Operators
> C. Ensuring PWS messages are delivered to UEs
> D. All of the above

正解：A. Initiating the broadcast of PWS messages
