# 2.2.7 規範用語：shall、should、may

所屬：[2.2 規格文件的結構](../2-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** According to TS 21.801 §6.6.1, why are clear rules for verbal forms essential?

- A. To enforce legal contracts automatically.
- B. To identify obligatory requirements and distinguish them from optional provisions.
- C. To simplify translation into other languages.
- D. To reduce the length of the specification.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The text states that clear rules are needed so users can identify mandatory requirements and separate them from provisions with freedom of choice.

</details>

**2.** When is it permissible to use an alternative expression to the primary verbal form prescribed in Annex E?

- A. Whenever the author prefers a shorter sentence.
- B. Only when the primary form cannot be used for linguistic reasons.
- C. When the clause concerns performance metrics.
- D. If the document is being translated into another language.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The standard allows alternative expressions only in exceptional linguistic cases.

</details>

**3.** Which statement best reflects the legal status of a 3GPP TS by itself?

- A. It automatically creates binding obligations for all implementers.
- B. It imposes obligations only when referenced by legislation or contracts.
- C. It nullifies any national regulations that conflict with it.
- D. It grants patent rights to the authors of the specification.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The TS does not impose obligations by itself; obligations arise from external sources such as legislation or contracts.

</details>
