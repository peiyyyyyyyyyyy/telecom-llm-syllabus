## 練習題

**1.** What prefix is used for a Technical Specification in the 3GPP numbering scheme?

- A. 3GPP TS
- B. 3GPP TR
- C. 3GPP TSR
- D. 3GPP SP

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The reference explicitly defines “3GPP TS aa.bbb” for Technical Specifications.

</details>

**2.** Is it permissible to deviate from the provisions of Table 2 for backwards compatibility?

- A. Yes
- B. No
- C. Only with 3GPP approval
- D. Only for Technical Reports

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The reference states that deviation from Table 2 is acceptable for backwards compatibility.

</details>
