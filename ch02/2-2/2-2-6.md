# 2.2.6 變更請求（CR）與版本號

所屬：[2.2 規格文件的結構](../2-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Who is allowed to raise a Change Request (CR) for a 3GPP specification?

- A. Only the Working Group Chair.
- B. Any individual member of the 3GPP community.
- C. Only the TSG Secretary.
- D. Only the Support Team.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The reference states that a CR may be raised by any individual member.

</details>

**2.** What must be allocated to each Change Request to ensure uniqueness?

- A. A version number.
- B. A unique reference number (CR‑No).
- C. A priority level.
- D. A deadline date.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The CR is assigned a unique reference number by the portal or Support Team, and numbers are never reused.

</details>

**3.** Who is responsible for collating all approved CRs and sending them to the parent TSG?

- A. The Support Team.
- B. The TSG Chair.
- C. The TSG WG Secretary.
- D. The Specification Owner.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The WG Secretary collects approved CRs and forwards them to the parent TSG.

</details>

**4.** When two or more CRs target the same specification version, what must the responsible Group do?

- A. Approve all of them automatically.
- B. Reject all of them to avoid conflict.
- C. Check for potential interaction amongst those CRs.
- D. Combine them into a single CR.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The reference requires the group to ensure that approved CRs do not contradict each other.

</details>
