## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleTables】**

> Considering the 'if reported' conditions in the table, which resource group has the highest number of mandatory fields that must always be included in the report?
>
> A. 1st resource group
> B. 2nd resource group
> C. 3rd resource group
> D. 4th resource group
> E. All groups have the same number of mandatory fields

正解：A. 1st resource group

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What standardized QCI values can be used for unicast delivery of V2X messages over LTE-Uu? [3GPP Release 14]
>
> A. QCI 1 and QCI 79
> B. QCI 3 and QCI 75
> C. QCI 5 and QCI 78
> D. QCI 7 and QCI 76
> E. QCI 9 and QCI 80

正解：B. QCI 3 and QCI 75
