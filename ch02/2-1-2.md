**1.** Which stage introduced eCall for EPS over E-UTRAN? [3GPP Release 14]

- A. Stage 1
- B. Stage 2
- C. Stage 3
- D. Stage 4
- E. Stage 5

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `teleqna-train-748`）

</details>

**2.** Which 3GPP working group is responsible for defining the security aspects of LTE based V2X communication? [3GPP Release 14]

- A. SA WG1
- B. SA WG2
- C. SA WG3
- D. CT WG1
- E. CT WG3

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C　（GSMA `teleqna-train-959`）

</details>

**3.** What is the purpose of standardising service capabilities? [3GPP Release 18]

- A. To make it difficult for operators to differentiate their services
- B. To reduce the complexity of providing services across different operators' networks
- C. To limit the types of services that can be introduced
- D. To increase the cost of introducing new services

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B　（GSMA `teleqna-train-3246`）

</details>

**4.** Which TSG (Technical Support Group) is responsible for security?

- A. RAN WG 1
- B. SA WG 1
- C. CT WG 1
- D. SA WG 3
- E. RAN WG 3

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D　（GSMA `teleqna-train-4505`）

</details>

**5.** What is the priority of NG-RAN SA, EN-DC, and MR-DC in RAN3? [3GPP Release 17]

- A. NG-RAN SA is prioritized; EN-DC and MR-DC are down-prioritized
- B. EN-DC and MR-DC are prioritized; NG-RAN SA is down-prioritized
- C. All are equally prioritized in RAN3
- D. NG-RAN SA, EN-DC, and MR-DC are not part of RAN3

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A　（GSMA `teleqna-train-6802`）

</details>

**6.** Which protocols are specified by CT WG1 for V2X communication over the PC5 interface? [3GPP Release 14]
