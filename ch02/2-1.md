## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What are the three groups into which SONs (Self-organizing networks) were divided by 3GPP Rel.8 (2008)?
>
> A. C-SONs, I-SONs, D-SONs
> B. C-SONs, H-SONs, R-SONs
> C. C-SONs, D-SONs, H-SONs
> D. D-SONs, G-SONs, L-SONs
> E. R-SONs, D-SONs, T-SONs

正解：C. C-SONs, D-SONs, H-SONs

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the maximum value for QoS Priority Level?
>
> A. 126
> B. 127
> C. 128
> D. 100

正解：B. 127

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What are the key functionalities supported by the common services core in MC services? [3GPP Release 14]
>
> A. Group management
> B. User authentication
> C. Service configurations
> D. All of the above

正解：D. All of the above
