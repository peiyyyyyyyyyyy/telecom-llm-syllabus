# 2.1.4 ITU 與 IMT 需求

所屬：[2.1 標準體系](../2-1.md)

## 練習題

**1.** What is the ITU‑R target peak data rate for downlink in IMT‑2020?

- A. 10 Gbps
- B. 20 Gbps
- C. 30 Gbps
- D. 40 Gbps

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The ITU‑R specifies 20 Gbps as the downlink peak‑data‑rate target.

</details>

**2.** According to ITU‑R, what is the downlink target for peak spectral efficiency?

- A. 15 bps/Hz
- B. 20 bps/Hz
- C. 30 bps/Hz
- D. 45 bps/Hz

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The downlink target is 30 bps/Hz, as stated in the KPI definition.

</details>

**3.** Which frequency range corresponds to FR1 as defined by 3GPP?

- A. 100 MHz – 300 MHz
- B. 410 MHz – 7125 MHz
- C. 10 GHz – 20 GHz
- D. 24.25 GHz – 52.6 GHz

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。FR1 is defined as 410 MHz to 7.125 GHz.

</details>
