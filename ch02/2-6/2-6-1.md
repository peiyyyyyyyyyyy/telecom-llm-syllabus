# 2.6.1 附上文件、章節、版本

所屬：[2.6 引用規則](../2-6.md)

## 練習題

**1.** How must references be identified according to the specifications?

- A. By the author's name only
- B. By date of publication, edition number, version number, etc.
- C. By the document's length in pages
- D. By the color of the cover page

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The reference must be specific and include identifiers such as date, edition, or version.

</details>

**2.** Which terms are required when referring to parts of another standard?

- A. section and appendix
- B. chapter and part
- C. clause and annex
- D. article and schedule

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The guidelines explicitly state that *clause* and *annex* must be used where applicable.

</details>

**3.** What form should be used in the body of the text when citing another document?

- A. “see page 10”
- B. “as noted in the bibliography”
- C. “… in accordance with TS 21.299 [n] …”
- D. “refer to the attached file”

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The prescribed citation format is the “in accordance with TS … [n]” construction.

</details>

**4.** When a TS or TR reaches at least 60 % stability and is presented to the TSG for the first time, what major version number should it have?

- A. 0
- B. 1
- C. 2
- D. 3

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The first presentation with ≥60 % stability requires the major version to be set to 1.

</details>
