## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which physical adversarial attack with objects is designed to misclassify an arbitrary non-traffic-sign image as a traffic sign?
>
> A. Lenticular attack
> B. Hiding attack
> C. Out-of-distribution attack
> D. Data-specific attack
> E. 2D patch attack

正解：C. Out-of-distribution attack

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is a potential countermeasure against model poisoning attacks in FL (Federated Learning)?
>
> A. Regularly checking the improvement of shared models by participants
> B. Enforcing strict validation rules for shared models
> C. Using blockchain technology to verify model updates

正解：A. Regularly checking the improvement of shared models by participants

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is one of the major concerns related to the operation of drones?
>
> A. Minimum distance to people
> B. Maximum flight height
> C. Daytime operation only
> D. Respecting personal privacy
> E. Insurance strongly recommended

正解：D. Respecting personal privacy
