## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the uncertainty associated with the power management for the wireless base station?
>
> A. Uncertainty in the renewable power source
> B. Uncertainty in the power price from electrical grid
> C. Uncertainty in the traffic load of the wireless base station
> D. All of the above
> E. None of the above

正解：D. All of the above

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which deployment scenario involves selecting landmarks for mobile chargers to visit by turns?
>
> A. Static Wireless Charger Deployment
> B. Mobile Wireless Charger Deployment
> C. Multihop Provisioning Scenario
> D. Landmark Provisioning Scenario
> E. Point Provisioning Scenario

正解：D. Landmark Provisioning Scenario

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the purpose of the Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) joint optimization framework?
>
> A. To allow efficient model updates and re-optimization based on the last-round experimental data
> B. To provide real-time feedback for the WET robot's route adjustments
> C. To minimize collisions with surrounding obstacles
> D. To generate the charging policy offline
> E. To perform object detection periodically using on-board sensors

正解：A. To allow efficient model updates and re-optimization based on the last-round experimental data
