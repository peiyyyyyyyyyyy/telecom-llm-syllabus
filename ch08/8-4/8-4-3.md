# 8.4.3 節能與服務品質的取捨

所屬：[8.4 方案與取捨](../8-4.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following statements best describes the condition that must be satisfied before a cell can enter the **energySaving** state?

- A. The cell may remain active as long as its transmit power is reduced by at least 30 %.
- B. All of its traffic must be drained to overlaid or umbrella candidate cells.
- C. The cell must report a fault to the network management system.
- D. The cell can enter the state only if a neighboring cell is already in *compensatingForEnergySaving*.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification requires that all traffic be offloaded before the cell switches to energySaving, to avoid coverage holes.

</details>

**2.** In an **intra‑RAT** energy‑saving scenario, which entities provide coverage for the switched‑off capacity‑booster cell?

- A. LTE eNBs.
- B. gNB candidate cells of the same RAT.
- C. Fixed wireless access nodes.
- D. Satellite terminals.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Intra‑RAT refers to the same radio access technology, i.e., gNBs providing coverage for the NR booster.

</details>

**3.** What is the purpose of the **energy saving compensation activation** procedure?

- A. To shut down all candidate cells to save additional energy.
- B. To increase the coverage area of the candidate cell(s) that take over traffic.
- C. To generate alarms indicating a cell outage.
- D. To reset the load thresholds of the switched‑off cell.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Compensation activation expands the coverage of the candidate cells to cover the area of the cell that entered energySaving.

</details>

**4.** The EE KPI defined in 3GPP is expressed in which unit?

- A. Watt per bit (W/bit)
- B. Bit per joule (bit/J)
- C. Joule per bit (J/bit)
- D. Kilowatt‑hour per megabit (kWh/Mbit)

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The definition states the KPI unit is bit/J.

</details>

**5.** According to the specifications, how should the network treat a cell that has entered the **energySaving** state?

- A. As a fault condition that triggers an alarm.
- B. As a normal outage that requires manual intervention.
- C. As a non‑fault condition; no alarms should be raised.
- D. As a permanently decommissioned element.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The spec explicitly says a cell in energySaving is not considered a fault and no alarms are raised.

</details>
