## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which of the following deployment scenarios involves deploying static chargers to charge mobile devices in their travel paths?
>
> A. Static Wireless Charger Deployment
> B. Mobile Wireless Charger Deployment
> C. Multihop Provisioning Scenario
> D. Landmark Provisioning Scenario
> E. Path Provisioning Scenario

正解：E. Path Provisioning Scenario

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which of the following is true about the multiple-charger strategies?
>
> A. They all adopt point-to-multipoint charging
> B. They all rely on centralized control
> C. They consider the coordination among mobile chargers
> D. They are more efficient than single-charger strategies

正解：C. They consider the coordination among mobile chargers

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What resources should be optimized in resource allocation for UAV-enabled MEC (mobile edge computing) networks?
>
> A. Communication bandwidth, offloading power, and offloading time
> B. CPU frequency and computation time
> C. Trajectory, fly speed, and accelerated velocity
> D. All of the above

正解：D. All of the above
