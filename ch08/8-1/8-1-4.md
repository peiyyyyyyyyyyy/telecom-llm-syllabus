# 8.1.4 服務目標與安全餘量

所屬：[8.1 設定問題](../8-1.md)

## 正文

In a sliced 5G system the operator must translate business‐level service goals (e.g., “99 % of users can attach within 200 ms”) into measurable performance indicators. 3GPP TS 28.554 defines a set of **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** that quantify **accessibility**, **integrity**, and **retainability** of a network slice. These KPIs become the **service objectives** that appear in the service‑level agreement (SLA). When planning capacity, engineers reserve a **safety margin** – additional resources beyond the nominal demand – to guarantee that the KPI targets are still met under peak load or unexpected failures.

### Accessibility KPIs

Accessibility expresses how easily a UE can reach the network and establish the required bearers. Three ratios are defined:

* **Registration Success Rate (RSR)** – the proportion of successful registration procedures to attempted ones for a given slice  

  $$\text{RSR} = \frac{\text{successful registrations}}{\text{registration attempts}} \times 100\%$$  

  The KPI type is **RATIO** and the result is a **percentage** [TS 28.554 §6.2.3].

* **Partial DRB Accessibility** – the product of three success rates: RRC connection setup, NG‑signalling connection setup, and DRB setup  

  $$\text{Partial DRB Accessibility}_{5\text{QI}} = 
  \frac{\sum \text{RRC\_ConnEstabSucc}}{\sum \text{RRC\_ConnEstabAtt}} \times
  \frac{\sum \text{UECNTXT\_ConnEstabSucc}}{\sum \text{UECNTXT\_ConnEstabAtt}} \times
  \frac{\text{DRB.EstabSucc}_{5\text{QI}}}{\text{DRB.EstabAtt}_{5\text{QI}}} \times 100$$  

  The same expression can be written for a whole **SNSSAI** (slice) by replacing the 5QI‑specific counters with SNSSAI counters [TS 28.554 §6.2.4].

* **PDU Session Establishment Success Rate (PDUSessionEstSR)** – the ratio of successful PDU session requests to attempts for a slice  

  $$\text{PDUSessionEstSR}= \frac{\text{successful PDU session requests}}{\text{attempted PDU session requests}} \times 100\%$$  

  Also a **percentage** KPI of type **RATIO** [TS 28.554 §6.2.5].

Other accessibility measures include the **mean** and **maximum** number of registered subscribers per slice, denoted **AMFMeanRegNbr** and **AMFMaxRegNbr** respectively.  Both are integer counters; the former is a **MEAN**, the latter a **CUMULATIVE** KPI [TS 28.554 §6.2.1, §6.2.6].

### Integrity KPIs – Latency

Integrity focuses on the timeliness of data transfer. The principal latency KPI for downlink is the **Integrated Downlink Delay in NG‑RAN**. At the sub‑network level it is the sum of the CU‑UP delay and the DU delay:

$$\text{DLDelay\_NR\_SNw}= \text{DLDelay\_gNBCUUP\_SNw} + \text{DLDelay\_gNBDU\_SNw}$$  

Both components are **MEAN** values expressed in **0.1 ms** units. When split per QoS class (5QI) or per slice (SNSSAI) the same additive relationship holds [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.2.1].

At the cell level the DU contribution is

$$\text{DLDelay\_gNBDU\_Cell}= \text{DRB.RlcSduDelayDl} + \text{DRB.AirIfDelayDl}$$  

again a **MEAN** time interval [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.3.1].

### Retainability KPIs – Flow Stability

Retainability measures how often a QoS flow or DRB is lost unexpectedly while active.

* **QoS Flow Retainability (QoSRetain\_R1 / R2)** – expressed as “active release / second”.  It is obtained by dividing the number of abnormal releases of a QoS flow by the total active data‑session time [TS 28.554 §6.5.1.1].

* **DRB Retainability (DRBRetain)** – analogous to QoS flow retainability but applied to the radio bearers themselves [TS 28.554 §6.5.2.1].

Both KPIs are of type **MEAN** and use the same unit “active release / second”.

### Defining Service Targets

A service target is typically expressed as a minimum acceptable value for each KPI. For example, an eMBB slice may require:

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| RSR | ≥ 98 % |
| Partial DRB Accessibility (5QI 9) | ≥ 95 % |
| DLDelay\_NR\_SNw (5QI 9) | ≤ 10 ms |
| QoSRetain\_R1 | ≤ 0.001 release / s |

These targets are enforced by monitoring the KPI measurements defined above. The **type** (MEAN, RATIO, CUM) determines the aggregation method (arithmetic mean, ratio, cumulative maximum) used by the O‑M‑I (Operations, Maintenance, and Administration) system.

### Safety Margin Concept

Even if the average demand fits within the calculated capacity, stochastic peaks or equipment failures can push KPI values beyond their thresholds. Therefore a **safety margin** – often expressed as a percentage of the nominal capacity – is added during the planning phase. The margin is applied to the **maximum registered subscriber count** (AMFMaxRegNbr) and to the **resource budget** needed to meet latency and retainability targets.  

A simple rule of thumb is:

$$\text{Planned Capacity} = \text{Peak Demand} \times (1 + \text{Safety Margin})$$  

where *Safety Margin* may be 10–20 % for high‑reliability slices. The margin must be validated against the KPI formulas: increasing the number of registered UE raises the denominator of ratios such as RSR, potentially lowering the measured success rate. Consequently, the margin should be sized so that even at the **maximum** registered subscriber count the KPI targets remain satisfied.

### Worked Example

Assume a slice that, during the busiest hour, has **5 000** registration attempts, of which **4 950** succeed.  The slice also records **9 800** successful DRB setups and **10 200** attempts for 5QI 9.  The measured downlink delay for the same slice is **8 ms** (average).  

1. **RSR**  

   $$\text{RSR}= \frac{4\,950}{5\,000}\times100\% = 99\%$$  

2. **Partial DRB Accessibility (5QI 9)** – suppose RRC and NG‑signalling success rates are both 99 %  

   $$\text{Partial DRB Accessibility}_{5\text{QI}} = 0.99 \times 0.99 \times \frac{9\,800}{10\,200}\times100\% \approx 94.1\%$$  

3. **Latency** – already given as 8 ms, which satisfies a ≤ 10 ms target.  

If the SLA requires ≥ 95 % Partial DRB Accessibility, the slice is **below** target. The planner would increase the safety margin, e.g., add more gNB‑DU capacity, to reduce the DRB attempt count or improve the underlying success rates.

### Practical Notes & Common Mistakes

* **Confusing KPI types** – MEAN values (e.g., AMFMeanRegNbr) cannot be used directly as thresholds for ratios; the correct KPI type must match the target formulation.  
* **Omitting exclusion rules** – the Partial DRB Accessibility formula explicitly excludes setups with the “mo‑Signalling” cause; forgetting this leads to an understated success rate [TS 28.554 §6.2.4].  
* **Aggregating QoS‑level retainability incorrectly** – the R1 definition assumes a single 5QI; using it on aggregated UE‑level data can over‑estimate session time and under‑state retainability [TS 28.554 §6.5.1.1].  
* **Latency units** – all delay KPIs are expressed in **0.1 ms** increments; reporting them in milliseconds without conversion introduces a factor‑10 error.  
* **Safety margin mis‑application** – applying the margin only to capacity but not to the **maximum** subscriber count (AMFMaxRegNbr) can cause KPI violations under bursty registration spikes [TS 28.554 §6.2.6].

By grounding service objectives in the KPI definitions above and by provisioning a disciplined safety margin, engineers can assure that a network slice meets its contractual performance even under adverse conditions.

**1.** Which KPI type is used for the “Maximum registered subscribers of network slice through AMF”?

- A. MEAN
- B. RATIO
- C. CUMULATIVE
- D. INTEGER

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The specification states the KPI type is CUM, i.e., cumulative.

</details>

**2.** The Partial DRB Accessibility formula excludes which establishment cause?

- A. mo‑Signalling
- B. mo‑Data
- C. mo‑Control
- D. mo‑Emergency

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The definition explicitly says the sum shall exclude the establishment cause “mo‑Signalling”.

</details>

**3.** What is the unit of the Downlink latency in gNB‑DU KPI (DLLat_gNBDU)?

- A. milliseconds
- B. 0.1 ms
- C. microseconds
- D. seconds

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The KPI is expressed as a time interval of 0.1 ms.

</details>

**4.** Which KPI measures “how often an end‑user abnormally loses a QoS flow during the time the QoS flow is used”?

- A. RSR
- B. QoSRetain_R1
- C. PDUSessionEstSR
- D. DLDelay_gNBDU_Cell

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。QoSRetain_R1 is defined exactly as the abnormal loss rate of a QoS flow.

</details>

**5.** In the formula for Integrated Downlink Delay in NG‑RAN (sub‑network), which two components are summed?

- A. DLDelay_gNBCUUP_SNw and DLDelay_gNBDU_SNw
- B. DLLat_gNBDU and DLDelay_gNBCUUP_SNw
- C. DRB.RlcSduDelayDl and DRB.AirIfDelayDl
- D. RRC connection setup time and NG signalling time

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The specification defines the integrated delay as the sum of the CU‑UP delay and the DU delay.

</details>
