# 8.1.4 服務目標與安全餘量

所屬：[8.1 設定問題](../8-1.md)

## 練習題

**1.** What is the unit of the Downlink latency in gNB‑DU KPI (DLLat_gNBDU)?

- A. milliseconds
- B. 0.1 ms
- C. microseconds
- D. seconds

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The KPI is expressed as a time interval of 0.1 ms.

</details>

**2.** Which KPI measures “how often an end‑user abnormally loses a QoS flow during the time the QoS flow is used”?

- A. RSR
- B. QoSRetain_R1
- C. PDUSessionEstSR
- D. DLDelay_gNBDU_Cell

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。QoSRetain_R1 is defined exactly as the abnormal loss rate of a QoS flow.

</details>

**3.** In the formula for Integrated Downlink Delay in NG‑RAN (sub‑network), which two components are summed?

- A. DLDelay_gNBCUUP_SNw and DLDelay_gNBDU_SNw
- B. DLLat_gNBDU and DLDelay_gNBCUUP_SNw
- C. DRB.RlcSduDelayDl and DRB.AirIfDelayDl
- D. RRC connection setup time and NG signalling time

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The specification defines the integrated delay as the sum of the CU‑UP delay and the DU delay.

</details>
