# 1.5.2 RAN 智慧控制器：Near-RT RIC、Non-RT RIC、xApp、rApp

所屬：[1.5 開放與虛擬化](../1-5.md)

## 正文

**RAN Intelligent Controllers (RICs) – Near‑RT RIC, Non‑RT RIC, xApp, rApp**

Modern O‑RAN deployments separate the radio access network (RAN) control logic from the lower‑layer radio functions.  This separation is realized by *RAN Intelligent Controllers* (RICs), which host algorithmic applications that can optimise radio resources in a programmable, vendor‑agnostic way.  By moving optimisation into software, operators can introduce new use‑cases (e.g., traffic‑aware scheduling, energy saving, or AI‑based beamforming) without changing the underlying hardware, thereby reducing OPEX and accelerating innovation.

### Control‑loop taxonomy

O‑RAN defines three families of control loops (Figure 5.21 in the specification) — *Non‑Real‑Time* (Non‑RT), *Near‑Real‑Time* (Near‑RT), and *Real‑Time* (RT) — each operating at a different time scale [O‑RAN OAD §5.2].  

* **Non‑RT loops** run with a typical execution time of **≥ 1 s** and are used for long‑term policy generation, training data collection, and network‑wide configuration [O‑RAN OAD §5.2].  
* **Near‑RT loops** execute in the **10 ms – 1 s** range, enabling fine‑grained, per‑UE or per‑cell optimisation that reacts quickly to traffic dynamics [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2].  
* **RT loops** (implemented inside the E2 node itself) can operate **< 10 ms**, handling functions such as HARQ, scheduling, and beamforming [O‑RAN OAD §5.2].

A stable solution always ensures that the slower Non‑RT loops do not interfere with the faster Near‑RT/RT loops [O‑RAN OAD §5.2].

### Near‑Real‑Time RIC (Near‑RT RIC)

The **Near‑RT RIC** is an O‑RAN network function that provides **near‑real‑time control and optimisation of E2 nodes** (e.g., O‑DU) via the **E2 interface** [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2].  Its control loops are in the **10 ms – 1 s** range [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2].  The Near‑RT RIC consists of two logical parts:

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Near‑RT RIC platform** | Provides common services (database, subscription management, conflict mitigation, messaging, security, OAM) to hosted applications [RICARCH §6.2]. |
| **xApps** | Small, specialised applications that **use the E2 interface** to collect near‑real‑time information (per‑UE or per‑cell) and **provide value‑added services** [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2].  Both the platform and an xApp can act as **service producers and/or consumers** via the Near‑RT RIC APIs [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2]. |

### Platform services (selected)

* **Database & Service Data Layer (SDL)** – stores RAN and UE context. The **UE‑NIB** maintains a list of UEs and associated data; the **R‑NIB** stores configuration and near‑real‑time information of connected E2 nodes [RICARCH §6.2.1.1‑6.2.1.2].  Applications access this data through the **SDL** [RICARCH §6.2.1.3].

* **xApp Subscription Management** – consolidates identical subscriptions from multiple xApps into a single E2 subscription, enforces policy‑based authorisation, and audits subscriptions [RICARCH §6.2.2].

* **Conflict Mitigation** – detects and resolves overlapping or contradictory actions requested by different xApps [RICARCH §6.2.3].

* **Messaging Infrastructure** – low‑latency point‑to‑point or publish/subscribe messaging between internal endpoints, with robust delivery guarantees [RICARCH §6.2.4].

* **Security & OAM** – protects radio‑network information from malicious xApps and provides fault, configuration, logging, tracing, and metrics services over the **O1** management interface [RICARCH §6.2.5‑6.2.6].

### Interaction with the Non‑RT RIC

The Near‑RT RIC receives **policies and enrichment data** from the **Non‑RT RIC** via the **A1 interface**; these policies steer the optimisation performed by xApps [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2].  Conversely, the Near‑RT RIC can expose analytics through the **Y1 interface** for consumption by higher‑layer functions.

### Non‑Real‑Time RIC (Non‑RT RIC)

The **Non‑RT RIC** lives within the Service Management and Orchestration (SMO) framework and operates on a **slow time scale (≥ 1 s)** [O‑RAN OAD §5.2].  It is composed of:

* **Non‑RT RIC Framework** – terminates the **A1** interface towards the Near‑RT RIC and provides **R1 services** to applications. [Non‑RT‑RIC‑ARCH §6.1]

* **Non‑RT RIC Applications (rApps)** – software entities that perform **long‑term RAN analytics, configuration management, training data generation, and policy creation**.  Typical rApp responsibilities include **producing A1 policies** and **providing enrichment information** for the Near‑RT RIC [Non‑RT‑RIC‑ARCH §6.1].

The **R1 interface** is a **service‑based** API that enables **rApps** (and other SMO functions) to **consume and produce services** such as fault, performance, or configuration data [Non‑RT‑RIC‑ARCH §6.2].  Communication can be realised via plain HTTP, service meshes, message buses, or API gateways, depending on the deployment [Non‑RT‑RIC‑ARCH §6.2].

### xApps vs. rApps – a concise comparison

| Aspect | xApp (Near‑RT RIC) | rApp (Non‑RT RIC) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Execution time** | 10 ms – 1 s (near‑real‑time) | ≥ 1 s (non‑real‑time) |
| **Hosted in** | Near‑RT RIC platform | Non‑RT RIC framework (SMO) |
| **Primary interface** | **E2** (to E2 nodes) | **A1** (to Near‑RT RIC) and **R1** (SMO services) |
| **Typical functions** | Per‑UE scheduling, QoS adaptation, AI‑based beamforming | Policy generation, data analytics, training set creation |
| **Service model** | Consumes Near‑RT RIC APIs; may also produce analytics via **Y1** | Produces **A1** policies; consumes **R1** services |

### Worked example – estimating loop frequency

Assume an operator deploys a traffic‑aware scheduling xApp that runs a Near‑RT control loop every **50 ms**.  The number of optimisation cycles per second, $N$, is:

$$
N = \frac{1\ \text{s}}{T_{\text{loop}}} = \frac{1}{0.050\ \text{s}} = 20\ \text{cycles/s}
$$

Over a **5‑minute** observation period the xApp would execute:

$$
N_{\text{total}} = N \times 5 \times 60 = 20 \times 300 = 6{,}000\ \text{cycles}
$$

This simple calculation shows how quickly Near‑RT xApps can react to traffic changes, compared with a Non‑RT rApp that might recompute policies only once every **30 s** (≈ 2 cycles per minute).

### Common pitfalls and practical notes

| Pitfall | Why it occurs | Mitigation |
|---------|---------------|------------|
| **Assuming Near‑RT RIC is mandatory for basic radio operation** | The specification states that E2 nodes must continue to provide services even if the Near‑RT RIC fails, though optimisation may be reduced [O‑RAN OAD §5.3.2]. | Design the E2 node with fallback behaviour; monitor Near‑RT RIC health via OAM. |
| **Over‑loading xApps with conflicting objectives** | Multiple xApps may request contradictory parameter changes, leading to instability [RICARCH §6.2.3]. | Use the **Conflict Mitigation** service to detect and resolve overlaps; enforce clear priority policies. |
| **Neglecting policy latency** | Near‑RT loops must be faster than the Non‑RT loops that generate the policies; otherwise the system can become throttled [O‑RAN OAD §5.2]. | Ensure Non‑RT policy generation intervals are significantly longer than the Near‑RT loop period. |
| **Improper subscription handling** | Duplicate subscriptions from several xApps can waste E2 bandwidth [RICARCH §6.2.2]. | Leverage the **xApp Subscription Management** service to merge identical subscriptions. |
| **Insufficient security isolation** | Malicious xApps could exfiltrate radio data or misuse control capabilities [RICARCH §6.2.5]. | Enforce the RIC security framework and audit xApp behaviour via OAM logs. |

Understanding the distinct roles of Near‑RT RIC, Non‑RT RIC, xApps, and rApps is essential for building scalable, programmable O‑RAN deployments that can adapt in near real time while maintaining long‑term network stability.

## 練習題

**1.** Near-RT RIC 中负责运行 xApp 的实体是哪个？

- A. Non-RT RIC
- B. Near-RT RIC
- C. O-CU-CP
- D. O-DU

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。xApp 是在 Near-RT RIC 内部运行的应用程序。

</details>

**2.** Near-RT 控制回路的典型执行时间范围是？

- A. 少于 1 毫秒
- B. 1 秒或更长
- C. 10 毫秒或更长
- D. 10 秒或更长

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Near-RT 控制回路的时延要求在毫秒级别，通常不低于 10 ms。

</details>

**3.** Non-RT RIC 向 Near-RT RIC 提供策略和丰富数据所使用的接口是？

- A. E2
- B. A1
- C. Y1
- D. O1

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。A1 接口专用于 Non-RT RIC 向 Near-RT RIC 传递策略与丰富信息。

</details>

**4.** Near-RT RIC 平台用于检测并解决多个 xApp 之间可能冲突请求的功能叫什么？

- A. Subscription Management
- B. Conflict Mitigation
- C. Messaging Infrastructure
- D. Security

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。冲突缓解（Conflict Mitigation）负责识别并调和不同 xApp 的冲突操作。

</details>

**5.** 在 Non-RT RIC 架构中，提供价值增值服务的应用程序被称为什么？

- A. xApp
- B. rApp
- C. UE-NIB
- D. R-NIB

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。价值增值服务由 Non-RT RIC 的 rApp 实现。

</details>
