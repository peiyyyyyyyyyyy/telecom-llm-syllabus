# 1.4.3 計費架構：離線、線上與融合計費（CHF）

所屬：[1.4 網管、計費、安全與密碼學](../1-4.md)

## 正文

The term *billing architecture* denotes the logical arrangement by which a telecommunications operator records service usage, computes charges, and generates invoices for its customers. In practice three major modes are distinguished:

* **Offline billing** – usage records are collected, stored, and processed after the service session has terminated.  
* **Online billing** – charging decisions are made in real‑time, often influencing session admission or QoS enforcement.  
* **Converged (Hybrid) Billing – CHF** – a combination of offline and online processing, aiming to balance the immediacy of online charging with the flexibility of offline aggregation.

The official 3GPP specifications that define performance measurement and aggregation for network functions (NFs) do **not** describe billing processes, offline/online charging, or the CHF model. Consequently, no technical definitions, formulas, or procedural details for these billing concepts can be derived from the provided reference material.  

*Scope limitation*: Because the reference **[TS 28.552 §4.1]** only addresses **performance indicators** – i.e., aggregated performance data over groups of NFs, latency measurements, and their exposure via management services – the subsection on billing architecture falls outside the covered domain. Therefore, this textbook section can only acknowledge the existence of offline, online, and converged billing modes without providing further technical elaboration, formulas, or examples.

*Practical note*: When studying billing architectures, engineers should consult the dedicated 3GPP charging specifications (e.g., TS 32.240, TS 32.299) and the ITU‑T recommendations on charging and billing, as the performance‑indicator reference does not contain the required information.

*Common mistake*: Attempting to infer billing‑related mechanisms (such as rating, discount policies, or CHF workflow) from performance‑indicator texts leads to unsupported conclusions. Always verify that the source document explicitly covers the billing topic before citing it.

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following statements is directly supported by the reference **[TS 28.552 §4.1]**?

- A. Offline billing processes are defined in the specification.
- B. Online charging decisions are made in real‑time.
- C. Performance indicators are aggregated over a group of network functions.
- D. Converged Hybrid Billing (CHF) combines offline and online charging.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The reference explicitly mentions that performance indicators are aggregated over a group of NFs.

</details>

**2.** According to the reference, where can performance indicators at the network slice level be made available?

- A. Only to the operator’s internal monitoring system.
- B. Via the corresponding performance management service for network slice.
- C. Directly to end‑users through the billing portal.
- D. Through the converged billing (CHF) interface.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states that network‑slice‑level indicators are made available via the appropriate performance management service.

</details>

**3.** Which of the following is *not* mentioned in the reference as a scope of performance indicators?

- A. Derivation from measurements collected at constituent network slice subnets.
- B. Availability to a tenant associated with an S‑NSSAI.
- C. Calculation of monetary charges for a subscriber.
- D. Aggregation method identified in the indicator definition.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The reference discusses measurement aggregation and tenant visibility but never mentions monetary charge calculation.

</details>

**4.** The reference indicates that performance indicators can be split per tenant. How is this achieved?

- A. By assigning each tenant a unique billing identifier.
- B. By associating tenants with S‑NSSAI or sNSSAIList and splitting sub‑counters per S‑NSSAI.
- C. By using separate offline billing databases for each tenant.
- D. By employing CHF to merge offline and online indicators.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Tenant association with S‑NSSAI allows the indicators to be divided into sub‑counters per S‑NSSAI.

</details>

**5.** Which of the following best describes the relationship between performance indicators at the network slice subnet level and those at the network slice level?

- A. Subnet indicators are derived from billing records, while slice indicators are derived from user surveys.
- B. Slice indicators are calculated independently of subnet indicators.
- C. Slice indicators are derived from subnet indicators and/or NF measurements.
- D. There is no relationship; they are defined in separate specifications.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The specification states that slice‑level indicators can be derived from subnet‑level indicators and/or NF measurements.

</details>
