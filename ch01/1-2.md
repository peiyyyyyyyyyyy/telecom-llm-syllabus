## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the purpose of passive scanning? [IEEE 802.11]
>
> A. To save battery power
> B. To transmit Probe Request frames
> C. To find BSSs in the area by listening to Beacon frames
> D. To gain access to the medium using the basic DCF access procedure

正解：C. To find BSSs in the area by listening to Beacon frames

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the `mac_expert_config` struct?
>
> A. It defines the initial C-RNTI for newly created UEs.
> B. It defines the maximum number of consecutive DL KOs before an RLF is reported.
> C. It defines the maximum number of consecutive UL KOs before an RLF is reported.
> D. It defines the maximum number of consecutive non-decoded CSI before an RLF is reported.

正解：A. It defines the initial C-RNTI for newly created UEs.

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the definition of Paging Block Periodicity?
>
> A. The act of seeking a User Equipment
> B. The frame where the UE receives actual paging message
> C. The period of the occurrence of Paging Blocks
> D. The frame where the UE monitors in FDD or the paging block, which consists of several frames, for TDD
> E. A network protocol data unit

正解：C. The period of the occurrence of Paging Blocks
