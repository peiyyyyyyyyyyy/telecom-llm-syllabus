## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which satellite access network type has the lowest end-to-end latency requirement in a 5G system? [3GPP Release 17]
>
> A. GEO based satellite access
> B. MEO based satellite access
> C. LEO based satellite access
> D. All have the same end-to-end latency requirement

正解：C. LEO based satellite access

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the maximum number of channels that a radio baseband unit can support?
>
> A. 2
> B. 4
> C. 8
> D. 16

正解：B. 4

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is a collocated radio?
>
> A. A radio that emits radio-frequency energy located in a different physical device than the reporting STA
> B. A radio that emits radio-frequency energy located in the same physical device as the reporting STA
> C. A radio that emits radio-frequency energy located in a different physical location than the reporting STA
> D. A radio that emits radio-frequency energy located far away from the reporting STA

正解：B. A radio that emits radio-frequency energy located in the same physical device as the reporting STA
