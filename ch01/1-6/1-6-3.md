# 1.6.3 吞吐量與延遲

所屬：[1.6 指標與名詞](../1-6.md)

## 正文

**Throughput and Delay in 5G Packet‑Switched Radio Access**  

In a mobile network the amount of user data that can be delivered per unit time (throughput) and the time it takes for a data packet to travel from the core to the UE (delay) are the two most visible performance indicators for both operators and subscribers. High throughput enables bandwidth‑hungry services such as video streaming, while low delay is essential for interactive applications (e.g., gaming, URLLC). 3GPP defines a set of standardized measurements that quantify these aspects on a per‑cell, per‑QoS, and per‑slice basis, allowing operators to monitor, compare, and guarantee service levels [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1] [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.1] [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.1].

### Average Air‑Interface Delay (Downlink)

The **average DL air‑interface delay** is the arithmetic mean of the time elapsed between the moment the first part of an RLC SDU arrives at the MAC layer and the moment the last part of that SDU is successfully transmitted to the UE and acknowledged (HARQ ACK for UM mode or RLC ACK for AM mode).  

The measurement is obtained as  

$$
\text{AvgDLDelay}= \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\bigl(t^{\text{sent}}_{i,\text{last}}-t^{\text{MAC}}_{i}\bigr)}
{N},
$$

where  

* $t^{\text{MAC}}_{i}$ – time the $i$‑th RLC SDU part arrives at the MAC,  
* $t^{\text{sent}}_{i,\text{last}}$ – time the last part of the same SDU is sent to the UE and the corresponding ACK is received,  
* $N$ – total number of successfully transmitted RLC SDUs.  

The result is expressed in **0.1 ms** and reported as *DRB.AirIfDelayDl* (or *DRB.AirIfDelayDl* *Filter*) [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.1].

### Average Air‑Interface Delay (Uplink)

The **average UL air‑interface delay** follows the same principle on the uplink direction. It is defined as the mean time from the moment a packet reaches the MAC layer at the UE side until the gNB‑DU confirms successful reception (ACK). The standard measurement name is *DRB.AirIfDelayUl* (or *DRB.AirIfDelayUl* *Filter*) and the unit is also **0.1 ms** [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.3].

### Average RLC Packet Delay (Uplink)

For the delay that occurs **inside the gNB‑DU** (i.e., between MAC and RLC layers) the standard provides *Average RLC packet delay in the UL*. It follows the definition in TS 38.314 and is reported as a real value in **0.1 ms** [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.4].

### UE Throughput (Downlink)

The **average DL UE throughput** quantifies how many kilobits per second are successfully delivered to a UE over a data radio bearer (DRB). The measurement is intended for data bursts that span multiple slots. The standard formula is  

$$
\boxed{
\text{UEThpDl}= 
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{\text{UE}}\sum_{\text{samples}} \text{ThpVolDl}}
{\displaystyle\sum_{\text{UE}}\sum_{\text{samples}} \text{ThpTimeDl}}\times 1000
& \text{if } \sum \text{ThpTimeDl}>0\\[6pt]
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}}
$$

where  

* **ThpVolDl** – RLC‑level volume of a data burst (kbit), *excluding* the last piece that empties the buffer,  
* **ThpTimeDl** – time required to transmit the burst *excluding* the slot that empties the buffer (ms),  
* the outer summations run over all UEs and all measured samples.  

The result is expressed in **kbit/s** and reported as *DRB.UEThpDl* (or with optional filters for QoS, S‑NSSAI, PLMN, BWP) [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.1].

### Distribution Measurements

To complement the averages, the specifications also define **histogram‑style** metrics:

* **Delay distribution** – each bin counts the number of RLC SDU packets whose DL delay falls into a vendor‑specific range. Reported as *DRB.AirIfDelayDist.Bin* (or with filters) [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.2].
* **Throughput distribution** – analogous counters for UE throughput samples, using the same formula per sample. Reported as *DRB.UEThpDl* distribution counters [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.2].

Both measurements are integer counters; the number of instances must respect the overall limit of 100 measurement instances per cell [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.2] [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.2].

### Latency KPIs in the RAN

Beyond per‑UE metrics, 3GPP defines **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** for network‑wide latency assessment:

* **DLLat_gNBDU** – the mean time from reception of an IP packet at the gNB‑DU until the first part is transmitted over the air‑interface, measured only when the transmission queue is empty. It is synonymous with *DRB.RlcSduLatencyDl* and expressed in **0.1 ms** [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.1].

* **Integrated downlink delay in NG‑RAN (sub‑network)** – the weighted average delay from the gNB‑CU‑UP (core‑to‑RAN interface) to the UE. It is the sum of the CU‑UP delay and the DU delay:  

$$
\text{DLDelay\_NR\_SNw}= \text{DLDelay\_gNBCUUP\_SNw}+ \text{DLDelay\_gNBDU\_SNw}
$$

with optional per‑QoS and per‑S‑NSSAI splits [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.2.1].

* **Integrated downlink delay in NG‑RAN (network‑slice subnet)** – analogous to the above but scoped to a slice, computed as  

$$
\text{DLDelay\_NR\_Nss}= \text{DLDelay\_gNBCUUP\_Nss}+ \text{DLDelay\_gNBDU\_Nss}
$$  

[TS 28.554 §6.3.1.2.2].

All latency KPIs share the same unit (0.1 ms) and are valid for packet‑switched traffic [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.1] [TS 28.554 §6.3.1.2.1].

### Worked Example – DL UE Throughput

Assume a single UE on a DRB with the following measured samples:

| Sample | ThpVolDl (kbit) | ThpTimeDl (ms) |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| 1      | 4 500          | 180            |
| 2      | 5 200          | 210            |
| 3      | 4 800          | 190            |

First compute the sums:

$$
\sum \text{ThpVolDl}=4\,500+5\,200+4\,800 = 14\,500\ \text{kbit}
$$

$$
\sum \text{ThpTimeDl}=180+210+190 = 580\ \text{ms}
$$

Since the total time is non‑zero, apply the formula:

$$
\text{UEThpDl}= \frac{14\,500}{580}\times 1000 \approx 25\,000\ \text{kbit/s}
$$

Thus the average downlink throughput for this UE is **25 Mbit/s**. This calculation follows directly the standard definition [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.1].

### Common Pitfalls and Practical Notes

| Pitfall | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| **Counting retransmissions** | The delay measurement includes only the *original* transmission time, even if the RLC SDU is later retransmitted (see the distribution definition) [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.2]. |
| **Mixing UL/DL counters** | UL and DL delays are distinct measurements (*DRB.AirIfDelayUl* vs *DRB.AirIfDelayDl*); using the wrong one yields misleading latency figures. |
| **Ignoring optional filters** | Per‑PLMN, per‑QoS, and per‑S‑NSSAI filters are optional but must be applied consistently when aggregating data; otherwise averages may mix heterogeneous traffic. |
| **Zero‑time division** | If the summed *ThpTimeDl* is zero, the throughput definition explicitly returns 0 kbit/s; a naïve division would cause an error [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.1]. |
| **Unit confusion** | All delay KPIs are expressed in **0.1 ms**, not milliseconds; forgetting the scaling factor leads to a ten‑fold discrepancy. |

When configuring counters, respect the global limit of 100 measurement instances per cell (including both DL and UL) [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.1.2] [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.3.2].
