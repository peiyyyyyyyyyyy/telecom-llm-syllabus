# 1.6.4 接通率（Accessibility）、掉話率（Retainability）與可用率（Availability）

所屬：[1.6 指標與名詞](../1-6.md)

## 正文

In a 5G (or LTE) system the user experience is not only determined by raw data rates or latency, but also by whether the network can **let the user connect**, **keep the connection alive**, and **remain operational** over time. 3GPP therefore defines three complementary KPI families:

* **Accessibility** – ability of a UE to register, obtain a DRB (Data Radio Bearer) or a PDU session.  
* **Retainability** – frequency with which an already‑established QoS flow or DRB is lost unexpectedly.  
* **Availability** – proportion of time that a cell (or NRCellDU) is capable of providing service.

These metrics are used by operators to negotiate Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and to drive network optimisation.

---

### 1.6.4.1 Accessibility KPIs  

Accessibility is expressed as a **ratio** (percentage) of successful procedures to attempted procedures.  The most common measurements are:

| KPI | Symbol | Definition | Formula |
|-----|--------|------------|---------|
| Registration success rate (single slice) | **RSR** | Ratio of successful registration procedures to attempted registration procedures for the AMF set belonging to one network slice. | $$\text{RSR} = \frac{N_{\text{reg\_succ}}}{N_{\text{reg\_att}}}\times 100\%$$ |
| Partial DRB Accessibility | – | Success rate of the three consecutive steps: RRC connection setup, NG‑signalling connection setup, and DRB establishment. | $$\text{Partial DRB Accessibility}= \frac{\sum \text{RRC.ConnEstabSucc}}{\sum \text{RRC.ConnEstabAtt}} \times \frac{\sum \text{UECNTXT.ConnEstabSucc}}{\sum \text{UECNTXT.ConnEstabAtt}} \times \frac{\text{DRB.EstabSucc}}{\text{DRB.EstabAtt}} \times 100$$ |
| PDU‑session establishment success rate (per S‑NSSAI) | **PDUSessionEstSR** | Ratio of successful PDU‑session establishment requests to attempted requests for a given network slice. | $$\text{PDUSessionEstSR}= \frac{N_{\text{PDU\_succ}}}{N_{\text{PDU\_att}}}\times100\%$$ |
| Total DRB Accessibility | – | Ratio of all successfully established DRBs (initial, added, resume) to the number of services intended to be set up, after correcting for the success of RRC and NG‑connection steps. The full expression is given in TS 28.554 §6.2.7. | – |

All Accessibility KPIs are **type RATIO** and reported as a **percentage**.  The underlying counters (e.g., *RRC.ConnEstabSucc.*Cause*) exclude setups whose cause is *mo‑Signalling* as required by the specifications.  

*Why it matters*: A low registration success rate directly translates to users being unable to attach to the network, while low DRB or PDU‑session success rates cause call drops or service denial even after attachment.

### 1.6.4.2 Retainability KPIs  

Retainability quantifies **abnormal releases** of already‑active flows.  The unit is **“active release / second”**, i.e. a mean value over the measurement period.

* **QoS‑flow Retainability** – two variants are defined:  

  * **R1** – counts releases of a single QoS level.  
  * **R2** – aggregates across all QoS levels for a UE, avoiding double‑counting when several flows are active simultaneously.  

  The generic expression (see TS 28.554 §6.5.1.2) is  

  $$\text{QoSRetain} = \frac{N_{\text{abnormal\_releases}}}{T_{\text{active\_session\_seconds}}}\;\text{(active release / s)}$$  

* **DRB Retainability** – analogous to QoS‑flow retainability but applied to DRBs.  The KPI shows how often a DRB is abnormally released while active.  The same “active release / second” unit is used, and separate formulas are provided for a single 5QI level (R1) or a single S‑NSSAI (R1).  

Both retainability KPIs rely on the definition of **active** flow:  

* *Bursty* – a flow is active if data are present in the PDCP queue or if any data were transferred within the last 100 ms.  
* *Continuous* – a flow is active as long as the UE stays in **RRC_CONNECTED** and the session timer runs from the first to 100 ms after the last transmission.  Continuous flows are identified by 5QI ∈ {1, 2, 65, 66}.  

*Why it matters*: High retainability values indicate frequent unexpected releases, which manifest as call drops, video freezes, or sudden loss of connectivity.

### 1.6.4.3 Availability KPIs  

Availability measures the **percentage of time** a cell can deliver service.

* **E‑UTRAN Cell Availability** (LTE) – defined as  

  $$\text{CellAvailability}= \frac{\text{measurement\_period} - \sum_{\text{cause}} \text{RRU.CellUnavailableTime[cause]}}{\text{measurement\_period}}\times100\%$$  

  The cell is regarded as *available* when the eNodeB can provide **E‑RAB** service.  

* **NRCellDU Availability** (5G) – the NRCellDU part of the cell is considered available when its **operational state (OST)** and **administrative state (AST)** are simultaneously *enabled* and *unlocked*.  The KPI is the ratio of cumulative seconds in this state to the granularity period, expressed as a percentage.  

Both KPIs are **type RATIO** and reported in **percentage**.  

*Why it matters*: Even with perfect accessibility and retainability, a cell that is unavailable for a large fraction of time cannot serve users, leading to coverage holes and capacity loss.

[TS 32.450 §6.4.1.1]  
[TS 28.552 §5.1.4.1.4]

### 1.6.4.4 Worked Example  

An operator monitors a single NRCellDU over a 1‑hour granularity period (3 600 s).  The collected counters are:

| Counter | Value |
|---------|-------|
| RRC.ConnEstabSucc (excluding mo‑Signalling) | 9 800 |
| RRC.ConnEstabAtt (excluding mo‑Signalling) | 10 000 |
| UE‑associated NG‑connection Succ | 9 500 |
| UE‑associated NG‑connection Att | 9 800 |
| DRB.EstabSucc (5QI = 1) | 9 200 |
| DRB.EstabAtt (5QI = 1) | 9 500 |
| QoS flow abnormal releases (R1) | 4 |
| Active QoS‑flow session seconds (R1) | 2 400 s |
| CellUnavailableTime (all causes) | 300 s |

1. **Partial DRB Accessibility**  

   $$\text{Partial DRB Acc}= \frac{9\,800}{10\,000}\times\frac{9\,500}{9\,800}\times\frac{9\,200}{9\,500}\times100\approx 89.8\%$$  

2. **QoS‑flow Retainability (R1)**  

   $$\text{QoSRetain}_{R1}= \frac{4}{2\,400}\approx 0.0017\;\text{active release/s}$$  

3. **Cell Availability**  

   $$\text{CellAvailability}= \frac{3\,600-300}{3\,600}\times100 = 91.7\%$$  

Interpretation: The cell is largely available, but the partial DRB accessibility (≈ 90 %) indicates that about one‑tenth of attempted DRB setups fail, which may be a target for optimisation.  The retainability value is very low, suggesting that once a QoS flow is active, abnormal releases are rare.

### 1.6.4.5 Common Pitfalls & Practical Notes  

| Pitfall | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| **Including *mo‑Signalling* causes** in the numerator/denominator of Partial DRB Accessibility. | The specification explicitly states that setups with cause *mo‑Signalling* must be excluded; otherwise the KPI is inflated. |
| **Counting all QoS‑flow releases for R2** without distinguishing active vs. idle periods. | R2 is meant to avoid double‑counting when multiple flows are active; using the naïve sum leads to an over‑estimated retainability. |
| **Confusing “availability” with “accessibility”.** | Availability is a time‑percentage (cell‑level), while accessibility is a success‑rate of signalling procedures (user‑level). |
| **Using raw counter totals instead of period‑averaged values for mean‑type KPIs (e.g., AMFMeanRegNbr).** | The KPI type *MEAN* requires averaging over the measurement interval; reporting a single snapshot misrepresents the metric. |
| **Neglecting the distinction between continuous and bursty flows** when classifying active time. | The active‑time definition differs for 5QI ∈ {1,2,65,66} (continuous) versus other QoS levels (bursty); applying the wrong rule skews retainability calculations. |

By observing these notes, engineers can ensure that KPI calculations faithfully reflect network performance and support accurate SLA reporting.

## 練習題

**1.** Which KPI explicitly measures the ratio of successful registration procedures to attempted registration procedures for a single network slice?

- A. Mean registered subscribers (AMFMeanRegNbr)
- B. Registration success rate (RSR)
- C. Partial DRB Accessibility
- D. PDU session establishment success rate

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。RSR is defined as the ratio of successful to attempted registration procedures for one slice.

</details>

**2.** What is the unit used for QoS‑flow and DRB retainability KPIs?

- A. Percentage (%)
- B. Seconds (s)
- C. Active release per second
- D. Number of releases

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Both retainability KPIs are expressed as “active release / second”.

</details>

**3.** Cell Availability is reported as which of the following?

- A. Ratio of successful registrations to attempts
- B. Percentage of time the cell is considered available
- C. Mean number of registered subscribers
- D. Active release per second

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The definition states it is the percentage of time the cell is considered available.

</details>

**4.** Partial DRB Accessibility combines the success rates of which three procedures?

- A. RRC connection setup, NG‑signalling connection setup, and DRB setup
- B. Registration, authentication, and security mode command
- C. PDU session establishment, QoS flow activation, and handover
- D. Cell activation, carrier aggregation, and beamforming

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The KPI multiplies the success rates of RRC connection, NG‑signalling, and DRB establishment.

</details>

**5.** DRB Retainability is defined as the number of DRBs abnormally released while active divided by what?

- A. Total number of DRB establishment attempts
- B. Number of data session time units
- C. Number of registered subscribers
- D. Measurement period in seconds

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The KPI normalises abnormal releases by the number of data session time units.

</details>
