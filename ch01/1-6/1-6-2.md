# 1.6.2 訊號品質：RSRQ、SINR、CQI

所屬：[1.6 指標與名詞](../1-6.md)

## 正文

In a live 5G NR system the raw power of a received carrier (signal strength) is only part of the story. A user equipment (UE) must also assess how much of that power is useful data versus interference and thermal noise. This assessment is called *signal quality* and it directly influences link adaptation, hand‑over decisions and the user‑perceived throughput. Three standardized metrics capture signal quality in the 3GPP specifications: **SS‑RSRQ**, **SS‑SINR**, and the **Channel Quality Indicator (CQI)**.

---

### SS‑RSRQ (Secondary‑Synchronization‑Signal Reference‑Signal Received Quality)

The SS‑RSRQ is defined as  

$$
\text{SS‑RSRQ}= \frac{N \times \text{SS‑RSRP}}{\text{NR carrier RSSI}}
$$

where  

* $N$ – number of resource blocks (RBs) that constitute the measurement bandwidth,  
* $\text{SS‑RSRP}$ – linear power of the secondary‑synchronisation signal measured over the same set of RBs,  
* $\text{NR carrier RSSI}$ – linear average of the total received power (in W) observed on the measurement bandwidth, *including* all co‑channel serving and non‑serving cells, adjacent‑channel interference, thermal noise, etc.  

Both numerator and denominator are obtained from the **same set of resource blocks** and over the same measurement time resources (the SS/PBCH Block Measurement Time Configuration, SMTC, window) unless higher‑layer parameters limit the measurement further [TS 38.215 §5.1.3].  

For frequency range 1 the reference point is the UE antenna connector; for range 2 the RSSI is measured after combining the signals of the antenna elements belonging to the selected receiver branch [TS 38.215 §5.1.3].

---

### SS‑SINR (Secondary‑Synchronization‑Signal Signal‑to‑Interference‑plus‑Noise Ratio)

SS‑SINR is the **linear average** of the power contributed by the resource elements that carry the secondary‑synchronisation signals, divided by the linear average of the power contributed by noise and interference:

$$
\text{SS‑SINR}= \frac{\displaystyle\text{average power of SS REs}}{\displaystyle\text{average noise + interference power}}
$$

The measurement is performed over the same frequency bandwidth as the SS‑RSRQ and is confined to the SMTC window duration, unless the UE uses the measurement for L1‑SINR reporting with dedicated interference measurement resources, in which case the SMTC restriction does not apply [TS 38.215 §5.1.5].  

For frequency range 1 the reference point is the UE antenna connector; for range 2 the measurement is based on the combined signal from antenna elements of the selected receiver branch, and diversity processing guarantees that the reported SS‑SINR is never lower than that of any individual branch [TS 38.215 §5.1.5].

---

### CQI (Channel Quality Indicator)

CQI is an **index** reported by the UE to the gNB that abstracts the instantaneous downlink channel quality into the highest modulation‑and‑coding scheme (MCS) that can be supported with a prescribed block error probability (BLER).  

*For an unrestricted observation interval in time and frequency*, the UE selects the **highest CQI index** that satisfies:

* a transport block error probability ≤ 0.1 when the higher‑layer parameter *cqi‑Table* selects *table1*, *table2* or *table4‑r17*;  
* a transport block error probability ≤ 0.00001 when *cqi‑Table* selects *table3* [TS 38.214 §5.2.2.1].

The mapping from CQI index to modulation, code‑rate (× 1024) and spectral efficiency is given in the 4‑bit CQI Table (Table 5.2.2.1‑2). A shortened excerpt is reproduced below:

| CQI index | Modulation | Code rate × 1024 | Efficiency (bits/RE) |
|----------|------------|------------------|----------------------|
| 1        | QPSK       | 78               | 0.1523               |
| 2        | QPSK       | 120              | 0.2344               |
| …        | …          | …                | …                    |
| 9        | 16QAM      | 616              | 2.4063               |
| 10       | 64QAM      | 466              | 2.7305               |
| 15       | 64QAM      | 948              | 5.5547               |

The UE derives CQI from measurements on the **NZP CSI‑RS** (or CSI‑IM when configured) that are taken **no later than** the CSI reference resource associated with the CSI‑ReportConfig. If *timeRestrictionForChannelMeasurements* is set to *Configured*, only the most recent CSI‑RS occurrence is used; otherwise any earlier measurement within the unrestricted interval may be used [TS 38.214 §5.2.2.1].

### Worked Example

A UE measures the following linear powers over a bandwidth of $N = 20$ RBs:

* SS‑RSRP = $2.0 \times 10^{-9}$ W  
* NR carrier RSSI = $1.0 \times 10^{-7}$ W  

Compute SS‑RSRQ:

$$
\text{SS‑RSRQ}= \frac{20 \times 2.0 \times 10^{-9}}{1.0 \times 10^{-7}}
               = \frac{4.0 \times 10^{-8}}{1.0 \times 10^{-7}}
               = 0.4
$$

If the UE also measures an average SS‑signal power of $2.0 \times 10^{-9}$ W and an average interference + noise power of $5.0 \times 10^{-10}$ W, the SS‑SINR is

$$
\text{SS‑SINR}= \frac{2.0 \times 10^{-9}}{5.0 \times 10^{-10}} = 4.0 \; (\text{linear}) \approx 6.0\ \text{dB}.
$$

Assume the UE, after applying the CQI derivation rule, finds that a transport block with **CQI 9** (16QAM, code‑rate ≈ 0.60) meets the BLER target of 0.1. The UE therefore reports CQI = 9 to the gNB [TS 38.214 §5.2.2.1].

### Common Mistakes & Practical Notes

| Pitfall | Why it happens | Correct practice |
|---------|----------------|------------------|
| **Using different RB sets for SS‑RSRP and RSSI** | The specification explicitly requires the same RB set; engineers sometimes forget when aggregating measurements across carriers. | Align measurement bandwidths and ensure both numerator and denominator are taken over the *same* RBs [TS 38.215 §5.1.3]. |
| **Confusing linear and dB values** | The formulas are defined in linear units (W). Converting to dB before division yields an incorrect RSRQ or SINR. | Keep all intermediate calculations linear; apply $\text{dB}=10\log_{10}(\text{linear})$ only for reporting. |
| **Assuming the SMTC restriction always applies to SINR** | For L1‑SINR reporting with dedicated interference resources the SMTC window limitation is waived. | Check the reporting configuration; omit the SMTC bound only when the UE uses dedicated interference measurement resources [TS 38.215 §5.1.5]. |
| **Selecting the wrong BLER target for CQI** | Different *cqi‑Table* values imply different error‑probability thresholds (0.1 vs. 0.00001). | Verify the *cqi‑Table* configuration in the CSI‑ReportConfig before mapping measurements to a CQI index [TS 38.214 §5.2.2.1]. |
| **Ignoring receiver diversity effects** | Diversity can improve measured quality, but some implementations mistakenly take the worst branch. | The reported SS‑RSRQ and SS‑SINR must not be lower than any individual branch’s value when diversity is active [TS 38.215 §5.1.3] and [TS 38.215 §5.1.5]. |

By respecting the exact definitions and measurement conditions prescribed in the 3GPP specifications, engineers can reliably translate raw power observations into the quality metrics that drive adaptive modulation, scheduling, and mobility management in 5G NR networks.
