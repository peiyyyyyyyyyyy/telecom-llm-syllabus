## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which of the following tools provides high-level toolboxes that enable network engineers to build purpose-specific deep learning architectures?
>
> A. Advanced parallel computing
> B. Dedicated deep learning libraries
> C. Fog computing
> D. Fast optimization algorithms
> E. Distributed machine learning systems

正解：B. Dedicated deep learning libraries

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the role of SIC (successive interference cancelation) in NOMA (Non-orthogonal multiple access)?
>
> A. To reduce co-channel interference
> B. To increase computational complexity
> C. To optimize network resources
> D. To maximize spectral efficiency
> E. None of the above

正解：A. To reduce co-channel interference

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the primary objective of the shielding layer in a discrete Holographic MIMO Surfaces (HMIMOS) ?
>
> A. To modify the behavior of the meta-material layer
> B. To achieve a continuous aperture
> C. To avoid mutual interferences between layers
> D. To update external wireless entities

正解：C. To avoid mutual interferences between layers
