# 1.3.3 數據服務與 QoS

所屬：[1.3 服務與多媒體](../1-3.md)

## 正文

In 5G the term *data service* refers to the exchange of user‑plane data packets between a user equipment (UE) and an external data network (DN) identified by a Data Network Name (DNN). The service is realized through a **PDU Connectivity Service**, which is instantiated by a **PDU Session**. QoS (Quality‑of‑Service) guarantees that the traffic of each session meets the performance expectations of the subscribed service (e.g., low latency for gaming, guaranteed bitrate for video). Without proper QoS, applications would experience jitter, packet loss or insufficient throughput, degrading user experience and violating service‑level agreements (SLAs).  

A PDU Session is requested by the UE via NAS signalling. The UE may include a DNN; if omitted, the AMF selects the **default DNN** associated with the UE’s S‑NSSAI (Single Network Slice Selection Assistance Information) from the subscription data, or a locally configured DNN when no default exists [TS 23.501 §5.6]. The AMF may also replace an UE‑requested DNN with another DNN according to operator policies supplied by the **PCF** (Policy Control Function) [TS 23.501 §5.6].  

Once the PDU Session is established, the **SMF** (Session Management Function) validates the request against the UE’s subscription. It obtains **QoS Information** – the subscribed Session‑AMBR (Aggregate Maximum Bit Rate), Default 5QI (5G QoS Identifier), and Default ARP (Allocation‑Retention‑Priority) – from the **UDM** (Unified Data Management) [TS 23.501 §5.6].  

### QoS Flow and identifiers
The finest granularity of QoS differentiation is the **QoS Flow**. Every flow is identified by a **QoS Flow Identifier (QFI)**, unique within the PDU Session [TS 23.501 §5.7]. The QFI is carried in the N3/N9 user‑plane header, leaving the original IP packet unchanged. A QoS Flow can be **GBR** (requires a guaranteed bit rate) or **Non‑GBR** (no guarantee).  

A QoS Flow is described by a **QoS profile** that contains:

* **5QI** – the standardized QoS class identifier.  
* **ARP** – priority used for admission and pre‑emption.  
* For GBR flows: **GFBR** (Guaranteed Flow Bit Rate) and **MFBR** (Maximum Flow Bit Rate) in both uplink (UL) and downlink (DL).  
* Optional parameters such as **Reflective QoS Attribute (RQA)** for Non‑GBR flows, **Notification control**, **Maximum Packet Loss Rate**, and **PDU Set QoS Parameters** [TS 23.501 §5.7].

The QoS profile is conveyed from the SMF to the NG‑RAN (via the AMF on N2) and to the UE (via N1).  

### Example of QoS parameter calculation
Assume a UE requests a single PDU Session of type IPv4. The subscription specifies:

* Session‑AMBR = 10 Mbps (downlink) / 2 Mbps (uplink)  
* Default 5QI = 9 (Non‑GBR) with ARP = 1  
* An additional GBR flow for a video stream: GFBR\_DL = 3 Mbps, GFBR\_UL = 1 Mbps; MFBR\_DL = 5 Mbps, MFBR\_UL = 2 Mbps.

The SMF creates two QoS Flows:

| Flow | QFI | 5QI | ARP | Type | GFBR (DL/UL) | MFBR (DL/UL) |
|------|-----|-----|-----|------|--------------|--------------|
| 1 (default) | 60 | 9 | 1 | Non‑GBR | – | – |
| 2 (video)   | 61 | 1 | 2 | GBR    | 3 Mbps / 1 Mbps | 5 Mbps / 2 Mbps |

The **effective downlink bitrate** that the network must guarantee is the sum of the GBR flow (3 Mbps) plus any best‑effort traffic up to the remaining Session‑AMBR capacity (10 Mbps − 3 Mbps = 7 Mbps). Thus the UE can receive up to **10 Mbps** total, with at least **3 Mbps** guaranteed for the video flow. A similar calculation applies for the uplink.  

### Interaction of network functions
The **AMF** selects the appropriate **SMF** by querying the **NRF** (Network Repository Function) using the (possibly replaced) DNN [TS 23.501 §5.6]. The **PCF** may provide **PDU Set QoS Parameters** for flows that require coordinated handling (e.g., latency‑critical traffic) [TS 23.501 §5.7].  

For multi‑access scenarios (ATSSS), a PDU Session can be **single‑access** (either 3GPP or non‑3GPP) or **multi‑access** (both simultaneously), each with independent N3/N9 tunnels [TS 23.501 §5.6].  

### Common mistakes and practical notes
1. **Omitting the default QoS Flow** – Every PDU Session must contain a default QoS Flow; it is recommended to be Non‑GBR to maintain connectivity across EPS interworking [TS 23.501 §5.7].  
2. **Confusing 5QI with QFI** – 5QI classifies the QoS characteristics (e.g., latency, packet loss), while QFI uniquely identifies a flow inside the session.  
3. **Assuming a single DNN per UE** – A UE may have multiple subscribed DNNs per S‑NSSAI; the network may replace the requested DNN based on policy [TS 23.501 §5.6].  
4. **Neglecting ARP in admission control** – ARP influences pre‑emption; lower ARP values have higher priority.  
5. **Forgetting to update the PCF policies** – When operator policies change (e.g., new slice‑specific DNNs), the PCF must inform the AMF during registration; otherwise the UE may receive a “DNN not supported” rejection [TS 23.501 §5.6].

Understanding these concepts enables engineers to design data services that meet performance targets while respecting subscription constraints and network policies.

## 練習題

**1.** 若 UE 在 PDU Session 建立請求中未提供 DNN，AMF 應如何決定使用哪個 DNN？

- A. 直接拒絕建立請求
- B. 使用 UE 訂閱資訊中的預設 DNN（若有）
- C. 隨機選擇一個已支援的 DNN
- D. 使用 UE 最近一次使用的 DNN

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。若 UE 未提供 DNN，AMF 會選擇該 S‑NSSAI 的預設 DNN（若訂閱資訊中有）。

</details>

**2.** 當 UE 請求的 DNN 不被網路支援且 AMF 無法透過 NRF 找到 SMF 時，除非有 PCF 政策，AMF 應如何回應？

- A. 繼續使用 UE 請求的 DNN
- B. 將請求轉交給其他 PLMN
- C. 以「DNN not supported」原因拒絕 NAS 訊息
- D. 自動替換為本地配置的 DNN

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。若沒有 PCF 提供的 DNN 替換政策，AMF 必須以 DNN 不支援為原因拒絕請求。

</details>

**3.** 在 PDU Session 中，哪一項描述了 QoS 的最細粒度區分？

- A. QoS Profile
- B. QoS Flow
- C. QoS Rule
- D. QFI

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。QoS Flow 被定義為 PDU Session 中最細的 QoS 區分單位。

</details>

**4.** 對於 GBR QoS Flow，QoS Profile 必須包含哪些必須的 QoS 參數？

- A. 5QI、ARP、RQA
- B. GFBR、MFBR、Notification control
- C. GFBR、MFBR
- D. PDU Set QoS Parameters、RQA

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。GBR QoS Flow 的 QoS Profile 必須包含保證流位元速率 (GFBR) 以及最大流位元速率 (MFBR)。

</details>

**5.** 依規範，與預設 QoS Rule 相關聯的 QoS Flow 在 PDU Session 整個生命週期內應為何類型？

- A. 必須為 GBR QoS Flow
- B. 必須為 Non‑GBR QoS Flow
- C. 可以是 GBR 或 Non‑GBR，視應用而定
- D. 不需要任何 QoS Flow

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。預設 QoS Rule 必須持續存在且應為 Non‑GBR，以確保 UE 在整個會話期間都有連線。

</details>
