## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【ORANBench】**

> Which of the following protocols is NOT used in the O-RAN test setup described in the context?
>
> A. F1AP
> B. NGAP
> C. GTP
> D. PFCP

正解：C. GTP

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> How does the Frugal 5G network improve network resilience?
>
> A. By diverting resources to overloaded networks
> B. By reducing the cost of network equipment
> C. By enabling fault tolerance in the equipment
> D. By avoiding Cloud-Fog signaling/data transfer
> E. By instantiating network functions in the Fog

正解：E. By instantiating network functions in the Fog

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the filename for the O-RAN CU-UP E2 PCAP configuration?
>
> A. /tmp/cu_up_e2ap.pcap
> B. /tmp/cu_up_e2ap.pcap.log
> C. /tmp/cu_up_e2ap.pcap.gz
> D. /tmp/cu_up_e2ap.pcap.txt
