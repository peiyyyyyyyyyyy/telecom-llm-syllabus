## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which attack method prevents access to the manufacturing systems?
>
> A. Data tampering
> B. Denial of service
> C. IP theft
> D. Reverse engineering
> E. Reliability degradation

正解：B. Denial of service

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the ciphering_engine_nea2 class?
>
> A. To provide a secure way to encrypt and decrypt data.
> B. To implement a specific algorithm for generating cryptographic keys.
> C. To handle the communication between the srsRAN network and the user.
> D. To manage the security settings for the srsRAN system.

正解：A. To provide a secure way to encrypt and decrypt data.

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the purpose of placing the IV in the header of the frame in WEP? [IEEE 802.11]
>
> A. To enable the receiver to decrypt the frame
> B. To ensure the integrity of the frame
> C. To protect the frame from brute-force decryption attacks
> D. To prevent the reuse of the RC4 keystream
> E. To identify the default key used to encrypt the frame

正解：A. To enable the receiver to decrypt the frame
