## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which entity is responsible for end-to-end network slicing for multiple operators in the Frugal 5G network?
>
> A. NFV Controller
> B. Cloud Controller
> C. RSCF
> D. SDN Controller
> E. Slice Management Function in the Fog

正解：E. Slice Management Function in the Fog

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the maximum number of QoS flows supported by srsRAN?
>
> A. 16
> B. 32
> C. 64
> D. 128

正解：C. 64

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which of the following is not a characteristic of massive machine type of communication in cellular networks?
>
> A. Sporadic traffic
> B. Large payload
> C. Low power consumption
> D. Limited capacity
> E. Extensive coverage

正解：B. Large payload
