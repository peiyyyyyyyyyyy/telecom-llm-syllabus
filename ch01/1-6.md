## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What does PDR stand for?
>
> A. Packet Delay Ratio
> B. Packet Duplication Rate
> C. Packet Data Rate
> D. Packet Delivery Ratio
> E. Packet Loss Rate

正解：D. Packet Delivery Ratio

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What does the Packet Error Rate (PER) represent?
>
> A. The rate of PDUs that are successfully delivered by the receiver.
> B. The rate of PDUs that are not successfully delivered by the receiver.
> C. The rate of PDUs that are processed by the sender.
> D. The rate of PDUs that are lost due to congestion.

正解：B. The rate of PDUs that are not successfully delivered by the receiver.

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is an Allowable PLMN?
>
> A. A defined set of procedures that is adopted at an interface at a specified reference point between a user and a network to enable the user to employ the services and/or facilities of that network
> B. A PLMN that is not in the list of forbidden PLMN in the UE
> C. A list stored in the UE containing the identities and associated PLMN identities of the CSGs to which the subscriber belongs
> D. A cell that the UE may camp on to make emergency calls
> E. A set of security attributes associated with a file

正解：B. A PLMN that is not in the list of forbidden PLMN in the UE
