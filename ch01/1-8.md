## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What advantage does tUAV (tethered UAVs) have over terrestrial BSs (base stations) in terms of maintenance?
>
> A. tUAVs require climbing high towers for maintenance.
> B. tUAVs have safer maintenance procedures compared to terrestrial BSs.
> C. tUAVs require more maintenance hours compared to terrestrial BSs.
> D. tUAVs have shorter maintenance intervals compared to terrestrial BSs.
> E. None of the above.

正解：B. tUAVs have safer maintenance procedures compared to terrestrial BSs.
