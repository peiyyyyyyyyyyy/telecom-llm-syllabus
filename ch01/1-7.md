## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the impact of proactive computing in the virtual reality gaming arcade scenario?
>
> A. It decreases the computing latency and increases the communication latency
> B. It decreases both the computing latency and the communication latency
> C. It increases both the computing latency and the communication latency
> D. It decreases the computing latency but has no impact on the communication latency
> E. It increases the computing latency but has no impact on the communication latency

正解：A. It decreases the computing latency and increases the communication latency

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the `trigger_handover_from_measurements` field in the `mobility_manager_cfg` struct?
>
> A. To configure the handover trigger based on the network signal strength.
> B. To configure the handover trigger based on the user's location.
> C. To configure the handover trigger based on the network latency.
> D. To configure the handover trigger based on the user's device preferences.

正解：A. To configure the handover trigger based on the network signal strength.

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the function of an enabling station (STA)?
>
> A. To generate emergency alerts
> B. To encapsulate plaintext data into protected frames
> C. To control when and how a dependent station (STA) can operate
> D. To detect radar signals in the 5 GHz band
> E. To interconnect access points (APs) and mesh gates

正解：C. To control when and how a dependent station (STA) can operate
