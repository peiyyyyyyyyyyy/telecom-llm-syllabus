## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the validate_hal_config function?
>
> A. To validate the logger configuration.
> B. To validate the HAL configuration.
> C. To validate the PLMN and TACs.
> D. To validate the application configuration.

正解：B. To validate the HAL configuration.

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the `phy_fapi_adaptor` class?
>
> A. It provides a collection of interfaces to translate FAPI messages into their PHY layer counterpart and vice versa.
> B. It manages the communication between the PHY and FAPI layers.
> C. It handles the configuration of the FAPI adaptor.
> D. It provides a way to access the slot data.

正解：A. It provides a collection of interfaces to translate FAPI messages into their PHY layer counterpart and vice versa.

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the prach_buffer_pool class?
>
> A. To manage the lifecycle of PRACH buffers.
> B. To provide a way to access PRACH buffers.
> C. To implement the PRACH protocol.
> D. To handle the synchronization of PRACH buffers.

正解：A. To manage the lifecycle of PRACH buffers.
