# 4.2.6 排程器與資源區塊分配

所屬：[4.2 基地台軟體](../4-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which identifier does the gNB use to dynamically allocate downlink resources to UEs?

- A. CS-RNTI
- B. C-RNTI
- C. CI-RNTI
- D. P-RNTI

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The gNB uses the C-RNTI on the PDCCH to assign downlink resources dynamically.

</details>

**2.** Which RNTI is employed by the gNB to indicate an interrupted transmission in the downlink?

- A. INT‑RNTI
- B. CS‑RNTI
- C. CI‑RNTI
- D. C‑RNTI

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The INT‑RNTI is specifically mentioned for monitoring interrupted transmission indications.

</details>

**3.** How many active configured downlink assignments may a UE have for a given BWP of a serving cell?

- A. 4
- B. 8
- C. 12
- D. 16

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification permits up to eight such assignments per BWP.

</details>

**4.** In uplink scheduling, which RNTI does the gNB use to signal a cancelled transmission indication?

- A. CI‑RNTI
- B. INT‑RNTI
- C. CS‑RNTI
- D. C‑RNTI

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。CI‑RNTI is defined for monitoring cancelled transmission indications in the uplink.

</details>

**5.** If a UE is not configured with enhanced intra‑UE overlapping resources prioritization, what happens when a dynamically allocated uplink transmission overlaps a configured uplink grant?

- A. The configured grant overrides the dynamic transmission.
- B. The dynamic transmission overrides the configured grant.
- C. Both transmissions are merged into a single grant.
- D. The UE discards both transmissions.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Without the enhanced prioritization feature, the dynamic allocation takes precedence over the configured grant.

</details>
