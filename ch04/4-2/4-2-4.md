# 4.2.4 程式對應的規格程序

所屬：[4.2 基地台軟體](../4-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** In Transparent Mode, what must the transmitting RLC entity do with an SDU before sending it to the lower layer?

- A. Add an RLC header with a new SN
- B. Fragment the SDU into multiple PDUs
- C. Submit the SDU without any modification
- D. Encrypt the SDU payload

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。TM transmits SDUs unchanged, as required by the specification.

</details>

**2.** Which condition determines that a received UM SN is inside the reassembly window?

- A. $(RX\_Next\_Highest - UM\_Window\_Size) \le \text{SN} < RX\_Next\_Highest$
- B. $\text{SN} = RX\_Next\_Reassembly$
- C. $\text{SN} > RX\_Next\_Highest$
- D. $\text{SN} < (RX\_Next\_Highest - UM\_Window\_Size)$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The standard defines the reassembly window with that inequality.

</details>

**3.** When a UM PDU header does **not** contain an SN, the receiving entity must:

- A. Discard the PDU
- B. Place it in the reception buffer
- C. Remove the RLC header and deliver the SDU to the upper layer
- D. Trigger a status report to the transmitter

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Absence of SN leads to immediate delivery after header removal.

</details>

**4.** In AM, what must the transmitter do if an AMD PDU’s SN is outside the transmitting window?

- A. Send it anyway but set a flag
- B. Buffer it for later transmission
- C. Not submit it to the lower layer
- D. Change its SN to the next valid value

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The spec forbids sending PDUs with SNs outside the window.

</details>

**5.** After a UL grant is received on the PDCCH, what is the immediate action required by the MAC entity?

- A. Store the grant for future use only
- B. Forward the grant to the PDCP layer
- C. Deliver the grant and the associated HARQ information to the HARQ entity
- D. Ignore the grant if NDI = 0

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The MAC must pass the grant to HARQ for transmission.

</details>
