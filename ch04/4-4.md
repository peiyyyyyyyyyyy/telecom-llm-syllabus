## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【srsRANBench】**

> What does the function `register_split6_o_du_loggers` do?
>
> A. It registers all the loggers for the O-RAN DU split 6.
> B. It configures the O-RAN DU split 6.
> C. It logs the O-RAN DU split 6 configuration.
> D. It handles the O-RAN DU split 6 communication.

正解：A. It registers all the loggers for the O-RAN DU split 6.
