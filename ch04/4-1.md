## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the `du_ue_manager_repository` class?
>
> A. To manage the resources of a DU.
> B. To manage the resources of a UE.
> C. To manage the resources of a GNB.
> D. To manage the resources of a F1-U bearer.

正解：A. To manage the resources of a DU.

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the responsibility of the code profiler in the offloading process?
>
> A. To determine what could be offloaded
> B. To monitor various parameters
> C. To decide whether to offload or not
> D. To allocate computation resources within the MEC

正解：A. To determine what could be offloaded

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the `create_sw_pdsch_processor_factory` function?
>
> A. To create a PUSCH processor factory.
> B. To create a PDSCH processor factory.
> C. To create a LDPC decoder factory.
> D. To create an asynchronous executor.

正解：B. To create a PDSCH processor factory.
