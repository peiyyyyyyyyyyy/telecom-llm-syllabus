# 6.3.2 需要工程師批准的處置

所屬：[6.3 處置](6-3.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

會改變網路元件（Network Element, NE）狀態或組態、可能影響使用者服務的處置，例如鎖定／解鎖小區、重啟、修改參數、人工切換備援、軟體回退，業者通常規定要由工程師批准才能執行。要注意兩件事：第一，「要誰批准、批准到什麼層級」是**業者自己的作業規定**，3GPP 並沒有規定；本節用虛構的甲電信規定舉例，正式流程見第 9 章異動流程。第二，有些恢復動作是 NE 依規格**自動**完成的，不需要、也來不及等人批准（TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clause 4.1.6，[規格檔](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/32111-1-i00.zip)）。維運人員看完 6.3.1 的查證結果、準備動手之前，就要分清楚哪些已經自動發生、哪些需要人工處置並送批准。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

**一、先分清「NE 自動做的」和「需要人工處置的」**（[TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/32111-1-i00.zip)）

| NE 依規格自動做的 | 需要人工（操作人員）處置的 |
|---|---|
| 偵測到故障後，NE 要評估對服務的影響，並自主（autonomously）採取恢復動作，以減少服務劣化或中斷（clause 4.1.6 第 1 點，shall） | 故障單元換好或修好之後，從 EM 把它放回服務（clause 4.1.6 第 2 點：原文是「it shall be possible from the EM」，即 EM 要具備這個能力） |
| 無備援時：隔離故障資源、把依賴它的資源移出服務、處理狀態、通知管理系統（clause 4.1.6 a～d，shall） | 操作人員依自己的分析，要求 NE 執行恢復動作，或為了驗證而主動要求（clause 4.1.6 第 3 點） |
| 有備援時：除了 a、c、d，還要執行該備援型式的切換程序；服務中的資源故障時，切換要立即開始，切換前後可以接受短暫、有限的服務損失（clause 4.1.6） | 以管理命令觸發的切換，NE 應（should）在不劣化服務的情況下完成（clause 4.1.6）；例如鎖定作用中資源，讓備援接手做預防性維護（clause 4.2.0） |
| 故障造成狀態改變時，自動把依賴它的資源改成 disabled，修復後再改回 enabled（clause 4.2.1） | 鎖定、解鎖資源；侵入式測試前用 administrative state 把資源移出服務（clause 4.2.0） |
| ADAC 故障消失時，NE 應能（should）自行辨識並清除告警；門檻跨越類告警不再成立時，NE 要（shall）自行清除（clause 4.1.1、4.1.3） | ADMC 故障一定要操作人員手動清除（clause 4.1.1：manual intervention ... shall always be necessary） |

另外，因故障後重新設定組態屬於組態管理（Configuration Management, CM），不在故障管理規格範圍內（TS 32.111-1 clause 1）。

**二、狀態名稱照原文**

- 通用的 `administrativeState`：`LOCKED`、`UNLOCKED`，由 MnS consumer 設定；`operationalState`：`ENABLED`、`DISABLED`，唯讀（TS 28.622 V18.14.0 clause 4.4.1）。
- 小區 `NRCellDU.administrativeState` 多一個值：`LOCKED`、`SHUTTING DOWN`、`UNLOCKED`，意義依 ITU-T X.731（TS 28.541 V18.15.0 clause 4.4.1）。依該規格附錄的小區狀態轉移表，收到 shut down 請求後，等最後一個使用者離開，gNB-DU 才送出刪除該服務小區的組態更新（TS 28.541 Annex A, table A.2-1 轉移 9、10）。

**三、送批准前要準備的內容**（一般實務）：要做什麼（對哪個物件、改哪個屬性、改成什麼值）、6.3.1 查到的依據、預期影響範圍與時間、回復方式、執行與確認的人。執行後要再用 6.3.1 的讀取動作確認結果，例如狀態是否真的改變、告警是否清除、KPI 是否回到正常；沒有確認就不能在工單上寫「已恢復」。若處置後情況沒有改善，先依事前寫好的回復方式退回原狀，再回到 6.2 重新檢討假設，不要在未經批准的情況下接連嘗試其他處置。

**甲電信（虛構）處置分級規定**

| 等級 | 例子 | 批准 |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 6.3.1 的讀取查證 | 不需批准 |
| 1 | 手動清除 ADMC 告警、確認告警 | 值班工程師自行執行，記入工單 |
| 2 | 單一小區 `SHUTTING DOWN`／`LOCKED`／`UNLOCKED`、修改單一參數 | 值班主管工程師批准，開立緊急異動單（第 9 章） |
| 3 | 整站重啟、人工切換備援、軟體回退、多站參數修改 | 網路維運經理批准，依第 9 章異動流程 |

#### 實際例子

延續 6.3.1 的 gNB-TPE-0417（設備與數值虛構），值班人員收到下列紀錄（告警欄位 `backedUpStatus`、`backUpObject` 見 [TS 28.111 V18.8.0 clause 7.3.1](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.111/28111-i80.zip)，其意義依 ITU-T X.733 clause 8.1.2.4）：

```
02:31:05  notifyNewAlarm  objectInstance=...,ManagedElement=gNB-TPE-0417,
          VsBoard=BB-1  alarmType=EQUIPMENT_ALARM  perceivedSeverity=MAJOR
          backedUpStatus=true  backUpObject=...,VsBoard=BB-2
02:31:05  VsBoard=BB-1  operationalState ENABLED -> DISABLED
02:31:07  (廠商日誌) BB-2 standby -> active
02:32:00  NRCellDU=1..3  operationalState=ENABLED  cellState=ACTIVE
```

（VsBoard 為虛構的廠商自訂物件類別；告警的 objectInstance 只需是 DN，不要求該物件可用 CRUD 操作存取，TS 28.111 V18.8.0 clause 6.3。）

逐步判斷：

1. BB-1 故障、有備援，NE 已自行隔離 BB-1 並切到 BB-2（TS 32.111-1 clause 4.1.6）。這一步**已經發生**，不是等待批准的處置；值班人員不該為了「先申請批准」而手動切回或重啟。
2. 三個小區仍為 `ENABLED`／`ACTIVE`，仍在服務；切換時若有通話中斷，原文要求系統立即釋放這些通話的資源，不是由人工處理。
3. 接下來需要人工的事：派員更換 BB-1（現場作業）、更換後由 EM 把 BB-1 放回服務、若業者要讓 BB-1 回到主用，則以管理命令切換。依虛構規定，放回服務屬第 2 級、主動切回屬第 3 級，排在離峰時段並走第 9 章流程。

不能下的結論：紀錄只顯示 BB-1 故障，不能據此判定 6.3.1 那個小區吞吐下降也是 BB-1 造成的，兩者要分開查證。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **「需要工程師批准」不是 3GPP 規定**：規格只規定 NE 要能自動恢復、EM 要能執行人工恢復；誰批准是業者規定（見第 9 章）。不可寫成「依 TS 32.111-1 須經批准」。
- **NE 自動恢復不需批准**：備援切換、隔離故障資源、ADAC 告警自動清除都是 NE 依規格做的；相反地，ADMC 告警規格明定要人工清除，NE 不會自己清掉（[TS 28.111 V18.8.0 clause 6.8](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.111/28111-i80.zip)）。
- **`SHUTTING DOWN` 不是 `LOCKED`**：前者讓既有使用者用完再停，後者立即禁止使用；而且 `SHUTTING DOWN` 是 `NRCellDU` 的值，TS 28.622 的通用 `administrativeState` 只有 `LOCKED`、`UNLOCKED`。
- **`operationalState` 不能用命令設定**：它是唯讀，由物件自己反映能否運作；人工能改的是 `administrativeState`。

#### 和其他小節的關係

處置之前要先完成 6.3.1 的查證，處置依據來自 6.2 的推理；類似處置是否有效可參考 6.3.3 過去案例。批准與執行要依第 9 章異動流程，處置內容記進 6.4.3 工單，未完成的處置寫進 6.5.1 交接單，最後在 6.5.2 障礙報告說明（[TS 32.111-1 規格檔](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/32111-1-i00.zip)）。

### 補充練習題（5 題）

#### NE autonomous recovery versus operator-requested recovery

**情境 001**　Radio unit failure without redundancy

Site KH-0412 (gNB-KH-0412, fictional) has three radio units, each serving one cell, with no spare radio path. The extract below is from the EM event log and the trouble ticket.

```
EM event log, gNB-KH-0412, 2026-11-18
02:14:37  notifyNewAlarm  objectInstance=...,ManagedElement=gNB-KH-0412,VsRadioUnit=RU-2
          alarmType=EQUIPMENT_ALARM  perceivedSeverity=CRITICAL
          specificProblem="RU-2 PA failure (vendor text)"  backedUpStatus=false
02:14:37  VsRadioUnit=RU-2  operationalState ENABLED -> DISABLED
02:14:38  NRCellDU=2  operationalState ENABLED -> DISABLED   (depends on RU-2)
02:14:39  notifyMOIAttributeValueChanges sent to OS for RU-2 and NRCellDU=2
02:14:40  NRCellDU=1  operationalState=ENABLED  cellState=ACTIVE  (served by RU-1)
02:14:40  NRCellDU=3  operationalState=ENABLED  cellState=ACTIVE  (served by RU-3)
02:15:02  VsRadioUnit=RU-2  administrativeState=UNLOCKED (unchanged)

Ticket TT-58812, note 02:31 (NOC engineer L. Hsu):
  "RU-2 down, no spare path. Requesting approval to LOCK RU-2
   to isolate the fault, then a full site restart to try recovery."
(VsRadioUnit is a fictional vendor-specific class.)
```

**004.** After RU-2 has been replaced, what does TS 32.111-1 clause 4.1.6 item 2 say about putting it back into service?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It shall be possible from the EM to put the previously faulty unit back into service, and this transition should be done so that the services currently provided are not, or only minimally, disturbed.

**詳解**　Item 2 places the capability in the EM (an operator action, not an autonomous NE action) and uses "shall" for the capability but "should" for the no-or-minimal-disturbance expectation. Whether that operator action needs approval is not stated by 3GPP; it is set by the operator's own change rule.

**出處**　3GPP TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clause 4.1.6 item 2 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/)

</details>

**005.** The engineer also proposes a full site restart "to try recovery". Can the log show whether a restart would recover RU-2?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the log contains only the alarm (a hardware PA failure) and the state changes; a test or diagnosis result on RU-2, or the alarm's documented repair action, is needed before a restart can be justified.

**詳解**　Clause 4.1.1 says the fault detection identifies the smallest replaceable unit for hardware faults, and clause 4.3 describes tests to localize faults when the alarm is not enough. Nothing in the data says the PA failure is recoverable by software means, while a full site restart would also interrupt the healthy NRCellDU=1 and NRCellDU=3.

**出處**　3GPP TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clauses 4.1.1 and 4.3 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/); Curriculum 6.3.2

</details>

**情境 003**　Handover note mixing NE and operator actions

The handover note below for site TN-0108 (fictional) lists who did each step. Under the Telco-A rule (fictional operator rule), alarm acknowledgement is level 1 (on-duty engineer), putting a unit back into service is level 2 (duty supervisor engineer, emergency change ticket), and manual redundancy switchover is level 3 (network operations manager, chapter 9 change process).

| Time | Actor | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| 11-21 14:05:10 | NE | BB-1 fault detected; alarm with backedUpStatus=true, backUpObject=...,VsBoard=BB-2 |
| 11-21 14:05:10 | NE | BB-1 operationalState ENABLED -> DISABLED; BB-2 standby -> active |
| 11-21 14:05:12 | NE | Alarm and state-change notifications sent to OS |
| 11-21 14:20 | NOC | Alarm acknowledged (ackState=ACKNOWLEDGED), level 1 |
| 11-22 10:12 | Field | BB-1 replaced with spare board |
| 11-22 10:40 | NOC via EM | BB-1 put back into service as standby (CHG-7731, level 2 approved) |
| 11-22 10:41 | NE | BB-1 operationalState DISABLED -> ENABLED; BB-1 alarm cleared |
| 11-22 23:30 (planned) | NOC via EM | Switchover BB-2 -> BB-1 by management command (CHG-7740, awaiting approval) |
| 11-23 02:00 (planned) | NOC via EM | Operator-requested recovery test on BB-2, proactive maintenance (not yet requested) |

**013.** Under the Telco-A rule (fictional operator rule), who must approve the 23:30 entry, CHG-7740?

1. The network operations manager, as a level 3 action under the chapter 9 change process
2. The duty supervisor engineer, as a level 2 action with an emergency change ticket
3. The on-duty engineer alone, as a level 1 action recorded in the ticket
4. Nobody, because redundancy changeovers are done by the NE automatically

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　In the Telco-A table, manual redundancy switchover is level 3 (network operations manager, chapter 9 change process). Option 2 is the level for single-cell state changes or single-parameter changes. Option 3 is the level given for alarm acknowledgement. Option 4 confuses the failure-triggered changeover (automatic, clause 4.1.6) with a changeover by management command, which is an operator action.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.3.2 (Telco-A approval levels, fictional operator rule) and chapter 9 change process; 3GPP TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clause 4.1.6 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/)

</details>

**014.** According to TS 32.111-1 clause 4.1.6, which three factors determine the recovery actions an NE performs, autonomously or on demand?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The nature and severity of the fault, the hardware and software capabilities of the NE, and the current configuration of the NE.

**詳解**　The clause states that the recovery actions "depend on the nature and severity of the faults, on the hardware and software capabilities of the NE and on the current configuration of the NE"; this is why the same alarm can lead to a changeover on one NE and only isolation on another.

**出處**　3GPP TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clause 4.1.6 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/)

</details>

**015.** Was the replacement BB-1 tested before it was put back into service at 10:40?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the handover note has no test entry between 10:12 and 10:40; the test result record (or the EM test log) for BB-1 is needed.

**詳解**　Clause 4.3 says tests can be executed on a repaired or replaced resource before it is restored to service, but the note only shows replacement, return to service and the state change. Absence of an entry is not evidence that no test was run.

**出處**　3GPP TS 32.111-1 V18.0.0 clause 4.3 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/32_series/32.111-1/)

</details>
