# 6.3.2 需要工程師批准的處置

所屬：[6.3 處置](../6-3.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following situations *must* involve engineer approval according to the standard?

- A. Automatic hot‑standby switchover after a hardware fault.
- B. Isolation and removal of a non‑redundant faulty resource.
- C. Generation of OS notifications after any recovery action.
- D. Download of a software load on a redundant unit.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Isolation and removal of a non‑redundant faulty resource requires manual assessment of service impact, thus needs engineer approval.

</details>

**2.** What does the specification say about the NE's ability to act on operator requests?

- A. The NE can only act on automatic detections, never on operator requests.
- B. The NE shall be able to perform recovery actions if requested by the operator.
- C. The NE must reject any operator‑initiated recovery to avoid service loss.
- D. The NE performs operator requests only after a software reboot.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The standard explicitly states the NE shall be able to perform recovery actions upon operator request.

</details>

**3.** When a faulty resource has redundancy, which recovery steps are *always* performed regardless of redundancy type?

- A. Activation of a fallback software load.
- B. Isolation of the faulty resource, state management, and notification generation.
- C. Full system reboot.
- D. Manual replacement of the hardware by the operator.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification lists actions a), c) and d) (isolate, state management, notifications) as always required, even with redundancy.

</details>

**4.** According to the reference, what is the acceptable condition during a changeover for a resource providing service?

- A. No loss of service is ever acceptable.
- B. A temporary and limited loss of service shall be acceptable.
- C. The service must be completely halted for at least 5 minutes.
- D. Only a permanent service degradation is allowed.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The standard permits a brief, limited loss of service during a changeover.

</details>

**5.** Which of the following is *not* listed as a recovery action for software faults in the specification?

- A. Activation of a backup software load.
- B. System initialization at different levels.
- C. Isolation and removal of a faulty hardware resource.
- D. Download of a software unit.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Isolation and removal of a faulty hardware resource pertains to hardware faults, not software faults.

</details>
