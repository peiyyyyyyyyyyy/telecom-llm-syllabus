# 6.3.1 可以先查證的動作

所屬：[6.3 處置](../6-3.md)

## 練習題

**1.** When an alarm does not contain enough detail to locate a fault, what does TS 32.111‑1 §4.3 recommend?

- A. Immediately replace the suspected hardware.
- B. Execute tests to better localize the fault.
- C. Ignore the alarm and wait for a second occurrence.
- D. Perform a full network reboot.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The standard explicitly states that tests can be executed to better localize the fault when alarm information is insufficient.

</details>

**2.** Who typically initiates the execution of a test according to the reference?

- A. The managed network element (NE) itself.
- B. The operator via the managing system (OS).
- C. An external third‑party testing service.
- D. The fault management database.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The operator requests the test from the OS, and the NE carries it out autonomously.

</details>

**3.** After a component has been repaired, when should a test be performed?

- A. Only if another alarm occurs later.
- B. Immediately before the component is restored to service.
- C. After the component has been in service for 24 hours.
- D. No test is required once the repair is completed.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification requires a test before returning the repaired resource to service.

</details>

**4.** Which of the following activities is explicitly stated as out of scope for TS 32.111‑1 §4.3?

- A. Autonomous NE self‑tests.
- B. Operator‑initiated test‑bed configuration.
- C. Local maintenance and inter‑NE testing.
- D. Alarm generation.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The document mentions that local maintenance and inter‑NE testing are out of scope.

</details>
