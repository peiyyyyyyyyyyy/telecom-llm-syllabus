# 6.2.4 分辨幾種可能

所屬：[6.2 推理](../6-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following problems is *not* listed as a target of Mobility Robustness Optimisation (MRO) in the reference specifications?

- A. Connection failure due to intra‑system mobility
- B. Inter‑system Unnecessary HO
- C. PSCell change failure
- D. Radio Link Failure timeout

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。The reference lists connection failure, unnecessary HO, ping‑pong, and PSCell change failure, but not a generic RLF timeout.

</details>

**2.** According to TS 28.627, HO failures are categorized into three groups. Which group is *not* part of this categorization?

- A. Too late HO triggering
- B. Too early HO triggering
- C. HO to a wrong cell
- D. Inter‑system ping‑pong HO

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。The three categories are too late, too early, and wrong cell; ping‑pong is mentioned elsewhere but not in the three‑group list.

</details>

**3.** In the “too early HO triggering” scenario, what is the primary cause of the hand‑over failure?

- A. The UE enters an unintended island of coverage of the target cell inside the serving cell’s area.
- B. The UE moves faster than the HO parameters can accommodate.
- C. The source cell sends a wrong HO command.
- D. The target cell’s transmit power is too low.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The reference describes the island‑coverage situation as the reason for too early failure.

</details>

**4.** Which definition matches “Inter‑system Unnecessary HO” as defined for MRO?

- A. A hand‑over from NR to E‑UTRAN that occurs too early and does not result in a radio link failure.
- B. A hand‑over that repeatedly switches between two NR cells.
- C. A hand‑over that is triggered after the serving cell’s signal has already become too low.
- D. A hand‑over to a cell that is not on the UE’s trajectory.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The standard defines unnecessary HO as a too‑early inter‑system HO from NR to E‑UTRAN with no RLF.

</details>

**5.** MRO provides observability of successful hand‑overs caused by which type of mobility?

- A. Intra‑NR mobility
- B. Inter‑system mobility
- C. Inter‑frequency mobility
- D. Inter‑RAT mobility

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The reference explicitly mentions successful HO due to intra‑NR mobility.

</details>
