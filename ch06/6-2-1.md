# 6.2.1 由時間軸提出假設

所屬：[6.2 推理](6-2.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

由時間軸提出假設，是在 5.5.1 把告警、KPI、日誌、變更紀錄排成同一條時間軸之後的下一步：從時間軸上讀出「可能是什麼造成的」，寫成可以檢驗的假設（hypothesis）。常見時機是：夜間維護窗後某些小區 KPI 下滑、短時間湧入同區域客訴、多個網元幾乎同時告警。這一步不下結論，只排出「該先查什麼」。Google SRE Book 第 12 章〈Effective Troubleshooting〉把排障描述成提出假設、再檢驗假設的反覆過程，並提醒最近的變更往往是好的起點（[Google SRE Book, Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。以下步驟是本教材整理的做法，不是任何 3GPP 規格的規定。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

1. **先統一時間基準。** 不同來源的時間格式不同：告警紀錄裡有 alarmRaisedTime、alarmChangedTime、alarmClearedTime 等時間屬性（[TS 28.111](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.111/) V18.8.0 clause 7.3.1.2、7.4）；syslog 的 TIMESTAMP 依 RFC 3339 格式並帶時區位移 TIME-OFFSET，「Z」表示 UTC（[RFC 5424](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5424) clause 6.2.3）；變更紀錄多半是人填的施工時段。全部換成同一時區再排序，日期也要一起換。
2. **標出每筆資料的時間精度。** KPI 是一個收集週期的統計值，只能說「這個週期內出現異常」，看不出是週期內哪一分鐘開始；變更紀錄的施工時段也不等於參數實際生效的時間，要找網元日誌裡的生效紀錄。
3. **找「最早出現的異常」。** 指時間軸上最早偏離平常水準的資料點，不是最早被人看到的告警。往前多看一兩個週期，確認在那之前真的正常。
4. **找「同時發生的變更」。** 包括參數修改、軟體升級、硬體更換、傳輸調整、鄰站開關，以及大型活動、天候等外部事件。「同時」的判斷以資料精度為準：異常落在某個 15 分鐘週期，那麼這 15 分鐘內生效的變更都算同時。
5. **比對範圍。** 劣化範圍和變更影響範圍是否吻合；只動一個小區卻整區劣化，就要懷疑另有共同上游原因。
6. **寫成可檢驗的假設。** 格式是「X 在 T 時生效，造成 Y」，並寫出「如果它成立，還應該看到什麼；如果不成立，會看到什麼」。一開始保留 2～3 個假設，不要只留一個。
7. **注意先後關係的例外。** 晚於異常開始的事件通常是結果或巧合；但告警可能延遲上報，所以「記錄時間晚」不一定是「發生時間晚」。

#### 實際例子

Telco-X（虛構）在 2026-11-19 凌晨對小區 TPE-GN-0412-2（虛構）做了參數變更。值班人員把四種來源排成下表（時間已統一為 +08:00；KPI 收集週期為該業者設定的 15 分鐘，虛構；量測名稱依 [TS 28.552](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/) V18.12.0 clause 5.1.1.15.1、5.1.1.15.2）。

| 時間（+08:00） | 來源 | 內容 |
|---|---|---|
| 11-19 01:30 | 變更紀錄 CHG-1118-031（虛構） | 施工時段開始：修改 TPE-GN-0412-2 接取相關參數 |
| 01:30–01:45 | KPI | RRC.ConnEstabAtt 1,162；RRC.ConnEstabSucc 1,150（成功比例 98.97%） |
| 01:45–02:00 | KPI | RRC.ConnEstabAtt 1,200；RRC.ConnEstabSucc 1,188（99.00%） |
| 02:03:40 | 網元 syslog，原文 `2026-11-18T18:03:40Z` | 參數組生效 |
| 02:00–02:15 | KPI | RRC.ConnEstabAtt 1,260；RRC.ConnEstabSucc 1,071（85.00%） |
| 02:15–02:30 | KPI | RRC.ConnEstabAtt 1,310；RRC.ConnEstabSucc 1,100（83.97%） |
| 02:21 | 告警 | 相鄰站 TPE-GN-0415（虛構）傳輸埠告警發生 |
| 02:30 | 變更紀錄 | 施工結束，施工人員填「完成，無異常」 |

（成功比例＝Succ ÷ Att，為本例自行計算，用來看趨勢。）

逐步判斷：

1. **時區陷阱。** syslog 原文是 UTC 的 11-18 18:03:40，直接照抄會以為它發生在前一天傍晚；換算後是 11-19 02:03:40，落在施工時段內。
2. **最早的異常**是 02:00–02:15 這個週期；前兩個週期都在 99% 左右，可以視為正常。
3. **同時發生的變更**：參數在 02:03:40 生效，和最早異常落在同一個週期，因此列為假設 H1：「CHG-1118-031 的參數生效，造成 TPE-GN-0412-2 的 RRC 建立失敗增加」。若 H1 成立，應該只有被改的小區劣化，回到舊值後應恢復。
4. **其他事件**：0415 的傳輸告警在 02:21，晚於異常開始且在另一站，不像起因，但列為次要假設 H2。另保留 H3：同一週期內有未記錄的外部因素，要看鄰近小區同時段是否也劣化。
5. **還不能下結論的地方**：目前只有時間吻合，這是相關，不是因果；15 分鐘的精度也無法確認劣化在 02:03:40 之後才開始；施工人員的「無異常」是主觀判斷，不是證據。這些交給 6.2.2 找支持證據與反證。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **「最早收到的告警」不等於「最早出現的異常」。** 告警有門檻與延遲，KPI 劣化可能更早出現；反過來，KPI 要等週期結束才產出，也可能比告警晚看到。
- **施工時段不等於生效時間。** 變更紀錄寫 01:30–02:30，真正的生效時間要看網元日誌；只用施工時段，容易把無關的劣化歸給變更。
- **時間吻合只是提出假設的理由，不是證明。** SRE Book 同一章也提醒相關不等於因果（[Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。
- **忘了換日期。** UTC 與 +08:00 相差 8 小時，凌晨事件換算時常常跨日（[RFC 5424](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5424)）。

#### 和其他小節的關係

本節的輸入是 5.5.1 的時間軸與 5.5.3 分開的事實與推測；假設交給 6.2.2 列證據，資料不夠依 6.2.3 補收，多個假設都說得通用 6.2.4 分辨。回退參數屬 6.3.2，依第 9 章異動流程執行；時間軸也放進 6.5.2 障礙報告（[Google SRE Book, Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。

**情境 002**　Night-shift note dismisses a router link flap

A wave of "no data" complaints came in around site TC-0218 (fictional) on the morning of 2026-11-23. The night-shift engineer pasted the raw lines below and added a note.

```

<187>1 2026-11-22T23:33:02.117Z tr-tc-07 ifmgr - LINK [timeQuality tzKnown="1" isSynced="1"] port 0/3 down

--- Microwave terminal MW-TC-118 (RFC 5424, as received) ---
<188>1 2026-11-23T07:30:41+08:00 mw-tc-118 radio - RSL [timeQuality tzKnown="1" isSynced="0"] receive level below threshold

--- Customer-care queue (+08:00) ---
2026-11-23 07:40-08:10  37 tickets "no mobile data", all near TC-0218

--- Change calendar ---
No planned work for the TC area on 2026-11-22 or 2026-11-23

--- Night-shift note ---
"The router link flaps were yesterday evening at 23:31, not related to this morning's complaints."
```

**006.** What is wrong with the night-shift note?

1. Nothing: the router lines are dated 2026-11-22 and the complaints 2026-11-23, so they are on different days.
2. Add eight hours to the router clock while retaining 2026-11-22 as the date, giving 07:31 on the previous day.
3. The router times should have 8 h subtracted, giving 2026-11-22 15:31, which is even earlier.
4. In UTC+08:00 the router event was at 07:31:15 on Nov 23, before both the alarm and complaints.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4

**詳解**　23:31:15 UTC + 8 h = 07:31:15 on the following day, 2026-11-23 (+08:00). The flap therefore sits just before the alarm at 07:33:40 and the tickets from 07:40. Option 1 compares raw dates without converting. Option 2 adds 8 h but forgets the date rollover. Option 3 applies the offset in the wrong direction.

**出處**　IETF RFC 5424 clause 6.2.3 (TIMESTAMP derived from RFC 3339; "Z" = UTC) (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5424); Curriculum 6.2.1, "Easy to confuse" (forgetting to change the date when converting UTC to +08:00).

</details>

**007.** When merging RFC 5424 syslog in UTC with OSS records in +08:00, which procedure is correct?

1. Convert every timestamp, date included, to one reference offset (or to UTC), then sort by the resulting instant; keep the original text alongside for traceability.
2. Sort the rows by the raw timestamp text, because all sources use year-month-day order.
3. Strip the "Z" and "+08:00" suffixes so all times look alike, then sort.
4. Convert only the time of day of the UTC rows and keep their original date, so that the date field still matches the source file.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　An RFC 3339-style timestamp identifies an instant only together with its offset, so every row must be converted to one common reference before ordering, and the date changes when the conversion crosses midnight. **014.** What does the "-" in the TIMESTAMP position of the second AMF syslog line mean under RFC 5424?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It is the NILVALUE: RFC 5424 requires a syslog application to send "-" as TIMESTAMP when it is incapable of obtaining system time, so the message carries no time of its own.

**詳解**　The TIMESTAMP ABNF is NILVALUE / FULL-DATE "T" FULL-TIME, and NILVALUE = "-". Clause 6.2.3 states the application MUST use NILVALUE if it cannot obtain system time. The line is valid syslog, but it cannot be placed on a timeline from its own content.

**出處**　IETF RFC 5424 clause 6 (ABNF) and 6.2.3 (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5424)

</details>

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　The two hypotheses make different predictions about the source counters, so this check discriminates. Option 1 would show the pattern again, which both hypotheses predict. Option 3 summarises the same data without separating causes. Option 4 is not evidence.

**104.** Why is a co-occurrence at the same time every day especially weak evidence of causation?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Because many things recur at the same time of day (traffic peaks, scheduled jobs, routines), two daily events can coincide every day without either causing the other; a test that breaks the coincidence, such as moving the job to another time, is needed.

**詳解**　Daily periodic patterns make repeated coincidence cheap. Shifting the backup to, say, 03:00 is a clean test: if the drop moves with it, the link is strong; if the drop stays at 20:00, the backup is not the explanation. Such a test would follow change management, which is outside this step.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.2.1, "Easy to confuse" (timing agreement is not proof); Google SRE Book, Chapter 12 "Effective Troubleshooting" (correlation is not causation) (https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)

</details>

**105.** On 11-24 the 20:00 period has only 1,830 RRC.ConnEstabAtt against about 3,600 usually. Does this mean fewer UEs tried to connect?
