# 6.1.4 容量與排程資源不足

所屬：[6.1 根因的類型](../6-1.md)

## 正文

In a 5G cell the radio interface is divided into physical resource blocks (PRBs).  The proportion of PRBs that are occupied determines how much traffic the cell can carry.  When the percentage of used PRBs approaches the limits of the available spectrum, the cell experiences **capacity insufficiency**: users may see higher latency, reduced throughput, or even radio link failures.  Operators therefore monitor PRB usage continuously and trigger corrective actions such as load‑balancing or scheduling parameter adjustments before service quality degrades.

### Key definitions
- **DL PRB usage ($U_{DL}$)** – percentage of downlink PRBs that are used for any traffic during a measurement interval.  
- **UL PRB usage ($U_{UL}$)** – percentage of uplink PRBs that are used for any traffic during a measurement interval.  
- **Total PRBs ($N_{PRB}$)** – the number of PRBs that the cell can allocate in a given subframe.  
- **Used PRBs ($N_{used}$)** – the count of PRBs actually occupied by scheduled transmissions.  

The 3GPP specification defines the usage as  

$$
U_{DL}= \frac{N_{used}^{DL}}{N_{PRB}^{DL}}\times 100\; \%
\qquad
U_{UL}= \frac{N_{used}^{UL}}{N_{PRB}^{UL}}\times 100\; \%
$$

where the result is an integer from 0 to 100 % [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1d].  
Both measurements are obtained by averaging the ratio over a configurable time period (the “SI”) and may be reported per PLMN, per S‑NSSAI, and per QoS resource type [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1a‑c].

### Histogram of PRB usage
To detect transient overloads, the specification also provides a **distribution of DL total PRB usage**.  Each sample $M[n]$ is computed as  

$$
M[n]=\Big\lfloor\frac{M1[n]}{P[n]}\times100\Big\rfloor
$$

where $M1[n]$ is the count of used PRBs in the $n$‑th short measurement (e.g., a millisecond) and $P[n]$ is the total PRBs available in that interval [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.3c].  The histogram bins count how many samples fall into predefined percentage ranges, revealing whether a cell experiences brief peaks of high load even when the long‑term average looks acceptable [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.3a].

### Load‑balancing as a remedy
When $U_{DL}$ or $U_{UL}$ consistently exceeds a safe threshold (commonly 80–85 %), the network should invoke **load‑balancing optimisation (LBO)**.  The 3GPP work item mandates that LBO be performed with minimal human intervention [TS 28.627 REQ‑SO\_LB‑CON‑1] and must consider intra‑frequency, inter‑frequency, and inter‑RAT scenarios [TS 28.627 REQ‑SO\_LB‑CON‑2].  

The standard LBO workflow (illustrated in TS 28.313) is:

1. **Enable D‑LBO** – the D‑SON management function requests the provisioning function to set handover and reselection parameter ranges and to activate the D‑LBO function [TS 28.313 Step 1].  
2. **Execute load‑balancing** – the D‑LBO function redistributes traffic according to the algorithms defined in TS 38.300 clause 15.5 [TS 28.313 Step 2].  
3. **Collect measurements** – the D‑SON function gathers PRB usage and distribution counters [TS 28.313 Step 3].  
4. **Analyse and adapt** – based on the collected data, the D‑SON may request updated handover/reselection ranges to fine‑tune the balance [TS 28.313 Step 4].

The goal is to keep PRB usage on each cell within a comfortable margin while preserving user‑perceived quality [TS 28.313 LBO Goal].

### Worked example
Assume a NR cell with 100 downlink PRBs per subframe. During a 1‑second measurement interval the scheduler reports that 85 PRBs were occupied on average.

$$
U_{DL}= \frac{85}{100}\times100 = 85\%
$$

Because the value is above the typical 80 % safety margin, the D‑SON function decides to trigger LBO.  It collects the DL PRB usage distribution and finds that 30 % of the 1‑ms samples fall into the 90‑100 % bin, indicating frequent short spikes.  The D‑SON therefore narrows the handover offset thresholds, encouraging users at the cell edge to attach to a neighboring cell with lower $U_{DL}$.

### Common pitfalls
| Pitfall | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Relying only on the average $U_{DL}$** | Short spikes can still cause packet loss; the histogram reveals these bursts [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.3a]. |
| **Ignoring UL usage** | Uplink congestion can also limit overall capacity, yet the same monitoring principles apply [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.2a]. |
| **Treating the PRB usage as a continuous value** | The specification defines the result as an integer 0‑100 % [TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1d]; rounding errors can mislead threshold checks. |
| **Manually adjusting handover parameters without LBO feedback** | The standard requires minimal human intervention; automated D‑LBO loops ensure consistent optimisation [TS 28.627 REQ‑SO\_LB‑CON‑1]. |

By continuously tracking DL/UL PRB usage and its distribution, and by coupling the data with the automated LBO workflow, engineers can prevent capacity‑related outages and maintain a balanced radio load across the network.

## 練習題

**1.** 哪一項測量提供了下行（DL）物理資源區塊 (PRB) 使用率的百分比？

- A. RRU.PrbUl
- B. RRU.PrbDl
- C. RRU.PrbTotDlDist
- D. RRU.PrbTotUlDist

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。下載測量的名稱為 RRU.PrbDl，代表全部流量的 DL PRB 使用率。

</details>

**2.** 下行總 PRB 使用率的數值範圍是什麼？

- A. 0‑255%
- B. 0‑100%
- C. 0‑1 000%
- D. 0‑10 000%

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。測量結果以百分比表示，範圍限定在 0 到 100% 之間。

</details>

**3.** 哪一條規範要求「負載平衡的最佳化應以最小的人為介入」？

- A. REQ‑SO_LB‑CON‑1
- B. REQ‑SO_LB‑CON‑2
- C. REQ‑DLBO‑FUN‑1
- D. REQ‑DLBO‑FUN‑2

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。該條款明確指出負載平衡的最佳化需盡量減少人工操作。

</details>

**4.** 「下行總 PRB 使用率分布」測量的結果以何種資料型別呈現？

- A. 浮點數列表
- B. 時間序列圖
- C. 一組整數
- D. 位元組陣列

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。此測量以每個區間樣本數的整數計數方式回傳。

</details>

**5.** 下列哪一項測量被標示為「Valid for packet switched traffic」？

- A. RRU.PrbTotUlDist
- B. RRU.PrbDl
- C. RRU.PrbTotDlDist
- D. RRU.PrbUl

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。DL PRB 使用率測量適用於分組交換流量，符合規格說明。

</details>
