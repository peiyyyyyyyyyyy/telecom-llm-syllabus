# 6.1.5 終端移動速度過快

所屬：[6.1 根因的類型](../6-1.md)

## 正文

In LTE‑Advanced and 5G NR the Radio Access Network (RAN) adapts cell reselection timers and hysteresis values based on how fast a User Equipment (UE) is moving. When the UE travels faster than the thresholds configured by the network, it is classified as being in a **Medium‑mobility** or **High‑mobility** state. This classification directly influences the speed‑dependent scaling factors that enlarge the reselection timers, thereby preventing premature handovers that would otherwise cause radio link failures or excessive signaling load. Consequently, diagnosing “terminal moving too fast” is essential for maintaining service continuity, especially on high‑speed trains or vehicular scenarios.  

### Mobility‑State Detection
The UE determines its mobility state from the broadcast system‑information parameters $T_{\text{CRmax}}$, $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$, $N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$, $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$ and, optionally, *cellEquivalentSize* [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3]. During each observation window of length $T_{\text{CRmax}}$, the UE counts the number of distinct cell reselections $N_{\text{reselect}}$ (consecutive reselections of the same cell are ignored). The criteria are:

* **Normal‑mobility**: $N_{\text{reselect}} < N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$  
* **Medium‑mobility**: $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}} \le N_{\text{reselect}} \le N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$  
* **High‑mobility**: $N_{\text{reselect}} > N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$  

These thresholds are applied after the UE has ignored any back‑to‑back reselection of the same cell, as stipulated in the standard [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3].  

### State Transitions
When the UE evaluates the above criteria it follows a deterministic transition rule [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3]:

1. If the **High‑mobility** condition is satisfied, the UE **enters High‑mobility**.  
2. Else, if the **Medium‑mobility** condition is satisfied, the UE **enters Medium‑mobility**.  
3. If neither Medium nor High conditions have been met for the hysteresis interval $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$, the UE **reverts to Normal‑mobility**.  

Thus, the UE can only be in one of the three states at any time, and the hysteresis interval prevents rapid oscillation between states.  

### Speed‑Dependent Scaling Rules
Once the UE is in a Medium‑ or High‑mobility state, it must apply the scaling factors advertised by the serving cell [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3.1]. The scaling affects two families of parameters:

* **Hysteresis scaling** – an additive offset *sf‑Medium* or *sf‑High* is added to $Q_{\text{hyst}}$ (if broadcast).  
* **Reselection‑timer scaling** – the timers $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}}$ and $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{EUTRA}}$ are multiplied by the corresponding factor *sf‑Medium* or *sf‑High*.  

If no scaling factor is broadcast, the UE leaves the parameter unchanged. After multiplication, the UE **rounds up** the result to the nearest whole second [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3.1].  

### Example – Determining Mobility State and Applying Scaling

Assume the following broadcast values for a serving LTE‑NR cell:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| $T_{\text{CRmax}}$ | 120 s |
| $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$ | 2 |
| $N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$ | 5 |
| $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$ | 300 s |
| *sf‑Medium* (for $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}}$) | 1.5 |
| *sf‑High* (for $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}}$) | 2.0 |

During the 120‑second observation window the UE records 4 distinct reselections (no consecutive repeats).  

1. **State detection**  
   * $N_{\text{reselect}} = 4$ satisfies $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}} \le N_{\text{reselect}} \le N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$ → **Medium‑mobility** [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3].  

2. **Scaling**  
   * Because the UE is in Medium‑mobility, it multiplies $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}}$ by *sf‑Medium* = 1.5.  
   * Suppose the original $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}} = 8$ s. After scaling: $8 \times 1.5 = 12$ s. The UE rounds up to the nearest second (already an integer).  

Result: the UE will wait **12 s** before considering a reselection to another NR cell, a longer interval that mitigates ping‑pong effects at moderate speeds.  

### Practical Notes & Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why it Happens | Correct Practice |
|---------|----------------|------------------|
| **Ignoring *cellEquivalentSize*** | Engineers assume the UE always counts each reselection equally. | Verify that the cell broadcasts *cellEquivalentSize*; if present, the UE may count a reselection as the configured size, effectively increasing $N_{\text{reselect}}$ [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3]. |
| **Applying scaling when in Normal‑mobility** | Misreading the scaling rule as always active. | Scaling is applied **only** if the UE is in Medium‑ or High‑mobility; otherwise no scaling is performed [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3.1]. |
| **Using the wrong hysteresis interval** | Mixing $T_{\text{CRmax}}$ (observation window) with $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$ (state‑change hysteresis). | $T_{\text{CRmax}}$ is used for counting reselections; $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$ governs when the UE may drop back to Normal‑mobility [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3]. |
| **Counting consecutive reselections of the same cell** | Over‑counting when the UE briefly returns to a previously visited cell. | The UE **shall not consider consecutive reselections** of the same cell for mobility‑state detection [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3]. |
| **Rounding down after scaling** | Assuming standard mathematical rounding. | The UE **rounds up** the scaled timer to the nearest second [TS 38.304 §5.2.4.3.1]. |

By systematically verifying the broadcast parameters, correctly counting distinct reselections, and applying the appropriate scaling only when the UE is in a non‑Normal state, network operators can diagnose and remedy “terminal moving too fast” scenarios without unnecessary parameter tweaks.

## 練習題

**1.** Which condition classifies a UE into High‑mobility state?

- A. $N_{\text{reselect}} < N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$
- B. $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}} \le N_{\text{reselect}} \le N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$
- C. $N_{\text{reselect}} > N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$
- D. $N_{\text{reselect}} = N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。High‑mobility is defined when the number of reselections exceeds $N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$.

</details>

**2.** When a UE is in Medium‑mobility state, how is the NR reselection timer modified?

- A. It is divided by *sf‑Medium*.
- B. It is multiplied by *sf‑Medium*.
- C. It is added to *sf‑Medium*.
- D. No change is applied.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The scaling rule for Medium‑mobility multiplies $T_{\text{reselection}}^{\text{NR}}$ by *sf‑Medium*.

</details>

**3.** Under which circumstance does the UE revert to Normal‑mobility state after being in Medium‑ or High‑mobility?

- A. As soon as $N_{\text{reselect}}$ falls below $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$.
- B. When the criteria for Medium‑ or High‑mobility are not detected during $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$.
- C. Immediately after a successful handover.
- D. Only after a network‑initiated reset.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The UE returns to Normal‑mobility if neither Medium nor High criteria are met for the hysteresis interval.

</details>

**4.** Which broadcast parameters are required for the UE to determine its mobility state?

- A. Only $T_{\text{CRmax}}$ and $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$.
- B. $T_{\text{CRmax}}$, $N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$, $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$, $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$, and *cellEquivalentSize*.
- C. Only $N_{\text{CR}_\text{H}}$ and $N_{\text{CR}_\text{M}}$.
- D. $T_{\text{CRmax}}$ and $T_{\text{CRmaxHyst}}$.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification lists all five parameters as necessary for mobility‑state determination.

</details>

**5.** How does the UE treat consecutive reselections of the same cell when counting for mobility‑state detection?

- A. Each reselection is counted separately.
- B. Only the first reselection is counted; subsequent ones are ignored.
- C. Consecutive reselections of the same cell are not considered.
- D. The UE doubles the count for consecutive reselections.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The standard explicitly excludes back‑to‑back reselections of the same cell from the count.

</details>
