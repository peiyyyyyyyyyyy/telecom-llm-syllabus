# 6.1.2 干擾：含 PCI mod 30 衝突

所屬：[6.1 根因的類型](../6-1.md)

## 正文

In a live 5G NR network, radio signals from neighboring cells inevitably overlap. When the overlap is strong enough to deteriorate the quality of the received control or data channels, the condition is called **inter‑cell interference**. Interference can cause missed control messages, reduced throughput, or even hand‑over failures. Therefore, detecting, diagnosing, and mitigating interference is a core task of network operations and of any fault‑diagnosis engine that the textbook aims to train.

### PCI and the “mod 30” conflict
Each NR cell is identified by a **Physical Cell Identifier (PCI)**, a 9‑bit value ranging from 0 to 503. The PCI is used by the UE for cell search, measurement reporting, and hand‑over decisions. A special property of the PCI is that many radio procedures (e.g., PUCCH group hopping) treat the PCI **mod 30** as a grouping index. Consequently, two cells that are geographically adjacent and share the same PCI mod 30 value can be mistakenly regarded as the same logical cell by the UE, leading to measurement ambiguity and hand‑over errors. This situation is commonly referred to as a **PCI mod 30 conflict**.

### Group hopping formula
Group hopping is defined in TS 38.211 §6.3.2.2.1. The **sequence group** $u$ is calculated as  

$$
u = \bigl(f_{\text{gh}} + f_{\text{ss}}\bigr)\ \bmod\ 30
$$

where  

* $f_{\text{gh}}$ – group hopping parameter configured by higher‑layer signalling,  
* $f_{\text{ss}}$ – slot‑specific offset, and  
* “mod 30” denotes the remainder after division by 30.  

The result $u$ is directly related to the PCI mod 30 value used in UE measurements. If two neighbouring cells produce the same $u$, the UE may not be able to distinguish them during the measurement phase, which is the root cause of a PCI mod 30 conflict. The reference text explicitly provides this expression and its context [TS 38.211 §6.3.2.2.1].

### PCI assignment mechanisms
The network can avoid PCI mod 30 conflicts by careful **PCI optimisation** (TS 38.300 §15.5.6).

* **Centralized PCI assignment** – The OAM (Operations, Administration and Maintenance) entity assigns a single PCI for each NR cell in the gNB, and the gNB uses exactly that value [TS 38.300 §15.5.6.1].
* **Distributed PCI assignment** – The OAM provides a **list** of candidate PCIs for each NR cell. The gNB selects one PCI from that list, possibly discarding values that are reported as conflicting by UEs or detected on the air [TS 38.300 §15.5.6.2].

Both mechanisms aim to guarantee that neighbouring cells do not share the same PCI mod 30, thereby preventing ambiguous measurements.

### Inter‑cell Interference Coordination (ICIC)
Beyond PCI optimisation, the 3GPP specifications define **ICIC** as the generic function that keeps inter‑cell interference under control [TS 36.300 §16.1.5]. ICIC comprises two complementary components:

* **Frequency‑domain ICIC** – coordinates the allocation of **radio resource blocks (RBs)** among cells [TS 36.300 §16.1.5.0].
* **Time‑domain ICIC** – uses **Almost Blank Subframes (ABS)** to mute or reduce transmission power in aggressor cells during selected subframes, protecting victim cells from strong interference [TS 36.300 §16.1.5.2].

Both components are configured by the OAM and exchanged over back‑haul links (e.g., X2/Xn) to achieve a network‑wide interference‑free schedule.

### Worked example: detecting a PCI mod 30 conflict
Assume two neighbouring cells, Cell A and Cell B, have the following parameters for PUCCH group hopping:

| Parameter | Cell A | Cell B |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| $f_{\text{gh}}$ | 12 | 42 |
| $f_{\text{ss}}$ | 7 | 7 |
| PCI | 125 | 185 |

1. Compute the sequence group $u$ for each cell:  

   *Cell A*: $u_A = (12 + 7) \bmod 30 = 19$  

   *Cell B*: $u_B = (42 + 7) \bmod 30 = 19$

2. Compute PCI mod 30:  

   *Cell A*: $125 \bmod 30 = 5$  

   *Cell B*: $185 \bmod 30 = 5$

Both cells produce the same $u$ (19) and the same PCI mod 30 (5). A UE measuring in the overlap region will see identical group‑hopping indices and PCI residues, making it impossible to differentiate the two cells for hand‑over. The network should resolve the conflict by either re‑assigning the PCI of one cell (centralized assignment) or removing the conflicting PCI from the gNB’s candidate list (distributed assignment).

### Common pitfalls and practical notes
* **Ignoring the mod 30 rule** – Engineers sometimes focus only on the full PCI value and overlook that many procedures only use the PCI mod 30. This leads to hidden conflicts that are only visible in measurement logs.  
* **Static PCI plans** – In dense deployments, a static PCI map can become obsolete after a network upgrade; periodic re‑optimization is required.  
* **Mis‑configuring group hopping** – Setting *pucch‑GroupHopping* to “enable” without proper *hoppingId* can change the pseudo‑random sequence and inadvertently align two cells’ $u$ values.  
* **Incomplete ICIC configuration** – Deploying only frequency‑domain ICIC while neglecting time‑domain ABS patterns may leave residual interference, especially for CRE (Cell‑Range‑Extension) users.  
* **Assuming OAM always enforces uniqueness** – In split‑gNB architectures, the distributed PCI list may contain duplicates if the OAM does not filter out UE‑reported conflicts; the gNB must still perform local validation.

By systematically checking the PCI mod 30 values, verifying group‑hopping parameters, and applying the appropriate PCI assignment strategy together with ICIC, engineers can efficiently diagnose and remediate interference‑related root causes.

## 練習題

**1.** Which formula defines the sequence group $u$ used in PUCCH group hopping?

- A. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} \times f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- B. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} + f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- C. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} - f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- D. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} + f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 31$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states that the sequence group is the sum of $f_{\text{gh}}$ and $f_{\text{ss}}$ modulo 30.

</details>

**2.** In centralized PCI assignment, which network entity determines the PCI for each NR cell?

- A. The gNB itself
- B. The UE during initial access
- C. The OAM
- D. The Xn interface peer

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Centralized assignment is performed by the Operations, Administration and Maintenance function, which provides a single PCI per cell.

</details>

**3.** Which component of ICIC explicitly manages the allocation of radio resource blocks?

- A. Time‑domain ICIC
- B. Frequency‑domain ICIC
- C. Beamforming coordination
- D. Handover control

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The frequency‑domain part of ICIC is responsible for coordinating RB usage across cells.

</details>

**4.** In distributed PCI assignment, what does the gNB do with the list of PCIs it receives from the OAM?

- A. It broadcasts the entire list to UEs.
- B. It selects a PCI value from the list.
- C. It ignores the list and generates a random PCI.
- D. It assigns the same PCI to all its cells.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The gNB picks one PCI from the OAM‑provided list, possibly after removing conflicting entries.

</details>

**5.** What is the primary purpose of Almost Blank Subframes (ABS) in time‑domain ICIC?

- A. To increase uplink power for all UEs.
- B. To protect resources in victim cells from strong interference.
- C. To synchronize the clocks of neighboring gNBs.
- D. To carry additional control information.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。ABS are subframes with reduced or no transmission in aggressor cells, shielding victim cells from interference.

</details>
