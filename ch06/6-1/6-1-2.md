# 6.1.2 干擾：含 PCI mod 30 衝突

所屬：[6.1 根因的類型](../6-1.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which formula defines the sequence group $u$ used in PUCCH group hopping?

- A. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} \times f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- B. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} + f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- C. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} - f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 30$
- D. $u = (f_{\text{gh}} + f_{\text{ss}}) \bmod 31$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states that the sequence group is the sum of $f_{\text{gh}}$ and $f_{\text{ss}}$ modulo 30.

</details>

**2.** In centralized PCI assignment, which network entity determines the PCI for each NR cell?

- A. The gNB itself
- B. The UE during initial access
- C. The OAM
- D. The Xn interface peer

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。Centralized assignment is performed by the Operations, Administration and Maintenance function, which provides a single PCI per cell.

</details>

**3.** Which component of ICIC explicitly manages the allocation of radio resource blocks?

- A. Time‑domain ICIC
- B. Frequency‑domain ICIC
- C. Beamforming coordination
- D. Handover control

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The frequency‑domain part of ICIC is responsible for coordinating RB usage across cells.

</details>

**4.** In distributed PCI assignment, what does the gNB do with the list of PCIs it receives from the OAM?

- A. It broadcasts the entire list to UEs.
- B. It selects a PCI value from the list.
- C. It ignores the list and generates a random PCI.
- D. It assigns the same PCI to all its cells.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The gNB picks one PCI from the OAM‑provided list, possibly after removing conflicting entries.

</details>

**5.** What is the primary purpose of Almost Blank Subframes (ABS) in time‑domain ICIC?

- A. To increase uplink power for all UEs.
- B. To protect resources in victim cells from strong interference.
- C. To synchronize the clocks of neighboring gNBs.
- D. To carry additional control information.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。ABS are subframes with reduced or no transmission in aggressor cells, shielding victim cells from interference.

</details>
