# 6.4.1 障礙等級

所屬：[6.4 流程](6-4.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

障礙等級（incident priority／severity）是把一個障礙依「影響多大、多急、持續多久」排出處理順序，並決定要動員多少人、要不要對外通報。值班人員在開立工單時就要先給等級，之後隨影響範圍變化再調整；客服人員依等級決定是否啟動對用戶的統一說法。

判斷等級時有兩把尺，不能混在一起：一把是業者自訂的內部分級（例如 P1～P4），用來排人力與升級；另一把是主管機關的法定通報門檻，依《電信管理法》第 11 條第 2 項授權訂定的《電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法》第 3 條（[全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=K0060127)）。前者各家不同，後者對所有電信事業一體適用。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

**1. 先看三個維度**

- 範圍：受影響的用戶數、基地臺數、行政區、電路數；是單點還是多點。
- 服務：語音、數據、簡訊、專線，是否波及緊急通信；是「完全中斷」還是「品質劣化」。
- 時間：從障礙**發生**（不是發現或開單）起已持續多久、預估多久恢復、備援是否生效。發生時間要從時間軸找（見 5.5.1）。

**2. 對照法定通報門檻**

通報辦法第 3 條（[原文](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)）的前提是：電信事業「因災害或其他重大事故導致電信機線障礙或發生資通安全事件，致無法提供電信服務」，且符合下列任一款：

| 款 | 網路 | 門檻（摘自原文） |
|---|---|---|
| 一 | 固定通信網路 | 市內用戶一萬戶以上中斷服務達三十分鐘以上 |
| 二 | 固定通信網路 | 長途電路一千路以上中斷服務達三十分鐘以上 |
| 三 | 固定通信網路 | 本島與離島間、國際間之海纜系統或其內陸介接站電路中斷服務達三十分鐘以上 |
| 四 | 行動通信網路 | 同一鄉（鎮、市、區）之基地臺同時三十臺或百分之五十以上之臺數，中斷服務達三十分鐘以上 |
| 五 | — | 其他中斷服務情事，經主管機關命限期辦理通報 |

另外，因受其他行政機關處分而無法提供服務也要通報（通報辦法第 4 條）；前兩條以外的重大事故，主管機關認有必要時得命限期通報（通報辦法第 5 條）。判斷第 3 條第 4 款時要注意三件事：以「同一鄉（鎮、市、區）」為單位逐區計算；30 臺與 50% 兩個條件符合其一即可；30 分鐘是中斷持續的時間。50% 的分母原文未寫明，以主管機關解釋為準。

**3. 定內部等級**

內部等級可以借用 ITIL 事件管理的概念：優先等級（priority）由影響度（impact）和緊急度（urgency）兩個維度組合而成，通常畫成矩陣查表。影響度看範圍與服務，緊急度看是否還在擴大、有沒有備援、恢復要多久。矩陣、級數與名稱由各業者自訂。

**4. 持續重新評估**

受影響站數改變、備援失效、預估恢復時間延長或趨近法定門檻時，都要重新評估等級；可能達到門檻時就先準備通報資料（見 6.4.2）。

#### 實際例子

虛構業者「乙電信」的內部分級規則（虛構）：

| 影響度＼緊急度 | 高 | 中 | 低 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 高 | P1 | P2 | P3 |
| 中 | P2 | P3 | P4 |
| 低 | P3 | P4 | P4 |

補充規則（虛構）：已達法定通報門檻，或依現有資料推估在修復前就會達到者，影響度一律列「高」；備援失效且預估修復超過 2 小時者，緊急度列「高」。

```
事件快照（虛構）　乙電信 NOC　2026-10-14 02:31
02:10:12  傳輸環 TR-K07 出現 LOS 告警，下游基地臺陸續斷站
行政區（虛構）   該區乙電信基地臺總數   中斷臺數   中斷比例
K市 東湖區              80             25       31.3%
K市 北嶺區              45             10       22.2%
東湖區另有 8 臺改由電池供電，依電池容量推估約 40 分鐘後耗盡（推估）
保護路由：與主路由同管道，未切換成功
現場初報：管道受損，修復需 3 小時以上；受損原因待現場確認
```

逐步判斷：

1. 法定門檻要逐區看。東湖區 25 臺（25÷80＝31.25%），北嶺區 10 臺（10÷45≈22.22%），兩區都未達「30 臺或 50%」；兩區合計 35 臺不能套第 4 款，因為原文以同一鄉（鎮、市、區）為單位（[通報辦法第 3 條](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)）。到 02:31 只中斷 21 分鐘，時間條件也未滿足。
2. 但若東湖區那 8 臺在電池耗盡後（推估約 03:11）斷站，東湖區會達 33 臺；再持續 30 分鐘（約 03:41）就符合第 3 條第 4 款。修復要 3 小時以上，推估會在修復前達到門檻，依補充規則影響度列「高」。
3. 保護路由失效、修復超過 3 小時，緊急度列「高」。查表得 **P1**。

不能下結論的地方：受損原因只是現場初報，工單上要寫「待確認」；電池剩餘時間是推估，不是確定的斷站時刻；鄰近站有沒有涵蓋補償目前沒有資料，不能說「用戶不受影響」。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **內部等級 ≠ 法定通報門檻**：未達法定門檻不代表等級低，例如影響緊急通信，業者可自訂為最高級；內部分級也不能取代法定通報義務。
- **行動網路的門檻是同一行政區的基地臺數**，不是全網總數，也不是用戶數；「一萬戶」是固定通信網路市內用戶的門檻（[通報辦法第 3 條](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)第 1 款），不能套到行動網路。
- **中斷與劣化不同**：第 3 條各款字面上講的是「中斷服務」；速率變慢、接通率下降可以列高的內部等級，但不在這幾款字面範圍內。
- **30 分鐘從中斷發生起算**，不是從 NOC 發現或開單起算，所以要先確定「發生時間」。

#### 和其他小節的關係

障礙發生時間怎麼從告警與日誌排出來見 5.5.1；本例的傳輸中斷屬於 6.1.8 的根因類型。等級定好後，誰要被通知、對外通報時限見 6.4.2；等級要記在工單上並隨狀態更新（6.4.3）；事後的障礙報告要說明影響範圍與是否達法定門檻（6.5.2）。法規原文見[通報辦法第 3 條](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)。

**出處**　《電信管理法》第 11 條（[全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060111&flno=11)）；《電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法》（民國 109 年 7 月 3 日發布，第 9 條定自 109 年 7 月 1 日施行）第 3、4、5 條（[全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=K0060127)）；ITIL 事件管理的優先等級（影響度×緊急度）僅作概念引用。

#### Grading by scope, service and duration

**情境 001**　User-plane fault: data degraded, all sites on air

Telco-X (fictional) is assessing a fault on a user-plane node that serves three districts of K City (fictional). The NOC snapshot below was taken at 14:52:00.

```
Ticket INC-26-11-0412 (fictional) - Telco-X NOC - snapshot 14:52:00
First alarm (from NE alarm log):   14:21:40  user-plane node UPF-03 process restart loop
Ticket opened:                     14:33:05
Area served by UPF-03:             K City - Riverside, Hillcrest, Old Port districts
Base stations:                     all in service (no site-down alarms in the three districts)
Service probes 14:45-14:50 (current / normal baseline):
  Mobile data session setup success     61.4%  / 99.2%
  Voice call setup success              98.9%  / 99.1%
  SMS delivery success                  99.5%  / 99.6%
  Emergency call test calls             3 of 3 connected
Failed data attempts:                   about 38% of attempts (probe sample)
Standby node UPF-04:                    switchover not triggered
Vendor estimate of restoration:         not yet given
Customer complaints since 14:30:        212
```

**002.** For the time dimension, how long had the fault lasted at the snapshot time?

1. 18 min 55 s
2. 22 min 00 s
3. 30 min 20 s
4. 31 min 00 s

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3

**詳解**　Duration is counted from the occurrence of the fault, which the timeline gives as the first alarm at 14:21:40, not from ticket opening. 14:52:00 − 14:21:40 = 30 min 20 s. Option 1 (18 min 55 s = 14:52:00 − 14:33:05) counts from the ticket. Option 2 (22 min) counts from 14:30, the start of the complaint count. Option 4 rounds the start to 14:21 instead of using the logged 14:21:40.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 "time: counted from occurrence, not from detection or ticket opening" (occurrence time from the timeline, Curriculum 5.5.1); 電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

**004.** How many subscribers are affected by the data degradation?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the snapshot gives only the share of failed probe attempts (about 38%) and a complaint count; the number of subscribers attempting data sessions on UPF-03 (or per-district subscriber/session counts) is needed.

**詳解**　A failure ratio from a probe sample is not a subscriber count, and 212 complaints only show how many people called, not how many were affected. To fill the scope dimension, the NOC needs the number of subscribers (or active sessions) served by UPF-03 and how many of them actually failed.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (scope = number of affected users) and "points that cannot be concluded"

</details>

**005.** Besides naming which services (voice, data, SMS, leased lines) are affected, what two aspects does the textbook require the service dimension to record?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Whether emergency communications are affected, and whether the impact is a complete interruption or a quality degradation.

**詳解**　The service dimension in the textbook lists the service types, then asks whether emergency communications are touched and whether the effect is "complete interruption" or "quality degradation". Both change the grade: emergency impact can justify the top internal level, and only interruptions fall within the literal wording of the statutory items.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 and "easily confused points" (interruption vs degradation); 電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

**情境 002**　Central office power loss on the fixed network

Harbor Fixed Net (fictional) lost power to the switching shelves of one central office. The outage record below was written after restoration.

```
Harbor Fixed Net (fictional) - outage record OB-0917 - 2026-09-17
Event:            rectifier failure at central office CO-Lakeview; battery string exhausted
Interruption start (power loss to switching shelves, CO alarm log):   09:04
Service restored (all shelves back, CO alarm log):                    09:41
Affected services homed on CO-Lakeview (line inventory):
  Local telephone subscribers interrupted                  7,420
  Long-distance circuits terminating at CO-Lakeview          240
  Enterprise leased lines interrupted                         86
Other central offices affected:                             none
Mobile base stations affected:                              none
Customer calls to the service centre 09:10-09:45:          1,126
Field note: generator failed to start (cause under investigation)
```

**006.** How long did the interruption last?

1. 35 min
2. 37 min
3. 41 min
4. 31 min

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　From interruption start to restoration in the CO alarm log: 09:41 − 09:04 = 37 min. Option 1 (35 min) is the span of the customer-call window 09:10–09:45. Option 3 (41 min) wrongly takes the restoration clock minute as the duration. Option 4 (31 min) counts from 09:10, when customer calls began, instead of from the interruption start.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (time counted from occurrence); 電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

**008.** Did any of the 86 interrupted enterprise leased lines serve an emergency-service organisation, such as a fire or police dispatch centre?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined from the record: it gives only the number of interrupted leased lines (86), not their customers or circuit roles; the leased-line customer list for CO-Lakeview is needed.

**詳解**　The service dimension asks specifically whether emergency communications are affected. Emergency organisations often use leased lines, but the outage record OB-0917 lists only a count, so neither "emergency services affected" nor "not affected" can be written until the circuits are matched to their customers.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (service dimension: whether emergency communications are affected)

</details>

**009.** Classify the scope of this outage as single-point or multi-point and give the reason from the record.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Single-point: only one central office (CO-Lakeview) was affected, with "other central offices affected: none" and no mobile base stations.

**詳解**　The scope dimension asks whether the fault is at one point or many. All affected services were homed on CO-Lakeview, and the record states no other office and no base station was affected, so it is a single-point outage, even though it carried several service types.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (scope: single point or multiple points)

</details>

**010.** For an interruption of fixed local telephone service, which quantity does Article 3 use as the scope measure?

1. The number of local subscribers whose service is interrupted (10,000 or more).
2. The number of base stations interrupted in the same township, town, city or district.
3. The number of customer calls received by the service centre.
4. The number of central offices affected.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　Article 3 item 1 is written for the fixed communication network: 10,000 or more local subscribers interrupted for 30 minutes or more. Option 2 is the measure of item 4 for the mobile network. Option 3 is a proxy for public reaction, not a statutory measure. Option 4 is not a measure in any item.

**出處**　電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 item 1 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

       "Both of our links to the dispatch centre have been down since about 10:00."

**012.** Which time should be recorded as the occurrence of the interruption?

1. 10:12, when the dispatch centre's message arrived
2. 09:58:17, when the first affected circuit (EL-2201) was last up
3. 10:05, when the civil-works crew reported the cut
4. 10:00, the time the customer gave

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　Occurrence is taken from the timeline: the earliest moment a circuit went down, which the inventory gives as EL-2201 last up at 09:58:17 (the other two followed at 09:58:19 and 09:58:21). Option 1 is the moment of detection by the desk. Option 3 is when the cause was reported, after the interruption had already begun. Option 4 is the customer's approximate time ("about 10:00"), less precise than the circuit record.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (time counted from occurrence; occurrence from the timeline, Curriculum 5.5.1); 電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

**013.** At 11:30:00, how long had EL-2201 been down?

1. 1 h 31 min 43 s
2. 1 h 18 min 00 s
3. 1 h 25 min 00 s
4. 1 h 30 min 00 s

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　11:30:00 − 09:58:17 = 1 h 31 min 43 s. Option 2 counts from the 10:12 message (11:30 − 10:12 = 1 h 18 min). Option 3 counts from the 10:05 cut report (1 h 25 min). Option 4 counts from the customer's "about 10:00".

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (time dimension); 電信事業重大事故無法提供電信服務通報辦法 Article 3 (https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=K0060127&flno=3)

</details>

**014.** Has Lakeview Hospital lost service?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: EL-3107 is down but backup EL-3108 is UP, and there is no measurement showing whether the hospital's traffic moved onto EL-3108 and fits its capacity; traffic or customer confirmation on EL-3108 is needed.

**詳解**　A backup circuit being UP only shows the link is available, not that traffic is carried. The record explicitly says "Hospital traffic on EL-3108: no measurement attached", so neither "no impact" nor "service lost" can be written.

**015.** In the textbook's three-dimension check, under which dimension is "whether the backup has taken effect" placed?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The time dimension (together with how long the fault has lasted since occurrence and the estimated time to recovery).

**詳解**　The textbook's time dimension lists: duration since occurrence, expected time to recovery, and whether backup is effective. Backup status matters because it decides whether the outage will keep running; in the ITIL concept it also feeds urgency.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.4.1, step 1 (time dimension) and step 3 (urgency: spreading, backup, time to restore)

**073.** In the impact × urgency concept as the textbook applies it, what does impact look at and what does urgency look at?
