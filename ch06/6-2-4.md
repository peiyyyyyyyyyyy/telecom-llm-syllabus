# 6.2.4 分辨幾種可能

所屬：[6.2 推理](6-2.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

分辨幾種可能，指手上的資料同時能被兩個以上的假設解釋時，刻意找出「哪個檢查的結果會因假設不同而不同」，用它把假設一個個排除。這是推理裡最容易偷懶的一步：看到換手失敗率升高，就直接認定是參數問題；看到 SINR 變差，就直接認定是干擾。實務上，維運人員最常在三種場合需要這一步：KPI 劣化有好幾個候選原因、交接時前一班留下「疑似 A 或 B」的結論，以及準備提出會影響服務的處置之前（處置錯了代價很高）。這種「列出競爭假設、設計能分辨它們的檢查」的做法屬於方法論，不是 3GPP 規格的規定；Google 的 SRE 書在疑難排解一章也把它描述為反覆提出假設並加以檢驗的過程（[Site Reliability Engineering, Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

**1. 把每個假設寫成「如果它成立，應該看到什麼」。** 每個假設至少寫出一個可觀察的預測；預測越具體，越容易被反證（承接 6.2.2）。

**2. 找出預測不同的地方。** 把假設排成列、可做的檢查排成欄，填上每個假設對每個檢查的預期結果。所有假設預期都一樣的檢查，做了也分不出來，先不做；預期結果互相矛盾的檢查，優先做。

**3. 優先選成本低、不影響服務的檢查。** 查計數器、查組態、拉 RLF 報告、對照變更紀錄，都比改參數試試看安全（見 6.3.1）。「改了參數看 KPI 有沒有好轉」不是好的分辨方式：它影響服務，而且好轉也可能是其他因素同時變化。

**4. 換手失敗的分類，用規格的判斷規則，不要自己發明。** 要分辨「過早換手、過晚換手、換到錯的小區」，TS 38.300 V18.11.0 clause 15.5.2.2.2 給了偵測規則（[TS 38.300](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/)），關鍵是兩個問題：失敗前是否「最近剛換手」，以及 UE 第一次嘗試重建的是哪個小區。

| 類型 | 失敗前是否最近剛換手 | 第一次重建（或重新連線）的小區 |
|---|---|---|
| 過晚換手（Too Late Handover） | 否：UE 回報的計時值不存在，或大於設定門檻（例如 Tstore_UE_cntxt） | 另一個小區 |
| 過早換手（Too Early Handover） | 是：計時值小於門檻 | 上次發起換手時服務 UE 的小區（即來源小區） |
| 換到錯的小區（Handover to Wrong Cell） | 是：計時值小於門檻 | 既不是來源小區，也不是發生 RLF 的小區或換手目標小區 |

原文對過早與錯誤小區的定義都包含「換手成功後不久發生 RLF，或換手過程中失敗」兩種情況；這裡的「換手成功」指 UE 端完成隨機存取程序（同條）。門檻 Tstore_UE_cntxt 必須能由 OAM 設定（clause 15.5.2.5），所以「多久算最近」是各網路自己的設定，不是固定值。

**5. 對應到 RLF 報告的欄位。** TS 38.331 V18.11.0 clause 6.2.2 的 RLF 報告提供：`connectionFailureType`（`rlf` 或 `hof`，即無線鏈路失敗或換手失敗）、`failedPCellId`（發生 RLF 的 PCell，或失敗換手的目標 PCell）、`previousPCellId`（上次換手的來源 PCell）、`reestablishmentCellId`（失敗後嘗試重建的小區）、`timeConnFailure`（上次換手執行到連線失敗經過的時間，實際值為欄位值 × 100 ms，1023 代表 102.3 秒以上）（[TS 38.331](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.331/)）。

**6. 記得還有「都不是」。** MRO 的目的之一就是把行動性問題和涵蓋問題及其他非行動性問題區分開（TS 38.300 clause 15.5.2.1）；分不進三類的失敗，不要硬塞。

#### 實際例子

甲電信（虛構）小區 A→B 的換手失敗變多，值班人員提出三個假設：H1 對 B 觸發太晚、H2 對 B 觸發太早、H3 與換手無關（A、B 交界本來就有涵蓋缺口）。三個假設都能解釋「換手失敗率升高」，所以先拉 RLF 報告。業者設定的門檻為 Tstore_UE_cntxt = 5 秒（範例中虛構的業者設定值）。

| # | connectionFailureType | previousPCellId | failedPCellId | timeConnFailure（欄位值→秒） | reestablishmentCellId | 依 TS 38.300 判定 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | rlf | A | B | 8 → 0.8 | A | 過早換手 |
| 2 | rlf | A | B | 12 → 1.2 | C | 換到錯的小區 |
| 3 | hof | A | B | 3 → 0.3 | A | 過早換手 |
| 4 | rlf | A | B | 15 → 1.5 | A | 過早換手 |
| 5 | rlf | （未出現） | A | （未出現） | B | 過晚換手 |
| 6 | rlf | （未出現） | A | （未出現） | A | 三類都不是 |

**逐步判斷：**

1. #1、#3、#4：計時值都小於 5 秒，重建在來源小區 A，符合過早換手；#3 是換手過程中失敗（`hof`），原文同樣歸為過早。
2. #2：最近剛換手，重建在 C，既不是 A 也不是 B，屬換到錯的小區。
3. #5：沒有最近的換手，在另一個小區 B 重建，屬過晚換手。
4. #6：沒有最近的換手，又回原小區重建，三類都不符合，偏向涵蓋或干擾，不算行動性問題。
5. 統計：6 件中過早 3 件、錯誤小區 1 件、過晚 1 件、其他 1 件。H2（觸發太早）得到最多支持，H1 只有 1 件支持；H3 不能完全排除（#6）。

**不能下結論的部分：** 6 件樣本太少，下一步應擴大期間，並對照 `HO.IntraSys.TooEarly` 等計數器（TS 28.552 clause 5.1.1.25.1，[TS 28.552](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)）與變更紀錄；#2 指向 C，要另外檢查 C 的鄰區關係。即使確認是過早換手，也還不知道是哪個參數造成的（見 6.1.3、6.1.6）。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **「最近剛換手」不是固定秒數。** 規則用的是 UE 回報計時值和可設定的門檻（例如 Tstore_UE_cntxt）比較，不要把範例裡的 5 秒當成規格值（[TS 38.300](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/)）。
- **換手過程中失敗不等於過晚換手。** 依 TS 38.300 clause 15.5.2.2.2，換手過程中失敗、回來源小區重建，歸過早換手；回第三個小區，歸換到錯的小區。
- **能解釋資料不代表就是原因。** 三個假設都能解釋 KPI 下降時，KPI 本身就沒有分辨力，要找預測不同的資料。
- **分類互斥。** 同一件失敗只能歸一類；同一件事件被重複觸發評估時，原文說只應算一次（clause 15.5.2.2.2）。

#### 和其他小節的關係

本節承接 6.2.1（從時間軸提出假設）與 6.2.2（支持證據與反證）；分辨所需的資料若不夠，依 6.2.3 補收 RLF 報告、MDT 或信令追蹤。換手類假設的背景在 6.1.3，參數類在 6.1.6；分辨完成後的處置見 6.3，報告裡要把確定與推測分開，見 5.5.3、6.5.2。（方法參考 [SRE Book](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.300 V18.11.0（clause 15.5.2.1、15.5.2.2.2、15.5.2.5）<https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/>；3GPP TS 38.331 V18.11.0（clause 6.2.2 UEInformationResponse 的 RLF 報告欄位）<https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.331/>；3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0（clause 5.1.1.25.1）<https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/>；Google, *Site Reliability Engineering*, “Effective Troubleshooting” <https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/>

#### Building a check that separates competing hypotheses

**情境 001**　Prediction matrix for rising handover failures

NorthLink Mobile (fictional) sees handover failures from NR cell KH-0412-1 to KH-0415-2 rising since 3 Nov. The night-shift engineer drafted the matrix below before collecting any data.

```
Hypotheses
  H1  Handover KH-0412-1 -> KH-0415-2 is triggered too late
  H2  Handover KH-0412-1 -> KH-0415-2 is triggered too early
  H3  Coverage gap between the two cells (not mobility-related)

Check                                   | H1 predicts            | H2 predicts             | H3 predicts             | Cost / impact
C1 HO failure ratio KH-0412-1->0415-2   | rises                  | rises                   | rises                   | read-only, 5 min
C2 RLF reports: re-establishment cell   | failure in KH-0412-1,  | failure in KH-0415-2,   | re-establishment in the | read-only, 30 min
   and timeConnFailure vs threshold     | no recent HO,          | recent HO, re-est. in   | failed cell or no       |
                                        | re-est. in KH-0415-2   | KH-0412-1 (source)      | suitable cell found     |
C3 RRU.PrbDl of KH-0412-1               | unchanged              | unchanged               | unchanged               | read-only, 5 min
C4 Lower cellIndividualOffset of        | failures rise          | failures fall           | little change           | service-affecting,
   KH-0415-2 by 2 dB for 48 h, watch C1 |                        |                         |                         | needs approval
C5 Change log of both cells, 1-10 Nov   | may show a change      | may show a change       | may show a tilt or      | read-only, 10 min
                                        |                        |                         | power change            |

Operator setting: Tstore_UE_cntxt = 4 s (fictional operator setting)
Data collected so far: none
```

**001.** Which check should the engineer run first to separate H1, H2 and H3?

1. C2, because each hypothesis predicts a different RLF-report pattern and the check is read-only.
2. C1, because it is the cheapest check and directly measures the reported symptom.
3. C4, because its predicted outcomes differ between H1 and H2.
4. C3, because a load change would show which hypothesis is true.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　C2 is the only read-only check whose predicted result differs for all three hypotheses (re-establishment cell and recent/non-recent handover separate too late, too early and non-mobility cases), so it has the highest discriminating power at low cost. C1 is cheap but all three hypotheses predict "rises", so it cannot separate them. C4 does separate H1 and H2 but is service-affecting and needs approval, so it is not a first step. C3 is predicted "unchanged" under every hypothesis and therefore has no discriminating power.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.2.4 (steps 2-3: compare predicted outcomes, prefer cheap read-only checks); Google SRE Book, "Effective Troubleshooting" (https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)

</details>

**002.** Why does check C1 add nothing to the discrimination between H1, H2 and H3?

1. It is service-affecting and would need engineer approval.
2. A handover failure ratio is only meaningful at the target cell, not the source cell.
3. Every hypothesis predicts the same outcome for it, so any result fits all three.
4. Handover failure ratios are not available until MRO has been enabled.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3

**詳解**　A check discriminates only when the hypotheses predict different results for it; for C1 all three predict "rises", so the result cannot favour one over another (it confirms the symptom, not the cause). It is read-only, not service-affecting; the ratio is a normal source-side statistic; and it does not depend on MRO being enabled.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.2.4 (step 2: a check for which all hypotheses predict the same result cannot separate them); Google SRE Book, "Effective Troubleshooting" (https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)

</details>

**004.** One C2 RLF report shows failedPCellId = KH-0415-2, timeConnFailure = 52, reestablishmentCellId = KH-0412-1. With the operator's threshold, how is it classified, and does it support H1 or H2?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It is a Too Late Handover in the opposite direction (KH-0415-2 should have handed over to KH-0412-1 earlier), so it supports neither H1 nor H2 as written.

**詳解**　timeConnFailure = 52 × 100 ms = 5.2 s, which is larger than Tstore_UE_cntxt = 4 s, so there was no recent handover. The UE failed in KH-0415-2 and attempted re-establishment in a different cell (KH-0412-1), which matches the Too Late Handover rule. H1 and H2 are about the KH-0412-1 → KH-0415-2 direction; this report points to the reverse direction and should be noted as a separate finding.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.300 V18.11.0 clause 15.5.2.2.2 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/); 3GPP TS 38.331 V18.11.0 clause 6.2.2, timeConnFailure (actual value = field value × 100 ms) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.331/)

</details>

**005.** Using only the scenario data, which of H1, H2 or H3 is currently best supported?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the matrix contains only predictions and no check has been run; the RLF reports for C2 (timeConnFailure, previousPCellId, failedPCellId, reestablishmentCellId) are needed first.

**018.** In the Too Early and Wrong Cell definitions, what does "successful handover" refer to?

1. The UE state: successful completion of the random access procedure.
2. The source gNB receiving UE CONTEXT RELEASE from the target gNB.
3. The source gNB transmitting the RRCReconfiguration with reconfigurationWithSync.
4. The AMF completing the path switch toward the target gNB.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　TS 38.300 states that "successful handover" in these definitions refers to the UE state, namely the successful completion of the RA procedure. UE CONTEXT RELEASE and path switch are network-side events that happen later, and sending the handover command is only the start of the handover.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.300 V18.11.0 clause 15.5.2.2.2 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/)

</details>

**019.** Why is Tstore_UE_cntxt given in the scenario as an operator setting instead of a value taken from the specification?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Because TS 38.300 uses Tstore_UE_cntxt only as an example of the configured threshold and requires it to be configurable by OAM, so "recent handover" depends on each network's setting.

**詳解**　The detection rules compare the UE reported timer with "the configured threshold (e.g. Tstore_UE_cntxt)", and clause 15.5.2.5 says the parameter shall be configurable by the OAM system; no fixed value is given.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.300 V18.11.0 clauses 15.5.2.2.2 and 15.5.2.5 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/38_series/38.300/)

</details>

**020.** Reports #6 and #8 fit none of the three mobility classes. Can the table tell whether they are caused by a coverage hole or by interference?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the table has no radio measurements; measResultLastServCell and measResultNeighCells (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR), rlf-Cause and location information from the same RLF reports are needed.

**詳解**　The table holds only the fields needed for the mobility classification. Separating coverage (low RSRP on serving and neighbours) from interference (adequate RSRP, poor SINR/RSRQ) needs the measurement fields that the RLF report can carry.
