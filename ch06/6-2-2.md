# 6.2.2 支持證據與反證

所屬：[6.2 推理](6-2.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

提出假設之後，要替每個假設分別列出「支持證據」（如果假設成立就應該看到、而且真的看到了的資料）和「反證」（如果假設成立就不該出現、卻出現了的資料）。用途是避免只挑對自己想法有利的資料。維運人員最常在這些時候用到：值班主管問「你為什麼認為是容量問題」、要決定先派人上站還是先調參數、交接單要寫明哪些可能已經排除。Google SRE Book 第 12 章〈Effective Troubleshooting〉把這一步稱為用實驗的方法把假設「納入或排除」（[Google SRE Book, Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。以下是本教材整理的做法，不是 3GPP 規格的規定。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

1. **先寫預測，再看資料。** 每個假設寫出「若成立，應看到 A；不應看到 B」。先寫預測，才不會看完資料再回頭改說法。
2. **做成四欄表**：假設｜支持證據｜反證｜尚缺的資料。每一格寫具體資料（量測名稱、時段、數值），不寫「感覺像」。
3. **分辨證據強弱。**
   - 直接量到的比推論出來的強；有對照組（同時段鄰區、上週同時段、未變更的小區）的比沒有對照組的強。
   - 「沒有告警」是弱證據：告警有門檻，也可能沒設定，不能單憑它排除硬體問題。
4. **一個可靠的反證就能推翻假設。** 支持證據再多也不能證明假設，但只要有一筆可靠的資料和預測矛盾，這個假設（至少作為完整解釋）就不成立。SRE Book 同章也提醒：「Negative results should not be ignored or discounted.」（[Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）
5. **先檢查反證本身可不可靠**：資料是否完整、統計口徑是否一致、時間是否對齊。錯的反證會誤殺正確的假設。
6. **相關不等於因果。** 兩個量一起變化，可能是：
   - 共同原因：兩者都被第三個因素推動；
   - 反向因果：你以為的「原因」其實是結果；
   - 巧合：只是剛好同時發生。
7. **被推翻的要寫下來。** 「排除 H1，理由是某筆反證」也是結論，要進交接單與報告。

#### 實際例子

Telco-X（虛構）小區 KHH-GN-0207-1（虛構）連續一週有客訴「晚上網路很慢」。值班人員從效能管理系統取出下表（量測名稱依 [TS 28.552](https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/) V18.12.0：RRU.PrbDl clause 5.1.1.2.1，單位為百分比；RRC.ConnMean clause 5.1.1.4.1；DRB.UEThpDl clause 5.1.1.3.1，單位 kbit/s。數值虛構）：

| 時段 | RRU.PrbDl（%） | RRC.ConnMean | DRB.UEThpDl（kbit/s） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 上月平日 20:00–21:00 平均 | 61 | 150 | 38,000 |
| 本週平日 20:00–21:00 平均 | 92 | 210 | 8,500 |
| 本週日 20:00–21:00 | 46 | 95 | 9,100 |
| 本週日 10:00–11:00 | 30 | 60 | 9,400 |

| 假設 | 支持證據 | 反證 | 尚缺 |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 容量不足（晚上人太多） | 平日晚上 PRB 92%、連線數 210，高於上月 | 週日晚上 PRB 只有 46%，比上月平日的 61% 還低，速率卻只有 9,100 | — |
| H2 硬體或射頻故障 | 無 | 無告警（弱證據） | 硬體狀態查詢 |
| H3 某時點後小區本身效率變差（組態、干擾等） | 週日上午低負載時速率也只有 9,400，約為上月晚上的四分之一 | 尚無 | 劣化開始日期、組態差異、上行干擾量測 |

逐步判斷：

1. 先確認週日資料完整（各週期都有值、沒有計數器重置），反證才算可靠。
2. H1 的預測是「負載降下來速率就會回升」。週日負載比上月尖峰還低，速率卻仍只有 9,100，與預測矛盾，所以「容量不足是唯一原因」被推翻；負載仍可能讓情況更糟，但不是主因。
3. 平日晚上 PRB 偏高，可能是反向因果：每個用戶效率變差，傳同樣的資料就要用更多 PRB。
4. 客訴集中在晚上，是因為晚上使用的人多（共同原因），不代表問題只在晚上。
5. H2 只有弱證據，不能排除；H3 目前說得通，但還沒有任何直接證據，不能下結論。下一步是用 6.2.1 的方法找出劣化開始的日期，並依 6.2.3 補收資料。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **「支持證據多」不等於「假設成立」。** 很多假設都能解釋同一批支持證據，能分出勝負的是反證和能區分假設的檢查（6.2.4）。
- **反證推翻的是「完整解釋」，不一定是「完全無關」。** 例子中負載仍可能是加重因素，報告裡要寫清楚。
- **「沒看到」不等於「不存在」。** 沒有告警、沒有客訴，可能只是沒有監測或門檻沒到。
- **只拿同一時段比，沒有對照組。** 要和低負載時段、上週同時段、鄰區同時段比，才看得出差異（[Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。

#### 和其他小節的關係

本節接在 6.2.1（由時間軸提出假設）之後；表中「尚缺」一欄就是 6.2.3 要補收的資料清單，幾個假設同時存活時用 6.2.4 設計能區分的檢查。判讀 KPI 的口徑見第 5 章 5.1～5.5，事實與推測的區分見 5.5.3。被推翻的假設與理由要寫進 6.5.1 交接單和 6.5.2 障礙報告（[Google SRE Book, Effective Troubleshooting](https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)）。

**出處**　Google SRE Book, Chapter 12 "Effective Troubleshooting"（https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/ ）；3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clause 5.1.1.2.1、5.1.1.3.1、5.1.1.4.1（https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/ ）

**情境 001**　RRC setup failures and a draft hypothesis table

Telco-X (fictional) NR cell TPE-NH-0311-2 has rising RRC setup failures on weekdays; the operator's admission limit is 400 RRC-connected UEs per cell (fictional operator setting). The on-duty engineer exported hourly counters for Tuesday 2026-11-17 and started a hypothesis table.

Hour  RRC.ConnEstabAtt  RRC.ConnEstabSucc  FailCause.NetworkReject  FailCause.NoReply  FailCause.Other  RRC.ConnMean  RRC.ConnMax
08    4200              4116               6                        70                 8                120           180
12    5100              4845               9                        236                10               160           230
18    6300              5733               12                       540                15               205           260
22    3000              2880               3                        110                7                90            140
(FailCause.* = RRC.ConnEstabFailCause.*)

Draft hypothesis table (engineer, 2026-11-18 09:40)
Hypothesis                                   | Supporting           | Refuting | Still missing
H1 Admission control rejects (cell full)     | "evening is busy"    | -        | -
H2 UEs cannot complete setup (UL problem)    | "feels like          | -        | -
                                             |  interference"       |          |

**001.** In which hour is the share of RRC.ConnEstabFailCause.NoReply among all RRC setup attempts highest?

1. 18:00, about 8.6 % of attempts
2. 12:00, about 4.6 % of attempts
3. 22:00, about 3.7 % of attempts, because its success rate is the lowest of the day
4. 08:00, about 1.7 % of attempts

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　NoReply ÷ attempts per hour: 08:00 70 ÷ 4200 = 1.67 %; 12:00 236 ÷ 5100 = 4.63 %; 18:00 540 ÷ 6300 = 8.57 %; 22:00 110 ÷ 3000 = 3.67 %. The highest is 18:00. Option 2 is the second highest. Option 3 has the right share but a false reason: the success rate at 22:00 is 2880 ÷ 3000 = 96.0 %, while 18:00 has the lowest at 5733 ÷ 6300 = 91.0 %. Option 4 is the lowest share.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clauses 5.1.1.15.1 (RRC.ConnEstabAtt) and 5.1.1.15.3 (RRC.ConnEstabFailCause) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)

</details>

**002.** Why should the engineer write, for each hypothesis, "if it is true we should see A and should not see B" before opening the KPI export?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　So that the predictions are fixed in advance and the hypothesis cannot be reshaped afterwards to fit whatever the data shows, which is what makes it possible to separate supporting from refuting data instead of picking only the favourable data.

**詳解**　The curriculum method starts with predictions written before the data is examined. A prediction written after seeing the data can always be made to "match", so it can neither support nor refute anything. Fixed predictions turn the data review into a test.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.2.2 (steps 1–2); Google SRE Book, Chapter 12 "Effective Troubleshooting" (https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)

</details>

**005.** Does the data show whether the NoReply failures are caused by uplink interference or by UEs at the uplink coverage edge?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: both causes predict the same symptom (RRCSetupComplete not received), so data that separates them is missing, e.g. uplink noise/interference counters (vendor-specific, since TS 28.552 has no standard NR uplink interference measurement), the Type 1 power headroom distribution L1M.PHR1.BinX, or MDT/location of the failing UEs.

**詳解**　NoReply only records that the gNB sent RRCSetup and never got RRCSetupComplete. High interference and weak uplink coverage both produce this, so the current table cannot decide between them; the missing items go into the "Still missing" column.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clauses 5.1.1.15.3 and 5.1.1.26.1 (L1M.PHR1.BinX) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/); Curriculum 6.2.2

</details>

**007.** By how much did DRB.UEThpDl of KH-0412-3 fall from 2 Nov to 4 Nov?

1. About 45.4 %
2. About 83.3 %
3. About 54.6 %
4. About 18.4 %

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　Drop = (40,500 − 22,100) ÷ 40,500 = 18,400 ÷ 40,500 = 0.4543 → 45.4 %. Option 2 is 40,500 ÷ 22,100 − 1 = 0.833, which uses the new value as the base. Option 3 is 22,100 ÷ 40,500 = 54.6 %, the remaining fraction, not the drop. Option 4 is the absolute difference 18,400 kbit/s (18.4 Mbit/s) mislabelled as a percentage.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clause 5.1.1.3.1 (DRB.UEThpDl, kbit/s) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)

</details>

**008.** What does the result of prediction P2b do to H2, and what narrower hypothesis remains?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　P2b fails (the three cells upgraded the same night changed only −3 %, +1 % and −2 %), so "version 24.4 degrades the scheduler" as a general explanation is refuted; what remains is a narrower hypothesis that the upgrade changed something specific to KH-0412-3 (for example a cell-level setting altered during the upgrade).

**詳解**　P2a (timing) is consistent with H2, but supporting evidence cannot prove a hypothesis; one reliable contradiction can refute it. The contradiction is about the version in general, so the refinement keeps the timing link and restricts the cause to this cell.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.2.2 (step 4 and common confusions); Google SRE Book, Chapter 12 "Effective Troubleshooting" (https://sre.google/sre-book/effective-troubleshooting/)

</details>

**010.** Can prediction P1b be evaluated with this export?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No: the export has only cell-level daily values, so per-SSB data is needed, e.g. L1M.SS-RSRP.Bin reported per SSB (its measured object is the beam) or per-SSB PRB usage such as RRU.PrbDlSSB.

**詳解**　P1b is about individual SSB beams. Aggregating to cell level hides beam-level differences, so P1b is neither supported nor refuted and goes to the "Still missing" column.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clauses 5.1.1.22.1 (L1M.SS-RSRP.Bin, object Beam) and 5.1.1.2.15 (RRU.PrbDlSSB) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)

**012.** What is the handover execution success rate of the pair 0108-1 → 0109-2 tonight and last Saturday?

1. 18.9 % tonight and 2.0 % last Saturday
2. 81.0 % tonight and 98.0 % last Saturday
3. 83.9 % tonight and 98.0 % last Saturday
4. 81.0 % tonight and 123.4 % last Saturday

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　Tonight 1,005 ÷ 1,240 = 0.8105 → 81.0 %; last Saturday 1,174 ÷ 1,198 = 0.9800 → 98.0 %. Option 1 gives the failure rates (100 − 81.05 = 18.9 %, 100 − 98.0 = 2.0 %). #### One reliable counter-example refutes a hypothesis

**情境 004**　Backhaul congestion versus air-interface delay

NorthLink Mobile (fictional) receives evening latency complaints from gamers at site TN-AP-0520, whose three cells share one backhaul link. The on-call engineer's hypothesis H1 is "backhaul congestion in the evening".

```
Site TN-AP-0520, 2026-11-19, hourly samples (TS 28.552 names)
Time   GTP.DelayDlPsaUpfNgranMean   DRB.AirIfDelayDl (unit 0.1 ms)
       (EP_N3, microseconds)        cell -1   cell -2   cell -3
18:00  2,150                        52        48        50
19:00  2,180                        55        50        51
20:00  2,210                        61        49        196
21:00  2,190                        63        51        214
22:00  2,160                        57        47        98

Sync status: PSA UPF and gNB both PTP-locked all evening (NMS view)
H1 prediction (logged 18:30): if the backhaul is congested, the UPF->gNB
GTP delay rises in the evening and all three cells degrade together.
Complaints received 19:30-21:30: 3, all from the coverage area of cell -3
```

**017.** Express cell -3's DRB.AirIfDelayDl and the GTP delay at 21:00 in milliseconds.

1. 214 ms and 2.19 ms
2. 21.4 ms and 2,190 ms
3. 2.14 ms and 0.219 ms
4. 21.4 ms and 2.19 ms

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4

**詳解**　DRB.AirIfDelayDl is reported in units of 0.1 ms: 214 × 0.1 = 21.4 ms. GTP.DelayDlPsaUpfNgranMean is in microseconds: 2,190 µs ÷ 1,000 = 2.19 ms. Option 1 ignores the 0.1 ms unit. Option 2 reads microseconds as milliseconds. Option 3 divides both by ten again.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clause 5.1.1.1.1 item d) ("mean delay in 0.1 millisecond") and clause 5.1.1.1.8.1 item d) ("average delay in microseconds") (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)

</details>

**019.** Under what condition is GTP.DelayDlPsaUpfNgranMean usable at all, and therefore usable as counter-evidence here?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Only when the PSA UPF and the NG-RAN node are time-synchronised; here both are reported PTP-locked, so the measurement is applicable.

**詳解**　The delay is computed from the UPF send timestamp T1 in the GTP-U header and the gNB receive time T2, so unsynchronised clocks would make the value meaningless; TS 28.552 states the measurement "is only applicable to the case the PSA UPF and NG-RAN are time synchronised". Checking this is part of checking that the counter-evidence is reliable.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clause 5.1.1.1.8.1 item a) and c) (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/)

</details>

**020.** Does the data show why cell -3's air-interface delay rose?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: only delay values are given; data such as cell -3's load (RRU.PrbDl, active UEs), radio quality (CARR.WBCQIDist, MCS distribution), HARQ errors (TB.IntialErrNbrDl versus TB.TotNbrDlInitial) or uplink interference is needed.

**詳解**　Refuting H1 does not identify the real cause; it only moves the search to cell -3's radio side. The missing items go into the "Still missing" column for the next collection step.

**出處**　3GPP TS 28.552 V18.12.0 clauses 5.1.1.2.1, 5.1.1.7.1, 5.1.1.7.2, 5.1.1.11.1 (https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/Specs/archive/28_series/28.552/); Curriculum 6.2.2
