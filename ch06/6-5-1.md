# 6.5.1 交接單要有什麼

所屬：[6.5 交接與報告](6-5.md)

## 內文

#### 是什麼、什麼時候會用到

交接單（handover note）是障礙還沒結案、處理責任要換手時，交出的人寫給接手的人的一份現況快照。最常見的場合是值班換班（日班交夜班）；其次是事件升級給上一層、轉給另一個專業組（例如從無線組轉給傳輸組），或交給原廠工程師。交接單的讀者沒有參與前面的處理，只能靠這張單子決定下一步，所以它要回答的是「現在怎樣、做過什麼、還差什麼、要小心什麼、接下來誰做什麼」。

Google SRE Book 第 14 章〈Managing Incidents〉把「清楚、當面的交接」（Clear, Live Handoff）列為事件管理的要點之一，並建議維護一份持續更新的事件狀態文件（[Managing Incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/)）。交接單就是在換手那一刻把這份狀態整理給下一個人。

#### 怎麼做／怎麼判斷

**1. 交接單的六個必要部分**

| 部分 | 要寫什麼 | 常見缺漏 |
|---|---|---|
| 目前狀態 | 工單號、等級、開始時間、目前影響範圍與服務狀態（中斷／部分恢復／觀察中），**附截至幾點的資料** | 只寫「處理中」 |
| 已做的查證與結果 | 每項動作的時間、做法、結果、證據放在哪裡；也包括「查過、沒發現異常」的項目及其查證範圍 | 只列動作不列結果 |
| 未完成的事 | 進行中的動作、等待中的回覆（廠商、現場）、已送出但未批准的變更 | 漏掉等待中的外部回覆 |
| 風險 | 可能惡化的條件、暫時措施的副作用與到期時間 | 完全不寫 |
| 下一步 | 具體動作、負責人、時間點；「如果 X 就做 Y」的條件式 | 寫成「持續觀察」 |
| 聯絡人 | 現場、廠商、主管、交出人本人可聯絡到幾點 | 只留姓名不留聯絡方式 |

**2. 寫法原則**

- **事實與推測分開**（承接 5.5.3）：每一句前面標「已確認」或「推測」，推測要寫依據與還缺什麼證據。
- **查證要寫範圍**：「組態沒問題」是結論，「小區層參數與昨日備份比對一致，傳輸參數未比對」才是事實。
- **數字帶單位、時間與來源**：例如「15 分鐘粒度、16:45～19:45 介於 93%～95%，來源為網管 KPI 報表」。
- **寫給沒參與的人看**：不用只有自己懂的簡稱，系統名、單號寫全。
- **書面加口頭確認**：書面交接後再用電話或當面講一次，接手人確認後在單上記下確認時間；SRE Book 第 14 章（[Managing Incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/)）也強調交接要明確，原文說「It's essential that the post of incident commander be clearly handed off at the end of the working day.」
- **待批准的處置要寫清楚狀態**：已批准未執行、未批准、已執行，三者不能混寫（處置的批准流程見 6.3.2）。

**3. 交接前自我檢查**

交出前問三個問題：接手人不打電話給我，能不能照單做下一步？哪一句是推測卻寫得像事實？有沒有正在倒數的時限（法定通報更新、廠商回覆、變更時窗）沒寫進去？

#### 實際例子

```
交接單（虛構）　丁電信 NOC　工單 INC-2026-1118-0418　等級 P2
交接時間 2026-11-18 20:00　交出：林○○（日班）　接手：陳○○（夜班）

一、目前狀態（資料截至 19:45）
 [已確認] 西峰區（虛構）gNB-WF-012、013、015 共 9 個小區，RRC 連線建立成功率
         自 16:45 起由平日約 99.5% 降到 93%～95%（15 分鐘粒度，網管 KPI 報表）
 [已確認] 服務未中斷；其他小區正常
 [推測]   受影響用戶約 1,200 人，依上週同時段連線數推估，非實際計數
二、已做的查證與結果
 17:05 查告警：3 站無設備告警、無傳輸告警 [已確認]
 17:20 組態比對：9 小區的小區層參數與 11/17 備份一致 [已確認]；傳輸參數未比對
 17:50 上行干擾量測：9 小區上行干擾比同區其他小區高約 8 dB [已確認]
 18:30 [推測] 附近有外部干擾源；依據為僅上行受影響且無告警；尚未掃頻，未證實
三、未完成的事
 - 掃頻車已派出，預計 21:30 到達（聯絡：王○○）
 - 原廠技術支援案號 TAC-77120，等待回覆
 - 變更申請 CHG-2026-1118-07（鎖定 WF-015 第 3 小區）已送，未批准、未執行
四、風險
 - [推測] 21:00～23:00 為該區尖峰，成功率可能再下降
 - 若鎖定小區，鄰近小區負載會上升
五、下一步
 - 21:30 起確認掃頻結果；找到干擾源→通知頻率管理窗口
 - 若成功率連續 2 個粒度低於 90% → 依升級表升為 P1（門檻為丁電信內部規定，虛構）
六、聯絡人
 掃頻：王○○ 分機 6123　值班經理：張○○ 分機 6001　林○○ 可聯絡至 22:00
接手確認：20:12 陳○○ 電話確認
```

讀這張交接單時：第一部分告訴接手人影響多大、資料新到什麼時候；第二部分已排除「設備告警」，但組態只比對了小區層參數，所以不能說「組態沒有問題」；外部干擾被明確標為推測，接手人知道 21:30 的掃頻結果才是驗證點。第三部分的變更單「未批准、未執行」，接手人不會誤以為已經鎖定小區。

不能下結論的地方：用戶數是推估；干擾高約 8 dB 只說明上行受干擾，不能據此認定干擾來源在站外，也可能是站內設備問題（例如 6.1.2、6.1.7 談的情形）。格式可參考 SRE Book 附錄的[事件狀態文件範例](https://sre.google/sre-book/incident-document/)。

#### 容易搞混的地方

- **交接單 ≠ 工單 ≠ 障礙報告**：工單（6.4.3）記錄事件從開立到結案的全部歷程；交接單是換手時的現況快照；障礙報告（6.5.2）是結案後的完整分析。交接單可以引用工單，但不能叫接手人「自己看工單」。
- **「沒有異常」和「沒有查」不同**：沒寫查證範圍的「正常」會讓接手人跳過該查的地方。
- **推測寫成事實**：交出人認為「應該是外部干擾」，寫成「外部干擾造成」，接手人就可能不再查站內設備。
- **只交書面、沒人確認**：沒有接手確認，責任在換手那段時間沒有人承接；SRE Book 把這點列為「Clear, Live Handoff」（[Managing Incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/)）。

#### 和其他小節的關係

交接單中「已確認／推測」的分法來自 5.5.3，時間戳寫法來自 5.5.1；已做的查證對應 6.3.1，待批准的處置對應 6.3.2；還缺的資料（例如掃頻）是 6.2.3 的補收計畫。正在進行的法定通報更新（6.4.2）要列在未完成的事裡。事件結束後，交接單是寫障礙報告（6.5.2）時間軸的素材。參考：[SRE Book 第 15 章](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)談事後報告如何使用這些紀錄。

**出處**　Google, *Site Reliability Engineering*（SRE Book）第 14 章〈Managing Incidents〉（[sre.google](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/)）、附錄 C〈Example Incident State Document〉（[sre.google](https://sre.google/sre-book/incident-document/)）；第 15 章〈Postmortem Culture: Learning from Failure〉（[sre.google](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)）；交接單格式與丁電信內部門檻為虛構範例，屬方法論，非任何標準規定。

#### Checking a handover note against the six required parts

**情境 001**　Night-shift note for a microwave link outage

NorthLink Mobile (fictional) lost an end site behind a microwave link during the night. The night engineer wrote the following note for the 08:00 shift change.

```
HANDOVER NOTE  NorthLink Mobile NOC (fictional)
Ticket INC-2026-1124-0093   Priority P2
Handover 2026-11-25 08:00   From: R. Okafor (night)   To: M. Haddad (day)

1. Current status (data as of 07:45)
 [Confirmed] Microwave link MW-HL-07 (hub HL-0201 <-> site HL-0233) down since 03:12
 [Confirmed] gNB HL-0233 (3 cells) out of service since 03:12; hub HL-0201 normal
 [Confirmed] No other sites behind HL-0233 (end site, per transport topology)
2. Checks done and results
 03:20 Alarm list: "link down" on both ends of MW-HL-07; no power alarm  [Confirmed]
 03:40 Remote reset of the outdoor unit at the HL-0233 end: no change  [Confirmed]
 04:10 Weather portal: heavy rain cell over HL area 02:50-04:30  [Confirmed]
3. Open items
 - Field team FT-3 dispatched 06:30, ETA 09:00
 - Vendor case MW-55410 opened 04:20, no reply yet
4. Next steps
 - Keep monitoring
```

**001.** Which two of the six required parts of a handover note are completely absent from this note?

1. Risks and contacts
2. Open items and contacts
3. Checks done and results, and risks
4. Current status and next steps

**答案**　1

**詳解**　The note has sections 1 (current status), 2 (checks done and results), 3 (open items) and 4 (next steps); there is no risk section and no contact section at all, so the incoming engineer does not even know how to reach FT-3, the vendor or R. Okafor. Option 2 is wrong because open items are present (FT-3, MW-55410). Option 3 is wrong because checks with results are present. Option 4 is wrong because status and next steps exist; the next step is weak, but not absent.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table "the six required parts")

</details>

**002.** Why is "Keep monitoring" not an acceptable entry in the next-steps part of a handover note?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Because it names no concrete action, no owner, no time and no "if X then do Y" condition, so the incoming engineer cannot act on it without calling the outgoing engineer.

**詳解**　The curriculum lists "written as 'keep monitoring'" as the typical omission in the next-steps part: a next step must be a specific action with an owner and a time, and conditional steps must state the trigger and the action. The self-check question "can the incoming engineer do the next step from the note without calling me?" fails for "Keep monitoring".

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "next steps"; section 3 self-check)

</details>

**003.** Which rewrite of the next-steps part best meets the requirements?

1. During the day shift monitor MW-HL-07 and respond as needed if the microwave situation changes.
2. M. Haddad checks FT-3 at 09:00 and escalates if the link is down and vendor silent at 10:00.
3. Wait until rainfall ends, when the link may recover without the team needing to take other steps.
4. Review the linked incident ticket INC-2026-1124-0093 and proceed according to what it contains.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　Option 2 gives concrete actions, an owner, times and an "if ... then" condition built from the open items that are really in the note (FT-3 ETA 09:00, vendor case MW-55410). Option 1 is "keep monitoring" in other words. Option 3 rests on an untested guess (the rain ended at 04:30 and the link was still down at 07:45). Option 4 sends the reader to the ticket instead of stating the next step.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "next steps")

</details>

**004.** What can the incoming engineer conclude from this note about heavy rain as the cause of the outage?

1. Rain is confirmed as the cause, because the outage started at 03:12 inside the rain period 02:50-04:30.
2. Rain is excluded, because the link was still down at 07:45, long after the rain ended at 04:30.
3. Time overlap alone is insufficient; check MW-HL-07 received signal levels and the FT-3 inspection.
4. Rain is confirmed as the cause, because the remote reset of the outdoor unit at 03:40 did not help.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3

**詳解**　The note contains a time coincidence and nothing more. A link still down after the rain argues against pure rain fading, but does not exclude rain-related damage, misalignment or an unrelated hardware fault, so neither confirmation nor exclusion is possible. Option 1 treats coincidence as cause. Option 2 overstates the persistence argument into an exclusion. Option 4: a failed reset says nothing about rain.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (writing principles: separate facts from guesses); Curriculum 6.2.2

</details>

**005.** Name the six required parts of a handover note.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Current status; checks done and their results; open (unfinished) items; risks; next steps; contacts.

**詳解**　These six parts answer the reader's questions "what is the situation now, what has been done, what is still pending, what to be careful about, who does what next, and whom to call". The curriculum table lists them in this order with what each must contain.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table "the six required parts")

</details>

```
[19:52] Priya (day, outgoing): handing INC-2026-1125-0211 (P3) to you Tom
[19:52] Priya: VoNR call drops at site TC-1107, cells TC-1107-1/2, since ~14:00

[19:57] Priya: next: Tom 21:00 collect SIP trace, send to TAC; if drop rate >3x normal, call duty mgr
```

**006.** Which part of this chat handover is the weakest against the requirements?

1. Contacts: no reachability time is given for anyone.
2. The checks have no recorded results.
3. Open items: the pending vendor reply is not mentioned.
4. Risks: no risk is stated at all.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　"did: alarm check, config compare ..." names actions without outcomes, the typical omission "only actions, no results"; the incoming engineer cannot tell whether alarms or configuration differences were found. Option 1 is wrong: Priya states "me until 21:30". Option 3 is wrong: TAC-80312 is listed as waiting. Option 4 is wrong: a risk (full card failure takes both cells down) is stated.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "checks done and results")

</details>

**007.** What is wrong with the status line "drop rate still about 2x normal, last checked the KPI report a while ago"?

1. It should give the exact number of affected subscribers instead of a ratio.
2. It should state the incident priority, which is missing.
3. It should name the root cause of the drops.
4. The note lacks a timestamp, granularity and time window.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4

**詳解**　The status must carry the time up to which the data is valid, and numbers need unit, time and source. "A while ago" makes "still" unverifiable. Option 1: an estimate is allowed if labelled; exact counts are not required. Option 2: the priority P3 is given at 19:52. Option 3: a handover note reports the current state; the root cause may still be unknown.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "current status"; writing principles: numbers with unit, time and source)

</details>

**008.** Did the configuration comparison against last week's backup find any difference?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: the chat gives neither the result nor the scope of the comparison (which parameter levels were compared); the outcome and the compared scope are needed.

**詳解**　"config compare vs last week's backup" is only an action. Without the result the reader cannot skip configuration as a cause, and without the scope a "no difference" would still not cover the uncompared levels.

</details>

**009.** For a change request mentioned in a handover note, which three statuses must never be written in a mixed or vague way?

1. Submitted, reviewed, closed
2. Planned, in progress, done
3. P1, P2, P3
4. Approved, pending, executed.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4

**詳解**　The curriculum requires the status of a pending action to be one of approved-not-executed, not approved, or executed, written explicitly. Option 1 and option 2 are generic workflow words that do not tell whether the change is allowed and whether it is already in effect. Option 3 lists incident priorities, not change statuses.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (writing principles: status of actions awaiting approval); Curriculum 6.3.2

</details>

**010.** Rewrite the line about CHG-2026-1125-03 so that its status is unambiguous.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　"CHG-2026-1125-03 (swap baseband card at TC-1107): submitted, not approved, not executed; CAB review 2026-11-26 10:00."

**詳解**　"is in" can be read as "approved" or even "done". The chat says the change is submitted and that the CAB meets tomorrow at 10:00, so it is not approved and therefore not executed; the date of "tomorrow" is written out for a reader who may read the note on another day.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (writing principles: status of actions awaiting approval)

#### Writing the current-status part: impact, service state and data-as-of time

**情境 005**　Partial restoration after a power fault

NorthLink Mobile (fictional): sites NL-0711 and NL-0712 reach the network through the transport at hub site NL-0710. The board below was exported for the 08:00 handover of ticket INC-2026-1125-0144 (P2).

```
Site status board, 2026-11-25
Event: mains power failure at hub site NL-0710 at 05:40 (cause of the failure under investigation)
06:55 Generator started by field team; rectifier back
Cell                State at 07:30   Source
NL-0710-1 (n78)     on air           NMS cell state
NL-0710-2 (n78)     on air           NMS cell state
NL-0710-3 (n78)     disabled         NMS cell state, RU alarm open
NL-0711-1 (n28)     on air           NMS cell state
NL-0711-2 (n28)     on air           NMS cell state
NL-0712-1 (n28)     disabled         NMS cell state; no alarm received from NL-0712 since 05:41
Test calls 07:20 (field team): NL-0710-1 OK, NL-0710-2 OK, NL-0711-1 OK
Generator fuel: about 6 h at current load (field estimate at 07:00)
Handover 08:00
```

**021.** Which service state should the status part give at 08:00?

1. Interrupted
2. Partially restored
3. Restored, under observation
4. Resolved

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2

**詳解**　Four of six cells are on air and two (NL-0710-3, NL-0712-1) are disabled, so service is back only in part. Option 1 ignores the four cells on air. Option 3 would require all affected cells to be back. Option 4 is a ticket closure state, not a status while cells are still down and the site runs on a generator.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "current status": interrupted / partially restored / under observation)

</details>

**022.** Is cell NL-0712-1 disabled because of the power failure at NL-0710?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Cannot be determined: NL-0712 has sent no alarm since 05:41, so its own fault state is unknown; the current alarm list and power status of NL-0712 and its transport reachability through NL-0710 are needed.

**詳解**　The timing fits the hub power failure, and NL-0711 (also behind NL-0710) recovered, but missing alarms can mean a lost management path as well as a local problem at NL-0712. The note must not state the cause as a fact.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (writing principles: separate facts from guesses)

**023.** Which sentence is a correctly written status entry?

1. Generator start at 06:55 restored service across all six affected cells.
2. [Confirmed] At 07:30 the NMS showed all n28 cells back on air at both sites.
3. [Confirmed] At 07:30 five of six cells were on air, according to the NMS.
4. [Confirmed] NMS at 07:30: four cells on air; NL-0710-3 and NL-0712-1 disabled.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4

**詳解**　Option 4 counts correctly (on air: NL-0710-1, NL-0710-2, NL-0711-1, NL-0711-2 = 4), names the disabled cells with what is known about them, and gives times and sources. Option 1 overstates: two cells are still down. Option 2 is false: NL-0712-1 is n28 and disabled. Option 3 miscounts.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (table, row "current status"; writing principles: numbers with time and source)

</details>

**024.** A cell shows "on air" in the NMS but no test call has been made on it. What may the status part claim for that cell?

1. The NMS reported the cell on air at that time.
2. That service on the cell is verified, because the NMS state is authoritative.
3. Nothing, because only test-call results may appear in a status part.
4. That it is "probably down" until a test call proves otherwise.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　1

**詳解**　A check must be reported with its scope: the NMS state is a fact about the cell state, a test call is a different check. Option 2 stretches one check into another. Option 3 throws away a valid, sourced fact. Option 4 turns the absence of a check into a negative claim.

**出處**　Curriculum 6.5.1 (writing principles: write the scope of a check; "no anomaly" is not "not checked")

</details>

**025.** Based on the field estimate, at about what time will the generator run out of fuel, and how must this be written?

**095.** After the live (phone or face-to-face) confirmation, what is recorded on the handover note?
