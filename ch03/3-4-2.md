# 3.4.2 Erlang B／C：阻塞機率與等待機率

所屬：[3.4 訊務與存取](3-4.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

知道話務量之後，下一個問題是「要準備幾條通道，才能讓用戶幾乎不會打不通」。Erlang B 公式回答「被拒絕就離開」的系統（傳統電路交換語音、細胞的語音通道、中繼線群）的阻塞機率；Erlang C 公式回答「佔滿時排隊等候」的系統（客服中心、調度臺、可排隊的信令處理）的等待機率。兩者都是容量規劃的基本工具：給定話務量與服務等級（grade of service, GoS）目標，反推需要的資源數；或者給定資源數，估計現網的阻塞率。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $A$ | 提供話務量 | Erl |
| $N$ | 通道數（伺服器數） | 條 |
| $B(N,A)$ | Erlang B：阻塞機率 | 無因次 |
| $C(N,A)$ | Erlang C：到達時必須等待的機率 | 無因次 |
| $h$ | 平均佔用時間 | 秒 |
| $W_q$ | 平均等待時間（對所有呼叫平均） | 秒 |
| $t$ | 等待時間門檻 | 秒 |

#### 公式與推導

**Erlang B（損失制）**　假設：呼叫為卜瓦松到達（來自很多獨立用戶）、被阻塞的呼叫直接消失不重撥、$N$ 條通道完全共用、系統處於穩態。這就是 M/M/N/N 模型，穩態時有 $k$ 條通道被佔用的機率正比於 $A^k/k!$，全部 $N$ 條都被佔用的機率是

$$B(N,A) = \frac{\dfrac{A^N}{N!}}{\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N}\dfrac{A^k}{k!}}$$

這個結果只和平均佔用時間有關，和佔用時間的分布形狀無關。$N$ 大時階乘會溢位，實際計算用遞迴式：

$$B(0,A)=1,\qquad B(n,A)=\frac{A\,B(n-1,A)}{n+A\,B(n-1,A)},\quad n=1,2,\dots,N$$

損失制不需要 $A<N$ 的條件：即使 $A>N$，系統也是穩定的，只是阻塞率很高。

**Erlang C（等待制）**　假設：卜瓦松到達、佔用時間為指數分布、佔滿時進入容量無限的佇列並一直等到被服務（不放棄）、先到先服務。這是 M/M/N 模型，**必須滿足 $A<N$**，否則佇列無限增長、沒有穩態。到達的呼叫必須等待的機率是

$$C(N,A)=\frac{\dfrac{A^N}{N!}\cdot\dfrac{N}{N-A}}{\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\dfrac{A^k}{k!}+\dfrac{A^N}{N!}\cdot\dfrac{N}{N-A}}$$

它可以直接由 Erlang B 換算，省去重算級數：

$$C(N,A)=\frac{N\,B(N,A)}{N-A\,\bigl(1-B(N,A)\bigr)},\qquad A<N$$

同樣的 $N$ 與 $A$ 下，$C\ge B$，因為等待的呼叫留在系統裡，會讓系統更忙。平均等待時間與超過門檻的機率是

$$W_q = C(N,A)\,\frac{h}{N-A},\qquad P(W>t)=C(N,A)\,e^{-(N-A)\,t/h}$$

只看「有等到的呼叫」，平均等待時間是 $h/(N-A)$。

#### 算例

**算例 1：用遞迴式求 Erlang B，並決定通道數**

已知：提供話務量 $A = 5$ Erl，GoS 目標為阻塞機率不超過 2%。求需要的最少通道數。

從 $B(0)=1$ 開始逐步代入 $B(n) = 5B(n-1)/(n+5B(n-1))$，取到小數第 4 位：

| $n$ | 算式 | $B(n,5)$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $5\times1/(1+5)$ | 0.8333 |
| 2 | $4.1667/(2+4.1667)$ | 0.6757 |
| 3 | $3.3784/(3+3.3784)$ | 0.5297 |
| 4 | $2.6483/(4+2.6483)$ | 0.3983 |
| 5 | $1.9917/(5+1.9917)$ | 0.2849 |
| 6 | $1.4243/(6+1.4243)$ | 0.1918 |
| 7 | $0.9592/(7+0.9592)$ | 0.1205 |
| 8 | $0.6026/(8+0.6026)$ | 0.0700 |
| 9 | $0.3502/(9+0.3502)$ | 0.0375 |
| 10 | $0.1873/(10+0.1873)$ | 0.0184 |

- 結論：9 條時阻塞率 3.75%，超過目標；10 條時 1.84%，符合。需要 10 條通道。
- 合理性檢查：通道數等於話務量（$N=5$）時阻塞率高達 28.5%，可見通道數必須明顯多於話務量。10 條通道承載 $5\times(1-0.0184)=4.91$ Erl，每條通道的平均利用率只有 49%；小規模系統要維持低阻塞，利用率必然偏低。對照之下，$A=50$ Erl 用同樣的遞迴算到 $N=61$ 時阻塞率為 1.75%（$N=60$ 時為 2.17%，尚未達標），利用率 $50\times(1-0.0175)/61 = 80.5\%$。話務量大 10 倍，通道只需要約 6 倍，這就是中繼效率（trunking efficiency）。

**算例 2：由 Erlang B 換算 Erlang C**

已知：客服中心忙時話務量 $A=5$ Erl，座席 $N=8$，平均處理時間 $h=180$ 秒（假設值）。求等待機率、平均等待時間，以及等待超過 20 秒的機率。

- 檢查條件：$A=5<N=8$，可以用 Erlang C。
- 由算例 1，$B(8,5)=0.0700$。
- $C(8,5)=\dfrac{8\times0.0700}{8-5\times(1-0.0700)}=\dfrac{0.5604}{3.3502}=0.1673$（中間值以未捨入的 $B=0.07005$ 計算）。
- 平均等待：$W_q = 0.1673\times180/(8-5)=0.1673\times60=10.04$ 秒。
- 超過 20 秒：$P(W>20)=0.1673\times e^{-3\times20/180}=0.1673\times e^{-0.3333}=0.1673\times0.7165=0.1199$，約 12.0%。
- 合理性檢查：$C=16.7\%$ 大於同條件的 $B=7.0\%$，方向正確；有等到的呼叫平均等 60 秒，乘上等待機率 0.1673 得 10.04 秒，前後一致。

#### 常見錯誤

1. **兩個公式用錯對象**：呼叫被拒就離開用 Erlang B；可以排隊等候用 Erlang C。把客服排隊系統套 Erlang B 會低估需要的座席數。
2. **$A\ge N$ 還硬套 Erlang C**：這時沒有穩態，公式會給出負值或大於 1 的結果，沒有意義。
3. **把承載話務量代進公式**：公式裡的 $A$ 是提供話務量，不是報表上量到的佔用量。
4. **通道數按比例縮放**：話務量加倍，通道數不需要加倍；反過來把一個大通道群拆成幾個小群，總阻塞率會變差。

#### 和其他小節的關係

輸入的 $A$ 來自 3.4.1。Erlang B 是 3.4.3 中 M/M/c/c 模型的阻塞機率，Erlang C 是 M/M/c 模型的等待機率，本節的 $W_q$ 和 3.4.3 的 M/M/c 公式相同。3.4.6 算出的每細胞通道數可代入本節求可承載的話務量。

**3.4.2S028**　Which assumptions of the Erlang C model are most questionable for a real customer-care hotline, and in which direction do they bias the result?

**答案**　Erlang C assumes an unlimited queue, no abandonment (callers wait forever), Poisson arrivals, exponential handling times and FCFS service with identical agents. **3.4.2S029**　For the same N = 21 and A = 18, why is the waiting probability C (0.396) much higher than the blocking probability B (0.086)?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　In a loss system blocked calls disappear, which relieves the servers. **詳解**　C = N·B/(N − A(1 − B)); since N − A(1 − B) < N when A > 0 and B < 1... more precisely C/B = N/(N − A(1 − B)) = 21/4.540764 = 4.62 > 1.

</details>

**3.4.2S055**　Identify the first invalid step in this attempt to analyze the delay-mode queue. Then give the smallest server count that yields a steady state, and its Erlang C waiting probability to four decimal places.

1. The Erlang B recurrence evaluated at $A=11$ with 10 servers gives $B(10,11)=0.259580328$ to nine decimals.
2. Apply the steady-state Erlang C expression at $A=11$, $N=10$.
3. Substituting into that expression gives the formal quotient $2.595803/(10-11(1-0.259580328))\approx1.3991$.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is invalid because $A\ge N$. The smallest stable server count is 12, and $C(12,11)=0.6939$.

**詳解**　The Erlang B loss model yields the stated input in Step 1. Its associated Erlang C delay model requires $A<N$. The formal quotient in Step 3 is arithmetically consistent with the substitution, but it has no probability interpretation at $A=11$, $N=10$. For $N=12$, continue the recurrence without rounding the intermediate results:

- $B(11,11)=11B(10,11)/(11+11B(10,11))\approx0.206084775$.
- $B(12,11)=11B(11,11)/(12+11B(11,11))\approx0.158894178$.
- $C(12,11)=12B(12,11)/(12-11(1-B(12,11)))\approx1.906730140/2.747835961=0.693902462$.

**出處**　[Villy B. Iversen, ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook*, Erlang B recurrence and Erlang C delay model](https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/stg/D-STG-SG02.16.1-2001-PDF-E.pdf).

**Scenario | Conference-bridge ports**

A company's audio-conference bridge has N = 8 dial-in ports. In the busy hour 40 participants try to join, and each participant stays connected for 15 min on average. A participant who finds all ports busy hears a busy tone and gives up. Assume Poisson arrivals and steady state.

**3.4.2S056**　What is the probability that a participant cannot join, in percent, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　33.83 %

**詳解**　Step 1: A = 40 × 15 min / 60 min = 10 Erl.
Step 2: recursion with A = 10: B(1) = 0.909091; B(2) = 0.819672; B(3) = 0.732064; B(4) = 0.646663; B(5) = 0.563952; B(6) = 0.484515; B(7) = 0.409041; B(8) = 4.090408/12.090408 = 0.338318.
Step 3: B = 33.83 %.

**出處**　ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook* (Erlang B).

</details>

**3.4.2S057**　The offered traffic (10 Erl) exceeds the number of ports (8). Is Erlang B still valid, and would Erlang C be?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Erlang B is valid for any A, including A > N: a loss system is always stable because excess calls are rejected; the result is simply a high blocking probability. Erlang C would not be valid, because a queue fed with A ≥ N grows without limit and has no steady state.

**詳解**　B(8, 10) = 0.338 is a valid probability; C requires A < N.

**出處**　ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook* (loss and delay systems).

</details>

**3.4.2S058**　What is the carried traffic on the bridge, and why can it never exceed 8 Erl, however many people try to join?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A_c = 10 × (1 − 0.338318) = 6.62 Erl. Carried traffic is the mean number of busy ports, and with 8 ports at most 8 can be busy at any instant, so its time average cannot exceed 8 Erl. As A grows, A_c approaches 8 Erl and blocking approaches 1 − 8/A.

**詳解**　A_c = 10 × 0.661682 = 6.6168 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (carried traffic).

</details>

**Scenario | Large transit trunk group**

A transit trunk group has N = 60 circuits and is offered A = 48 Erl in the busy hour. An Erlang B table (computed with the recursion) gives B(60, 48) = 0.013356 and B(60, 55) = 0.053294. Erlang B conditions apply.

**3.4.2S061**　What are the lost and the carried traffic at 48 Erl, in Erlang, rounded to three decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Lost 0.641 Erl; carried 47.359 Erl

**詳解**　A_l = 48 × 0.013356 = 0.641088 ≈ 0.641 Erl. A_c = 48 − 0.641088 = 47.358912 ≈ 47.359 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (lost and carried traffic).

</details>

**3.4.2S062**　The group runs at about 79 % utilisation with only 1.3 % blocking. What is the price of this efficiency when traffic grows?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Large groups run at high utilisation, but they have little relative headroom, so blocking rises very steeply with overload. Here a 14.6 % increase in traffic (48 → 55 Erl) multiplies the blocking by about 4 (1.34 % → 5.33 %). Large, efficient groups must be monitored closely and expanded before the busy-hour traffic reaches the design limit.

**詳解**　Utilisation = 47.3589/60 = 78.93 %; 55/48 = 1.146; 0.053294/0.013356 = 3.99.

**出處**　ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook* (overload sensitivity of large groups); T. S. Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice*.

</details>

**3.4.2S063**　Why are values such as B(60, 48) computed with the recursion rather than directly from A^N/N! divided by the sum?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The direct formula involves very large numbers: 60! ≈ 8.3 × 10⁸¹ and 48⁶⁰ ≈ 7.5 × 10¹⁰⁰, which overflow or lose precision in ordinary floating-point or calculator arithmetic. The recursion B(n) = A·B(n−1)/(n + A·B(n−1)) only handles numbers between 0 and about A + n, is numerically stable and needs N simple steps.

**詳解**　log10(60!) ≈ 81.92; 60·log10(48) ≈ 100.87.

**出處**　ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook* (recursive evaluation of the B-formula).

</details>

**3.4.2S064**　A planner estimates the blocking at 55 Erl without a table. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct blocking (percent, two decimals)?
1. B(60, 48) = 0.013356.
2. Traffic grows by the factor 55/48 = 1.145833.
3. B(60, 55) ≈ 0.013356 × 1.145833 = 0.015304 = 1.53 %.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 3 is wrong: blocking is a strongly nonlinear function of A; it cannot be scaled in proportion to the traffic. The correct value from the table (recursion) is B(60, 55) = 5.33 %, about 3.5 times the linear estimate.

**詳解**　B(60, 55) = 0.053294 = 5.33 %.

**出處**　ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook* (Erlang B).

</details>
