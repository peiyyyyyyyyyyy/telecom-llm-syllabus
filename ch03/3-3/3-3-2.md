# 3.3.2 天線增益與方向性

所屬：[3.3 鏈路預算](../3-3.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which equation correctly relates antenna gain $G$, directivity $D$, and radiation efficiency $\eta$?

- A. $G = D + \eta$
- B. $G = \eta \, D$
- C. $G = D / \eta$
- D. $G = \eta - D$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。By definition, gain equals directivity multiplied by the radiation efficiency: $G = \eta D$.

</details>

**2.** A transmitter outputs 10 W (40 dBm). Feed‑line loss is 2 dB and the antenna efficiency is 85 %. The network requires an EIRP of 50 dBm. What is the minimum antenna gain in dBi?


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. Convert power to dBm: $P_{\text{tx}}=40$ dBm.  
2. Subtract feed‑line loss: $P_{\text{after loss}} = 40 - 2 = 38$ dBm.  
3. Account for efficiency: $10\log_{10}(\eta)=10\log_{10}(0.85) = -0.70$ dB.  
4. Required gain: $G_{\text{dBi}} = \text{EIRP} - P_{\text{after loss}} - 10\log_{10}(\eta) = 50 - 38 - (-0.70) = 12.7$ dBi.  
$\boxed{12.7}$

</details>

**3.** An antenna operating at 3.5 GHz has a gain of 18 dBi and a radiation efficiency of 0.80. Determine its effective aperture $A_e$ in square meters (rounded to three decimal places).


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. Wavelength: $\lambda = c/f = 3\times10^{8} / 3.5\times10^{9} = 0.085714\,$m.  
2. Linear gain: $G = 10^{18/10}=63.0957$.  
3. Effective aperture: $A_e = \dfrac{G\lambda^{2}}{4\pi} = \dfrac{63.0957 \times (0.085714)^{2}}{12.5664}=0.03686\,$m$^{2}$.  
Rounded to three decimals: $0.037\,$m$^{2}$.  
$\boxed{0.037}$

</details>
