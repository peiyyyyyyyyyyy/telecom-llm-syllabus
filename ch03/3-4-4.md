# 3.4.4 隨機存取：ALOHA、CSMA

所屬：[3.4 訊務與存取](3-4.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

終端要開始傳送之前，網路還不知道它的存在，沒辦法事先替它排程，所以第一步一定是「不經協調、自己挑時機送」，這就是隨機存取（random access）。行動網路的初始接取（PRACH 前導碼）、大量物聯網終端的上行接取、衛星終端的登入、Wi-Fi 的通道競爭，都屬於這一類。隨機存取的核心問題是碰撞（collision）：負載太低浪費通道，負載太高碰撞暴增、吞吐量反而下降。ALOHA 模型用很簡單的數學給出這條曲線與它的上限，是判斷接取通道會不會壅塞、該配置多少接取資源的起點。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $T$ | 一個封包（訊框）的傳送時間 | 秒 |
| $G$ | 提供負載：每個 $T$ 內平均的傳送嘗試數（含重傳） | 封包/封包時間 |
| $S$ | 吞吐量：每個 $T$ 內平均成功傳送的封包數 | 封包/封包時間 |
| $P_s$ | 一次嘗試成功的機率 | 無因次 |
| $\tau$ | 最大傳播延遲 | 秒 |
| $a$ | 正規化傳播延遲 $\tau/T$ | 無因次 |
| $M$ | 可選的前導碼（正交資源）數 | 個 |
| $n$ | 同一接取時機內同時嘗試的終端數 | 個 |

#### 公式與推導

**共同假設**　用戶數無限多（每個用戶貢獻的流量極小）；所有傳送嘗試（新封包加重傳）合起來是卜瓦松過程，平均每個封包時間 $G$ 次；封包等長；只要兩個封包在時間上有任何重疊就全部失敗（無捕獲效應）；通道本身無雜訊錯誤；回饋即時而正確。卜瓦松過程在長度 $t$ 的區間內恰有 $k$ 次到達的機率是 $(Gt/T)^k e^{-Gt/T}/k!$。

**純 ALOHA（pure ALOHA）**　想送就送。一個在時刻 $t_0$ 開始的封包，會被任何在 $(t_0-T,\ t_0+T)$ 內開始的其他封包撞到，脆弱期（vulnerable period）長 $2T$。脆弱期內沒有其他傳送的機率是 $e^{-2G}$，所以

$$S=G\,e^{-2G}$$

對 $G$ 微分：$dS/dG=e^{-2G}(1-2G)=0$，得 $G=0.5$ 時最大，

$$S_{\max}=\frac{1}{2e}=0.1839$$

**時槽 ALOHA（slotted ALOHA）**　時間切成長度 $T$ 的時槽，只能在時槽起點開始送，需要全網同步。兩個封包要嘛完全重疊、要嘛完全不重疊，脆弱期縮短為 $T$：

$$S=G\,e^{-G}$$

$dS/dG=e^{-G}(1-G)=0$，得 $G=1$ 時最大，

$$S_{\max}=\frac{1}{e}=0.3679$$

在時槽 ALOHA 裡，一個時槽是空的機率為 $e^{-G}$，剛好一個傳送（成功）的機率為 $Ge^{-G}$，碰撞的機率為 $1-e^{-G}-Ge^{-G}$。一次嘗試成功的機率 $P_s=S/G=e^{-G}$，每個封包平均要送 $1/P_s=e^{G}$ 次。$G$ 超過最佳點之後，$S$ 隨 $G$ 增加而下降；重傳又會使 $G$ 更大，所以實際系統必須有退避（backoff）與接取管制來把 $G$ 壓回去。

**CSMA 概念**　載波感測多重存取（carrier sense multiple access, CSMA）是「先聽再送」：通道忙就不送，碰撞只會發生在別人已經開始送、但訊號還沒傳到自己這裡的那段時間，所以效能取決於 $a=\tau/T$。Kleinrock 與 Tobagi 對非時槽、非堅持型（nonpersistent）CSMA 推得

$$S=\frac{G\,e^{-aG}}{G(1+2a)+e^{-aG}}$$

$a$ 愈小吞吐量愈高；$a$ 接近 1 時感測到的資訊已經過時，CSMA 就失去優勢。乙太網路的 CSMA/CD（邊送邊偵測碰撞）與無線區域網路的 CSMA/CA（以隨機退避避免碰撞）都由此衍生。

**多個正交資源的情形**　若每個接取時機有 $M$ 個前導碼可選，$n$ 個終端各自獨立、均勻地隨機挑一個，某個終端沒有和別人選到同一個的機率是

$$P_s=\left(1-\frac{1}{M}\right)^{n-1}$$

依 3GPP TS 38.211，NR 每個 PRACH 時機定義 64 個前導碼（其中可供競爭式接取使用的數量由網路設定）。

#### 算例

**算例 1：兩種 ALOHA 的吞吐量曲線**

求 $G=0.25$、0.5、1、2 時的吞吐量，取到小數第 4 位。

| $G$ | 純 ALOHA $Ge^{-2G}$ | 時槽 ALOHA $Ge^{-G}$ |
|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | $0.25\times e^{-0.5}=0.1516$ | $0.25\times e^{-0.25}=0.1947$ |
| 0.5 | $0.5\times e^{-1}=0.1839$ | $0.5\times e^{-0.5}=0.3033$ |
| 1 | $1\times e^{-2}=0.1353$ | $1\times e^{-1}=0.3679$ |
| 2 | $2\times e^{-4}=0.0366$ | $2\times e^{-2}=0.2707$ |

- 最大值分別出現在 $G=0.5$（18.39%）與 $G=1$（36.79%），後者剛好是前者的 2 倍。
- 合理性檢查：$G=2$ 時負載比最佳點高，兩者的吞吐量都比最大值低，符合「過載反而變差」；吞吐量都沒有超過 1。

**算例 2：物聯網上行接取（時槽 ALOHA）**

已知：接取通道每秒有 100 個時槽；細胞內 2,000 個終端，每個終端平均每 60 秒發起一次傳送嘗試（含重傳；皆為假設值）。求負載、成功機率與每秒成功數。

- 每秒嘗試數：$2000/60=33.33$ 次；$G=33.33/100=0.3333$ 次/時槽。
- $P_s=e^{-0.3333}=0.7165$；平均嘗試次數 $e^{0.3333}=1.396$ 次。
- $S=0.3333\times0.7165=0.2388$ 封包/時槽，每秒成功 $100\times0.2388=23.88$ 個。
- 時槽狀態：空 71.65%、成功 23.88%、碰撞 $1-0.7165-0.2388=4.46\%$。
- 合理性檢查：$G=0.333<1$，位於吞吐量曲線上升段，但系統穩定仍需靠退避或接取管制來維持；三種狀態機率相加為 1。

**算例 3：前導碼碰撞**

已知：$M=64$，同一 PRACH 時機有 $n=5$ 個終端同時嘗試（假設 64 個都可用於競爭、各自均勻隨機選取）。$P_s=(63/64)^4=0.9389$；若 $n=10$，$P_s=(63/64)^9=0.8679$。同時嘗試的終端愈多，碰撞機率上升得愈快。

#### 常見錯誤

1. **兩個最大值與對應的 $G$ 記反**：純 ALOHA 是 $1/(2e)$、出現在 $G=0.5$；時槽 ALOHA 是 $1/e$、出現在 $G=1$。
2. **把 $G$ 當成只有新封包**：$G$ 包含重傳；穩態下新封包的到達率等於 $S$，不是 $G$。
3. **把 36.8% 當成任何條件下的上限**：它建立在無限用戶、卜瓦松到達、無捕獲的假設上。用戶數有限或接收機能解出較強的那個訊號時，數值會不同。
4. **以為負載愈高吞吐量愈高**：超過最佳點後 $S$ 下降，這正是接取通道壅塞崩潰的原因。

#### 和其他小節的關係

卜瓦松到達的假設與 3.4.2、3.4.3 相同。本節的吞吐量與重傳次數會影響 3.4.5 的延遲分析（每次重傳都要再加一次等待）。3.4.6 的 FDMA／TDMA／OFDMA 是有排程的存取，和本節的競爭式存取互補：先用隨機存取取得連線，再用排程存取傳資料。

Tobagi, "Packet Switching in Radio Channels: Part I—Carrier Sense Multiple-Access Modes and Their Throughput-Delay Characteristics," *IEEE Transactions on Communications*, 1975；3GPP TS 38.211 V18.10.0 第 6.3.3.1 條（每個 PRACH 時機定義 64 個前導碼）。

**3.4.4G001**　A pure (unslotted) ALOHA channel carries equal-length frames. Assume an infinite population of users whose transmission attempts (new frames plus retransmissions) form a Poisson process, no capture and an error-free channel apart from collisions. The offered load is G = 0.3 attempts per frame time. Determine the throughput S in successful frames per frame time, rounded to four decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　0.1646

**3.4.4S003**　Why is the vulnerable period of a pure ALOHA burst two burst times long, rather than one?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A burst that starts at t₀ is destroyed by any other burst that overlaps it. A burst that started up to T earlier is still on the channel at t₀ (its tail overlaps), and any burst starting before t₀ + T overlaps its own tail. So any other start in (t₀ − T, t₀ + T), an interval of 2T, causes a collision, and the success probability with Poisson attempts is e^{−2G}.

**詳解**　P(no other start in 2T) = e^{−G·2T/T} = e^{−2G}.

#### 情境題：Retransmissions, backoff and stability

**Scenario | Two operating points of a slotted ALOHA channel**

A slotted ALOHA access channel receives new messages at 0.3 messages per slot in total, from a very large number of devices (infinite-population Poisson model for the attempts, no capture). Each collided message is retransmitted after a random backoff, and in steady state the throughput S equals the new-message rate.

**3.4.4S021**　Find the low-load equilibrium value of G and the mean number of transmissions per message there. Round both to four decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　G = 0.4894; 1.6313 transmissions per message

**詳解**　Step 1: equilibrium requires G e^{−G} = 0.3 with G < 1.
Step 2: numerically, G = 0.489402 (check: e^{−0.489402} = 0.612993; 0.489402 × 0.612993 = 0.3000).
Step 3: mean transmissions = 1/P_s = e^{G} = 1.631341 ≈ 1.6313.

**出處**　D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks* (slotted ALOHA equilibria).

</details>

**3.4.4S022**　Find the high-load equilibrium value of G and the mean number of transmissions per message there. Round both to four decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　G = 1.7813; 5.9378 transmissions per message

**詳解**　Step 1: G e^{−G} = 0.3 with G > 1.
Step 2: numerically, G = 1.781337 (check: e^{−1.781337} = 0.168413; 1.781337 × 0.168413 = 0.3000).
Step 3: mean transmissions = e^{1.781337} = 5.937790 ≈ 5.9378.

**出處**　D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks* (slotted ALOHA equilibria).

</details>

**3.4.4S023**　Both operating points deliver the same throughput of 0.3. Why is the high-load point undesirable and unstable in practice?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　At G = 1.78 each message needs about 5.9 transmissions, so delay is much longer and energy is wasted. It is also unstable: if G drifts slightly upward, S falls below the new-message rate, the backlog grows, more retransmissions raise G further, and throughput collapses toward zero; if it drifts downward, the system moves back toward the low-load point. Backoff that grows with the number of collisions (or access barring) keeps the channel near the low-load equilibrium.

**詳解**　For G > 1, dS/dG < 0: more load → less throughput → more backlog.

**出處**　D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks* (stability of slotted ALOHA); L. Kleinrock, *Queueing Systems, Volume 2: Computer Applications*.

</details>

**3.4.4S024**　If the new-message rate rose to 0.4 messages per slot, could any backoff setting keep the channel stable under this model?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No. The maximum of G e^{−G} is 1/e ≈ 0.368 messages per slot, so with 0.4 new messages per slot no value of G gives S equal to the arrival rate; the backlog grows without bound whatever the backoff. The only remedies are more channel resources (more slots or more preambles) or admission control that reduces the new-message rate below 0.368.

**詳解**　max_G G e^{−G} = e^{−1} = 0.3679 < 0.4.

**出處**　D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks* (slotted ALOHA maximum throughput).

**3.4.4S028**　The calculation assumes that each attempt succeeds independently with probability e^{−G}. When does this assumption break down?

**詳解**　At unchanged G = 0.6: delay = 0.822119 × 11 + 1 = 10.04 slots versus 5.93 slots.

Unconfirmed frames are never retransmitted. Assume Poisson traffic from an infinite population, a single shared channel with no capture, and that any overlap destroys both frames.

**3.4.4S061**　What fraction of frames is received successfully? Give it in percent, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　71.65 %

**詳解**　Frame rate = 1000/600 = 1.6667 frames/s; G = 1.6667 × 0.1 = 0.16667 per frame time.
Pure ALOHA: P_s = e^{−2G} = e^{−0.33333} = 0.716531 ⇒ 71.65 %.

**出處**　N. Abramson, AFIPS FJCC, 1970; D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks*.

</details>

**3.4.4S062**　In this network G equals the rate of new frames, while in the earlier ALOHA analyses G was larger than the new-frame rate. Why, and what is the consequence for the 28 % of frames that collide?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Because uplinks are unconfirmed and never retransmitted, every attempt is a new frame, so G is just the new traffic (0.167). The throughput S = G e^{−2G} = 0.119 is then smaller than the new-frame rate: the frames that collide (about 28.3 %) are simply lost, not delivered later. The application must tolerate this loss (or use confirmed uplinks, which add retransmissions and raise G).

**詳解**　S = 0.16667 × 0.716531 = 0.119422; loss = 1 − 0.716531 = 28.35 %.

**出處**　D. Bertsekas and R. Gallager, *Data Networks* (ALOHA with and without retransmission).

</details>

**3.4.4S063**　Measured delivery in the field is about 80 %, better than the 71.65 % predicted. Name two assumptions of the model that could explain the difference.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　(1) No capture: in practice, when two frames overlap, the much stronger one (e.g. from a nearby device) is often still decoded, so not every overlap destroys both frames. (2) A single shared channel: if devices spread frames over several channels or transmission modes that do not interfere, each channel sees a lower G. Other possibilities: traffic that is more regular than Poisson, or partial overlaps that the receiver survives.

**詳解**　Capture and orthogonal channels both reduce the effective collision probability below 1 − e^{−2G}.

**出處**　L. G. Roberts, "ALOHA Packet System With and Without Slots and Capture," *ACM SIGCOMM CCR*, 1975.

</details>

**3.4.4S064**　A product manager estimates the delivery ratio as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct value (percent, two decimals)?
1. G = (1000/600) × 0.1 = 0.1667.
2. Success probability = e^{−G} = 0.8465, i.e. 84.65 %.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the devices transmit at random times without slotting, so the vulnerable period is two frame times and the success probability is e^{−2G}, not e^{−G}. Correct delivery = e^{−0.3333} = 71.65 %.

**詳解**　e^{−2 × 0.166667} = e^{−0.333333} = 0.716531.

**出處**　N. Abramson, AFIPS FJCC, 1970; L. G. Roberts, *ACM SIGCOMM CCR*, 1975.

</details>
