## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleMath】**

> Determine the normalized throughput S for a sender A transmitting to receiver B via SR-ARQ over a wireless channel, where the round-trip time is 500 ms, the transmission rate is 1 Mbit/s, PDU headers are 100 bits, payloads are 4900 bits, the SR-ARQ window size is 50 PDUs, and the channel bit error rate is 10−4.

正解：0.30000000000000004

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What growth rate does the computational complexity of the MMSE (Minimum Mean-Squared Error) estimator have with the number of antennas?
>
> A. Linear
> B. Quadratic
> C. Exponential
> D. Logarithmic
> E. Constant

正解：B. Quadratic

---

**【TeleMath】**

> Determine the value of c when λ equals 10 for the random vector *x* = [x1, x2] with the probability density function (PDF) defined as: $$p_{\boldsymbol{x}}(a_1, a_2) = \begin{cases} e^{\lambda(a_1 + a_2) + c}, & |a_1| + |a_2| \le 1 \\ 0, & |a_1| + |a_2| > 1 \end{cases}$$

正解：-7.697
