# 3.6.3 單位一致

所屬：[3.6 解題方法](3-6.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

電信的數值橫跨很多個數量級，而且同一個量常有好幾種寫法：距離用 m 或 km，頻率用 MHz 或 GHz，功率用 W、mW、dBm 或 dBW，資料量用 bit、byte、MB，速率用 kbps 或 Mbps。公式裡的常數是配著特定單位推出來的，單位放錯，結果常常差 $10^3$ 倍或 60 dB；bit 與 byte 混用則差 8 倍。這類錯誤算式看起來完全正確，所以很難靠重算發現。實務上，鏈路預算表、流量報表、計費資料來自不同系統，各用各的單位，動手計算前先把單位統一，是最便宜的防錯手段。

#### 定義與符號

| 項目 | 意思 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| SI 詞頭（SI prefix） | 十進位倍數：k $=10^3$、M $=10^6$、G $=10^9$；m $=10^{-3}$、μ $=10^{-6}$、n $=10^{-9}$ | 2.6 GHz $= 2600$ MHz $= 2.6\times10^9$ Hz |
| 二進位詞頭（binary prefix） | IEC 定義的二進位倍數：Ki $=2^{10}$、Mi $=2^{20}$、Gi $=2^{30}$，不屬於 SI | 1 MiB $= 1{,}048{,}576$ byte |
| bit 與 byte | 1 byte $= 8$ bit；速率通常用 bit，檔案大小通常用 byte | 8 Mbps $= 1$ MB/s（十進位） |
| 絕對功率單位 | dBm 以 1 mW 為基準，dBW 以 1 W 為基準，兩者差 30 dB | 20 W $= 43.01$ dBm $= 13.01$ dBW |
| 相對量 | dB、dBi、dBd 是比值，沒有絕對大小 | 增益 15 dBi、損耗 2 dB |
| 配對常數 | 公式中隨輸入單位改變的常數 | FSPL：32.44 配 km 與 MHz，92.45 配 km 與 GHz |
| 因次檢查（dimensional check） | 檢查等號兩邊的單位是否相同 | bit ÷ (bit/s) $=$ s |

#### 公式與推導

單位一致的做法分成四步。

**第一步：列出每個量的單位，選定一套工作單位。** 最不容易出錯的做法是全部換成基本單位（m、Hz、W、s、bit）再代入原始公式，例如自由空間路徑損耗的原式（3.1.2）：

$$\text{FSPL} = 20\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{4\pi d f}{c}\right),\quad d\ \text{用 m},\ f\ \text{用 Hz},\ c\ \text{用 m/s}$$

如果要用帶常數的簡化式，就把輸入換成常數所配的單位：

$$\text{FSPL} = 32.44 + 20\log_{10} d_{\text{km}} + 20\log_{10} f_{\text{MHz}} = 92.45 + 20\log_{10} d_{\text{km}} + 20\log_{10} f_{\text{GHz}}$$

兩個常數相差 60 dB，正好是 $20\log_{10}(1000)$。嚴格來說兩者差 60.00 dB，但習慣寫法各自取位後差了 0.01 dB，所以用兩式算同一題，結果可能有 0.01 dB 的出入，這不是計算錯誤。

**第二步：換算時把換算因子寫成「等於 1 的分數」。** 例如 $800\ \text{m} \times \dfrac{1\ \text{km}}{1000\ \text{m}} = 0.8\ \text{km}$，單位可以像代數符號一樣約掉。乘還是除不用背，看單位能不能約掉就知道。

**第三步：分清楚線性量與對數量。** 功率的換算（3.1.1）為

$$P_{\text{dBm}} = 10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{P}{1\ \text{mW}}\right),\qquad P_{\text{dBW}} = P_{\text{dBm}} - 30$$

判斷準則：dBm 加減 dB 仍是 dBm；dBm 減 dBm 得到 dB；dBm 與 dBm 不能直接相加，W 與 dBm 也不能出現在同一個加減式裡。算式中只要出現 W，就先換成 dBm（或全部換成線性）再往下算。

**第四步：資料量與速率，先確認 bit 或 byte、十進位或二進位。** 通訊速率的 k、M、G 一律是十進位：1 kbps $=10^3$ bit/s，1 Mbps $=10^6$ bit/s。檔案大小的「MB」則有兩種用法：依 SI 是 $10^6$ byte；作業系統與部分軟體把 $2^{20}$ byte 也叫 MB，IEC 另訂 MiB 來表示後者。兩者相差

$$\frac{2^{20}}{10^6} = 1.048576,\ \text{約}\ 4.9\%$$

到 GB 與 GiB 則差約 7.4%。題目沒有說明時，採用 SI 的十進位定義（1 MB $=10^6$ byte），並在答案中寫明；題目寫 MiB 或註明 $2^{20}$ 時才用二進位。傳輸時間的基本式為

$$t = \frac{\text{檔案大小（byte）} \times 8}{\text{速率（bit/s）}}$$

最後做因次檢查：byte × (bit/byte) ÷ (bit/s) $=$ s，單位對了才代數字。

#### 算例

**算例 1（km 與 m、MHz 與 GHz、W 與 dBm）** 假設基地台發射功率 20 W，天線增益 15 dBi，頻率 2.6 GHz，手機在 800 m 處，手機天線增益 0 dBi。傳播環境未指定，採自由空間。求接收功率，以 dBm（小數第 2 位）與 W（3 位有效數字）表示。

整理單位：功率是 W，要換成 dBm；距離是 m，要換成 km；頻率是 GHz，用 92.45 的式子可直接代入，用 32.44 的式子要換成 2600 MHz。

$$P_t = 10\log_{10}(20\times10^3) = 43.01\ \text{dBm},\qquad d = 800\ \text{m} = 0.8\ \text{km}$$

用 km 與 MHz：

$$\text{FSPL} = 32.44 + 20\log_{10}(0.8) + 20\log_{10}(2600) = 32.44 - 1.94 + 68.30 = 98.80\ \text{dB}$$

用 km 與 GHz 複核：$92.45 - 1.94 + 8.30 = 98.81$ dB，兩者差 0.01 dB，來自常數取位。以下採 98.80 dB。

$$P_r = 43.01 + 15 + 0 - 98.80 = -40.79\ \text{dBm}$$

換成線性：$10^{-40.79/10} = 8.34\times10^{-5}$ mW $= 8.34\times10^{-8}$ W（約 83.4 nW；若 $P_r$ 不先取位而用 $-40.791$ dBm 換算，會得到 $8.33\times10^{-8}$ W，末位的差異來自取位）。

陷阱示範：若把 800 直接當成 km 代入，FSPL 會變成 158.80 dB，多出 60 dB；若把 2.6 代入配 MHz 的式子，FSPL 會變成 38.80 dB，少了 60 dB。若把 20 W 直接和 15 dBi 相加得到「35」，則完全沒有物理意義。

**算例 2（kbps 與 MB、bit 與 byte）** 以固定速率 2500 kbps 下載一個 150 MB 的檔案，需要多久？

整理單位：速率 2500 kbps $= 2.5\times10^6$ bit/s。檔案大小題目只寫 MB，採 SI 定義，$150\ \text{MB} = 150\times10^6$ byte。

$$t = \frac{150\times10^6 \times 8}{2.5\times10^6} = \frac{1.2\times10^9}{2.5\times10^6} = 480\ \text{s} = 8\ \text{分鐘}$$

如果檔案大小其實是作業系統顯示的 150 MiB：

$$t = \frac{150 \times 1{,}048{,}576 \times 8}{2.5\times10^6} = 503.3\ \text{s}$$

比十進位的結果多 23.3 秒，約 4.9%。

陷阱示範：忘記乘 8（把 byte 當 bit）會得到 60 秒，只有正確值的八分之一。合理性檢查：2500 kbps 相當於每秒 0.3125 MB，150 MB 需要 $150/0.3125 = 480$ 秒，兩種算法一致。

#### 常見錯誤

1. **輸入單位和常數不配。** 32.44 的式子代入 GHz 或 m；$-174$ dBm/Hz 配的是 Hz，卻把頻寬以 MHz 的數字直接取對數。
2. **bit 與 byte 混用。** Mbps 是每秒百萬 bit，MB/s 是每秒百萬 byte，差 8 倍；小寫 b 通常指 bit，大寫 B 指 byte。
3. **$10^6$ 與 $2^{20}$ 不說明。** 檔案大小沒寫採用哪種定義，結果差約 5%；通訊速率的詞頭不可以用二進位。
4. **dBm 與 dBW、W 與 mW 差 30 dB 或 1000 倍。** 靈敏度公式用 dBm/Hz 的雜訊密度，發射功率卻代 dBW。

#### 和其他小節的關係

功率單位換算的公式在 3.1.1，自由空間路徑損耗的兩組常數在 3.1.2，熱雜訊密度的單位在 3.1.5，速率與延遲的單位用在 3.4.5。3.6.1 讀題時標出的單位差異在這裡統一；3.6.5 的單位檢查是本節因次檢查的再確認。

**3.6.3G006**　A base-station radio delivers 40 W to a feeder with a loss of 2.5 dB, which feeds an antenna with a gain of 17.5 dBi. Find the EIRP in dBW, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　31.02

**詳解**　Step 1, convert the linear power to dBW: $10\log_{10}(40) = 16.0206$ dBW. Step 2, gains and losses are ratios, so they are added in dB: $\text{EIRP} = 16.0206 - 2.5 + 17.5 = 31.0206$ dBW, which rounds to 31.02 dBW.

**3.6.3G007**　A spectrum analyser reports one interferer at −95 dBm, while a second interferer is logged by another tool as 0.15 pW. The two interferers are uncorrelated. Find the total interference power in dBm, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　-93.31

**詳解**　Step 1, bring both to milliwatts: $10^{-95/10} = 3.1623\times10^{-10}$ mW, and $0.15\ \text{pW} = 0.15\times10^{-12}\ \text{W} = 1.5\times10^{-10}$ mW. Step 2, add in linear units: $3.1623\times10^{-10} + 1.5\times10^{-10} = 4.6623\times10^{-10}$ mW. Step 3, convert back: $10\log_{10}(4.6623\times10^{-10}) = -93.3140$ dBm, which rounds to −93.31 dBm.
\boxed{-93.31}

**出處**　ITU-R Recommendation V.574; T. S. Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice* (powers of uncorrelated signals add in linear units).

</details>

**3.6.3G008**　A driver stage delivers 0.5 mW into a power amplifier with a gain of 33 dB. Find the amplifier output power in W, rounded to three decimal places.

#### 單題：Bits, bytes and data-rate prefixes

**3.6.3G011**　A 450 MB file (1 MB = $10^{6}$ bytes) is downloaded over a link with a physical rate of 12 Mbps. Protocol overhead takes 8% of the link rate, so the useful throughput is 92% of 12 Mbps. Find the download time in seconds, rounded to one decimal place.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　326.1

**詳解**　Step 1, file size in bits: $450\times10^{6}\ \text{bytes}\times8\ \text{bit/byte} = 3.6\times10^{9}$ bit. Step 2, useful rate: $0.92\times12\times10^{6} = 1.104\times10^{7}$ bit/s. Step 3, $t = 3.6\times10^{9}/1.104\times10^{7} = 326.087$ s, which rounds to 326.1 s.
\boxed{326.1}

**出處**　BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)* (decimal prefixes); IEC 80000-13 (byte = 8 bit).

</details>

**3.6.3G012**　A voice codec produces 64 kbps and is packetised every 30 ms. Each packet carries 40 bytes of IP, UDP and RTP headers in addition to the voice payload. Find the bit rate at the IP layer in kbps, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　74.67

**詳解**　Step 1, payload per packet: $64000\ \text{bit/s}\times0.030\ \text{s} = 1920$ bit $= 240$ bytes. Step 2, packet size: $240 + 40 = 280$ bytes $= 2240$ bit. Step 3, packet rate: $1/0.030 = 33.333$ packets/s. Step 4, IP-layer rate: $2240\times33.333 = 74666.7$ bit/s $= 74.667$ kbps, which rounds to 74.67 kbps.
\boxed{74.67}

**出處**　IETF RFC 3550 (RTP); RFC 768 (UDP); RFC 791 (IPv4) for the header structure; bit rate = bits per packet × packets per second.

</details>

#### 情境題：Bits, bytes and data rates

**Scenario | Software update over a cellular link**

A 1.2 GB software update (1 GB = $10^{9}$ bytes) is pushed to a laptop over a cellular link with a sustained physical-layer rate of 35 Mbps. **3.6.3S026**　Find the download time in minutes, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**詳解**　Step 1, size in bits: $1.2\times10^{9}\times8 = 9.6\times10^{9}$ bit. Step 2, goodput: $0.94\times35\times10^{6} = 3.29\times10^{7}$ bit/s. Step 3, $t = 9.6\times10^{9}/3.29\times10^{7} = 291.793$ s. Step 4, in minutes: $291.793/60 = 4.8632$ min, which rounds to 4.86 min.

**出處**　BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)*; IEC 80000-13 (byte = 8 bit).

</details>

**3.6.3S027**　Someone asks whether "35 Mbps" might mean $35\times2^{20}$ bit/s, as with some file sizes. What is the convention for data-rate prefixes?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No. Data-rate prefixes in communications are always decimal SI prefixes: 1 kbps = $10^{3}$ bit/s, 1 Mbps = $10^{6}$ bit/s. Binary prefixes (Ki, Mi, Gi) are used, if at all, only for amounts of memory or file sizes, and IEC gives them distinct symbols. So 35 Mbps = $3.5\times10^{7}$ bit/s.

**詳解**　Using $2^{20}$ would inflate the rate by $1.048576$, about 4.9%, and shorten the computed download time by the same factor, a systematic error.

**出處**　IEC 80000-13 (binary prefixes); NIST, "Definitions of the SI units: the binary prefixes".

</details>

**3.6.3S028**　The download manager shows about 4.11 MB/s while the operator advertises 35 Mbps. Explain the difference in the unit symbols and how to compare the two figures.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　"MB/s" is megabytes per second (capital B = byte) and "Mbps" is megabits per second (lower-case b = bit); they differ by a factor of 8. The goodput 32.9 Mbps corresponds to $32.9/8 = 4.11$ MB/s, so the display is consistent with the link rate minus the 6% overhead. To compare, convert both to the same unit, for example bit/s.

**詳解**　$3.29\times10^{7}\ \text{bit/s}\div8 = 4.1125\times10^{6}$ byte/s $= 4.11$ MB/s; conversely $4.1125\times8 = 32.9$ Mbps $= 0.94\times35$ Mbps.

**出處**　IEC 80000-13 (symbols B for byte and bit); BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)*.

</details>

**3.6.3S029**　A colleague computes "35 Mbps ÷ 1.2 GB" and gets 0.029. Use a dimensional check to explain what this number is and why it is not the download time.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The unit of the result is (bit/s)/byte, which after using 1 byte = 8 bit becomes 1/(8 s): it is a rate of completing files per second, not a time. **詳解**　$35\times10^{6}/1.2\times10^{9} = 0.02917$ (bit/s)/byte $= 0.02917/8$ files/s $= 3.646\times10^{-3}$ files/s at the full 35 Mbps, whose reciprocal is 274.3 s (the time without overhead), not 0.029 of anything.

</details>

**3.6.3S057**　Where does the factor 3.6 in "km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s" come from, and how can a solver avoid having to remember whether to multiply or divide?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　$1\ \text{km/h} = 1000\ \text{m}/3600\ \text{s} = 1/3.6$ m/s. Writing the conversion as a product of fractions that each equal 1, $300\ \frac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}\times\frac{1000\ \text{m}}{1\ \text{km}}\times\frac{1\ \text{h}}{3600\ \text{s}}$, lets the units cancel like algebraic symbols; whether to multiply or divide follows from which units must cancel.

**詳解**　$300\times1000/3600 = 83.333$ m/s. The km and h cancel, leaving m/s.

**出處**　BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)* (quantity calculus).

**3.6.3S058**　To judge channel variation within a radio frame, the distance moved during one 10 ms frame is computed as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected distance in centimetres rounded to two decimal places.

**3.6.3S060**　An analyst notes that the ratio of the Doppler shifts on the two layers can be found without converting the speed at all. Explain why, and give the ratio.

**答案**　$f_D = v f/c$, so for the same train $f_{D,3.5}/f_{D,0.9} = f_{3.5}/f_{0.9}$; the speed and $c$ cancel in the ratio, along with their units. Any frequency unit works as long as both frequencies use the same one: $3.5\ \text{GHz}/0.9\ \text{GHz} = 3500\ \text{MHz}/900\ \text{MHz} = 3.889$.

**詳解**　$972.222/250.0 = 3.8889 = 3.5/0.9$.

**Scenario | Fronthaul latency budget**

A distributed unit is connected to a radio unit by 18 km of fibre with a group index of 1.47. The path passes through three packet switches, each adding 4.5 µs of latency. The fronthaul transport specification allows 100 µs of one-way latency. Use $c = 299{,}792{,}458$ m/s.

**3.6.3S061**　Find the one-way propagation delay in the fibre in µs, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　88.26 µs

**詳解**　Step 1, length in metres: $18\ \text{km} = 18000$ m. Step 2, $t = L n_g/c = 18000\times1.47/299{,}792{,}458 = 8.82611\times10^{-5}$ s. Step 3, in µs: $88.2611$ µs, which rounds to 88.26 µs.

**出處**　G. P. Agrawal, *Fiber-Optic Communication Systems* (group index).

</details>

**3.6.3S062**　Find the total one-way latency in µs, rounded to two decimal places, and state whether the 100 µs budget is met.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　101.76 µs; the budget is exceeded by 1.76 µs.

**詳解**　Step 1, switches: $3\times4.5 = 13.5$ µs. Step 2, total: $88.2611 + 13.5 = 101.7611$ µs, which rounds to 101.76 µs. Step 3, $101.76 > 100$, so the budget is not met.

**出處**　G. P. Agrawal, *Fiber-Optic Communication Systems*; latency budget = sum of component delays.

</details>

**3.6.3S063**　Planners often use the rule of thumb "5 µs per km of fibre". Show how this rule follows from a unit conversion, and judge whether it is good enough for this budget.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Delay per km $= n_g\times1000\ \text{m}/c$. With $n_g = 1.47$ this is $1.47\times1000/299{,}792{,}458 = 4.903\times10^{-6}$ s = 4.90 µs/km, so the rule is close. For this link the rule gives 90 µs instead of 88.26 µs; with a budget missed by only 1.76 µs, the 1.7 µs difference matters, so the exact value should be used.

**詳解**　$1.47\times10^{3}/2.99792458\times10^{8} = 4.9034\times10^{-6}$ s; $18\times5 = 90$ µs versus $18\times4.9034 = 88.26$ µs.

**出處**　G. P. Agrawal, *Fiber-Optic Communication Systems*.

</details>

**3.6.3S064**　To find how long the fibre may be while keeping the 100 µs budget with three switches, an engineer writes the following. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected maximum fibre length in km rounded to two decimal places.

1. Time left for the fibre: $100 - 13.5 = 86.5$ µs.
2. $86.5\ \mu\text{s} = 86.5\times10^{-3}$ s.
3. $L = t c/n_g = 0.0865\times299{,}792{,}458/1.47 = 1.764\times10^{7}$ m $= 17{,}641$ km.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the prefix µ means $10^{-6}$, not $10^{-3}$ (that is m, milli). With $t = 86.5\times10^{-6}$ s the maximum length is 17.64 km.

**詳解**　$L = 86.5\times10^{-6}\times299{,}792{,}458/1.47 = 17{,}640.8$ m $= 17.64$ km. The wrong result, 17,641 km, is longer than the Earth's diameter, a clear sign of a prefix error.

**出處**　BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)* (SI prefixes); G. P. Agrawal, *Fiber-Optic Communication Systems*.

</details>

**3.6.3S065**　The transport vendor reports the switch latency as "0.0045 ms" while the fibre delay was computed in µs. What is the rule for adding such contributions, and what result would a careless addition of "88.26 + 0.0045 × 3" give?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Quantities can be added only when they are in the same unit; convert first (0.0045 ms = 4.5 µs) and then add. A careless addition of the raw numbers gives $88.26 + 0.0135 = 88.27$, which hides 13.5 µs of switch latency and makes the link appear to meet the budget when it does not.

**詳解**　$0.0045\ \text{ms}\times1000\ \mu\text{s/ms} = 4.5$ µs; correct total $88.26 + 13.5 = 101.76$ µs versus the wrong 88.27.

**出處**　BIPM, *The International System of Units (SI)* (expressing values of quantities).
