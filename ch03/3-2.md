## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the key performance benefit of a fast fading MIMO channel?
>
> A. Minimum distance between PAM points.
> B. Spatial multiplexing capability.
> C. Outage performance.
> D. Reliable communication.
> E. Worst-case pairwise error probability.

正解：B. Spatial multiplexing capability.

---

**【TeleMath】**

> Calculate the maximum achievable bit rate Rb, in kbit/s, for a bandpass telephone channel that has a bandwidth of BCh = 3 kHz and utilizes 2-PAM modulation, given that the minimum bandwidth Bmin is equal to the channel bandwidth BCh.

正解：6.0
