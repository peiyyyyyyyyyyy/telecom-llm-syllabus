## 練習題

**1.** Which theorem states that the power spectral density of a wide‑sense stationary process is the Fourier transform of its autocorrelation function?

- A. Parseval’s theorem
- B. Wiener–Khinchin theorem
- C. Nyquist‑Shannon sampling theorem
- D. Shannon‑Hartley capacity theorem

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The Wiener–Khinchin theorem directly links PSD and autocorrelation as a Fourier‑transform pair.

</details>

**2.** A cosine signal $x(t)=3\cos(2\pi\cdot2\times10^{6} t)$ is observed over a rectangular window of length $T=1\ \text{ms}$. Using the periodogram definition $S_x(f)=|X_T(f)|^{2}/T$, compute the two‑sided PSD value at the peak frequency $f=2\ \text{MHz}$.  Unit: V$^2$/Hz.


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. Amplitude $A=3$ V, observation time $T=1\text{ ms}=1\times10^{-3}$ s.  
2. For a cosine multiplied by a rectangular window, the FT at the tone frequency is $|X_T(f_0)| = \frac{A}{2}T$.  
3. Compute $|X_T(f_0)| = \frac{3}{2}\times1\times10^{-3}=1.5\times10^{-3}$ V·s.  
4. Square magnitude: $(1.5\times10^{-3})^{2}=2.25\times10^{-6}$ V$^2$·s$^2$.  
5. Divide by $T$: $S_x(f_0)=\frac{2.25\times10^{-6}}{1\times10^{-3}}=2.25\times10^{-3}$ V$^2$/Hz.  
$\boxed{2.25e-3}$

</details>

**3.** A wide‑sense stationary random process has autocorrelation $R_X(\tau)=e^{-|\tau|/\tau_0}$ with $\tau_0 = 5\ \mu\text{s}$. Compute its two‑sided PSD value at zero frequency $f=0$.  Unit: (arbitrary power) per Hz.


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. The Fourier transform of $e^{-|\tau|/\tau_0}$ is $S_X(f)=\frac{2\tau_0}{1+(2\pi f\tau_0)^2}$.  
2. At $f=0$: $S_X(0)=2\tau_0$.  
3. Insert $\tau_0 = 5\ \mu\text{s}=5\times10^{-6}$ s: $S_X(0)=2\times5\times10^{-6}=1.0\times10^{-5}$.  
$\boxed{1.0e-5}$

</details>
