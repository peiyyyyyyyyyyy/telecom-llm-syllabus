## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is a linear program?
>
> A. An optimization problem with linear constraints
> B. An optimization problem with a linear objective function
> C. An optimization problem with both linear objective and constraint functions
> D. An optimization problem with non-linear constraints
> E. An optimization problem that cannot be solved

正解：C. An optimization problem with both linear objective and constraint functions

---

**【TeleMath】**

> Determine the probability that in two independent and homogeneous Poisson processes, x(t) with a rate of λx = 10 · 10^3 s−1 and y(t) with a rate of λy = 1 · 10^3 s−1, the first occurrence of y(t) happens before the first occurrence of x(t).

正解：0.09090909090909001

**【TeleQnA】**

> Which method is commonly used to solve MDPs (Markov decision processes) with finite time horizons?
>
> A. Approximation method
> B. Forward induction
> C. Linear programming
> D. Backwards induction

正解：D. Backwards induction
