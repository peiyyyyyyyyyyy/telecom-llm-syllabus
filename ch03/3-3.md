## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the doubly-near-far problem in a wireless powered communication network (WPCN)?
>
> A. Users that are far away from the HAP harvest more energy in the DL and consume less to transmit data in the UL
> B. Users that are far away from the HAP harvest less energy in the DL and consume more to transmit data in the UL
> C. Users that are far away from the HAP harvest less energy in the DL and consume less to transmit data in the UL
> D. Users that are far away from the HAP harvest more energy in the DL and consume more to transmit data in the UL

正解：B. Users that are far away from the HAP harvest less energy in the DL and consume more to transmit data in the UL

**【TeleMath】**

> What is the maximum bit rate Rb in Mbit/s that can be achieved by a space probe transmitting binary data via BPSK from a distance of 2e5 km, given a transmitted power of 35 dBm over a narrowband channel at 0.5 GHz, with transmit and receive antenna gains of 15 dB and 35 dB respectively, and an effective noise temperature of Teff,Rc = 350 K at the receiver, while maintaining a bit-error probability of Pbit = 1e-5?

正解：0.41009608158701805

**【TeleQnA】**

> In mmWave-UAV communications, which factor has a significant impact on the channel quality due to the narrow beamwidth?
>
> A. Airframe shadowing
> B. Doppler effect
> C. Penetration loss
> D. Antenna gain
> E. Fluctuations of UAV positions

正解：E. Fluctuations of UAV positions
