# 3.1.7 訊號與頻譜分析：傅立葉轉換、功率頻譜密度、自相關

所屬：[3.1 訊號與通道](3-1.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

通訊系統的規格幾乎都寫在頻率域：載波頻寬、頻譜遮罩、鄰頻洩漏、每赫茲的雜訊密度。頻譜分析儀上看到的也是功率隨頻率的分布。要把時間上的波形（脈衝多寬、符號多長）和頻率上的占用（主瓣多寬、旁瓣多高）對起來，靠的是傅立葉轉換（Fourier transform）；要描述雜訊這類隨機訊號在各頻率上的功率，靠的是功率頻譜密度（power spectral density, PSD）和自相關函數（autocorrelation function）。本節重點放在會算、會判讀：由脈衝寬度估頻寬、由 PSD 積分求功率、由頻譜儀讀值換算通道功率。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $x(t)$ | 時間訊號 | V（或任意振幅單位） |
| $X(f)$ | $x(t)$ 的傅立葉轉換 | V/Hz（即 V·s） |
| $f$、$t$、$\tau$ | 頻率、時間、時間差 | Hz、s、s |
| $T$ | 脈衝寬度或符號長度 | s |
| $R_x(\tau)$ | 自相關函數 | V²（功率型訊號，以 1 Ω 為基準） |
| $S_x(f)$ | 功率頻譜密度 | W/Hz（或 dBm/Hz） |
| $H(f)$ | 線性非時變系統的頻率響應 | 無因次 |
| $P$ | 平均功率 | W（或 dBm） |
| $N_0$ | 白雜訊的單邊功率頻譜密度 | W/Hz |
| $B$ | 頻寬 | Hz |
| $\text{RBW}$ | 頻譜分析儀的解析頻寬（resolution bandwidth） | Hz |
| $\text{sinc}(x)$ | $\sin(\pi x)/(\pi x)$ | 無因次 |

#### 公式與推導

**傅立葉轉換對**（本章採用以頻率 $f$ 為變數的寫法）：

$$X(f) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(t)\,e^{-j2\pi ft}\,dt,\qquad x(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} X(f)\,e^{j2\pi ft}\,df$$

計算與判讀時最常用的性質：

| 性質 | 時間域 | 頻率域 |
|---|---|---|
| 線性 | $a\,x_1(t) + b\,x_2(t)$ | $a\,X_1(f) + b\,X_2(f)$ |
| 時間平移 | $x(t - t_0)$ | $X(f)\,e^{-j2\pi ft_0}$（只改相位，不改振幅頻譜） |
| 頻率平移（調變） | $x(t)\,e^{j2\pi f_ct}$ | $X(f - f_c)$ |
| 尺度變換 | $x(at)$ | $\dfrac{1}{\lvert a\rvert}X\!\left(\dfrac{f}{a}\right)$（時間壓縮，頻譜展寬） |
| 摺積 | $x(t) * h(t)$ | $X(f)\,H(f)$ |
| 相乘 | $x(t)\,y(t)$ | $X(f) * Y(f)$ |

能量守恆（Parseval 定理）：$\int\lvert x(t)\rvert^2dt = \int\lvert X(f)\rvert^2df$。最重要的一組轉換對是寬度 $T$、高度 1 的矩形脈衝：

$$\text{rect}\!\left(\frac{t}{T}\right)\ \longleftrightarrow\ T\,\text{sinc}(fT)$$

它的頻譜在 $f = \pm1/T, \pm2/T, \dots$ 為零：脈衝越窄，頻譜越寬，時間寬度與頻寬成反比。OFDM 正是利用這一點：有效符號長度 $T_u$ 的子載波頻譜是 sinc 形，零點間隔 $1/T_u$，把子載波間隔取成 $\Delta f = 1/T_u$，每個子載波的峰值就落在其他子載波的零點上而互相正交；例如 TS 38.211 表 4.2-1 的 30 kHz 子載波間隔對應 $T_u = 1/30000 = 33.33$ μs。

**功率頻譜密度與自相關。** 對廣義穩態的隨機訊號，自相關函數 $R_x(\tau) = E[x(t)\,x(t+\tau)]$ 只和時間差有關，PSD 是它的傅立葉轉換（Wiener–Khinchin 定理）：

$$S_x(f) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} R_x(\tau)\,e^{-j2\pi f\tau}\,d\tau,\qquad P = R_x(0) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} S_x(f)\,df$$

訊號通過頻率響應為 $H(f)$ 的線性系統後，$S_y(f) = \lvert H(f)\rvert^2S_x(f)$。白雜訊的 PSD 是常數：雙邊寫法為 $N_0/2$（正負頻率都算），單邊寫法為 $N_0$（只算正頻率），兩種寫法積分出來的功率相同。白雜訊通過頻寬 $B$ 的理想低通濾波器後，功率是 $N_0B$，自相關是 $R(\tau) = N_0B\,\text{sinc}(2B\tau)$，第一個零點在 $\tau = 1/(2B)$：頻寬越寬，雜訊在時間上「記得自己」的時間越短。

**頻寬的幾種定義**要先講清楚再比較：零點到零點頻寬（主瓣寬度）、3 dB 頻寬（PSD 降到峰值一半的寬度）、占用頻寬（包含總功率某個百分比的寬度，無線電規則常用 99%，即上下各留 0.5% 在外）、等效雜訊頻寬（和實際濾波器通過相同白雜訊功率的理想矩形濾波器寬度）。同一個訊號用不同定義得到的數字可以差好幾倍。

**PSD 平坦時的功率換算**：$P(\text{dBm}) = S(\text{dBm/Hz}) + 10\log_{10}B_{\text{Hz}}$。

#### 算例

**算例 1：矩形脈衝的頻譜**

已知：寬度 $T = 1$ μs 的矩形基頻脈衝。求頻譜第一個零點、調變到載波後的零點到零點頻寬、基頻的 3 dB 點，以及第一旁瓣相對主瓣的高度。

- 第一零點：$f = 1/T = 1$ MHz。
- 調變到載波後頻譜平移到 $f_c$ 兩側，零點到零點頻寬為 $2/T = 2$ MHz。
- 3 dB 點：解 $\text{sinc}^2(fT) = 0.5$ 得 $fT = 0.443$，即 $f = 443$ kHz；調變後的 3 dB 頻寬為 $2\times443 = 886$ kHz，不到零點到零點頻寬的一半。
- 第一旁瓣：峰值出現在 $fT \approx 1.43$，$\lvert\text{sinc}(1.43)\rvert = 0.2172$，$20\log_{10}0.2172 = -13.26$ dB。
- 合理性檢查：若脈衝縮短為 0.1 μs，各頻率數字都變 10 倍（零點在 10 MHz），符合尺度變換性質；旁瓣只比主瓣低 13 dB 左右，說明未經整形的矩形脈衝頻譜洩漏嚴重，實際系統需要脈衝整形或濾波。

**算例 2：由頻譜分析儀讀值換算通道功率**

已知（假設值）：頻譜分析儀的解析頻寬為 100 kHz，在一個 20 MHz 寬、頻譜大致平坦的訊號上，游標讀到 −80 dBm。求功率頻譜密度與整個 20 MHz 的通道功率。

- PSD：$-80 - 10\log_{10}(10^5) = -80 - 50 = -130$ dBm/Hz。
- 通道功率：$-130 + 10\log_{10}(20\times10^6) = -130 + 73.01 = -56.99$ dBm。
- 另一種算法：頻寬比 $20\ \text{MHz}/100\ \text{kHz} = 200$，$10\log_{10}200 = 23.01$ dB，$-80 + 23.01 = -56.99$ dBm，一致。
- 合理性檢查：通道功率一定比單一解析頻寬內的讀值高。和 3.1.5 的熱雜訊密度 −174 dBm/Hz 相比，這個訊號的密度高出 44 dB。此換算假設頻譜平坦，並把解析頻寬當成等效雜訊頻寬，實際儀器還有濾波器形狀與檢波方式的修正量。

#### 常見錯誤

- **單邊與雙邊 PSD 混用**：雙邊密度 $N_0/2$ 要對正負頻率一起積分；用單邊 $N_0$ 卻又乘 2，功率會多 3 dB。
- **頻寬定義沒對齊**：3 dB 頻寬、零點到零點頻寬、99% 占用頻寬數字不同，比較前先確認定義。
- **把頻譜儀讀值當成總功率**：讀值是解析頻寬內的功率，改變解析頻寬，雜訊類訊號的讀值就跟著變；要換成 dBm/Hz 或積分成通道功率才能比較。
- **dBm/Hz 換成功率時用乘的**：dB 域要加 $10\log_{10}B$，而且 $B$ 用 Hz。

#### 和其他小節的關係

3.1.5 的熱雜訊密度 $N_0 = kT$ 就是本節的白雜訊 PSD，$kTB$ 是對它積分；3.1.4 的延遲擴散與同調頻寬是時間域、頻率域的一組對應；3.1.8 的取樣定理用到「時間域取樣等於頻譜週期複製」；3.2.5 的 OFDM 子載波正交性與 3.2.7 的脈衝整形、升餘弦頻寬直接建立在本節的轉換對上；自相關與穩態的機率基礎見 3.5.4。

#### 單題：Parseval's relation and energy spectral density

**3.1.7G006**　A rectangular pulse has amplitude 2 V and duration $T = 1$ ms (normalized 1 Ω convention). With $X(f)=\int x(t)e^{-j2\pi ft}dt$ and $\mathrm{sinc}(u)=\sin(\pi u)/(\pi u)$, its energy spectral density is $|X(f)|^2 = A^2T^2\,\mathrm{sinc}^2(fT)$. Given $\int_{-1}^{1}\mathrm{sinc}^2(u)\,du = 0.9028$, determine the energy contained in the main lobe $|f| \le 1$ kHz, in mJ, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3.61

**詳解**　Total energy (time domain): $E = A^2T = 4 \times 1\times10^{-3} = 4\times10^{-3}$ J $= 4$ mJ. Main-lobe energy: $\int_{-1/T}^{1/T} A^2T^2\mathrm{sinc}^2(fT)\,df = A^2T\int_{-1}^{1}\mathrm{sinc}^2(u)\,du = 4 \times 0.9028 = 3.6112$ mJ.
\boxed{3.61}

**出處**　Parseval's (Rayleigh's energy) theorem; S. Haykin, *Communication Systems*.

</details>

**3.1.7G007**　The signal $x(t) = e^{-at}u(t)$ with $a = 2000\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$ has Fourier transform $X(f) = 1/(a + j2\pi f)$ under the convention $X(f)=\int x(t)e^{-j2\pi ft}dt$. Determine the percentage of the total signal energy that lies in the band $|f| \le 500$ Hz, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　63.91

**詳解**　$|X(f)|^2 = 1/(a^2 + 4\pi^2f^2)$. Energy in $|f|\le B$: $\int_{-B}^{B}\frac{df}{a^2+4\pi^2f^2} = \frac{1}{\pi a}\arctan\!\left(\frac{2\pi B}{a}\right)$. Total energy ($B\to\infty$): $1/(2a)$. Fraction $= \frac{2}{\pi}\arctan(2\pi B/a)$. Here $2\pi B/a = 2\pi\times500/2000 = 1.5708$, $\arctan(1.5708) = 1.0039$ rad, fraction $= 0.63662 \times 1.0039 = 0.6391$, i.e. 63.91 %.
\boxed{63.91}

**出處**　Parseval's theorem and the transform pair $e^{-at}u(t) \leftrightarrow 1/(a+j2\pi f)$; A. V. Oppenheim and A. S. Willsky, *Signals and Systems*.

</details>

**3.1.7G011**　A rectangular pulse centred at $t=0$ has amplitude 5 V and duration $T = 2$ ms. With $X(f)=\int x(t)e^{-j2\pi ft}dt$ and $\mathrm{sinc}(u)=\sin(\pi u)/(\pi u)$, its transform is $X(f) = AT\,\mathrm{sinc}(fT)$. **3.1.7G013**　A distortionless delay line has a phase response that decreases linearly by 72° for every 1 MHz increase in frequency. Using the time-shift property $x(t-t_0) \leftrightarrow X(f)e^{-j2\pi f t_0}$, determine the delay $t_0$ in ns, rounded to the nearest integer.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　200

**詳解**　Phase $\phi(f) = -2\pi f t_0$ rad $= -360^\circ \times f t_0$. A slope of $-72^\circ$ per $10^6$ Hz gives $360 \times 10^6 \times t_0 = 72$, so $t_0 = 72/(360\times10^6) = 2.0\times10^{-7}$ s $= 200$ ns.
\boxed{200}

**出處**　Time-shift property of the Fourier transform; A. V. Oppenheim and A. S. Willsky, *Signals and Systems*.

</details>

**3.1.7G014**　The two-sided exponential pulse $x(t) = A\,e^{-a|t|}$ has the transform $X(f) = \dfrac{2aA}{a^2 + 4\pi^2 f^2}$ (convention $X(f)=\int x(t)e^{-j2\pi ft}dt$). For $a = 5000\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}$, determine the frequency at which the energy spectral density $|X(f)|^2$ has fallen to one half of its value at $f=0$, in Hz, rounded to one decimal place.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The single pulse is an energy signal: its energy is finite (640 µJ) and its average power over all time is zero. **詳解**　Energy signal: $0 < E = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|x|^2dt < \infty$, which forces $P = \lim_{T\to\infty} E/T = 0$. Power signal: $0 < P < \infty$, which forces $E = \infty$. A periodic signal repeats a non-zero energy per period for ever, so it is a power signal.

**詳解**　$A\cos(2\pi ft) = \tfrac{A}{2}e^{j2\pi ft} + \tfrac{A}{2}e^{-j2\pi ft}$. Parseval: $P = \sum_k |c_k|^2$ over positive and negative $k$. $8 \times 0.0625 = 0.5$ V² $= 4 \times 0.125$ V². In 50 Ω: $0.5/50 = 10$ mW, as before.

**出處**　Parseval's relation for the Fourier series; A. V. Oppenheim and A. S. Willsky, *Signals and Systems*.

</details>

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**3.1.7S018**　In hardware the pulse must be truncated to a finite length, e.g. to $|t| \le 4T_s$. Explain, using a Fourier transform property, why the truncated pulse is no longer strictly band-limited to 2 MHz.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Truncation is multiplication of $p(t)$ by a rectangular window in time. **3.1.7S024**　If the reflected path disappears and only a single path with some delay $t_0$ remains, the magnitude response becomes flat. Why does a delay alone cause no frequency-selective fading?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A single delayed path has $H(f) = e^{-j2\pi ft_0}$, whose magnitude is 1 at every frequency; the delay only adds a phase that is linear in frequency, which shifts the waveform in time without changing its shape. **3.1.7S037**　Determine the signal-to-noise ratio over the whole occupied bandwidth (273 RBs), in dB, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−5.92 dB

**詳解**　Occupied bandwidth: $273 \times 360$ kHz $= 98.28$ MHz. $10\log_{10}(98.28\times10^{6}) = 79.925$ dB-Hz. Noise: $-174 + 5 + 79.925 = -89.075$ dBm. SNR $= -95 - (-89.075) = -5.925$ dB.

**出處**　Thermal noise power $N = kTBF$; D. M. Pozar, *Microwave Engineering*.

</details>

**3.1.7S038**　A colleague expects the SNR measured in a single RB to be much higher than the wideband SNR "because one RB collects far less noise". Is that right for this scenario?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No. One RB does collect less noise (by $10\log_{10}273 = 24.36$ dB), but because the signal is spread evenly it also contains less signal by exactly the same factor. With flat signal and flat noise densities the SNR is the ratio of the two densities and is the same in any sub-band: −119.36 dBm − (−113.44 dBm) = −5.92 dB. Narrowing the bandwidth improves SNR only for a signal whose power stays inside the narrower band (e.g. a CW tone or a narrowband transmission).

**詳解**　Signal per RB: $-95 - 10\log_{10}273 = -95 - 24.362 = -119.362$ dBm. Noise per RB: $-113.437$ dBm. Difference: $-5.925$ dB, equal to the wideband value.

**出處**　Power spectral density and band power; J. G. Proakis and M. Salehi, *Communication Systems Engineering*.

</details>

**3.1.7S039**　Some textbooks write the white-noise density as $N_0/2$ and others as $N_0$. For the noise in one 360 kHz RB, how are the two used so that both give the same power?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　$N_0$ is the one-sided density, defined for positive frequencies only; the power in an RB of width $B$ is $N_0B$. $N_0/2$ is the two-sided density, defined for both positive and negative frequencies; a real band-pass signal of width $B$ then occupies $B$ around $+f_c$ and $B$ around $-f_c$, so the power is $(N_0/2)\times2B = N_0B$. The results agree as long as a two-sided density is integrated over both bands. The figure −174 dBm/Hz is the one-sided value $kT$; the two-sided value is 3.01 dB lower (−177.01 dBm/Hz).

**詳解**　One-sided: $P = N_0B$. Two-sided: $P = \int_{-f_c-B/2}^{-f_c+B/2}\frac{N_0}{2}df + \int_{f_c-B/2}^{f_c+B/2}\frac{N_0}{2}df = N_0B$. $10\log_{10}(1/2) = -3.01$ dB.

**出處**　S. Haykin, *Communication Systems* (white noise, one-sided and two-sided power spectral density).

</details>

**3.1.7S042**　When the RBW is reduced from 100 kHz to 10 kHz, the displayed level of the modulated signal drops by 10 dB but the CW tone stays at −110 dBm. Explain the different behaviour.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A CW tone is a single spectral line: all of its power passes through the RBW filter whatever its width, so the reading is the tone power. A noise-like signal (and the analyser's own noise) has power distributed over frequency; the filter passes power in proportion to its bandwidth, so a tenfold narrower RBW shows a level $10\log_{10}10 = 10$ dB lower. For such signals the meaningful quantities are the density (dBm/Hz) or the power integrated over the channel, not the raw trace level.

**詳解**　CW: $P_{disp} = P_{tone}$. Noise-like: $P_{disp} = S_0 \times \mathrm{RBW}$, so $\Delta = 10\log_{10}(10\ \mathrm{kHz}/100\ \mathrm{kHz}) = -10$ dB.

**3.1.7S043**　Narrowing the RBW lowers the displayed noise floor. What is the price paid in a swept analyser with analogue-type RBW filters, and how does it scale?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Sweep time. The RBW filter needs time to respond as the signal sweeps through it, and for a given span the minimum sweep time is proportional to span/RBW². Reducing the RBW by a factor of 10 lowers the noise floor by 10 dB but lengthens the sweep by about a factor of 100. (FFT-based analysers relax this, but the acquisition time still grows as roughly 1/RBW.)

**詳解**　Sweep time $\approx k \cdot \mathrm{span}/\mathrm{RBW}^2$, with $k$ depending on the filter shape. RBW 100 kHz → 10 kHz: noise floor $-105 \to -115$ dBm, sweep time ×100.

**出處**　Keysight Technologies, Application Note 150, *Spectrum Analysis Basics* (sweep time and resolution bandwidth).

</details>

#### 情境題：Autocorrelation and the Wiener–Khinchin relation

**Scenario | Band-pass noise at a 70 MHz IF**

Amplified receiver noise with a flat one-sided power spectral density $N_0 = 1\times10^{-14}$ W/Hz passes through an ideal unit-gain band-pass filter centred at $f_c = 70$ MHz with bandwidth $B = 2$ MHz. The noise is zero-mean and wide-sense stationary. The autocorrelation of the filter output is $R_Y(\tau) = N_0B\,\mathrm{sinc}(B\tau)\cos(2\pi f_c\tau)$ with $\mathrm{sinc}(u) = \sin(\pi u)/(\pi u)$.

**3.1.7S051**　Determine the noise power at the filter output in dBm, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−46.99 dBm

**詳解**　$P = R_Y(0) = N_0B = 1\times10^{-14} \times 2\times10^{6} = 2\times10^{-8}$ W $= 2\times10^{-5}$ mW. $10\log_{10}(2\times10^{-5}) = -46.990$ dBm.

**出處**　Autocorrelation at zero lag equals average power; S. Haykin, *Communication Systems* (narrowband noise).

</details>

**3.1.7S052**　Compute $R_Y(\tau)$ at $\tau = 0.1$ µs, in nW, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　18.71 nW

**詳解**　$B\tau = 2\times10^{6}\times1\times10^{-7} = 0.2$; $\mathrm{sinc}(0.2) = \sin(0.2\pi)/(0.2\pi) = 0.587785/0.628319 = 0.935489$. $2\pi f_c\tau = 2\pi\times70\times10^{6}\times1\times10^{-7} = 14\pi$, so $\cos(14\pi) = 1$. $R_Y = 2\times10^{-8}\times0.935489\times1 = 1.8710\times10^{-8}$ W $= 18.71$ nW.

**出處**　Wiener–Khinchin relation applied to ideal band-pass filtered white noise; S. Haykin, *Communication Systems*.

</details>

**3.1.7S053**　The filter bandwidth is widened from 2 MHz to 8 MHz. Describe what happens to the output power and to the width of the envelope of the autocorrelation, and relate the two through the Fourier transform.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The power $N_0B$ rises by a factor of 4 (+6.02 dB, to 80 nW). The envelope $\mathrm{sinc}(B\tau)$ has its first zero at $\tau = 1/B$, which shrinks from 0.5 µs to 0.125 µs. Autocorrelation and power spectral density are a Fourier pair, so a wider spectrum corresponds to a narrower autocorrelation: wideband noise decorrelates faster. In the limit of infinite bandwidth the autocorrelation tends to an impulse (white noise).

**詳解**　$N_0B = 10^{-14}\times8\times10^{6} = 8\times10^{-8}$ W; $10\log_{10}4 = 6.02$ dB. First envelope zero: $1/(2\times10^{6}) = 0.5$ µs; $1/(8\times10^{6}) = 0.125$ µs.

**出處**　Wiener–Khinchin relation and the time-scaling property; A. Papoulis and S. U. Pillai, *Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes*.

</details>

**3.1.7S054**　The Wiener–Khinchin relation was used to write $R_Y(\tau)$ as a function of the lag only. What must be true of the noise process for this to be valid, and why would the relation not apply directly to the noise during the first microseconds after the receiver is switched on?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The process must be (at least) wide-sense stationary: its mean is constant and its autocorrelation $E[Y(t)Y(t+\tau)]$ depends only on the lag $\tau$, not on the absolute time $t$. Only then is there a single function $R_Y(\tau)$ whose Fourier transform is the power spectral density. During switch-on the gain and filter state are still settling, so the statistics change with time; the autocorrelation then depends on $t$ as well as $\tau$ and a time-invariant PSD is not defined (a time-varying or averaged description is needed instead).

**詳解**　WSS: $E[Y(t)] = \mu$ and $R_Y(t, t+\tau) = R_Y(\tau)$. Wiener–Khinchin: $S_Y(f) = \int R_Y(\tau)e^{-j2\pi f\tau}d\tau$ for a WSS process.

**出處**　Wiener–Khinchin theorem; A. Papoulis and S. U. Pillai, *Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes*.

</details>

Corrected: $5\times10^{-15}\times4\times10^{6} = 2\times10^{-8}$ W $= 20$ nW, the same as $N_0B$.

**詳解**　$P = (N_0/2)\times2B = 5\times10^{-15}\times2\times2\times10^{6} = 2\times10^{-8}$ W $= 20$ nW ($-46.99$ dBm). The wrong result is 3.01 dB too low.

**出處**　S. Haykin, *Communication Systems* (white noise; one-sided and two-sided power spectral density).

</details>
