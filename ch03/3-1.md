## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What can cause non-stationary characteristics in the AG (Air to ground) channel?
>
> A. Doppler effects
> B. Scattering objects
> C. UAV velocities
> D. Communication applications
> E. Antenna characteristics

正解：C. UAV velocities

---

**【TeleMath】**

> What is the minimum sampling rate required for the musical signal a(t), which is a Gaussian signal with zero mean and a power spectral density given by $\mathcal{P}_a(f) = A \operatorname{triangle} \left( \frac{f}{10000} \right)$ in $\text{V}^2/\text{Hz}$, and express this minimum sampling frequency in Hz?

正解：20000.0

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the clipping process in the amplitude controller?
>
> A. To amplify the input signal.
> B. To reduce the amplitude of the input signal to a safe level.
> C. To convert the input signal to a different frequency.
> D. To filter the input signal.

正解：B. To reduce the amplitude of the input signal to a safe level.
