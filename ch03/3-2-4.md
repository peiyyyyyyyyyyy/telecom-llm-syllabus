# 3.2.4 編碼率與 MCS

所屬：[3.2 容量](3-2.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

實際鏈路不會直接傳未編碼的位元，而是先加上冗餘再調變。碼率（code rate）決定冗餘多寡，調變階數決定每個符號載幾個編碼位元，兩者的組合就是調變與編碼方案（modulation and coding scheme, MCS）。5G NR 的基地台每次排程都會依終端回報的通道品質選一個 MCS 索引，寫在下行控制資訊裡；終端查表得到調變階數與目標碼率，再算出傳輸區塊大小。會讀 MCS 表，才能從排程紀錄或路測資料估算速率、判斷鏈路調適是否合理，也才能理解為什麼通道變差時速率是一階一階往下掉。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $k$ | 編碼前的資訊位元數 | bit |
| $n$ | 編碼後的位元數 | bit |
| $R$ | 碼率，$R=k/n$（3GPP 表中以 $R\times1024$ 表示） | 無單位（0～1） |
| $Q_m$ | 調變階數，每個調變符號的位元數（QPSK 2、16QAM 4、64QAM 6、256QAM 8） | bit/符號 |
| $I_{MCS}$ | MCS 索引（0～31） | 無單位 |
| $\upsilon$ | 傳輸層數 | 無單位 |
| $N_{RE}$ | 分配給資料通道的資源單元（resource element, RE）數 | 個 |
| $n_{PRB}$ | 分配的實體資源區塊數 | 個 |
| $N_{info}$ | 未量化的資訊位元數中間值 | bit |

#### 公式與推導

碼率是資訊位元占編碼後位元的比例：

$$R = \frac{k}{n}$$

$R$ 越小冗餘越多、越耐雜訊，但淨速率越低。每個調變符號載 $Q_m$ 個編碼位元，其中只有 $R$ 的比例是資訊，所以單層的頻譜效率（每個資源單元的資訊位元數）為

$$\text{頻譜效率} = Q_m\times R = Q_m\times\frac{(R\times1024)}{1024}\quad[\text{bit/符號}]$$

這裡沒有計入循環字首、參考訊號等開銷，是 MCS 表「Spectral efficiency」欄的定義。

TS 38.214 第 5.1.3.1 節為下行資料通道（PDSCH）定義了數張 MCS 表。下面摘錄表 1（最高 64QAM）與表 2（最高 256QAM）的部分列：

| 表 | $I_{MCS}$ | $Q_m$ | 目標碼率 $R\times1024$ | 頻譜效率 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 表 1（Table 5.1.3.1-1） | 0 | 2 | 120 | 0.2344 |
| 表 1 | 9 | 2 | 679 | 1.3262 |
| 表 1 | 10 | 4 | 340 | 1.3281 |
| 表 1 | 16 | 4 | 658 | 2.5703 |
| 表 1 | 17 | 6 | 438 | 2.5664 |
| 表 1 | 28 | 6 | 948 | 5.5547 |
| 表 2（Table 5.1.3.1-2） | 0 | 2 | 120 | 0.2344 |
| 表 2 | 4 | 2 | 602 | 1.1758 |
| 表 2 | 5 | 4 | 378 | 1.4766 |
| 表 2 | 10 | 4 | 658 | 2.5703 |
| 表 2 | 11 | 6 | 466 | 2.7305 |
| 表 2 | 19 | 6 | 873 | 5.1152 |
| 表 2 | 20 | 8 | 682.5 | 5.3320 |
| 表 2 | 27 | 8 | 948 | 7.4063 |

表 1 的索引 29～31、表 2 的索引 28～31 是保留值，只指示調變階數，沒有目標碼率。兩張表的最高碼率都是 $948/1024 = 0.9258$。

**用哪一張表不是固定的**，而是由上層參數與排程條件決定：例如 PDSCH-Config 裡的 mcs-Table 設為 'qam256' 且由特定下行控制資訊格式、以 C-RNTI 排程時用表 2；設為 'qam64LowSE'（或使用 MCS-C-RNTI）時用低頻譜效率的表 3；設定 1024QAM 的參數時用表 4；都不符合時用表 1。所以同一個索引在不同表代表不同的調變與碼率，讀紀錄時要先確認用的是哪張表。

查到 $Q_m$ 與 $R$ 後，TS 38.214 第 5.1.3.2 節先算資源單元數，再算資訊位元數的中間值：

$$N'_{RE} = 12\times N^{sh}_{symb} - N^{PRB}_{DMRS} - N^{PRB}_{oh},\qquad N_{RE} = \min(156,\,N'_{RE})\times n_{PRB}$$

$$N_{info} = N_{RE}\times R\times Q_m\times\upsilon$$

其中 $N^{sh}_{symb}$ 是時槽內分配給 PDSCH 的符號數，$N^{PRB}_{DMRS}$ 是每個資源區塊裡解調參考訊號占的資源單元數，$N^{PRB}_{oh}$ 是上層設定的額外開銷（未設定時為 0）。$N_{info}$ 還要經過量化與查表才是最後的傳輸區塊大小，但用它估算速率已經夠準。

#### 算例

**算例 1（調變切換點）** 比較表 1 的索引 16 與 17。

1. 索引 16：$Q_m=4$，$R = 658/1024 = 0.6426$，頻譜效率 $4\times0.6426 = 2.5703$。
2. 索引 17：$Q_m=6$，$R = 438/1024 = 0.4277$，頻譜效率 $6\times0.4277 = 2.5664$。
3. 兩者效率幾乎相同，索引 17 甚至略低一點。這是調變切換點：16QAM 配較高碼率，與 64QAM 配較低碼率，承載的資訊量相近。
4. 合理性檢查：計算值與表中「頻譜效率」欄一致。索引變大不一定代表碼率變大，要看 $Q_m\times R$。

**算例 2（估算一個時槽的資訊位元數）** 假設子載波間隔 30 kHz（時槽 0.5 ms），分配 50 個資源區塊、整個時槽 14 個符號，每個資源區塊的解調參考訊號占 12 個資源單元（假設），未設定額外開銷，單層傳輸，使用表 1 的索引 20（$Q_m=6$，$R\times1024=567$，頻譜效率 3.3223）。

1. $N'_{RE} = 12\times14-12-0 = 156$；$\min(156,156) = 156$。
2. $N_{RE} = 156\times50 = 7{,}800$ 個。
3. 編碼後可承載的位元數：$7{,}800\times6 = 46{,}800$ bit。
4. $N_{info} = 7{,}800\times\dfrac{567}{1024}\times6\times1 = 25{,}914$ bit（四捨五入到整數）。
5. 若每個時槽都這樣排程，速率約 $25{,}914/(0.5\times10^{-3}) = 5.18\times10^{7}$ bit/s，約 51.8 Mbps。
6. 合理性檢查：$25{,}914/46{,}800 = 0.5537$，正好是碼率 $567/1024$；結果是未量化的估算值，實際傳輸區塊大小會落在它附近，而且 TDD 系統不是每個時槽都是下行。

#### 常見錯誤

- 把表中的 $R\times1024$ 直接當碼率用。948 代表 $948/1024=0.9258$，不是 0.948，更不是 948。
- 沒有確認用的是哪一張 MCS 表就查索引。索引 20 在表 1 是 64QAM、在表 2 是 256QAM。
- 把頻譜效率欄的數值當成已含 MIMO。它是單層的數值，多層要再乘層數。
- 把 $N_{info}$ 當成精確的傳輸區塊大小。它是量化前的中間值。
- 以為索引越高碼率一定越高；在調變切換點，碼率會先降下來。

#### 和其他小節的關係

調變階數與錯誤率的關係在 3.2.3；$Q_m\times R$ 就是 3.2.2 的鏈路頻譜效率，可和 3.2.1 的 Shannon 上限比較；最高碼率 $948/1024$ 會出現在 3.2.5 的峰值速率公式；資源單元的數量來自 3.2.5 的資源格；碼率與錯誤更正能力的理論在 3.7 細項。

**Scenario | Fitting a transport block into an allocation**

A gNB must send a transport block of 2856 bits; with a 24-bit CRC attached (CRC length taken as given) the channel encoder receives 2880 bits. <details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　0.6000

**詳解**　R = 2880 / 4800 = 0.6000.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, clause 5.1.3 (effective channel code rate); S. Lin and D. J. Costello, *Error Control Coding* (code rate).

</details>

**3.2.4S002**　Express this code rate in the "R × 1024" form used by the MCS tables, and determine how far (in the same ×1024 units) it lies above the target rate of Table 5.1.3.1-1 index 8. Round to one decimal place.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　R × 1024 = 614.4, which is 12.4 above index 8 (602)

**詳解**　Step 1: 0.6000 × 1024 = 614.4.
Step 2: 614.4 − 602 = 12.4. (It is 64.6 below index 9, 679.)

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, Table 5.1.3.1-1.

</details>

**3.2.4S003**　If the gNB were forced to send the same 2880 bits with a lower code rate, what would that buy and what would it cost?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A lower code rate means more parity bits per information bit, so the decoder can correct more errors: the same block error rate is reached at a lower SINR (a more robust link). The cost is resources: for the same number of information bits more coded bits, and therefore more resource elements, are needed, which lowers the spectral efficiency (information bits per RE = Qm × R).

**詳解**　For example, R = 0.3 would need 2880/0.3 = 9600 coded bits = 4800 QPSK REs, twice the present allocation.

**出處**　S. Lin and D. J. Costello, *Error Control Coding* (code rate versus error-correcting capability).

</details>

**3.2.4S004**　Why does the scheduler not simply shrink the allocation until the code rate approaches 1?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A code rate near 1 leaves almost no redundancy, so the decoder cannot correct errors and the block error rate becomes very high. TS 38.214 even states that the UE may skip decoding a transport block in an initial transmission if the effective channel code rate (information bits including CRC divided by physical channel bits) is higher than 0.95. The highest target code rate in the PDSCH MCS tables is 948/1024 = 0.9258.

**詳解**　Clause 5.1.3 of TS 38.214 V18.11.0 defines the 0.95 threshold; Table 5.1.3.1-1 tops out at R × 1024 = 948.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, clause 5.1.3 and Table 5.1.3.1-1.

</details>

**3.2.4S009**　At such low spectral efficiencies, roughly how much SNR is saved by halving the code rate, and why is it different at high spectral efficiency?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　About 3 dB. At low SNR the Shannon capacity log2(1 + SNR) is nearly proportional to SNR (power-limited regime), so halving the information bits per RE roughly halves the SNR needed. At high spectral efficiency (bandwidth-limited regime) capacity grows only logarithmically, and removing a fraction of the bits per RE saves several dB per bit/RE rather than a fixed 3 dB per halving.

**詳解**　log2(1 + x) ≈ x/ln 2 for x ≪ 1: η = 0.0977 → SNR ≈ 2^0.0977 − 1 = 0.0700; η = 0.0488 → 0.0344; ratio 2.03 (3.08 dB).

**出處**　C. E. Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication"; J. G. Proakis and M. Salehi, *Digital Communications* (power-limited and bandwidth-limited regimes).

**3.2.4S052**　Why must the rates (or spectral efficiencies) be averaged, rather than the MCS indices?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The MCS index is a label, not a quantity: the spectral efficiency is not a linear function of the index (steps are uneven, and at switching points the efficiency barely changes between neighbouring indices). Averaging indices and then looking up a row gives an entry that does not represent the mix. Averaging the bits per slot (or Qm × R) weighted by time share gives the correct mean.

**詳解**　Steps in Table 5.1.3.1-1: index 16 → 17 changes the efficiency by −0.004 bit/RE, index 22 → 23 by +0.311 bit/RE.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　About 10 % of the first transmissions fail and must be retransmitted, so the useful (first-transmission) throughput is roughly 90 % of the offered rate, about 113.5 Mbit/s. A non-zero BLER target is chosen because HARQ recovers most failed blocks cheaply, and aiming for, say, 0.1 % would force a much lower MCS in every slot, costing more throughput than the occasional retransmission.

**詳解**　0.9 × 126.14 = 113.53 Mbit/s.

**Scenario | A simple link-adaptation rule**

A lab scheduler for a UE using 3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, Table 5.1.3.1-2 applies a simple rule (an assumption for this exercise, not a 3GPP procedure): pick the highest MCS index whose single-layer spectral efficiency Qm × R does not exceed 75 % of the AWGN Shannon capacity log2(1 + SINR), with one RE treated as one channel use. The measured SINR is 15 dB. Relevant rows of Table 5.1.3.1-2: index 10 (Qm = 4, R × 1024 = 658), 11 (6, 466), 12 (6, 517), 13 (6, 567), 14 (6, 616), 15 (6, 666).

**3.2.4S056**　Which MCS index does the rule select?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　MCS index 14 (Qm = 6, R × 1024 = 616)

**詳解**　Step 1: SINR = 10^1.5 = 31.623; capacity = log2(32.623) = 5.0278 bit/RE.
Step 2: 75 % of it = 3.7709 bit/RE.
Step 3: Qm × R: index 14 = 6 × 616/1024 = 3.6094 (≤ 3.7709); index 15 = 6 × 666/1024 = 3.9023 (> 3.7709). Highest allowed: index 14.

**3.2.4S057**　Why does a practical scheduler back off from the Shannon capacity rather than choose the MCS whose spectral efficiency equals log2(1 + SINR)?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Shannon capacity assumes infinitely long ideal codes, Gaussian signalling and perfect channel knowledge. Real LDPC codes of finite block length, QAM constellations, channel-estimation errors, the DM-RS and the need to meet a target BLER (around 10 %) all leave a gap of several dB. Choosing the MCS at the capacity limit would produce a BLER close to 100 %.

**詳解**　The 0.75 factor corresponds here to about 3.7709 vs 5.0278 bit/RE.

**出處**　C. E. Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication"; A. Goldsmith, *Wireless Communications* (practical adaptive modulation gap to capacity).

The modulation stays 64QAM; only the code rate drops. A change of modulation occurs only when the limit crosses a switching point (below index 11 in this table, 16QAM is used).

**詳解**　10^1.2 = 15.849; log2(16.849) = 4.0746; × 0.75 = 3.0560; index 12 = 3.0293 ≤ 3.0560 < index 13 = 3.3223.

**3.2.4S060**　Another student computes the capacity as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct MCS index?
1. SINR = 10^1.5 = 31.623.

**Scenario | MCS for system information**

A gNB broadcasts system information on a PDSCH scheduled by DCI format 1_0 with CRC scrambled by SI-RNTI. No condition for another table applies, so 3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, Table 5.1.3.1-1 is used. The PDSCH spans 10 symbols on 48 PRBs with 12 DM-RS RE per PRB and one layer. For SI-RNTI the overhead N_oh is assumed to be 0. Use $N'_{RE} = 12 \cdot N^{sh}_{symb} - N^{PRB}_{DMRS} - N^{PRB}_{oh}$, $N_{RE} = N'_{RE} \cdot n_{PRB}$ and $N_{info} = N_{RE} \cdot R \cdot Q_m \cdot \upsilon$. Relevant rows of Table 5.1.3.1-1: index 6 (Qm = 2, R × 1024 = 449), index 9 (Qm = 2, R × 1024 = 679), index 10 (Qm = 4, R × 1024 = 340).

**3.2.4S061**　What is the unquantized Ninfo with MCS index 6, rounded to the nearest integer?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4546 bits

**詳解**　Step 1: N'RE = 12 × 10 − 12 − 0 = 108.
Step 2: NRE = 108 × 48 = 5184.
Step 3: Ninfo = 5184 × 449/1024 × 2 = 5184 × 0.438477 × 2 = 4546.13 ≈ 4546 bits.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, Table 5.1.3.1-1 and clause 5.1.3.2.

**3.2.4S062**　Why are system-information PDSCHs limited to QPSK, and where is this stated?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　System information must be decodable by every UE in the cell, including those at the cell edge and those that have not yet reported any channel quality, so the most robust modulation is used. TS 38.214 clause 5.1.3.1 states that the UE is not expected to decode a PDSCH scheduled with P-RNTI, RA-RNTI or SI-RNTI with Qm > 2.

**詳解**　Clause 5.1.3.1, last sentence before Table 5.1.3.1-1.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, clause 5.1.3.1.

</details>

**3.2.4S063**　Why is N_oh taken as 0 for SI-RNTI even if the cell configures xOverhead for its connected UEs?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　xOverhead is a UE-specific parameter in PDSCH-ServingCellConfig. A broadcast PDSCH is received by UEs that may not have (or not yet have) that configuration, so all receivers must compute the same TBS from information in the DCI alone. TS 38.214 therefore assumes N_oh = 0 for PDSCH scheduled with SI-RNTI, RA-RNTI, MSGB-RNTI or P-RNTI.

**詳解**　Clause 5.1.3.2: "If the PDSCH is scheduled by PDCCH with a CRC scrambled by SI-RNTI, RA-RNTI, MSGB-RNTI or P-RNTI, N_oh is assumed to be 0."

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.214 V18.11.0, clause 5.1.3.2.

</details>
