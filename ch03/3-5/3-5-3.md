## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following definitions correctly describes the bias of an estimator $\hat\theta$?

- A. $\text{Bias}(\hat\theta)=\mathbb{E}[\hat\theta]-\theta$
- B. $\text{Bias}(\hat\theta)=\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta)$
- C. $\text{Bias}(\hat\theta)=\sqrt{\mathbb{E}\!\big[(\hat\theta-\theta)^{2}\big]}$
- D. $\text{Bias}(\hat\theta)=\hat\theta-\theta$ (after the data are observed)

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。By definition bias is the difference between the estimator’s expected value and the true parameter.

</details>

**2.** For i.i.d. Gaussian samples $x_i\sim\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$ with known variance $\sigma^{2}$, the Cramér–Rao lower bound (CRLB) for estimating the mean $\mu$ is

- A. $\displaystyle \frac{\sigma^{2}}{N}$
- B. $\displaystyle \sigma^{2}$
- C. $\displaystyle \frac{N}{\sigma^{2}}$
- D. $\displaystyle \frac{1}{\sigma^{2}N}$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The Fisher information for the mean is $N/\sigma^{2}$, so $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\mu)\ge 1/(N/\sigma^{2})=\sigma^{2}/N$.

</details>

**3.** You collect $N=25$ independent samples from a Gaussian distribution with unknown mean $\mu$ and known variance $\sigma^{2}=9\;\text{V}^{2}$. What is the variance (in V$^2$) of the unbiased sample‑mean estimator $\hat\mu=\frac{1}{N}\sum x_i$?


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

The variance of the sample mean for i.i.d. Gaussian data is  
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\mu)=\frac{\sigma^{2}}{N}.
$$  
Insert the given numbers:  
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\mu)=\frac{9\;\text{V}^{2}}{25}=0.36\;\text{V}^{2}.
$$  
$\boxed{0.36}$

</details>

**4.** Eight i.i.d. observations from an exponential distribution are $\{0.5,\,0.8,\,0.3,\,0.6,\,0.9,\,0.4,\,0.7,\,0.2\}$ (units: seconds). Using the method of moments, estimate the rate parameter $\lambda$ (units: s$^{-1}$).


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

For an exponential distribution the theoretical mean is $1/\lambda$.  
1. Compute the sample mean:  
$$
\bar{x}= \frac{0.5+0.8+0.3+0.6+0.9+0.4+0.7+0.2}{8}
      = \frac{4.4}{8}=0.55\ \text{s}.
$$  
2. Equate the sample mean to the theoretical mean: $ \bar{x}=1/\lambda$.  
3. Solve for $\lambda$: $\lambda = 1/\bar{x}=1/0.55\approx1.8181818\ \text{s}^{-1}$.  
$\boxed{1.8181818}$

</details>
