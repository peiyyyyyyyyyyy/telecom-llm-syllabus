## 練習題

**1.** What condition on the load impedance maximises the real power transferred from a source with internal impedance $Z_{\mathrm{s}}$?

- A. $Z_{\mathrm{l}} = Z_{\mathrm{s}}$
- B. $Z_{\mathrm{l}} = Z_{\mathrm{s}}^{*}$
- C. $|Z_{\mathrm{l}}| = |Z_{\mathrm{s}}|$
- D. $Z_{\mathrm{l}} = 1/Z_{\mathrm{s}}$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Maximum power transfer requires the load to be the complex conjugate of the source impedance, cancelling the reactive part and equalising resistances.

</details>

**2.** For a loss‑less transmission line, the characteristic impedance $Z_{0}$ is given by which expression?

- A. $Z_{0}= \sqrt{L C}$
- B. $Z_{0}= \sqrt{\dfrac{L}{C}}$
- C. $Z_{0}= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{L C}}$
- D. $Z_{0}= \dfrac{L}{C}$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。In a TEM line $Z_{0}= \sqrt{L/C}$; the product $LC$ gives the propagation velocity, not the impedance.

</details>

**3.** A sinusoidal source delivers $V_{\text{s}} = 10\;\text{V}_{\text{rms}}$ into a source resistance $R_{\mathrm{s}} = 50\;\Omega$. The load is a purely resistive $R_{\mathrm{l}} = 200\;\Omega$. Compute the average power dissipated in the load (in watts) without any matching network.


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

Total series resistance $R_{\mathrm{tot}} = R_{\mathrm{s}} + R_{\mathrm{l}} = 250\;\Omega$.  
Load voltage $V_{\mathrm{l}} = V_{\text{s}}\frac{R_{\mathrm{l}}}{R_{\mathrm{tot}}}=10\frac{200}{250}=8\;\text{V}_{\text{rms}}$.  
Power $P = V_{\mathrm{l}}^{2}/R_{\mathrm{l}} = 8^{2}/200 = 64/200 = 0.32\;\text{W}$.  
$\boxed{0.32}$

</details>

**4.** A coaxial cable has an attenuation constant $\alpha = 0.017\;\text{Np/m}$. The line length is $L = 150\;\text{m}$. If the input power is $P_{\text{in}} = 1\;\text{W}$, what is the output power at the far end (in watts)?


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

Power decays as $P_{\text{out}} = P_{\text{in}} e^{-2\alpha L}$.  
Compute exponent: $2\alpha L = 2 \times 0.017 \times 150 = 5.1$.  
Thus $P_{\text{out}} = 1 \times e^{-5.1}$.  
$e^{-5.1} \approx 0.006074$.  
$\boxed{0.0061}$

</details>
