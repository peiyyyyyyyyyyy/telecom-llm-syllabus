# 3.2.6 MIMO 容量

所屬：[3.2 容量](../3-2.md)

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following expressions correctly gives the capacity (bits s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$) of a narrow‑band $N_t\times N_r$ MIMO channel with total transmit power $P$, noise variance $\sigma^2$, and equal power allocation across the transmit antennas?

- A. $C = \log_2\!\det\!\bigl(\mathbf I_{N_r} + \frac{P}{\sigma^2}\,\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}\bigr)$
- B. $C = \log_2\!\det\!\bigl(\mathbf I_{N_r} + \frac{P}{N_t\sigma^2}\,\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}\bigr)$
- C. $C = N_t\,\log_2\!\bigl(1+\frac{P}{N_t\sigma^2}\bigr)$
- D. $C = \sum_{i=1}^{\min(N_t,N_r)}\log_2\!\bigl(1+\frac{P}{\sigma^2}\lambda_i\bigr)$

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The equal‑power constraint divides the total SNR $\rho=P/\sigma^2$ by $N_t$, giving the factor $\frac{P}{N_t\sigma^2}$ inside the determinant.

</details>

**2.** For a $4\times4$ i.i.d. Rayleigh MIMO channel at asymptotically high SNR, the multiplexing (spatial‑degrees‑of‑freedom) gain equals

- A. 1
- B. 2
- C. 3
- D. 4

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。The maximum number of independent data streams equals $\min(N_t,N_r)=4$.

</details>

**3.** Which statement about water‑filling power allocation in a MIMO system with full CSI at the transmitter is true?

- A. Water‑filling always allocates the same power to every eigen‑mode, regardless of channel gains.
- B. Water‑filling concentrates all power on the strongest eigen‑mode and turns off the others.
- C. Water‑filling distributes power inversely proportional to the eigen‑values of $\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}$.
- D. Water‑filling allocates more power to eigen‑modes with larger eigen‑values, but still uses all modes unless a mode is extremely weak.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。Water‑filling raises a water level over the inverse of each eigen‑value; stronger modes receive more power, weaker ones may be turned off only if their inverse exceeds the water level.

</details>

**4.** Compute the MIMO capacity (bits s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$) for a $2\times2$ system with $P=5\,$W, $\sigma^2=0.5\,$W, and channel matrix $\mathbf H=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$.


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. SNR: $\rho = P/\sigma^2 = 5/0.5 = 10$.  
2. Per‑antenna factor: $\rho/N_t = 10/2 = 5$.  
3. $\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}= \begin{bmatrix}4&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$.  
4. Inside determinant: $\mathbf I_2 + 5\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}= \begin{bmatrix}1+5\cdot4 & 0\\0 & 1+5\cdot1\end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix}21&0\\0&6\end{bmatrix}$.  
5. Determinant: $21\times6 =126$.  
6. Capacity: $C=\log_2(126) \approx 6.97\,$bits/s/Hz.  
$\boxed{6.97}$

</details>

**5.** A $3\times2$ MIMO link has total transmit power $P=2\,$W, noise variance $\sigma^2=0.2\,$W, and channel matrix  
$$
\mathbf H=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0.5 & -0.3\\
0.2 & -1 & 0.4
\end{bmatrix}.
$$  
Compute the capacity (bits s$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$).


<details><summary>看答案</summary>

詳解（式號指本頁正文的公式）：

1. $\rho = P/\sigma^2 = 2/0.2 = 10$.  
2. $\rho/N_t = 10/3 \approx 3.3333$.  
3. Compute $\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}$:  
   * Row 1·Row 1$^{\!*}$ = $1^2+0.5^2+(-0.3)^2 = 1.34$.  
   * Row 1·Row 2$^{\!*}$ = $1\cdot0.2 + 0.5\cdot(-1) + (-0.3)\cdot0.4 = -0.42$.  
   * Row 2·Row 2$^{\!*}$ = $0.2^2+(-1)^2+0.4^2 = 1.20$.  
   Hence $\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}= \begin{bmatrix}1.34 & -0.42\\ -0.42 & 1.20\end{bmatrix}$.  
4. Multiply by $\rho/N_t$: $3.3333\!\times\!\mathbf H\mathbf H^{\dagger}= \begin{bmatrix}4.4667 & -1.4\\ -1.4 & 4.0\end{bmatrix}$.  
5. Add identity: $\mathbf I_2 + \dots = \begin{bmatrix}5.4667 & -1.4\\ -1.4 & 5.0\end{bmatrix}$.  
6. Determinant: $5.4667\times5.0 - (-1.4)^2 = 27.3335 - 1.96 = 25.3735$.  
7. Capacity: $C = \log_2(25.3735) \approx 4.66\,$bits/s/Hz.  
$\boxed{4.66}$

</details>
