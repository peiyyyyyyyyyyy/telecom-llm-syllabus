# 3.1.4 多路徑與衰落通道

所屬：[3.1 訊號與通道](3-1.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

無線訊號會經由反射、繞射、散射從許多條路徑到達接收端，各路徑的延遲和相位不同，疊加後有時相長、有時相消；手機只要移動半個波長左右，接收功率就可能變化二、三十 dB。這種小尺度衰落（small-scale fading）決定了通道在時間上多久變一次、在頻率上多寬以內可以視為平坦，進而影響 OFDM 的子載波間隔與循環字首長度、參考訊號要放多密、通道狀態回報多久一次，以及鏈路預算要留多少快衰落餘量。判斷一個通道是 Rayleigh 還是 Rician、是平坦衰落還是頻率選擇性衰落，是讀通道模型與分析效能的基本功。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $v$ | 終端相對於基地臺的移動速度 | m/s |
| $f_c$、$\lambda$ | 載波頻率、波長 | Hz、m |
| $\theta$ | 來波方向與移動方向的夾角 | 度或弧度 |
| $f_d$、$f_m$ | 都卜勒頻移、最大都卜勒頻移 | Hz |
| $T_c$ | 同調時間（coherence time） | s |
| $\tau_k$、$P_k$ | 第 $k$ 條路徑的額外延遲、功率（線性值） | s、W（或相對值） |
| $\bar\tau$ | 平均額外延遲（mean excess delay） | s |
| $\sigma_\tau$ | 方均根延遲擴散（RMS delay spread） | s |
| $B_c$ | 同調頻寬（coherence bandwidth） | Hz |
| $B_s$、$T_s$ | 訊號頻寬、符號時間 | Hz、s |
| $K$ | Rician $K$ 因子（直射路徑功率÷散射路徑總功率） | 無因次或 dB |
| $\bar\gamma$ | 平均接收 SNR（線性值） | 無因次 |

#### 公式與推導

**都卜勒頻移**（Doppler shift）。終端以速度 $v$ 移動，來波與移動方向夾角 $\theta$ 時，接收頻率的偏移量為

$$f_d = \frac{v}{\lambda}\cos\theta = \frac{v f_c}{c}\cos\theta,\qquad f_m = \frac{v f_c}{c}$$

多路徑從各個方向來，頻移分布在 $\pm f_m$ 之間，這個寬度稱為都卜勒擴散。

**同調時間**是通道響應大致不變的時間長度，和 $f_m$ 成反比。常見的幾種估計式差在「多相關才算不變」：最寬鬆的是 $T_c \approx 1/f_m$；以時間相關係數高於 0.5 為準是 $T_c \approx 9/(16\pi f_m) = 0.179/f_m$；工程上常取兩者的幾何平均

$$T_c \approx \sqrt{\frac{9}{16\pi f_m^2}} = \frac{0.423}{f_m}$$

**延遲擴散**。由功率延遲剖面（power delay profile）的各路徑 $(\tau_k, P_k)$ 計算，$P_k$ 要用線性值：

$$\bar\tau = \frac{\sum_k P_k\tau_k}{\sum_k P_k},\qquad \overline{\tau^2} = \frac{\sum_k P_k\tau_k^2}{\sum_k P_k},\qquad \sigma_\tau = \sqrt{\overline{\tau^2} - \bar\tau^{\,2}}$$

**同調頻寬**是通道頻率響應大致相同的頻率範圍，和 $\sigma_\tau$ 成反比。以頻率相關係數高於 0.9 為準時 $B_c \approx 1/(50\sigma_\tau)$；放寬到 0.5 時 $B_c \approx 1/(5\sigma_\tau)$。這些都是經驗近似式，用的時候要說明採用哪一個定義。

**通道分類**用訊號參數和通道參數相比：

| 比較 | 結果 |
|---|---|
| $B_s \ll B_c$（等價於 $T_s \gg \sigma_\tau$） | 平坦衰落（flat fading） |
| $B_s > B_c$ | 頻率選擇性衰落，會產生符號間干擾 |
| $T_s \ll T_c$ | 慢衰落（slow fading） |
| $T_s > T_c$ | 快衰落（fast fading） |

**Rayleigh 與 Rician**。沒有主導路徑、只有大量強度相近的散射路徑時（典型的非視距環境），接收訊號的包絡服從 Rayleigh 分布，瞬時功率（或瞬時 SNR $\gamma$）服從指數分布，所以瞬時 SNR 低於門檻 $\gamma_{\text{th}}$ 的機率是

$$\Pr[\gamma < \gamma_{\text{th}}] = 1 - e^{-\gamma_{\text{th}}/\bar\gamma}$$

留 10 dB 餘量（$\gamma_{\text{th}}/\bar\gamma = 0.1$）時中斷機率是 $1 - e^{-0.1} = 9.5\%$；留 20 dB 是 $1 - e^{-0.01} = 1.0\%$。也就是 Rayleigh 通道下每多 10 dB 餘量，中斷機率大約只降 10 倍，這是需要分集技術的原因。

有一條明顯的直射或強反射路徑時（視距環境），包絡服從 Rician 分布，用 $K$ 因子描述直射路徑功率和散射功率總和的比值。$K = 0$（$-\infty$ dB）退化成 Rayleigh；$K$ 越大衰落越淺，$K \to \infty$ 時就是沒有衰落的固定通道。

#### 算例

**算例 1：都卜勒頻移與同調時間**

已知：載波 3.5 GHz，車速 120 km/h，光速取 $299{,}792{,}458$ m/s。求最大都卜勒頻移與同調時間（用 $0.423/f_m$），並和子載波間隔 30 kHz 的 NR 時槽長度比較。

- 換單位：$v = 120/3.6 = 33.33$ m/s。
- $f_m = 33.33\times3.5\times10^9/299{,}792{,}458 = 389.2$ Hz。
- $T_c = 0.423/389.2 = 1.087\times10^{-3}$ s，約 1.09 ms。
- 依 3GPP TS 38.211 表 4.3.2-1，子載波間隔 30 kHz（$\mu = 1$）時每個 1 ms 子訊框有 2 個時槽，一個時槽 0.5 ms。同調時間約為 $1.087/0.5 = 2.2$ 個時槽。
- 合理性檢查：如果改成步行 3 km/h，速度是 1/40，$f_m = 9.73$ Hz，$T_c = 43.5$ ms，約 87 個時槽。高速時通道在兩、三個時槽內就變了，通道回報容易過時，一個時槽內也要放比較多的解調參考訊號；步行時通道幾乎靜止。量級和直覺一致。

**算例 2：延遲擴散與同調頻寬**

已知（假設的三路徑功率延遲剖面）：延遲 0 ns、200 ns、500 ns，相對功率 0 dB、−3 dB、−10 dB。求 $\sigma_\tau$ 與同調頻寬。

- 功率換線性：1、0.5012、0.1，總和 1.6012。
- $\bar\tau = (0 + 0.5012\times200 + 0.1\times500)/1.6012 = 150.24/1.6012 = 93.83$ ns。
- $\overline{\tau^2} = (0.5012\times200^2 + 0.1\times500^2)/1.6012 = 45047.5/1.6012 = 28133.8$ ns²。
- $\sigma_\tau = \sqrt{28133.8 - 93.83^2} = \sqrt{28133.8 - 8803.8} = \sqrt{19330.0} = 139.0$ ns。
- $B_c \approx 1/(5\times139.0\times10^{-9}) = 1.44$ MHz（相關 0.5）；$B_c \approx 1/(50\times139.0\times10^{-9}) = 143.9$ kHz（相關 0.9）。
- 判讀與檢查：單一個 30 kHz 子載波遠小於 143.9 kHz，每個子載波看到的是平坦衰落；整個 20 MHz 載波遠大於 1.44 MHz，整體是頻率選擇性衰落——這正是 OFDM 把寬頻切成許多窄子載波的理由。$\sigma_\tau$ 落在最大延遲 500 ns 以內，量級合理。

#### 常見錯誤

- **速度沒有換成 m/s**：km/h 要除以 3.6，否則 $f_m$ 會差 3.6 倍。
- **延遲擴散用 dB 值加權**：$P_k$ 一定要先換成線性值，而且 $\sigma_\tau$ 要減掉平均延遲的平方。
- **把同調時間和同調頻寬配錯對象**：同調時間由都卜勒擴散（移動速度）決定，同調頻寬由延遲擴散（環境大小）決定，兩者互相獨立。
- **「快衰落」和「頻率選擇性」混為一談**：前者比的是 $T_s$ 與 $T_c$，後者比的是 $B_s$ 與 $B_c$；另外 $0.423/f_m$、$1/(5\sigma_\tau)$ 都是近似式，不是嚴格等式。

#### 和其他小節的關係

3.1.3 處理大尺度的平均損耗與陰影，本節處理疊加在上面的小尺度衰落；兩者的餘量在 3.3.3 分開列。同調頻寬與延遲擴散決定 3.2.5 的子載波間隔與循環字首長度；Rayleigh 通道下的錯誤率與分集增益見 3.2.3、3.2.6；瞬時 SNR 的指數分布用到 3.5.2 的機率基礎，延遲擴散與頻率響應的對應關係則是 3.1.7 傅立葉轉換的應用。

#### 單題：Maximum Doppler shift

**3.1.4G001**　A vehicle travels at 120 km/h directly toward a base station that transmits at a carrier frequency of 2.1 GHz. Taking c = 3e8 m/s, compute the maximum Doppler shift $f_D = v f_c / c$ in Hz, rounded to two decimal places.

**答案**　233.33

**詳解**　$v = 120/3.6 = 33.3333$ m/s.
$f_D = v f_c / c = 33.3333 \times 2.1\times10^{9} / (3\times10^{8}) = 33.3333 \times 7 = 233.3333$ Hz, which rounds to 233.33 Hz.
\boxed{233.33}

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**3.1.4G004**　A handset moving at 90 km/h receives a 1.8 GHz signal over exactly two paths: one arriving from straight ahead (0° relative to the direction of motion) and one arriving at 120° relative to the direction of motion. Define the Doppler spread here as the difference between the largest and the smallest Doppler shift among the paths. With c = 3e8 m/s, find this Doppler spread in Hz, rounded to one decimal place.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

\boxed{225.0}

**3.1.4G011**　A two-path channel has a normalized linear power of 0.6 at an excess delay of 0 ns and 0.4 at an excess delay of 500 ns. Compute the RMS delay spread $\sigma_\tau = \sqrt{\overline{\tau^2} - \bar{\tau}^2}$ in ns, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　244.95

**詳解**　Total power $= 0.6 + 0.4 = 1$.

$\sigma_\tau = \sqrt{100000 - 200^2} = \sqrt{60000} = 244.949$ ns, which rounds to 244.95 ns.
\boxed{244.95}

**3.1.4S008**　Suppose both cars drive in the same direction at exactly the same speed. The LOS path then has zero Doppler shift. Does this mean the channel between them is not time-varying?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No. The LOS component has no shift because the distance between the cars is constant, but paths reflected from stationary roadside objects change length as both cars move, so they carry non-zero and mutually different Doppler shifts. The channel therefore still has a Doppler spread and still fades in time.

**詳解**　The Doppler shift of each path equals the rate of change of that path's length divided by the wavelength. Only the LOS path length is constant here; the scattered paths via fixed objects shorten or lengthen as the cars pass, giving a spread of Doppler frequencies and hence a finite coherence time.

**出處**　A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, chapter on statistical multipath channel models (time-varying channel impulse response, Doppler spread).

**3.1.4S009**　Explain the difference between a Doppler shift and a Doppler spread, and state which of them a receiver can remove with a single carrier-frequency-offset correction.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　A Doppler shift is one common frequency offset on a single path (or on a dominant LOS path); it looks like a carrier frequency offset and can be removed by one frequency correction. A Doppler spread arises when many paths arrive from different angles with different shifts; the received spectrum is broadened, the channel gain varies in time (time-selective fading), and no single frequency correction can remove it.

**詳解**　With one path, $r(t) = a\,e^{j2\pi f_d t}s(t)$, and multiplying by $e^{-j2\pi f_d t}$ restores the signal. With several paths of different $f_{d,k}$, the sum $\sum a_k e^{j2\pi f_{d,k}t}$ has a time-varying magnitude; correcting for one $f_{d,k}$ leaves the others. The spread determines the coherence time ($T_c \propto 1/f_D$).

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice, 2nd ed., chapter on small-scale fading and multipath, Doppler spread and coherence time.

</details>

**3.1.4S013**　If the same channel were evaluated with $T_c \approx 1/f_D$ or with $T_c \approx 9/(16\pi f_D)$ instead of $0.423/f_D$, the results would be about 385.7 μs and 69.1 μs. Why do these definitions give different values, and which one is the most conservative for design?

**答案**　They are rules of thumb that correspond to different amounts of allowed decorrelation: $1/f_D$ is the loosest order-of-magnitude estimate, $9/(16\pi f_D) \approx 0.179/f_D$ is the time over which the time correlation stays above 0.5, and $0.423/f_D$ is the geometric mean of the two. **詳解**　$1/f_D = 1/2592.593 = 385.7$ μs; $9/(16\pi f_D) = 0.179049/2592.593 = 69.1$ μs; $\sqrt{385.7 \times 69.1} \approx 163.2$ μs. The channel does not change abruptly at $T_c$; each rule marks a different correlation level.

**詳解**　Slow fading with respect to an interval requires that interval $\ll T_c$. Here $T_{slot}/T_c = 0.125/0.163 = 0.77$. An estimate taken at the first symbols is partly decorrelated by the last symbols of the slot, which degrades demodulation of higher-order modulation.

#### 情境題：Rayleigh versus Rician fading

**Scenario | Street-level handset in a dense urban canyon**

A handset at street level in a dense urban canyon has no line of sight to the base station; the signal arrives through many reflected and diffracted paths of comparable strength. The in-phase and quadrature components of the received narrowband signal are modelled as independent zero-mean Gaussian processes, each with standard deviation σ = 20 μV. The envelope is $r = \sqrt{I^2 + Q^2}$ and its distribution function is $P(r \le R) = 1 - \exp(-R^2/(2\sigma^2))$.

**3.1.4S046**　Compute the probability, in percent, that the envelope is below 10 μV. Round to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　11.75 %

**詳解**　$2\sigma^2 = 2 \times 20^2 = 800$ μV². $R^2/(2\sigma^2) = 100/800 = 0.125$. $P = 1 - e^{-0.125} = 1 - 0.882497 = 0.117503$, i.e. 11.75 %.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice, 2nd ed., chapter on small-scale fading and multipath, Rayleigh cumulative distribution function.

</details>

**3.1.4S047**　Why is the Rayleigh distribution, rather than the Rician, the appropriate envelope model in this setting?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　With many paths of comparable strength and no dominant one, the in-phase and quadrature components are each sums of many independent contributions with random phases, so by the central limit theorem they are zero-mean Gaussian with equal variance. The envelope of such a complex Gaussian is Rayleigh distributed and its phase is uniform. A Rician model would require a dominant steady (usually LOS) component, which is absent here.

**詳解**　$I = \sum a_k\cos\phi_k$, $Q = \sum a_k\sin\phi_k$ with $\phi_k$ uniform on $[0, 2\pi)$: $E[I] = E[Q] = 0$ and $\text{var}(I) = \text{var}(Q) = \sigma^2$. Then $r = \sqrt{I^2+Q^2}$ has pdf $(r/\sigma^2)e^{-r^2/(2\sigma^2)}$. A non-zero mean in $I$ or $Q$ (a dominant path) would give the Rician pdf.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice, 2nd ed., chapter on small-scale fading and multipath, Rayleigh and Ricean distributions; R. H. Clarke, "A Statistical Theory of Mobile-Radio Reception," Bell System Technical Journal, 1968.

</details>

**3.1.4S048**　For this Rayleigh channel, what distribution does the instantaneous received power follow, and for what fraction of the time is the instantaneous power below its own average?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The power (proportional to $r^2$) is exponentially distributed. The probability that it is below its mean is $1 - e^{-1} \approx 63.2\,\%$, so the signal is below its average power most of the time; the average is pulled up by relatively short strong peaks.

**詳解**　If $r$ is Rayleigh, $p = r^2$ has cdf $1 - \exp(-p/\bar{p})$ with $\bar{p} = 2\sigma^2$. Setting $p = \bar{p}$ gives $1 - e^{-1} = 0.6321$. This is why the median power lies below the mean power ($\ln 2 \approx 0.693$ of the mean, i.e. about 1.6 dB lower).

**出處**　A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, chapter on statistical multipath channel models, exponential distribution of received power in Rayleigh fading.

</details>

#### 情境題：Rayleigh outage probability and fade margin

**Scenario | Basement utility meter on a narrowband uplink**

A utility meter in a basement transmits on a narrowband uplink that experiences flat Rayleigh fading (no line of sight). The average received SNR is 16 dB and the demodulator needs an instantaneous SNR of at least 5 dB. The outage probability is $P_{out} = P(\gamma < \gamma_{th}) = 1 - \exp(-\gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma})$ with SNRs in linear units and the natural exponential.

**3.1.4S051**　Compute the outage probability in percent, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　7.64 %

**詳解**　$\gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma} = 10^{(5-16)/10} = 10^{-1.1} = 0.0794328$. $P_{out} = 1 - e^{-0.0794328} = 1 - 0.923640 = 0.076360$, i.e. 7.64 %.

**出處**　A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, chapter on performance of digital modulation over wireless channels, outage probability in Rayleigh fading.

</details>

**3.1.4S052**　What average SNR, in dB, would be needed to bring the outage probability down to 2 %? Round to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　21.95 dB

**詳解**　$\gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma} = -\ln(1 - 0.02) = -\ln(0.98) = 0.0202027$. Required margin $= -10\log_{10}(0.0202027) = 16.9459$ dB. $\bar{\gamma} = 5 + 16.9459 = 21.9459$ dB, which rounds to 21.95 dB.

**出處**　A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, chapter on performance of digital modulation over wireless channels, outage probability in Rayleigh fading.

</details>

**3.1.4S053**　For small outage probabilities the Rayleigh formula can be approximated. State the approximation and what it implies about how much extra average SNR is needed to reduce the outage by a factor of 10.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　For $\gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma} \ll 1$, $P_{out} \approx \gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma}$, the reciprocal of the fade margin. The outage is therefore inversely proportional to the average SNR: every factor of 10 reduction in outage costs 10 dB more average SNR (a diversity order of 1). This slow improvement is why power alone is an expensive remedy for Rayleigh fading.

**詳解**　$1 - e^{-x} = x - x^2/2 + \dots \approx x$ for small $x$. Check: a 2 % outage needed a margin of 16.95 dB (approximation: $10\log_{10}(1/0.02) = 16.99$ dB); 0.2 % needs about 26.99 dB, i.e. 10 dB more.

**出處**　D. Tse and P. Viswanath, Fundamentals of Wireless Communication, Chapter 3 (error and outage probability decaying as 1/SNR in Rayleigh fading).

</details>

**3.1.4S054**　Under what conditions is $P_{out} = 1 - \exp(-\gamma_{th}/\bar{\gamma})$ valid, and name one situation in this deployment where it would not apply.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It requires flat (frequency-non-selective) Rayleigh fading on a single branch, so that the instantaneous SNR is exponentially distributed about a known average $\bar{\gamma}$; $\bar{\gamma}$ is the local mean, i.e. shadowing and path loss are treated as fixed. It does not apply if the meter had a dominant LOS path (Rician fading gives a smaller outage for the same mean), if the receiver used diversity combining, if the signal were wide enough to be frequency selective, or if log-normal shadowing variation must also be included.

**詳解**　In Rayleigh fading the envelope is Rayleigh and the power (hence SNR with constant noise) is exponential with cdf $1 - e^{-\gamma/\bar{\gamma}}$; the outage probability is that cdf evaluated at $\gamma_{th}$. Any change in the distribution of $\gamma$ (Rician, combined branches, composite shadowing) changes the formula.

**出處**　A. Goldsmith, Wireless Communications, chapters on statistical multipath channel models and on performance over fading channels (outage probability).

</details>

#### 情境題：Level crossing rate and average fade duration

**Scenario | Vehicular voice link at 850 MHz**

A vehicle travels at 72 km/h (20 m/s) in an NLOS suburban area and receives an 850 MHz narrowband signal with Rayleigh fading.
