# 3.3.3 各項損耗與餘量

所屬：[3.3 鏈路預算](3-3.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

EIRP 與接收靈敏度之間的差距，並不能全部拿來「花」在路徑損耗上。訊號從功放到天線要經過饋線，進入室內要穿過牆面與玻璃，手機被手握住、貼在頭邊又會被人體遮擋；除此之外，路徑損耗模型給的只是中位數，實際場強會因建物遮蔽上下起伏，鄰近細胞的干擾也會把雜訊底墊高。鏈路預算把前者列為**損耗**（確定會發生的衰減），把後者列為**餘量**（為了達到一定可靠度而預留的緩衝）。這些項目加起來常有二三十 dB，足以讓涵蓋半徑縮到一半以下，所以每一項的來源、數值依據與是否重複計算，是檢查一份鏈路預算時最需要細看的部分。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $L_f$ | 饋線與接頭損耗 | dB |
| $\alpha$ | 饋線單位長度衰減（隨線徑與頻率而定） | dB/m |
| $\ell$ | 饋線長度 | m |
| $L_{\text{body}}$ | 人體損耗（手握、貼近頭部造成的遮擋與天線失調） | dB |
| $PL_{\text{tw}}$ | 建物外牆穿透損耗 | dB |
| $PL_{\text{in}}$ | 室內損耗，隨進入建物的深度增加 | dB |
| $d_{\text{2D-in}}$ | 室內段的水平距離 | m |
| $L_{\text{glass}},\ L_{\text{IRRglass}},\ L_{\text{concrete}}$ | 一般多層玻璃、紅外線反射鍍膜玻璃、混凝土的材料穿透損耗 | dB |
| $f$ | 頻率（穿透損耗模型中） | GHz |
| $M_{\text{SF}}$ | 陰影衰落餘量（shadow fading margin） | dB |
| $\sigma$ | 對數常態陰影衰落的標準差 | dB |
| $p$ | 細胞邊緣的目標涵蓋機率 | 無因次 |
| $M_I$ | 干擾餘量（interference margin） | dB |
| $\eta$ | 上行負載因子，$0 \le \eta < 1$ | 無因次 |

#### 公式與推導

**饋線損耗**：$L_f = \alpha\,\ell + (\text{各接頭與跳線損耗})$。$\alpha$ 隨頻率升高而變大，應查纜線規格書。把射頻單元裝在天線旁或與天線整合，可以讓這一項接近零。

**建物穿透損耗**：數值隨頻率、建材與入射角差異很大。3GPP TR 38.901 的室外到室內（O2I）模型把它拆成外牆與室內兩段，總路徑損耗為室外基本路徑損耗加上 $PL_{\text{tw}}$、$PL_{\text{in}}$ 與一個標準差為 $\sigma_P$ 的常態隨機項。材料損耗隨頻率線性增加（$f$ 以 GHz 計）：

$$L_{\text{glass}} = 2 + 0.2f,\quad L_{\text{IRRglass}} = 23 + 0.3f,\quad L_{\text{concrete}} = 5 + 4f$$

外牆損耗依建物類型分兩種，都是把不同材料的「穿透率」依面積比例加權後再取對數，另加 5 dB 反映非垂直入射：

$$PL_{\text{tw}}^{\text{低損耗}} = 5 - 10\log_{10}\!\left(0.3\times10^{-L_{\text{glass}}/10} + 0.7\times10^{-L_{\text{concrete}}/10}\right)$$

$$PL_{\text{tw}}^{\text{高損耗}} = 5 - 10\log_{10}\!\left(0.7\times10^{-L_{\text{IRRglass}}/10} + 0.3\times10^{-L_{\text{concrete}}/10}\right)$$

室內損耗兩種模型都是 $PL_{\text{in}} = 0.5\,d_{\text{2D-in}}$；$\sigma_P$ 分別為 4.4 dB 與 6.5 dB。同一份報告的車輛穿透損耗以平均 9 dB、標準差 5 dB 的常態分布描述，金屬鍍膜車窗可改用平均 20 dB。這些是通道模擬用的模型，不是量測保證值；實際規劃仍應以當地建物的量測或業者經驗值校正。

**人體損耗**：沒有通用公式，隨頻段、握持方式與終端型態而變，鏈路預算中通常取一個假設值，必須標明。

**陰影衰落餘量**：對數常態陰影下，接收功率（dB）在中位數附近呈常態分布、標準差 $\sigma$（見 3.1.3）。若要求細胞邊緣有機率 $p$ 高於門檻，需預留

$$M_{\text{SF}} = Q^{-1}(1-p)\cdot\sigma$$

例如 $p = 90\%$ 時 $Q^{-1}(0.1) = 1.2816$，$p = 95\%$ 時 $Q^{-1}(0.05) = 1.6449$。這是邊緣機率；整個細胞面積的平均涵蓋機率會比邊緣機率高。

**干擾餘量**：干擾功率 $I$ 加在熱雜訊 $N$ 之上，等效雜訊底抬升

$$M_I = 10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{N+I}{N}\right)$$

上行常用負載因子 $\eta$ 定義為 $\displaystyle \eta = 1 - \frac{N}{I_{\text{total}}}$，其中 $I_{\text{total}}$ 為含所有使用者訊號與雜訊的總接收功率。此時等效雜訊底抬升因子為 $\displaystyle \frac{I_{\text{total}}}{N}= \frac{1}{1-\eta}$，故 $M_I = -10\log_{10}(1-\eta)$。負載越接近 1，餘量急遽增加，這就是負載升高時涵蓋縮小的原因。

所有損耗與餘量在 dB 域直接相加，從可用的路徑損耗中扣除（見 3.3.5）。

#### 算例

**算例 1（依 TR 38.901 計算外牆穿透損耗）**　已知：頻率 3.5 GHz，比較低損耗與高損耗兩種建物；室內深度假設 10 m。

1. 材料損耗：$L_{\text{glass}} = 2 + 0.2\times3.5 = 2.70$ dB；$L_{\text{IRRglass}} = 23 + 0.3\times3.5 = 24.05$ dB；$L_{\text{concrete}} = 5 + 4\times3.5 = 19.00$ dB。
2. 低損耗：$0.3\times10^{-0.270} = 0.16111$，$0.7\times10^{-1.900} = 0.00881$，和為 0.16992；$-10\log_{10}(0.16992) = 7.70$ dB，加 5 dB 得 $PL_{\text{tw}} = 12.70$ dB。
3. 高損耗：$0.7\times10^{-2.405} = 0.00275$，$0.3\times10^{-1.900} = 0.00378$，和為 0.00653；$-10\log_{10}(0.00653) = 21.85$ dB，加 5 dB 得 $PL_{\text{tw}} = 26.85$ dB。
4. 室內段：$PL_{\text{in}} = 0.5\times10 = 5$ dB。兩種建物的穿透總損耗中位數分別為 17.70 dB 與 31.85 dB。

合理性檢查：低損耗建物的結果由玻璃主導（混凝土那一項只占和的約 5%），表示訊號主要從窗戶進入；高損耗建物換成鍍膜玻璃後多了約 14 dB。同樣的算式在 28 GHz 時，外牆損耗 $PL_{\text{tw}}$ 為 17.83 dB／37.95 dB；若再加上室內 10 m（5 dB）的室內段，則總損耗分別為 22.83 dB／42.95 dB，頻率越高穿透越困難，趨勢合理。

**算例 2（陰影衰落餘量與干擾餘量）**　已知：陰影衰落標準差 $\sigma = 8$ dB（假設），細胞邊緣涵蓋機率目標 90%；上行負載 50%。

1. $M_{\text{SF}} = 1.2816\times8 = 10.25$ dB。若目標提高到 95%，則為 $1.6449\times8 = 13.16$ dB，多付出約 2.9 dB。
2. $M_I = -10\log_{10}(1-0.5) = 3.01$ dB。若負載升到 75%，則為 $-10\log_{10}(0.25) = 6.02$ dB。

合理性檢查：負載 50% 表示干擾與雜訊一樣大，雜訊底加倍，正好是 3 dB。

#### 常見錯誤

- **把穿透損耗當成固定常數**：同一棟建物在不同頻段、不同建材下可差十幾 dB；引用數值時要連同頻率與建物類型一起寫。
- **重複計算或漏算**：饋線損耗若已算進 EIRP（3.3.1 的 $L_t$），就不能在損耗表再扣一次；陰影衰落餘量若已包含在所用傳播模型的涵蓋機率設定裡，也不可重複。
- **把陰影衰落餘量與快衰落餘量混為一談**：前者對付建物遮蔽造成的慢變化，後者對付多路徑造成的快速起伏（見 3.1.4），成因與統計分布都不同。
- **直接把 dB 值加權平均**：各材料的穿透率要先換成線性比例加權，再取對數；直接把 dB 值按比例平均會得到錯誤結果。

#### 和其他小節的關係

陰影衰落的對數常態模型與 Q 函數來自 3.1.3；快衰落的成因在 3.1.4；干擾把雜訊底墊高的概念與 3.1.6 的 SINR 一致。饋線損耗與 3.3.1 的 $L_t$ 是同一項。本節所有損耗與餘量都會在 3.3.5 從最大允許路徑損耗中扣除，並在 3.3.6 檢查上下行是否使用一致的假設。

#### 單題：Feeder and connector losses

**3.3.3G001**　A 1800 MHz macro site connects its radio cabinet to the antenna with 48 m of 7/8-inch coaxial feeder whose datasheet attenuation is 6.5 dB per 100 m at this frequency. The run also includes two connectors of 0.1 dB each and one jumper cable of 0.4 dB. Find the total feeder-system loss in dB, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3.72

**詳解**　Cable: $L_{\text{cable}} = \alpha\ell = (6.5/100)\times48 = 0.065\times48 = 3.12$ dB.
Connectors: $2\times0.1 = 0.2$ dB. Jumper: 0.4 dB.
$L_f = 3.12 + 0.2 + 0.4 = 3.72$ dB.
\boxed{3.72}

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (link budget); feeder loss $L_f = \alpha\ell + \sum L_{\text{connector}}$.

</details>

**3.3.3G002**　A remote radio unit delivers 40 W at its antenna port into a 2.6 GHz feeder made of 12 m of 1/2-inch cable with attenuation 11.2 dB per 100 m, plus four connectors of 0.05 dB each. How much power, in W, reaches the antenna input? Round to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　28.03

**詳解**　Cable loss: $0.112\times12 = 1.344$ dB. Connectors: $4\times0.05 = 0.2$ dB. Total $L_f = 1.544$ dB.
Linear transmission factor: $10^{-1.544/10} = 10^{-0.1544} = 0.70081$.
Power at the antenna: $40\times0.70081 = 28.0324$ W, which rounds to 28.03 W.
\boxed{28.03}

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (link budget); conversion between dB and power ratio.

</details>

**3.3.3G003**　A site design allows at most 2.5 dB of total feeder-system loss. Jumpers and connectors already use 0.65 dB, and the main feeder has an attenuation of 4.1 dB per 100 m at the operating frequency. What is the longest main feeder that can be installed, in m, rounded to one decimal place?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**詳解**　Loss left for the main cable: $2.5 - 0.65 = 1.85$ dB.

\boxed{45.1}

**3.3.3G004**　A 7/8-inch feeder has an attenuation of 3.9 dB per 100 m at 900 MHz. Assume its attenuation in dB per unit length scales with the square root of frequency (conductor loss dominant). The same 60 m feeder is reused for a 2600 MHz carrier, and the connectors and jumpers add 0.3 dB. Find the total feeder-system loss at 2600 MHz in dB, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　4.28

**詳解**　Scaling factor: $\sqrt{2600/900} = \sqrt{2.88889} = 1.69967$.
Attenuation at 2600 MHz: $3.9\times1.69967 = 6.6287$ dB per 100 m.
Cable loss over 60 m: $6.6287\times0.6 = 3.9772$ dB.
Total: $3.9772 + 0.3 = 4.2772$ dB, which rounds to 4.28 dB.
\boxed{4.28}

**出處**　D. M. Pozar, Microwave Engineering (transmission lines, conductor loss proportional to $\sqrt{f}$ through the skin effect).

</details>

**3.3.3G005**　On the uplink, a feeder with 3.2 dB loss at a physical temperature of 290 K sits between the antenna and a receiver whose noise figure is 2.5 dB. For a matched passive line at 290 K the noise factor equals its loss, so the cascade noise factor is $F = L\cdot F_{\text{rx}}$. Find the equivalent input noise temperature of the feeder-plus-receiver cascade, $T_e = 290\,(F-1)$, in K, rounded to one decimal place.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

$T_e = 290\times(3.71535 - 1) = 290\times2.71535 = 787.452$ K, which rounds to 787.5 K.
\boxed{787.5}

#### 單題：Shadow fading margin

**3.3.3G011**　Log-normal shadowing at a cell edge has a standard deviation of 6 dB. The design target is a 95 % probability that the received power at the edge exceeds the receiver threshold. With $M_{\text{SF}} = Q^{-1}(1-p)\,\sigma$ and $Q^{-1}(0.05) = 1.6449$, find the shadow fading margin in dB, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　9.87

**詳解**　$M_{\text{SF}} = 1.6449\times6 = 9.8694$ dB, which rounds to 9.87 dB.
\boxed{9.87}

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (log-normal shadowing and percentage of coverage area).

**3.3.3G012**　In a dense urban area the shadowing standard deviation is 9 dB. How many dB of additional shadow fading margin are needed to raise the cell-edge coverage probability from 90 % to 98 %? Use $M_{\text{SF}} = Q^{-1}(1-p)\,\sigma$ with $Q^{-1}(0.10) = 1.2816$ and $Q^{-1}(0.02) = 2.0537$. Round to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**詳解**　Margin at 90 %: $1.2816\times9 = 11.5344$ dB.

An operator compares indoor coverage of houses from the 700 MHz band and the 3.3 GHz band using the 3GPP TR 38.901 low-loss O2I model: $L_{\text{glass}} = 2 + 0.2f$, $L_{\text{concrete}} = 5 + 4f$ ($f$ in GHz), $PL_{\text{tw}} = 5 - 10\log_{10}\left(0.3\cdot10^{-L_{\text{glass}}/10} + 0.7\cdot10^{-L_{\text{concrete}}/10}\right)$ and $PL_{\text{in}} = 0.5\,d_{\text{2D-in}}$ dB. $PL_{\text{tw}} = 5 + 7.6146 = 12.6146$ dB. The indoor term (6 dB) is the same in both bands.

**3.3.3S013**　At 0.7 GHz the concrete term contributes almost 40 % of the transmittance sum, but at 3.3 GHz only about 6 %. What does this say about how the signal enters the house in each band?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　At 700 MHz the concrete wall still lets a useful share of power through (7.8 dB loss), so the signal enters through both walls and windows. At 3.3 GHz the concrete loss is 18.2 dB, its transmittance becomes negligible and the signal enters almost only through the glass; the external-wall loss then approaches $5 - 10\log_{10}(0.3) + L_{\text{glass}}$, i.e. it is set by the window fraction.

**3.3.3S014**　TR 38.901 gives the low-loss model a standard deviation $\sigma_P$ = 4.4 dB. What does this mean for using 16.24 dB in a coverage plan?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The computed value is the median for this depth; the actual penetration loss varies from house to house and is modelled as a Gaussian in dB with 4.4 dB standard deviation, added to shadow fading in the log domain. A plan that only subtracts the median gives about 50 % probability per location; to reach a higher indoor coverage probability the variation must be covered by a margin (usually by combining $\sigma_P$ with the shadowing $\sigma$). The model is meant for simulation and should be calibrated with local measurements.

**詳解**　Penetration term: $PL_{\text{tw}} + PL_{\text{in}} + N(0, \sigma_P^2)$ with $\sigma_P$ = 4.4 dB (low-loss).

**出處**　3GPP TR 38.901 V18.1.0, clause 7.4.3.1, equation (7.4-2) and Table 7.4.3-2.

</details>

**3.3.3S038**　TR 38.901 also has a backward-compatible O2I model for single-frequency simulations below 6 GHz with a 20 dB wall loss, $\sigma_P$ = 0 dB and the shadow-fading standard deviation set to 7 dB. Why can the penetration term have zero standard deviation there?

**答案**　In that model the randomness of the penetration loss is not lost but folded into the shadowing: the shadow-fading standard deviation for indoor users is replaced by a single value of 7 dB. **3.3.3S039**　Under what conditions is $\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma_{\text{SF}}^2 + \sigma_P^2}$ valid?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Both terms must be Gaussian in dB (log-normal in linear power) and statistically independent, and they must add in dB, as they do when they are attenuations along the same path. **詳解**　$\operatorname{Var}(X+Y) = \sigma_X^2 + \sigma_Y^2 + 2\rho\sigma_X\sigma_Y$, with $\rho = 0$ for independent variables.

**3.3.3S043**　The cellular link budgets of the same operator have a shadow fading margin but no explicit Rayleigh fade margin. Is that an omission?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Not necessarily. The shadow margin covers slow, location-dependent variation (log-normal, caused by obstacles); the fast-fading margin covers rapid multipath fluctuation (Rayleigh/Rician) within a few wavelengths. In cellular systems the required SNR is normally taken from link-level results in fading channels, where coding, interleaving, HARQ and receive diversity already absorb the fast fading, so adding a separate Rayleigh margin would double-count it. A narrowband uncoded link without those mechanisms needs it explicitly.

**詳解**　Different causes, different distributions and different time scales: margins are not interchangeable.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (large-scale versus small-scale fading).

</details>

**3.3.3S044**　On a part of the route the vehicle has a strong line-of-sight path, so the fading is Rician with a large K-factor. What happens to the required margin?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It decreases. With a dominant line-of-sight component the envelope fluctuates much less and deep fades become rare, so the 2 % point of the distribution is much closer to the mean than for Rayleigh. Rayleigh (K = 0) is the worst case; as K grows the margin approaches the value for a non-fading channel.

**詳解**　Rician fading reduces to Rayleigh for K = 0 and to a constant envelope for K → ∞.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (Ricean fading distribution).

</details>

**3.3.3S045**　A designer uses the small-outage approximation $P_{\text{out}} \approx 1/M$ as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected (approximate) margin in dB to two decimal places.
1. $M \approx 1/P_{\text{out}} = 1/0.02 = 50$.
2. Fade margin $= 50$ dB.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: $M = 50$ is a linear power ratio and must be converted to dB ($10\log_{10}$), not used as a dB value. Corrected approximate margin: 16.99 dB (the exact formula gives 16.95 dB).

**詳解**　$10\log_{10}(50) = 16.9897$ dB ≈ 16.99 dB; exact: $10\log_{10}(49.4983) = 16.9459$ dB.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (Rayleigh fading distribution); definition of the decibel.

</details>

#### 情境題：Interference margin

**Scenario | Busy-hour uplink load in a 5G macro cell**

A 5G macro cell with 15 kHz subcarrier spacing is dimensioned for an uplink load factor of 60 % at the busy hour. The gNB receiver noise figure is 3 dB; use −174 dBm/Hz for thermal noise. A cell-edge user is scheduled on 2 resource blocks (each 12 subcarriers × 15 kHz = 180 kHz). Use $M_I = -10\log_{10}(1-\eta)$.

**3.3.3S046**　Find the uplink interference margin at the planned load, in dB, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　3.98 dB

**詳解**　$M_I = -10\log_{10}(1 - 0.6) = -10\log_{10}(0.4) = 3.9794$ dB ≈ 3.98 dB.

**出處**　H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (radio network planning: uplink load factor and interference margin).

</details>

**3.3.3S047**　Find the noise-plus-interference level in the 2-resource-block allocation at the planned load, referred to the gNB input, in dBm rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−111.46 dBm

**詳解**　Bandwidth: $2\times180$ kHz $= 360$ kHz; $10\log_{10}(360\,000) = 55.5630$ dB-Hz.
Noise floor: $-174 + 55.5630 + 3 = -115.4370$ dBm.
Add the margin: $-115.4370 + 3.9794 = -111.4576$ dBm ≈ −111.46 dBm.

**出處**　D. M. Pozar, Microwave Engineering ($kT_0BF$); H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (noise rise).

</details>

**3.3.3S048**　Why do planners avoid dimensioning the uplink for loads close to 100 %?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　$M_I = -10\log_{10}(1-\eta)$ has a pole at $\eta = 1$: the margin grows slowly at low load (1.25 dB at 25 %, 3 dB at 50 %) but explodes near full load (10 dB at 90 %, 20 dB at 99 %). Each extra dB of margin removes a dB of allowable path loss, so coverage collapses and the system becomes unstable as users raise power against each other. A moderate planned load keeps the noise rise, and therefore the coverage shrinkage, under control.

**詳解**　$-10\log_{10}(0.75) = 1.25$, $-10\log_{10}(0.5) = 3.01$, $-10\log_{10}(0.1) = 10$, $-10\log_{10}(0.01) = 20$ dB.

**出處**　H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (load factor, noise rise and cell breathing).

</details>

**3.3.3S049**　At night the uplink load falls to 10 %. What happens to the uplink coverage of the cell?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The interference margin falls from 3.98 dB to 0.46 dB, so about 3.5 dB more path loss can be tolerated and the uplink coverage area grows; during the busy hour it shrinks again. This load-dependent coverage is called cell breathing, and it is why the budget must be made at the planned (busy-hour) load, not the average.

**詳解**　$-10\log_{10}(0.9) = 0.4576$ dB; $3.9794 - 0.4576 = 3.52$ dB.

**出處**　H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (cell breathing).

</details>

**3.3.3S050**　After the operator raises the planned load to 70 %, a new margin is computed as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected margin in dB to two decimal places.
1. $1 - \eta = 0.3$.
2. $M_I = -10\ln(0.3) = 12.04$ dB.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the decibel uses $\log_{10}$, not the natural logarithm. Corrected margin: 5.23 dB.

**詳解**　$-10\log_{10}(0.3) = 5.2288$ dB ≈ 5.23 dB. (The wrong value is 2.3026 times too large, the factor $\ln 10$.)

**出處**　H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (interference margin); definition of the decibel.

</details>

**Scenario | Downlink cell-edge measurement**

A user at the boundary between three cells measures, in its receive bandwidth: noise floor (including the receiver noise figure) −104.5 dBm, interference from neighbour A −106 dBm, interference from neighbour B −109.5 dBm, and the wanted signal −97.2 dBm. The interferers are uncorrelated with each other and with the noise.

**3.3.3S051**　Find the total interference power in dBm, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−104.40 dBm

**詳解**　$10^{-10.6} = 2.51189\times10^{-11}$ mW; $10^{-10.95} = 1.12202\times10^{-11}$ mW.
Sum: $3.63391\times10^{-11}$ mW.
$10\log_{10}(3.63391\times10^{-11}) = -104.3963$ dBm ≈ −104.40 dBm.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (addition of uncorrelated interference powers).

</details>

**3.3.3S052**　What determines how large the downlink interference margin is at this location, and why can it change during the day?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It depends on how strong the neighbour cells are at this point (path loss to them relative to the serving cell) and on how much of their resources they are transmitting on, i.e. their load. When the neighbours are lightly loaded, many resources carry no data and the interference falls; at busy hour the neighbours transmit on most resources and the margin grows. That is why it must be set for an assumed neighbour load.

**詳解**　$I = \sum_j P_j\,\text{(activity)}_j / L_j$; the activity factor scales with the neighbours' load.

**出處**　H. Holma and A. Toskala, WCDMA for UMTS (downlink load and interference).

</details>

**3.3.3S053**　Here interference and noise are about equal. If interference were 10 dB above the noise, would raising the transmit power of all cells by 3 dB improve the user's SINR?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Hardly. When $I \gg N$ the link is interference-limited: raising all transmitters by 3 dB raises both the wanted signal and the interference by 3 dB, so $S/I$ stays the same and the only gain comes from the now relatively smaller noise term, which is small. Only when noise dominates (noise-limited) does extra power improve SINR nearly dB for dB.

**詳解**　With $I/N$ = 10 dB: $\text{SINR} = S/(N + I)$; after +3 dB on S and I, the improvement is $10\log_{10}\left((1 + 10)/(0.5012 + 10)\right) = 0.20$ dB.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (interference-limited versus noise-limited systems).

</details>

**3.3.3S054**　A planner computes the interference margin as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected margin in dB to two decimal places.
1. Total interference: $I = -104.40$ dBm; noise $N = -104.5$ dBm.
2. $I/N = 10^{0.0104} = 1.0242$.
3. $M_I = 10\log_{10}(I/N) = 0.10$ dB.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 3 is wrong: the margin is the rise of the floor from $N$ to $N + I$, i.e. $10\log_{10}\left((N+I)/N\right) = 10\log_{10}(1 + I/N)$; the "+1" (the noise itself) was dropped. Corrected margin: 3.06 dB.

**詳解**　$M_I = 10\log_{10}(1 + 1.02417) = 10\log_{10}(2.02417) = 3.0625$ dB ≈ 3.06 dB.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (noise plus interference).

</details>

**3.3.3S055**　A planner computes the user's SINR as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected SINR in dB to two decimal places.
1. $N = -104.5$ dBm, $I = -104.40$ dBm.
2. $N + I = (-104.5) + (-104.40) = -208.90$ dBm.
3. $\text{SINR} = -97.2 - (-208.90) = 111.70$ dB.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: powers in dBm cannot be added; they must be converted to milliwatts, added, and converted back. Corrected SINR: 4.24 dB.

**詳解**　$N = 3.54813\times10^{-11}$ mW, $I = 3.63391\times10^{-11}$ mW; $N + I = 7.18204\times10^{-11}$ mW $= -101.4375$ dBm.
$\text{SINR} = -97.2 - (-101.4375) = 4.2375$ dB ≈ 4.24 dB.

**出處**　Definition of dBm; T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (SINR).

</details>
