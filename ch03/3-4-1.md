# 3.4.1 話務量（Erlang）

所屬：[3.4 訊務與存取](3-4.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

網路資源（語音通道、中繼線、信令通道、客服座席、封包排程的時槽）永遠比用戶數少，因為不是所有人同時使用。要決定「該準備多少資源」，第一步是把需求量化，這個量就是話務量（traffic intensity），單位是厄蘭（Erlang, Erl）。細胞的語音通道數、交換機之間的中繼線數、VoLTE／VoNR 的同時通話容量、忙時 KPI 報表，都以 Erlang 為共同語言。先把話務量算對，才能接著用 3.4.2 的 Erlang B／C 換算成需要的通道數。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $A$ | 提供話務量（offered traffic） | Erl |
| $A_c$ | 承載話務量（carried traffic） | Erl |
| $A_l$ | 損失話務量（lost traffic） | Erl |
| $\lambda$ | 呼叫到達率（單位時間內的平均呼叫次數） | 次/秒 或 次/小時 |
| $h$ | 平均佔用時間（mean holding time） | 秒 或 小時 |
| $n$ | 觀察時間 $T$ 內的呼叫次數 | 次 |
| $T$ | 觀察時間（通常取忙時 1 小時） | 秒 或 小時 |
| $N_u$ | 用戶數 | 人 |
| $a$ | 每個用戶的平均話務量 | Erl/用戶（常寫成 mErl） |
| $B$ | 阻塞機率（被拒絕的呼叫比例） | 無因次 |

#### 公式與推導

話務量是「到達率乘以平均佔用時間」：

$$A = \lambda h = \frac{n\,h}{T}$$

$\lambda$ 和 $h$ 必須用同一個時間單位，乘起來時間單位互相消掉，所以 Erlang 是無因次量。它有兩個等價的直觀意義：第一，$A$ 是「平均同時被佔用的資源數」；第二，$A$ 是「在一段平均佔用時間內到達的平均呼叫數」。1 Erl 相當於一條通道在整個觀察時間內被連續佔滿。一條通道最多只能承載 1 Erl，所以 $N$ 條通道的承載話務量不會超過 $N$ Erl。

另一個等價的算法是把所有通話的佔用時間加總，再除以觀察時間：

$$A = \frac{\sum_i t_i}{T}$$

例如 1 小時內的通話總時間是 3,000 分鐘，話務量就是 $3000/60 = 50$ Erl。

提供、承載、損失三種話務量要分清楚。提供話務量是「如果資源無限多，會被承載的量」，它是需求面的量；承載話務量是實際被服務的量，也就是量測得到的平均佔用通道數。在呼叫被拒絕就直接離開（不重撥、不排隊）的假設下：

$$A_c = A(1-B),\qquad A_l = A\,B$$

網路統計量到的是 $A_c$；做容量規劃要用的是 $A$。阻塞率很低時兩者幾乎相等，阻塞嚴重時 $A_c$ 會明顯低估需求。

把用戶數帶進來：若每個用戶在忙時平均撥打 $c$ 次、每次平均 $h$，則每用戶話務量 $a = c\,h/T$，整個區域的話務量是

$$A = N_u\,a$$

忙時（busy hour）是一天當中話務量最高的連續 1 小時。規劃用忙時而不用全日平均，因為資源要撐得住尖峰。北美的舊單位 CCS（hundred call seconds，百秒呼）和 Erlang 的關係是 1 Erl＝3,600 秒÷100 秒＝36 CCS。

這些公式只是平均值的定義，不需要假設到達過程的分布；只有進一步算阻塞機率（3.4.2）時，才需要卜瓦松到達等假設。

#### 算例

**算例 1：由呼叫次數與平均時長求話務量**

已知：某細胞在忙時 1 小時內共有 1,200 次通話嘗試，平均通話時長 150 秒（假設值）。求提供話務量。

- 選公式：$A = n\,h/T$，時間全部換成秒，$T = 3600$ 秒。
- 代入：$A = 1200\times150/3600 = 180000/3600 = 50$ Erl。
- 換算：$50\times36 = 1800$ CCS；總通話時間 $1200\times150 = 180{,}000$ 秒＝3,000 分鐘。
- 若量測到的阻塞率是 2%（假設），承載話務量 $A_c = 50\times(1-0.02) = 49$ Erl，損失 1 Erl。
- 合理性檢查：50 Erl 表示平均同時有 50 通電話在進行，所以通道數一定要多於 50 條才有可能維持低阻塞率；如果這個細胞只有 40 條通道，承載量不可能超過 40 Erl，題目的 2% 阻塞率就不可能成立。

**算例 2：由用戶數推估話務量**

已知：某區域有 3,000 個用戶，每人在忙時平均撥打 1.5 次、每次 120 秒（假設值）。求每用戶話務量與總話務量。

- 每用戶：$a = 1.5\times120/3600 = 180/3600 = 0.05$ Erl＝50 mErl。
- 總量：$A = 3000\times0.05 = 150$ Erl。
- 合理性檢查：50 mErl 表示每個用戶在忙時有 5% 的時間在通話，也就是 60 分鐘裡 3 分鐘，和「1.5 次×2 分鐘＝3 分鐘」一致。

#### 常見錯誤

1. **時間單位沒有統一**：到達率用「次/小時」、佔用時間用「秒」直接相乘，會得到大 3,600 倍的數字。先全部換成秒或全部換成小時。
2. **把 Erlang 當成通話次數或用戶數**：50 Erl 不是 50 通電話，也不是 50 個用戶，而是平均同時佔用 50 條通道。
3. **把承載話務量當成提供話務量**：統計報表上的佔用量是 $A_c$。阻塞率高時要用 $A = A_c/(1-B)$ 還原需求，不然會低估要擴充的通道數。
4. **用全日平均代替忙時**：全日平均話務量遠低於忙時值，拿來規劃會在尖峰時段大量阻塞。

#### 和其他小節的關係

本節算出的 $A$ 是 3.4.2 Erlang B／C 的輸入，用來換算通道數與阻塞機率。$A=\lambda h$ 和 3.4.3 排隊模型的 $\lambda/\mu$ 是同一個量（$h = 1/\mu$）。3.4.6 算出的每細胞通道數則是 Erlang B 的另一個輸入。

**出處**　ITU-T 建議書 E.600（03/93）〈Terms and definitions of traffic engineering〉；ITU-T 建議書 E.500（11/98）〈Traffic intensity measurement principles〉；ITU-T 建議書 E.492（02/96）〈Traffic reference period〉；Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice*，中繼（trunking）與服務等級的章節。

#### 單題：Solving for holding time and call rate

**3.4.1G031**　A trunk group carries 36 Erl in the busy hour from 1,512 calls. Determine the mean holding time in seconds, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　It holds for "blocked calls cleared": a blocked call disappears and does not come back. If blocked callers redial, the repeat attempts add to the offered stream, so the attempts seen by the group exceed the true first-attempt demand, the blocking rises, and A·B no longer equals the demand that is lost (much of it is eventually carried on a retry).

**詳解**　With clearing: A_l = A − A_c = A − A(1 − B) = A·B.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (repeated attempts); ITU-D, *Teletraffic Engineering Handbook*.

</details>

**3.4.1S035**　A billing analyst computes the lost call-minutes as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct value (call-minutes, one decimal place)?
1. A_c = 205 × 0.988 = 202.54 Erl.
2. Lost traffic = A_c × B = 202.54 × 0.012 = 2.4305 Erl.
3. Lost call-minutes = 2.4305 × 60 = 145.8.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the blocking probability applies to the offered traffic, so A_l = A·B = 205 × 0.012, not A_c·B. Correct lost traffic 2.46 Erl and 147.6 call-minutes.

**詳解**　A_l = 205 × 0.012 = 2.46 Erl; 2.46 × 60 = 147.6 call-minutes.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

#### 情境題：Busy hour

**Scenario | Quarter-hour profile of a business-district cell**

A business-district cell reports its mean traffic for eight consecutive quarter-hours starting at 10:00: 31.2, 35.8, 39.4, 42.6, 44.1, 40.3, 36.7 and 33.0 Erl. The busy hour is defined as the four consecutive quarter-hours with the highest total traffic (time-consistent busy hour).

**3.4.1S041**　Which hour is the busy hour, and what is the busy-hour traffic in Erlang, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　10:30–11:30, 41.60 Erl

**詳解**　Step 1: four-quarter-hour sums: 10:00 → 149.0; 10:15 → 161.9; 10:30 → 166.4; 10:45 → 163.7; 11:00 → 154.1.
Step 2: the maximum is 166.4 for 10:30–11:30.
Step 3: busy-hour traffic = 166.4 / 4 = 41.60 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (time-consistent busy hour); ITU-T Recommendation E.500.

</details>

**3.4.1S042**　What is the total traffic volume in the busy hour, in call-minutes, rounded to the nearest whole number?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　2496 call-minutes

**詳解**　Volume = A × T = 41.60 Erl × 60 min = 2496 call-minutes.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (traffic volume).

</details>

**3.4.1S043**　The highest quarter-hour value is 44.1 Erl. Why is it not used as the busy-hour traffic, and when would one look at it?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　44.1 Erl is the mean over only 15 minutes, a short peak inside the busy hour; the busy-hour value averages over 60 minutes and is lower (41.60 Erl). Traffic standards and dimensioning tables are defined for the busy hour, so using a quarter-hour peak would over-dimension relative to the agreed reference. The quarter-hour peak is useful for diagnosing short congestion events.

**詳解**　44.1 > 41.6; the busy-hour value is the average of 39.4, 42.6, 44.1 and 40.3.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (busy hour); ITU-T Recommendation E.492 (traffic reference period).

</details>

**3.4.1S044**　Why does the busy hour here start at 10:30 rather than on a clock hour, and what would be lost by using only clock hours?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The time-consistent busy hour is the busiest 60-minute window, which can start at any quarter-hour. Here the peak is centred around 11:00, so the clock hours 10:00–11:00 (37.25 Erl) and 11:00–12:00 (38.53 Erl) both split it and underestimate it compared with 41.60 Erl.

**詳解**　Clock hour 10:00–11:00: 149.0/4 = 37.25 Erl; 11:00–12:00: 154.1/4 = 38.525 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (time-consistent busy hour).

</details>

**3.4.1S045**　A report generator computes the busy-hour traffic as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct value (Erlang, two decimal places)?
1. The highest sum of four consecutive quarter-hours is 39.4 + 42.6 + 44.1 + 40.3 = 166.4 (10:30–11:30).
2. Busy-hour traffic = 166.4 Erl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: each quarter-hour value is already a mean traffic intensity in Erlang, so the hourly traffic is their average, not their sum. Correct value 41.60 Erl.

**詳解**　166.4 / 4 = 41.60 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.500 (traffic intensity as a mean over the period).

</details>

**Scenario | Daily volume and busy-hour share**

A suburban cell carries a total of 18,720 call-minutes over 24 hours. Long-term statistics show that 10.5 % of the daily traffic volume falls in the busy hour.

**3.4.1S046**　What is the 24-hour average traffic intensity, in Erlang, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　13.00 Erl

**詳解**　24 h = 1440 min. A_avg = 18 720 / 1440 = 13.00 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

**3.4.1S047**　What is the busy-hour traffic, in Erlang, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　32.76 Erl

**詳解**　Step 1: busy-hour volume = 0.105 × 18 720 = 1965.6 call-minutes.
Step 2: A_BH = 1965.6 / 60 = 32.76 Erl (2.52 times the daily average).

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (busy hour).

</details>

**3.4.1S048**　Why must channels be dimensioned for the busy-hour traffic rather than for the 24-hour average?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Calls are blocked when the instantaneous demand exceeds the resources, and demand is concentrated in a few hours. A network sized for the 13 Erl average would face 32.76 Erl in the busy hour and block a large share of calls exactly when most customers want to call. The busy hour is the period in which the grade of service must be met.

**詳解**　32.76 / 13.00 = 2.52.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (busy hour); ITU-T Recommendation E.492.

</details>

**3.4.1S049**　The busy-hour share of 10.5 % comes from one particular day. Why should a planner not take the busy hour of a single day (for example the busiest day of the year) as the design value?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The busy-hour traffic varies from day to day. A single ordinary day may be unrepresentative, and the single busiest day (e.g. a holiday or emergency) would lead to costly over-dimensioning. Traffic engineering practice uses a reference value obtained by averaging busy-hour traffic over a number of high-traffic days, as described for traffic reference periods in ITU-T E.492.

**詳解**　Design value = average of busy-hour traffic over a set of representative high days, not a single sample.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.492 (traffic reference period).

</details>

**3.4.1S050**　An intern converts the busy-hour share into Erlang as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct busy-hour traffic (Erlang, two decimal places)?
1. Busy-hour volume = 0.105 × 18 720 = 1965.6 call-minutes.
2. A_BH = 1965.6 / 3600 = 0.546 Erl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the volume is in call-minutes, so it must be divided by 60 minutes, not by 3,600 (seconds). Correct A_BH = 32.76 Erl.

**詳解**　1965.6 / 60 = 32.76 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

#### 情境題：Signalling and multi-service traffic

**Scenario | Signalling channel pool in a dense cell**

A dense urban cell uses a pool of dedicated signalling channels. In the busy hour the pool handles 6,800 location/tracking area updates holding a channel for 2.4 s each, 3,900 SMS transfers holding a channel for 3.6 s each, and 2,700 call set-ups holding a channel for 4.1 s each.

**3.4.1S051**　What is the busy-hour traffic of the signalling channel pool, in Erlang, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　11.51 Erl

**詳解**　Step 1: occupancy: 6800 × 2.4 = 16 320 s; 3900 × 3.6 = 14 040 s; 2700 × 4.1 = 11 070 s.
Step 2: total = 41 430 s.
Step 3: A = 41 430 / 3600 = 11.5083 Erl ≈ 11.51 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (traffic intensity of any group of resources).

</details>

**3.4.1S052**　Is it legitimate to express signalling-channel load in Erlang, although no "call" is carried on these channels?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Yes. The Erlang measures the mean number of simultaneously occupied resources of any kind. Each transaction occupies one signalling channel for its holding time, so the same definition A = Σ(n·h)/T applies. What matters is to use the channel occupancy time per transaction, not the duration of the user's call.

**詳解**　A = (16 320 + 14 040 + 11 070) / 3600 = 11.51 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (traffic intensity, occupation).

</details>

**3.4.1S053**　There are 74 % more location updates than SMS transfers (6,800 vs 3,900). Do location updates therefore generate 74 % more signalling traffic than SMS?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　No. Traffic is count × holding time, not count alone. Location updates generate 4.533 Erl and SMS 3.900 Erl, only about 16 % more, because each SMS holds the channel longer (3.6 s vs 2.4 s).

**詳解**　16 320 / 3600 = 4.5333 Erl; 14 040 / 3600 = 3.9000 Erl; 4.5333 / 3.9000 = 1.162.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

**3.4.1S054**　A vendor engineer computes the pool traffic as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct traffic (Erlang, two decimal places)?
1. Total occupancy = 16 320 + 14 040 + 11 070 = 41 430 s.
2. Number of transactions = 6800 + 3900 + 2700 = 13 400.
3. A = 41 430 / 13 400 = 3.09 Erl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 3 is wrong: dividing total occupancy by the number of transactions gives the mean holding time per transaction (3.09 s), not the traffic. The traffic is total occupancy divided by the observation period (3,600 s). Correct A = 11.51 Erl.

**詳解**　A = 41 430 / 3600 = 11.5083 ≈ 11.51 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

**3.4.1S055**　Another engineer computes each component separately and rounds early. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct total (Erlang, two decimal places)?
1. Components: 16 320/3600 = 4.5333 Erl; 14 040/3600 = 3.9000 Erl; 11 070/3600 = 3.0750 Erl.
2. Rounded to whole Erlang: 5, 4 and 3 Erl.
3. Total = 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 Erl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: rounding each component to a whole Erlang before adding introduces errors of up to 0.5 Erl each (here +0.47, +0.10 and −0.08 Erl). Keep the unrounded components and round only the total. Correct A = 11.51 Erl.

**詳解**　4.5333 + 3.9000 + 3.0750 = 11.5083 ≈ 11.51 Erl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600.

</details>

#### 情境題：Traffic forecasting

**Scenario | Three-to-five-year voice forecast for a site**

A site currently has 1,840 subscribers and a busy-hour offered voice traffic of 46 Erl. The subscriber base is forecast to grow by 6 % per year and the busy-hour traffic per subscriber to fall by 2 % per year (users moving to over-the-top voice), both compounded annually.

**3.4.1S061**　What is the forecast busy-hour traffic after three years, in Erlang, rounded to two decimal places?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　51.56 Erl

**詳解**　Step 1: annual traffic factor = 1.06 × 0.98 = 1.0388.
Step 2: three-year factor = 1.0388³ = 1.120975.
Step 3: A₃ = 46 × 1.120975 = 51.5648 Erl ≈ 51.56 Erl.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice* (A = U·Au); R. L. Freeman, *Telecommunication System Engineering* (traffic forecasting).

</details>

**3.4.1S062**　Why may the growth rates of subscribers and of per-subscriber traffic be multiplied to get the growth of total traffic?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Because total traffic is the product A = N_u·a. If N_u is multiplied by 1.06 and a by 0.98 each year, A is multiplied by 1.06 × 0.98 = 1.0388. Adding the percentages (6 % − 2 % = 4 %) is only an approximation that ignores the cross term (0.06 × −0.02 = −0.0012).

**詳解**　1.06 × 0.98 = 1.0388 vs 1.04.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice*.

</details>

**3.4.1S063**　What is the busy-hour traffic per subscriber today, and why does the forecast use offered rather than carried traffic as the base?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　a = 46 / 1840 = 25 mErl. The forecast must start from offered traffic (demand) because carried traffic is limited by the existing equipment and would hide blocked demand; a forecast built on carried traffic of a congested site would underestimate future needs.

**詳解**　46 / 1840 = 0.025 Erl = 25 mErl.

**出處**　ITU-T Recommendation E.600 (offered traffic).

</details>

**3.4.1S064**　A planner forecasts the traffic after five years as follows. Which step is wrong, why, and what is the correct five-year traffic (Erlang, two decimal places)?
1. Annual factor = 1.06 × 0.98 = 1.0388.
2. Five-year factor = 1 + 5 × 0.0388 = 1.194.
3. A₅ = 46 × 1.194 = 54.92 Erl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: the growth is compounded annually, so the five-year factor is 1.0388⁵, not 1 + 5 × 0.0388 (simple growth). Correct A₅ = 55.64 Erl.

**詳解**　1.0388⁵ = 1.209650; A₅ = 46 × 1.209650 = 55.6439 ≈ 55.64 Erl.

**出處**　R. L. Freeman, *Telecommunication System Engineering* (traffic forecasting).

</details>

1. Per-subscriber traffic today a = 46 / 1840 = 25 mErl.
2. In two years: subscribers = 1840 × 1.06² = 2067.42; a = 25 × 0.98² = 24.01 mErl.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>
