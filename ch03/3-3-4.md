# 3.3.4 接收靈敏度

所屬：[3.3 鏈路預算](3-3.md)

## 內文

#### 為什麼要學

鏈路預算的另一端是接收機：訊號再弱，只要還高過某個門檻，就能以要求的錯誤率或吞吐量解出來，這個門檻就是接收靈敏度（receiver sensitivity）。它決定鏈路能承受多少路徑損耗，數值每改善 1 dB，可容許的路徑損耗就多 1 dB。靈敏度不是憑空指定的數字，而是由三個物理量疊出來的：熱雜訊在接收頻寬內的總功率、接收機自己多加的雜訊（雜訊指數）、以及解調與解碼所需的訊號雜訊比。3GPP 對基地臺與終端規定的參考靈敏度（reference sensitivity）也是同一個結構，會拆這條式子，就能判斷一個靈敏度數字合不合理，以及頻寬、調變編碼方式改變時它會怎麼動。

#### 定義與符號

| 符號 | 意思 | 單位 |
|---|---|---|
| $k$ | 波茲曼常數，$1.380649\times10^{-23}$ | J/K |
| $T$ | 參考雜訊溫度，慣例取 290 | K |
| $N_0$ | 熱雜訊功率頻譜密度，$N_0 = kT$ | W/Hz 或 dBm/Hz |
| $B$ | 接收訊號所占的雜訊頻寬 | Hz |
| $NF$ | 接收機雜訊指數（noise figure） | dB |
| $\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ | 達到目標錯誤率或吞吐量所需的訊號雜訊比（解調器輸入端） | dB，可為負值 |
| $N$ | 折算到接收機輸入端的等效雜訊功率 | dBm |
| $S_{\min}$ | 接收靈敏度：接收機輸入端所需的最小訊號功率 | dBm |
| $N_{\text{RB}}$ | 配置的資源區塊（resource block）數 | 個 |

#### 公式與推導

熱雜訊在頻寬 $B$ 內的功率是 $kTB$（見 3.1.5）。先算每赫茲的密度，取 $T = 290$ K：

$$N_0 = kT = 1.380649\times10^{-23}\times290 = 4.0039\times10^{-21}\ \text{W/Hz}$$

換成 dBm/Hz（1 mW $=10^{-3}$ W）：

$$N_0 = 10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{4.0039\times10^{-21}}{10^{-3}}\right) = -173.98\ \text{dBm/Hz} \approx -174\ \text{dBm/Hz}$$

接收機內部的放大器與混頻器會再加入雜訊，效果等同把輸入端的雜訊功率放大 $F$ 倍（$NF = 10\log_{10}F$）。因此折算到輸入端的等效雜訊功率是

$$N = -174 + 10\log_{10}B + NF\quad(\text{dBm；}B\text{ 以 Hz 計})$$

要讓解調器得到至少 $\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$，輸入訊號功率必須比 $N$ 高出這麼多（$\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ 為負時則是可以比 $N$ 低），於是

$$S_{\min} = -174 + 10\log_{10}B + NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}}\quad(\text{dBm})$$

各項的正負號與單位如下：$-174$ 是 dBm/Hz，為 $-173.98$ 的取位值，兩者只差 0.02 dB，下面的算例 1 用 $-173.98$ 計算，算例 2 用 $-173.975$ 計算；$10\log_{10}B$ 是把 Hz 換成 dB 的頻寬項，恆為正，頻寬加倍多 3.01 dB；$NF$ 是正的 dB 值；$\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ 是 dB，低碼率搭配低階調變時可以是負值。結果是負的 dBm，**數值越負代表靈敏度越好**。

適用條件與提醒：（1）式子假設雜訊是白色的、接收機前端為線性，且天線看到的雜訊溫度接近 290 K；（2）$B$ 是訊號實際占用的頻寬，不是標稱通道頻寬，例如 NR 以 15 kHz 子載波間隔配置 $N_{\text{RB}}$ 個資源區塊時，$B = N_{\text{RB}}\times12\times15\ \text{kHz} = N_{\text{RB}}\times180$ kHz；（3）式子只考慮熱雜訊，若有干擾，要另外加干擾餘量（3.3.3）；（4）實作損失若沒有包含在 $\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ 內，要另加一項；多支接收天線合成帶來的增益則會使所需訊號功率下降，應另列為增益項，不要混進 $NF$。

#### 算例

**算例 1（終端下行靈敏度）**　已知：通道頻寬 20 MHz、子載波間隔 15 kHz，依 TS 38.101-1 表 5.3.2-1 最大配置為 106 個資源區塊；終端雜訊指數 7 dB（假設）；所需 SNR 為 −1 dB（假設）。

1. 頻寬：$B = 106\times180\ \text{kHz} = 19.08$ MHz，$10\log_{10}(19.08\times10^6) = 72.81$ dB。
2. 熱雜訊功率：$-173.98 + 72.81 = -101.17$ dBm。
3. 加雜訊指數：$-101.17 + 7 = -94.17$ dBm。
4. 加所需 SNR：$S_{\min} = -94.17 + (-1) = -95.17$ dBm。

換成線性：$10^{-9.517}\ \text{mW} = 3.04\times10^{-10}$ mW，約 0.30 pW。合理性檢查：若用整數 −174 計算會得到 −95.19 dBm，只差 0.02 dB；靈敏度落在熱雜訊（−101.17 dBm）之上 6 dB，正是 $NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}} = 7 - 1$ 的結果。

**算例 2（由 3GPP 參考靈敏度反推）**　已知：TS 38.104 表 7.2.2-1 規定廣域基地臺（Wide Area BS）在通道頻寬 5、10、15 MHz、15 kHz 子載波間隔、參考量測通道 G-FR1-A1-1 下的參考靈敏度為 −101.7 dBm；同一份規格表 A.1-1 載明該參考通道配置 25 個資源區塊、QPSK、碼率 1/3。求這個數字隱含的 $NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$。

1. 頻寬：$B = 25\times180\ \text{kHz} = 4.5$ MHz，$10\log_{10}(4.5\times10^6) = 66.53$ dB。
2. 熱雜訊功率：$-173.975 + 66.532 = -107.44$ dBm。
3. 差值：$-101.7 - (-107.44) = 5.74$ dB。

也就是說，這項最低要求相當於「雜訊指數、該參考通道所需 SNR 與實作餘量」合計約 5.7 dB。合理性檢查：QPSK、碼率 1/3 所需的 SNR 在 0 dB 上下，剩下幾 dB 留給接收機雜訊指數，量級合理。要注意規格值是測試用的最低要求，實際產品通常優於此值；規格並未在該表中把 5.7 dB 拆成各項，拆法屬於推估。

#### 常見錯誤

- **頻寬單位沒換成 Hz**：把 20 MHz 當成 20 代入，$10\log_{10}B$ 只得 13 dB，少了 60 dB。
- **用通道頻寬而不是占用頻寬**：窄頻配置（例如只配幾個資源區塊）的雜訊頻寬小得多，靈敏度會好很多；上行鏈路預算尤其要注意。
- **正負號方向弄反**：$NF$ 與正的 $\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ 都讓靈敏度數值變大（變差）；看到「−110 dBm 比 −100 dBm 好」要能立即反應。
- **把 −174 dBm/Hz 當成不變的常數**：它是 290 K 的值；另外 −174 是 dBm/Hz，若改用 dBW/Hz 則是 −204。

#### 和其他小節的關係

$kTB$ 與雜訊指數的定義、多級串接的總雜訊指數見 3.1.5；$\text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ 與調變編碼方式的關係可對照 3.1.6 與 3.2 各小節。干擾存在時要搭配 3.3.3 的干擾餘量。本節的 $S_{\min}$ 會直接代入 3.3.5 的最大允許路徑損耗，並在 3.3.6 分別計算終端與基地臺兩端。

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.104 V18.15.0 第 7.2 節表 7.2.2-1（廣域基地臺參考靈敏度）與附錄 A 表 A.1-1（參考量測通道 G-FR1-A1-1 參數）；3GPP TS 38.101-1 V18.15.0 表 5.3.2-1（最大傳輸頻寬配置 $N_{\text{RB}}$）；Pozar, *Microwave Engineering*，雜訊與雜訊指數的討論；Rappaport, *Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice*，鏈路預算與雜訊指數的討論。

The product of bandwidth and achievable spectral efficiency can then be larger in 20 MHz than in 80 MHz at very low SNR, while close to the access point the 80 MHz channel still wins.

**詳解**　Capacity in the low-SNR regime grows roughly linearly with SNR, so trading bandwidth for SNR can pay off at the edge.

**出處**　C. E. Shannon, "A Mathematical Theory of Communication," Bell System Technical Journal, 1948 (capacity versus bandwidth and SNR).

</details>

**3.3.4S023**　Written with $E_b/N_0$, the sensitivity formula contains $R_b$ but no bandwidth. Why does the bandwidth disappear, and when does it come back?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　$S = \text{SNR}\cdot N_0 B$ and $\text{SNR} = (E_b/N_0)(R_b/B)$, so $B$ cancels and $S = (E_b/N_0)\,N_0 R_b$: the energy needed per bit times bits per second. **詳解**　$\text{SNR}\cdot N_0 B = (E_b/N_0)(R_b/B)\,N_0 B = E_b R_b$.

**出處**　B. Sklar, Digital Communications: Fundamentals and Applications ($E_b/N_0$ and matched filtering).

It is a conformance minimum under test conditions for one specific channel (QPSK, rate 1/3, 106 blocks), not the sensitivity for every format, and real products are usually better. Feeder losses outside the connector are not included.

**詳解**　The requirement fixes only the sum $NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ (5.87 dB here); the split between the two is not specified.

**出處**　3GPP TS 38.104 V18.15.0, clauses 7.2.1 and 7.2.2.

</details>

**3.3.4S050**　For the Local Area class, a student computes the implied $NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$ as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected value in dB to two decimal places.

#### 情境題：Comparing receivers

**Scenario | Choosing an NB-IoT module for smart meters**

A utility compares two NB-IoT modules for smart meters, both receiving a 180 kHz channel. Module A: noise figure 5 dB, demodulator required SNR −4 dB, implementation loss 1.0 dB. Module B: noise figure 7 dB, advanced demodulator with required SNR −6.5 dB, implementation loss 2.0 dB. Implementation loss adds to the required SNR. Use −174 dBm/Hz.

**3.3.4S056**　Find the sensitivity of module A in dBm, rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−119.45 dBm

**詳解**　$10\log_{10}(180\,000) = 52.5527$ dB-Hz.
$S_A = -174 + 52.5527 + 5 + (-4) + 1.0 = -119.4473$ dBm ≈ −119.45 dBm.

**出處**　D. M. Pozar, Microwave Engineering ($kT_0BF$); T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (receiver sensitivity).

</details>

**3.3.4S057**　Which module is more sensitive, and by how many dB? Round to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Module A, by 0.50 dB

**詳解**　$S_B = -174 + 52.5527 + 7 + (-6.5) + 2.0 = -118.9473$ dBm.
$S_B - S_A = -118.9473 - (-119.4473) = 0.50$ dB, so A is 0.50 dB more sensitive. (Directly: A sums to $5 - 4 + 1 = 2.0$ dB, B to $7 - 6.5 + 2 = 2.5$ dB.)

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (receiver sensitivity).

</details>

**3.3.4S058**　Module B's datasheet emphasises its "advanced demodulator", A's its "low noise figure". Why can neither number alone rank the modules?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　With the same bandwidth, sensitivity depends on the sum $NF + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}} + \text{implementation loss}$. A 2 dB worse noise figure can be more than compensated by a better demodulator, or vice versa; here the totals are 2.0 dB versus 2.5 dB. Only the complete sum (or a measured sensitivity under identical conditions) allows a comparison.

**詳解**　Bandwidth term identical; compare $5 - 4 + 1 = 2.0$ dB with $7 - 6.5 + 2 = 2.5$ dB.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (link budget).

</details>

**3.3.4S059**　A third vendor advertises "−129 dBm sensitivity". What must be known before comparing it with A and B?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The conditions behind it: the bandwidth (for example a 15 kHz or 3.75 kHz single tone instead of 180 kHz), the data rate and format, the number of repetitions used, and the error-rate or throughput criterion. NB-IoT gains sensitivity through narrow tones and repetition, so a much better number may simply reflect a much slower configuration, not a better receiver.

**詳解**　E.g. a 15 kHz tone instead of 180 kHz alone gives $10\log_{10}(12) = 10.8$ dB.

**出處**　3GPP TS 36.211 (NB-IoT numerology and repetitions); T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice.

</details>

**3.3.4S060**　For a deep-basement meter the network uses 16 repetitions, which with ideal combining add the received energy of all copies. An engineer computes module A's sensitivity with repetitions as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected sensitivity in dBm to two decimal places.
1. Single-transmission sensitivity: −119.45 dBm.
2. Repetition gain: $20\log_{10}(16) = 24.08$ dB.
3. Sensitivity with repetitions: $-119.45 - 24.08 = -143.53$ dBm.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 2 is wrong: combining 16 copies adds 16 times the signal energy, a power (energy) ratio, so the gain is $10\log_{10}(16)$ = 12.04 dB, not $20\log_{10}$. Corrected sensitivity: −131.49 dBm.

**詳解**　$-119.4473 - 12.0412 = -131.4885$ dBm ≈ −131.49 dBm.

**出處**　Definition of the decibel for power ratios; B. Sklar, Digital Communications: Fundamentals and Applications (energy accumulation over repeated symbols).

</details>

**Scenario | Receive diversity at a macro gNB**

A macro gNB measures a sensitivity of −101.0 dBm per antenna with a single receive branch. The operator considers two-branch and four-branch maximum-ratio combining (MRC). Assume ideal MRC in AWGN with independent noise in each branch, so that the required power per branch falls by $10\log_{10}N$ for $N$ branches. The input impedance is 50 Ω.

**3.3.4S061**　Find the per-branch sensitivity with two-branch MRC, in dBm rounded to two decimal places.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　−104.01 dBm

**詳解**　$-101.0 - 10\log_{10}2 = -101.0 - 3.0103 = -104.0103$ dBm ≈ −104.01 dBm.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (maximal ratio combining).

</details>

**3.3.4S062**　A spreadsheet folds the 3 dB MRC gain into the noise figure ("effective NF = NF − 3 dB"). Why is that bad practice?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　The noise figure describes the noise added by one receiver chain and cannot be lower than 0 dB in a meaningful sense; MRC is a separate signal-processing gain obtained by combining several chains. Mixing them hides the assumption, makes the budget hard to audit (the gain depends on the number of branches, their correlation and the channel) and can lead to counting the gain twice when diversity is also included in the required SNR. It should be listed as its own gain row.

**詳解**　Keep $NF$ per chain; add "diversity/combining gain" separately.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (diversity techniques).

</details>

**3.3.4S063**　In a fading channel, is the benefit of MRC larger or smaller than the 3 dB AWGN array gain?

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Usually larger. Besides adding the signal powers coherently (array gain), independent branches rarely fade at the same time, so the probability of a deep fade falls sharply (diversity gain). At low outage targets the reduction in required fade margin can exceed 10 dB for two branches, far more than 3 dB. The AWGN figure is a lower bound used in sensitivity specifications.

**詳解**　Compare single-branch and two-branch Rayleigh outage behaviour: outage probability falls from proportional to $1/M$ to proportional to $1/M^2$.

**出處**　T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (diversity in fading channels).

</details>

**3.3.4S064**　For four-branch MRC an engineer writes the following. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected per-branch sensitivity in dBm to two decimal places.
1. Combining gain for four branches: $\log_{10}(4) = 0.60$ dB.
2. Sensitivity: $-101.0 - 0.60 = -101.60$ dBm.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

**答案**　Step 1 is wrong: the factor 10 of the decibel definition is missing; the gain is $10\log_{10}4$ = 6.02 dB. Corrected sensitivity: −107.02 dBm.

**詳解**　$-101.0 - 6.0206 = -107.0206$ dBm ≈ −107.02 dBm.

**出處**　Definition of the decibel; T. S. Rappaport, Wireless Communications: Principles and Practice (maximal ratio combining).

</details>

**3.3.4S065**　A technician converts the two-branch per-branch sensitivity (−104.01 dBm) into an rms voltage at the 50 Ω input as follows. Identify the wrong step, explain why, and give the corrected voltage in microvolts to three decimal places.
1. $P = 10^{-104.0103/10}$ mW $= 3.97164\times10^{-14}$ W.

</details>
