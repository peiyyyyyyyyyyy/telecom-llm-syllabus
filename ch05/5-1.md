## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What are the two main phases involved in building the DeepSense 6G dataset?
>
> A. Data generation and data verification
> B. Data collection and data processing
> C. Data synchronization and data filtering
> D. Data alignment and data validation

正解：B. Data collection and data processing

---

**【srsRANBench】**

> What is the purpose of the get_kpis function?
>
> A. To get KPIs from gnb, ue and viavi
> B. To get KPIs from gnb and ue
> C. To get KPIs from gnb and viavi
> D. To get KPIs from gnb

正解：A. To get KPIs from gnb, ue and viavi

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the purpose of using floating cars (FCs) in traffic sensing?
>
> A. To adjust the patrolling area for taxies
> B. To provide more data for better traffic sensing
> C. To increase the coverage of taxies' traces
> D. To reduce the estimation error in traffic matrix completion
> E. To improve the accuracy of GPS reports from taxies

正解：B. To provide more data for better traffic sensing
