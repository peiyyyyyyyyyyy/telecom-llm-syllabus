# 5.4.1 正常基線

## 練習題

**1.** According to TS 28.104 §7.2.8, what does “statistics of the management data” include?

- A. Only the mean value of a metric over a past period.
- B. Only the prediction of future metric values.
- C. Statistical calculation on the data over a past period and/or using a sliding window.
- D. Configuration of performance metric thresholds.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：C。The specification says statistics include calculation over a past period and/or a sliding‑window procedure.

</details>

**2.** What may a prediction of management data involve, as described in the same specification?

- A. Only a single future value without any statistical context.
- B. Predictions over a future period and/or using a sliding window.
- C. Adjustment of alarm thresholds based on past data.
- D. Calculation of the mean and standard deviation.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The text explicitly mentions predictions over a future period and/or using a sliding window.

</details>

**3.** Why might a consumer configure a performance metric threshold according to the use case description?

- A. To generate alarms regardless of traffic conditions.
- B. To monitor threshold crossings of performance metric values.
- C. To replace statistical analysis entirely.
- D. To predict future traffic without using past data.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states the purpose of configuring thresholds is to monitor threshold crossings.

</details>

**4.** How can correlation information (statistics and/or predictions) be used with thresholds?

- A. To permanently fix thresholds at a static value.
- B. To assess threshold configuration and support auto‑configuration.
- C. To eliminate the need for any threshold at all.
- D. To increase the number of alarms generated.

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The reference indicates that statistics and predictions can assess and auto‑configure thresholds.

</details>

**5.** Which requirement label corresponds to the MDA capability to produce statistics for requested management data?

- A. REQ‑SPM\_MDA‑01
- B. REQ‑SPM\_MDA‑02
- C. REQ‑SPM\_MDA‑0A
- D. REQ‑SPM\_MDA‑03

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。Requirement REQ‑SPM\_MDA‑02 explicitly mentions the ability to produce statistics.

</details>
