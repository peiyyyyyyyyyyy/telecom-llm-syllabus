# 5.4.4 流量與容量的變化

所屬：[5.4 指標趨勢](../5-4.md)

## 正文

In a 5G radio access network the amount of traffic that a cell can carry is limited by the number of physical resource blocks (PRBs) that are available on the downlink (DL) and uplink (UL).  Monitoring how many of these PRBs are actually occupied gives a direct view of **traffic volume** (how much data is being transmitted) and **capacity utilisation** (how close the cell is to its theoretical maximum).  When the PRB usage approaches the upper limit, the cell may experience congestion, degraded quality of service, or may need additional resources such as carrier aggregation or a new cell.  Conversely, persistently low usage can indicate over‑provisioning.  Therefore, traffic‑and‑capacity trends are a core element of alarm management and performance optimisation.  The 3GPP specification TS 28.552 defines the PRB usage counters that are the standard way to quantify these trends【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2】.

### Definitions and Formulas
| Symbol | Meaning | Unit / Range |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| $U_{\text{DL}}$ | Downlink PRB usage (percentage) | 0 – 100 % |
| $U_{\text{UL}}$ | Uplink PRB usage (percentage) | 0 – 100 % |
| $N_{\text{used}}$ | Number of PRBs actually used for traffic (including full blocks and partial blocks) | integer |
| $N_{\text{total}}$ | Total number of PRBs that could be scheduled during the measurement interval | integer |
| $T$ | Measurement time interval (e.g., 1 s, 1 min) | seconds |
| $M$ | Integer result reported by the O‑M interface | 0 – 100 |

The specification states that the usage is obtained as  

$$
U_{\text{DL}} = \frac{N_{\text{used}}}{N_{\text{total}}}\times 100\quad\text{and}\quad
U_{\text{UL}} = \frac{N_{\text{used}}}{N_{\text{total}}}\times 100,
$$  

where the ratio is averaged over the time period $T$【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑c】.  The reported value $M$ is a **single integer** between 0 and 100【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑d】.  

The measurement names are  

* **RRU.PrbDl** – DL PRB usage for all traffic【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑e】  
* **RRU.PrbUl** – UL PRB usage for all traffic【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.2‑e】

Optional dimensions (PLMN, S‑NSSAI, QoS resource type) can be added, but the base counter already conveys the overall traffic‑capacity picture.  The counters are defined for the **NRCellDU** entity【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑f】 and are **valid for packet‑switched traffic**【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑g】.

### Worked Example
Consider a gNodeB cell that, during a 10‑second measurement window, has 100 PRBs available on the DL.  Over that interval the scheduler reports that a total of 1 200 PRB‑units were transmitted (including full blocks and partial allocations).  The average DL PRB usage is  

$$
U_{\text{DL}} = \frac{1\,200}{100 \times 10}\times 100 = \frac{1\,200}{1\,000}\times 100 = 120\%.
$$

Because the definition caps the reported value at 100 %, the O‑M interface will send **M = 100**.  This indicates that the cell was **fully loaded** for the whole period, and any additional traffic would be blocked or delayed.  

On the UL side, suppose only 300 PRB‑units were used while the same 100 PRBs were available for 10 s.  

$$
U_{\text{UL}} = \frac{300}{1\,000}\times 100 = 30\%.
$$

The reported UL value would be **M = 30**, showing ample uplink capacity.  

Network operators can plot $U_{\text{DL}}$ and $U_{\text{UL}}$ over time; a rising trend toward 100 % in the DL dimension is a clear **capacity‑stress indicator** that may trigger alarms or trigger traffic‑steering actions.  

### Practical Notes and Common Mistakes

* **Confusing raw PRB counts with percentages.** The counters $N_{\text{used}}$ and $N_{\text{total}}$ are raw numbers; the reported KPI is the **percentage** after averaging. Reporting the raw count directly would violate the specification.  

* **Ignoring optional dimensions.** While per‑PLMN or per‑QoS breakdowns are optional, using only the aggregate value can mask congestion that affects a single QoS class (e.g., GBR traffic).  

* **Assuming the value can exceed 100 %.** The spec defines the result as a **single integer value from 0 to 100**【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑d】; any calculation above 100 % must be capped.  

* **Applying the metric to circuit‑switched traffic.** The counters are explicitly **valid for packet‑switched traffic**【TS 28.552 §5.1.1.2.1‑g】; using them for legacy CS services would be unsupported.  

* **Neglecting the measurement interval.** The averaging period $T$ influences the smoothness of the trend. A very short $T$ (e.g., 1 ms) may show high variability, while a long $T$ (e.g., 1 min) may hide short spikes that could still cause user‑experience issues. For short‑term spikes, the **distribution of DL total PRB usage** counters (bins) are more appropriate, but they belong to a different subsection.  

By consistently tracking DL and UL PRB usage, engineers obtain a quantitative view of traffic growth, capacity consumption, and the need for corrective actions such as load balancing, additional spectrum, or slice re‑allocation.

## 練習題

**1.** Which of the following best describes the unit and range of the DL PRB usage measurement reported by the O‑M interface?

- A. A floating‑point number from 0.0 to 1.0
- B. An integer from 0 to 100 representing a percentage
- C. A count of PRBs used, ranging from 0 to the total number of PRBs
- D. A binary flag indicating overload (0 = normal, 1 = overload)

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification states the result is a single integer value from 0 to 100, i.e., a percentage.

</details>

**2.** What is the official measurement name that indicates downlink PRB usage for all traffic?

- A. RRU.PrbDl
- B. RRU.PrbUl
- C. RRU.PrbTotDlDist
- D. RRU.PrbTotUlDist

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。The name RRU.PrbDl is defined as the DL PRB usage for all traffic.

</details>

**3.** For which type of traffic are the PRB usage counters defined as valid?

- A. Circuit‑switched traffic only
- B. Packet‑switched traffic
- C. Both circuit‑ and packet‑switched traffic
- D. Only control‑plane signaling traffic

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The specification explicitly limits the counters to packet‑switched traffic.

</details>

**4.** According to TS 28.554, the KPI type used for “Virtualised Resource Utilisation of Network Slice Instance” is:

- A. MEAN
- B. RATIO
- C. COUNTER
- D. ENUMERATION

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。The KPI is defined as a percentage and the type is “Ratio”.

</details>
