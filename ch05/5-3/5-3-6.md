# 5.3.6 告警類型與可能原因

所屬：[5.3 告警](../5-3.md)

## 正文

In a 3GPP system every abnormal condition that can be detected by a network entity (NE) is reported as an **alarm** (also called a fault).  The alarm type tells the operator *what* kind of problem occurred (e.g. communication, equipment) and therefore drives the subsequent troubleshooting steps.  Because operators must prioritize limited resources, knowing the exact type and probable cause of an alarm is essential for rapid restoration, for avoiding unnecessary manual interventions, and for keeping the network’s quality of service within contractual limits.  The 3GPP specification requires that alarms be generated **immediately** when a fault occurs, and that they contain a rich set of attributes that describe the fault in detail [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Standardised fault classification

The specification adopts the ITU‑T X.733 classification.  For each detected fault the NE shall indicate:

* **Fault type** – one of *communication, environmental, equipment, processing error, QoS* [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  
* **Severity** – one of *indeterminate, warning, minor, major, critical* [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  
* **Probable cause** – a textual description of why the fault happened.  
* **Nature of the fault** – either **ADAC** (detectable + clearable) or **ADMC** (detectable + not clearable) [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

These attributes are part of the mandatory information set that must accompany every alarm (device/resource, smallest replaceable unit, affected functionality, etc.) [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Detection mechanisms

Alarms are produced by **autonomous self‑check circuits or procedures** that monitor measurements, counters and thresholds inside the NE [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  The thresholds may be manufacturer‑defined or derived from performance data collected by an Element Management System (EMS).  The detection logic applies to **both active and standby components**, ensuring that a failure in a backup line is reported as soon as it occurs [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Fault occurrence and clearing conditions

Most faults have **well‑defined occurrence and clearing conditions**.  When the condition is met, the NE declares an ADAC fault; when the condition disappears, the NE clears the alarm using the same detection logic [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  Some faults **lack a clearing condition**; these are classified as **ADMC faults** and require manual operator intervention to clear [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  An example is a software process restart caused by internal inconsistencies – the process restarts, operation resumes, but the underlying cause remains until an operator fixes it [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Short‑lived faults – “no‑action” alarms

A subset of faults exists only for a single sampling interval.  If a measured value crosses a threshold but returns to normal in the next interval, the fault is considered transient and **no short‑term action** is required from either the NE or the operator [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].  The specification cites this situation as an example where a NE detects a threshold crossing and the next sample stays within limits [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Worked example

Consider a base station (eNodeB) that monitors **CPU utilisation**.  

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Threshold for overload alarm | 85 % |
| Sampling interval | 10 s |
| Measured CPU at *t* = 30 s | 90 % |
| Measured CPU at *t* = 40 s | 78 % |

1. At *t* = 30 s the measurement exceeds the threshold, so the NE generates an alarm.  
2. The alarm type is **equipment overload** (a subtype of *equipment*), the severity is **warning** (as defined by X.733), and the nature is **ADAC** because the overload condition can be cleared when CPU drops below the threshold.  
3. At *t* = 40 s the CPU falls back under 85 %, satisfying the clearing condition; the NE automatically clears the alarm.  

This scenario matches the specification’s description of a fault that “lasted for a short period of time only and then disappeared” [TS 32.111-1 §4.1.1].

### Common mistakes and practical notes

| Mistake | Why it occurs | Correct practice |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Treating every alarm as ADMC** | Assuming that any alarm needs manual clearance. | Verify the fault’s clearing condition; many alarms are ADAC and auto‑clear. |
| **Ignoring the fault type** | Relying only on severity to prioritize. | Use the ITU‑T X.733 type to route alarms to the appropriate functional team (e.g., environmental alarms to facilities). |
| **Missing transient faults** | Filtering out short‑lived threshold crossings as noise. | Log transient violations; they may indicate intermittent issues that need attention. |
| **Applying thresholds only to active components** | Forgetting that standby units are also monitored. | Ensure self‑check procedures cover both active and standby components. |
| **Omitting probable cause** | Leaving the “probable cause” field blank. | Populate the field with the best‑guess description, as required by the mandatory alarm information set. |

By adhering to the classification, detection, and reporting rules defined in TS 32.111‑1, engineers can ensure that alarms are both meaningful and actionable, reducing mean‑time‑to‑repair (MTTR) and improving overall network reliability.

## 練習題

**1.** 下列哪一項 **不是** ITU‑T X.733 所定義的故障類型？

- A. communication
- B. environmental
- C. equipment
- D. hardware

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。ITU‑T X.733 列出的類型包括 communication、environmental、equipment、processing error 與 QoS，未提及 hardware。

</details>

**2.** ADMC 故障的全稱是什麼？

- A. Automatic Detection and Manual Clearing
- B. Automatic Detection and Automatic Clearing
- C. Automatic Diagnosis and Manual Correction
- D. Automatic Diagnosis and Manual Clearing

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：A。文中將此類故障稱為 ADMC，意指自動偵測但需手動清除。

</details>

**3.** 根據 ITU‑T X.733，以下哪個不是定義的嚴重度等級？

- A. minor
- B. major
- C. critical
- D. fatal

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。嚴重度等級列為 indeterminate、warning、minor、major、critical，沒有 fatal。

</details>

**4.** 誰必須手動介入才能清除 ADMC 故障？

- A. 網路實體 (NE) 自行清除
- B. 系統操作員
- C. 自動診斷模組
- D. 不需要清除，故障自行消失

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：B。文字明確指出只能由系統操作員手動清除 ADMC 故障。

</details>

**5.** 以下哪項 **不是** 故障偵測過程必須提供的資訊？

- A. 受影響的設備/資源/檔案/功能單元
- B. 故障類型
- C. 故障的可能原因
- D. 廠商名稱

<details><summary>看答案</summary>

正解：D。規範列出需提供的資訊項目，未包含廠商名稱。

</details>
