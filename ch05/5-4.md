## GSMA 例題（訓練集）

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is the purpose of user data congestion analytics? [3GPP Release 18]
>
> A. To collect measurements from the OAM
> B. To determine the area where a specific UE is located
> C. To provide statistics or predictions or both related to congestion experienced while transferring user data
> D. To subscribe to user data congestion analytics

正解：C. To provide statistics or predictions or both related to congestion experienced while transferring user data

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is anomaly detection?
>
> A. The process of identifying data instances that are considered normal
> B. The process of detecting data instances that significantly deviate from the majority of data instances
> C. The process of predicting future data instances based on historical data
> D. The process of clustering similar data instances together
> E. The process of transforming data instances into a lower-dimensional space

正解：B. The process of detecting data instances that significantly deviate from the majority of data instances

---

**【TeleQnA】**

> What is an anomaly?
>
> A. An observation that lies in a low probability region under the ground-truth law of normality.
> B. An observation that lies in a high probability region under the ground-truth law of normality.

正解：A. An observation that lies in a low probability region under the ground-truth law of normality.
